Chapter 999: Cultivating Techniques is Also Cultivating the Mind

Ning Zhuo's gaze began to lose focus.

He wanted to catch it all!

Every piece of comprehension was fresh and bright, like a newly peeled spirit fruit - sweet-scented, dewy, and making him loath to let go.

Buzz.

Buzz, buzz, buzz...

Countless small mirrors shook violently at the same time.

Ning Zhuo's face suddenly turned pale. His stomach churned, and his head spun dizzily as if he had been spun around in circles countless times. His shoulders twitched slightly; light and shadow overlapped before his eyes, while wind, earth, and forging patterns intertwined into a soft yet heavy, colorful curtain.

Ning Zhuo abruptly opened his eyes, bent over, and propped his hands against the ground, his fingertips digging into the earth.

He dry-heaved a couple of times. Large drops of cold sweat rolled down his forehead, his cheeks lost all color, and his lips became so pale they were nearly transparent.

The second attempt had failed!

This time, there was none of the pain from his previous attempt, but a far deeper sense of vertigo.

It was like an infant being rocked in a cradle for too long; heaven and earth turned upside down, and his mind consequently scattered into a cloud of fog.

Ning Zhuo rested for a long time before the dark shadows before his eyes slowly receded.

"Heaven and earth is the cradle, and all creations serve as enlightenment!"

"So the cradle sways. Heaven and earth can change their faces in an instant, bringing a comprehensive experience."

Without a doubt, this was an intense shock.

Ning Zhuo closed his eyes and slowly steadied his mind.

He deeply realized that while the Myriad Phenomena Cradle seemed gentle, it actually demanded an exceedingly high state of mind from the Cultivator.

If a Cultivator harbored too much doubt in their heart, they wouldn't be able to resist digging into their past knowledge, and the cradle would immediately shatter.

If their greed was too heavy and they tried to take away every principle they saw, they would be taught by all creations until they grew dizzy and disoriented. Their divine sense would be drained dry, and their divine sea would collapse on a massive scale.

"This method requires the Cultivator to be like an infant."

"When an infant sees a bell, they only look at the bell. When they smell the aroma of food, they only smell the food. When the wind comes, they laugh; when the wind leaves, they do not chase it. They know nothing of possession, nor do they know loss, let alone think of collecting all the sounds of heaven and earth into their embrace."

"The greed in my heart caused my failure!"

Ning Zhuo was practicing a Technique, but the cultivation of a Technique still pertained to one's state of mind.

Without a qualified state of mind, there was no way to successfully cast the Myriad Phenomena Cradle!

The true essence of Cultivation lay exactly in this.

Alchemical cultivators, martial cultivators, technique cultivators...

Though seemingly different paths, in reality, all of them required tempering and elevating the Cultivator's state of mind through practice.

Failure brought reflection, and reflection allowed Ning Zhuo to brush the dust off his state of mind once more.

Ning Zhuo suddenly understood one of his shortcomings.

"Cultivating techniques is also cultivating the mind."

"Previously, I didn't practice Five Elements Techniques extensively, relying instead on the Spirit Gods of the Five Viscera Temple to take a shortcut. Without massive amounts of practice, I could still cast Five Elements Techniques with exquisite proficiency, commanding them as easily as my own arms."

"This seemed clever, like I had gained a massive advantage. But in reality, by not building a solid foundation step by step, I paid a huge, invisible price."

"If ordinary people proceed steadily and practice Techniques bitterly in large quantities, the tempering of their state of mind would be immense."

Drawing inferences from this, Ning Zhuo reflected on his own cultivation process and finally realized what he had lost. Taking a shortcut was also taking a detour!

"In the future, when time is abundant, I must make up for this one by one."

Having comprehended this point, the restlessness in Ning Zhuo's heart dissipated by another layer.

Ning Zhuo sat quietly for a long time. His breathing slowed down inch by inch.

In his divine sea, those small mirrors were restored to their complete state.

"Myriad Phenomena Cradle." Ning Zhuo cast the Technique for the third time.

As his voice fell, a mist of white light once again rippled across his divine sea.

The sharp glint in Ning Zhuo's eyes completely retracted.

His eyes were blank and pure, reflecting a speck of light nearby and a wisp of dust afar. In his gaze, there was no defensiveness, no calculation, no need to prove himself, and no urgency.

He just watched.

The wind passed by his ear first. Hearing the wind, Ning Zhuo only listened to the wind.

It carried a hint of coolness, a hint of lightness, and a hint of the carefree nature of brushing past all things without leaving a trace. A very small comprehension sprouted in Ning Zhuo's heart, like a dewdrop hanging on the tip of a blade of grass.

The wind passed, and the dewdrop remained there. The young man did not reach out to grab it.

He lowered his head slightly, and the earth beneath his feet leapt into his vision. Ning Zhuo felt his body being steadily supported.

That bearing was rich, slow, and tolerant, like an old man reaching out to hold a child - the movement wasn't beautiful, but it was sufficiently steady.

Ning Zhuo understood the feeling of being "grounded," and he only absorbed that feeling.

In the sky, the forging patterns softly chimed.

Ding.

The sound was very light.

From it, Ning Zhuo heard "movement." One sound was enough. He no longer chased the bell's chime into its depths, nor did he urge the next chime to arrive faster.

Then, the cradle gently swayed once.

Heaven and earth tilted slightly along with it.

This time, Ning Zhuo did not panic, nor did he give rise to greed; instead, he let nature take its course!

When the wind revealed the shape of a hand, he let that transparent hand stroke his forehead.

When the earth revealed the shape of a quilt, he let that thick swaddle wrap around his body.

When the forging patterns revealed the shape of a bell, he listened to that clear chime gradually fade away.

All creations approached, then walked away.

Each brought him a new feeling and touch, and he only learned a tiny bit from each.

"Of the heavens, earth, mountains, and seas, I only take a single scoop to drink."

The second sway arrived leisurely.

The cradle's amplitude was slightly larger, causing the watery light in Ning Zhuo's divine sea to gently ripple outward. The other side of the wind emerged. It didn't just move; it also transmitted. It transmitted the chime of the bell, and it transmitted the subtlest tremors of heaven and earth.

Ning Zhuo's heart sank, but quickly relaxed again.

The third sway.

The other side of the earth emerged. It didn't just support; it also accommodated. Sweat, dust, and scattered Literary Qi were all silently borne by it. Ning Zhuo's chest warmed slightly, as if someone was telling him not to fear falling.

The fourth sway.

The other side of the forging patterns emerged. After the bell's chime came the sound of a hammer. After gentleness came tempering. It turned out the bell on the cradle could also transform into a hammer that forged the Divine Soul.

Ning Zhuo's mind trembled slightly, but he steadily withstood it.

Right before the fifth sway could arrive, Ning Zhuo already felt dizzy.

His divine sense was depleting extremely fast; thoughts were generated, then rapidly consumed. The myriad creations of heaven and earth before his eyes were still bright and gentle, still willing to lean down and teach him, but he already knew that today, he could only learn up to this point.

Thus, he proactively retracted the technique.

The cradle slowly came to a halt.

The wind left first.

The bell chimes of the forging patterns quieted down.

The earth beneath his feet released its swaddling embrace, leaving behind only a trace of profound, lingering warmth.

His past knowledge, which had been set aside at the bottom of the water, began to awaken one by one.

The Five Elements returned to their places, like five lanterns being relit.

Mechanisms hummed back to life, gears gently turning.

The path of formations unfolded; lines quietly extended, paving the entire world.

Alchemical fire flickered, as if the control over its heat had regained its exact measure.

His various insights into talismans, blood meridians, and spirit communication also returned to their respective places.

Ning Zhuo became Ning Zhuo again.

But he knew he was already a little bit different.

Ning Zhuo gently clenched his fist, then slowly released it. His face was still pale, his forehead was covered in cold sweat, and his divine sea felt terribly empty. Yet, in the depths of his eyes lingered a bit of infant-like clarity, a hint of joy from seeing heaven and earth for the first time.

The third time, he finally succeeded!

"Then next..."

With limited time, Ning Zhuo rushed to regulate his breathing. He popped Medicinal Pills continuously and circulated his Cultivation Art with all his might, truly racing against time.

Not long after, he once again "swallowed" the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill.

In his divine sea, a sun appeared.

"So big, so hot, so bright!" Ning Zhuo successfully cast the technique and was instantly dumbfounded.

Boom!

The red-gold true intent poured down, like a golden waterfall hanging upside down from the very edge of the Nine Heavens.

Vast and mighty, roaring like thunder, it carried the painstaking efforts, cultivation, pride, pain, and oaths of past generation sect masters of the Pure Yang Palace, forcefully pouring into Ning Zhuo's mouth.

Ning Zhuo's body stiffened on the spot.

Immediately after, he began to tremble violently all over, as if struck by lightning.

His neck jerked forcefully backward, and his eyes rolled back so sharply that only a sliver of his black irises quivered at the top of his sockets. His entire face turned terrifyingly pale.

Subconsciously, his fingers dug into the ground, his fingernails scraping out a harsh, thin sound as he barely managed to steady his posture.

A muffled groan tore from his throat. The veins on his forehead bulged, and beads of sweat rapidly broke out on his large head.

He was like an infant lying in a cradle; he had only wanted a sip of water, but a vast, mighty river was forced down his throat.

There was simply too much Pure Yang true intent!

Ning Zhuo's cradle shook frantically. The red-gold waterfall poured in madly, filling his divine sea to the bursting point, burning his thoughts dry, and causing his entire body to convulse as if he was having a seizure.

"I... I was too careless..."

Great Noon Heaven Terrace Martial Arena.

Yang fire surged, fiercely burning six yin graves. Under the blinding light of the fire, the yin graves were constantly consumed, yet they were continuously replenished by the yin soil thrown in by Liang Ming.

"After this battle is fought, all my past accumulated wealth will be emptied." Liang Ming abruptly raised his head, his eyes densely covered in bloodshot veins like a spiderweb.

Having paid such a massive price, he finally seized an opportunity amidst the bitter fighting.

Above his head, his Nascent Soul manifested, raising the earth jade tablet for the seventh time.

Having fought to this point, his Nascent Soul's body was covered in dense cracks, looking like porcelain on the verge of shattering.

"Seven tablets return to their positions, burying the sun to form a grave. I invite the Sun-Burying Jade Sovereign to guard my seventh grave!"

The six yin graves vibrated simultaneously. The six gods - Bone Embracer, Lantern Hider, Sun Subduer, Ash Fetus, Mirror Corrupter, and Earth Belly - provided support at the same time.

The seventh grave rose!

In the center of this yin grave stood a black and yellow earth jade tablet, looking like an isolated peak.

The Sun-Burying Jade Sovereign emerged from beside the earth jade tablet. He wore profound black ceremonial robes as deep as the night sky, his facial features indistinct.

Chunyangzi met His gaze, and immediately before his eyes flashed various scenes: the Pure Yang Palace decaying and collapsing, the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar being reduced to ruins, and his own elderly body dying.

"Hahaha." Liang Ming laughed loudly. "Chunyangzi, witness my Divine Art - Seven Tablets Death Sacrifice!"

The entire arena erupted in an uproar.

Many spectating Cultivators couldn't help but stand up to watch the battle.

"A Divine Art?!"

"So he intends to cast the Divine Art: Seven Tablets Death Sacrifice. I have heard of this Divine Art before."

"Liang Ming lives in seclusion and calls himself a True Monarch. Sure enough, he possesses a Divine Art. After six days and six nights of battle, I thought his Divine Art was like Shi Heng's, unable to be used in combat."
