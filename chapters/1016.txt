Chapter 1016: Only Tonight Left

The Nine Palaces Formation Base that Shen Xi had taken the lead in setting up now extended from the southeast corner to the northwest corner. These formation bases were buried three feet below the surface, with only small cyan-white stakes exposed above ground. The upper ends of the stakes were carved with Nine Palaces symbols, which glowed with a faint watery light in the darkness.

Whenever an Artisan dragging formation artifacts passed by, the stakes would automatically shift half an inch to clear the path, only to return to their positions a moment later.

This formation was called the [Nine Palaces Illusory Bewildering Gate Formation], and its main purpose was to confuse enemies, disrupt their steps, and alter their directions. If an enemy breached the outer ring, the paths they saw and the directions they stepped in would be quietly scrambled.

The spirit trees planted by Lin Jinglong had already taken root in the outer ring.

One by one, the Cyan Wyrm Armor-Clasped Trees rose from the underground. Their trunks were not tall, but their branches were twisted and coiled, and scale-like cyan patterns appeared on the bark. They were connected to one another through Wood Element spiritual energy, forming a layer of the [Cyan Wyrm Coiled Root Defensive Wall]. In normal times, they were merely spirit trees, but in times of war, their roots would entangle to form a wall, their bark would transform into armor, and their branches would act like dragon tendons to strangle incoming foes.

Buried beneath the trees were numerous Wood Element precious materials, all of which had been sent over later by the Lin Clan. The quantity wasn’t earth-shattering, but it was just enough to perfectly supplement the foundation that the Naming Stronghold lacked the most at the moment.

Situ Xing’s side, on the other hand, had brought in a large amount of star-patterned formation artifacts.

Star-patterned iron pillars, starlight copper plates, and star-sand small pagodas were carefully placed one by one at high vantage points surrounding the Cloud Altar. Together, they formed a set of the [Big Dipper Star-Guiding Shadow-Revealing Restriction], which was even more potent at night. Once the starlight descended, any stealth, Shadow Escape, or soul escape techniques would leave faint shadow traces within the formation.

Chunyangzi was quite satisfied with this. Among foreign enemies, it was inevitable that some would excel at infiltration and assassination. Most of all, Chunyangzi was worried that Ning Zhuo would sneak in to scout the area. This layer of shadow-revealing restriction came at the perfect time.

Further inward was the framework of the Grand Formation that Chunyangzi had personally decided upon.

The [Three Luminaries Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Grand Formation]!

This was the main formation he had spent a heavy sum on, hiring Grand Formation Masters to work together and rush its completion. The formation was rooted in the original terrain of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar and was divided into three layers.

The outer layer was the [Yang-Patrolling Night-Illuminating Ring], which constantly gathered sun qi, starlight, and underground yang fire day and night, ensuring that the entire Cloud Altar was protected by a faint golden light film regardless of whether it was day or night. If ordinary yin entities or evil spirits approached, they would be revealed instantly, like thin ice touching a furnace.

The middle layer was the [Crimson Parasol Meridian-Locking Formation]. Using Crimson Parasol roots as the formation veins, it was buried into forty-nine Earth Qi nodes. They acted like a root network deeply planted in the earth, firmly locking down the Cloud Altar's earth veins to prevent the yang qi from leaking out or being easily severed by external forces. If an Earth Element cultivator attempted to invade from underground, they would be sensed by the Crimson Parasol roots at the very first moment.

The inner layer was the true key, named the [Sun-Supporting Light-Sealing Pavilion].

It did not focus on killing, but on concealment.

Once closed, the true appearance of the entire Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar would be enveloped layer by layer by the formation. An outsider looking down from high in the sky would only see the pavilions, pill rooms, Dao Fields, and courtyard walls built by the Naming Stronghold. The true yang qi convergence points, the yang-locking deep wells, the sun-supporting stone patterns, and the cloud-ascending hidden orifices would all be hidden within the Light-Sealing Pavilion.

This was the layer Chunyangzi cared about the most.

He narrowed his eyes, gazing down at the several earth vein openings beneath the central Cloud Altar that had not yet been completely sealed.

There, a crimson-gold mist was slowly rising. Within the mist, fragmented sun patterns occasionally appeared, as if countless tiny solar discs were rotating underground.

These spots were the true secrets of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

"Ning Zhuo didn't come."

Over these past few days, Ning Zhuo had never once come to inspect the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

This finally allowed the stone hanging in Chunyangzi’s heart to drop slightly.

If Ning Zhuo came, things would be troublesome.

"In the past, Ning Zhuo's Yang Path cultivation realm was shallow, so even if he looked, he might not have understood. But things are different now. The Pure Yang Ancestor Pill has been completely hollowed out by that brat."

Whenever he thought of this, Chunyangzi felt as though someone had gouged his chest with a blunt knife; the dull pain was excruciating.

Even now, he didn’t want to think too deeply about it.

"Ning Zhuo is already at the Yang Path Grandmaster realm! The pill’s medicinal power even contained the complete text of the Pure Yang Pill Scripture."

"If he is allowed to personally inspect the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, the secret of the Soul Formation fortuitous encounter will be completely exposed!"

Chunyangzi’s fingers slowly tightened within his sleeves.

"Fortunately, he didn't come."

A trace of relief flashed deep in Chunyangzi’s eyes.

Regardless of the reason, as long as Ning Zhuo missed tonight, it would be enough.

"This is the last night," he thought to himself, looking at the several unsealed formation openings below.

At this moment, a trusted subordinate hurried over to report.

He said respectfully, "Peak Master, the three shifts of manpower have finished their rotation. The Nine Palaces outer ring is nearly complete. The wood wall’s root system only needs a bit more spiritual water irrigation. The star-patterned formation artifacts provided by the Situ Clan are only missing the final seven, which are expected to arrive and be fully installed before midnight. Our people from Chunyang Peak are currently rushing to forge the final three formation locks for the Sun-Supporting Light-Sealing Pavilion, and it will definitely be done before dawn."

Chunyangzi nodded.

The trusted subordinate paused for a moment, then added, "It's just that rushing the work like this is consuming a tremendous amount of resources. The usage of Vermilion Yang Crystals, Golden Scale Fire Jade, and Crimson Heart Sand has already exceeded the original estimate by three times. The storehouse..."

Chunyangzi raised a hand to stop him.

"Keep using them."

The subordinate’s eyelids twitched slightly, and his voice grew somewhat hoarse. "Palace Master, those are the accumulations that Pure Yang Palace has saved up over many years."

Chunyangzi turned to look at him.

In the night wind, his face was pale, but his eyes were strikingly bright. "Pure Yang Palace has already joined the Myriad Manifestations Sect. Those accumulations sitting in the treasury are just dead objects. Once the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is established, Chunyang Peak will have its foundation."

The subordinate’s heart trembled, and he lowered his head. "This subordinate understands."

Chunyangzi looked into the distance again.

Countless Cultivators were still rushing about.

Some were sprinting while carrying formation plates, their faces drenched in sweat. Some knelt on the ground, using formation brushes to inch by inch trace formation patterns into the stone crevices. Others controlled furnaces, softening Crimson Parasol roots before swiftly pressing them into the underground veins. There were also over a dozen Golden Core cultivators stationed at various points, constantly using their spiritual energy to suppress the yang qi and prevent the underground fire intent from disrupting the formation bases before they stabilized.

Working day and night.

Rotating in three shifts!

At any cost!!

The results were naturally extremely fruitful.

As Chunyangzi watched all of this, he felt both exhaustion and an almost irrepressible elation in his heart.

"No sleep tonight," Chunyangzi said in a deep voice. "I will personally stand the final watch. Before all the formation openings, all the formation locks, and all the restrictions are closed, they must be reported to me."

The trusted subordinate immediately bowed. "Yes!"

Chunyangzi looked up at the sky.

Midnight was approaching.

In a few more hours, when dawn arrived, the Sun-Supporting Light-Sealing Pavilion would be completely closed.

At that time, if anyone wanted to see the full picture of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, he would have to personally open all the formation's concealments.

If Ning Zhuo wanted to look again, he would have to ask him, Chunyangzi, first!

Heavy Formation Peak.

The night enveloped the peak in extreme darkness.

Heavy Formation Peak was not as elegant and spirited as the other peaks. Its mountainside was broad and thick, layered on top of itself like countless formation plates stacked together. Looking at it at night, there weren’t many lights, but dim formation lights roamed within the mountain walls like the meridians of a giant beast.

Inside a quiet room at the summit, Dong Chen sat in front of a formation map.

He wore dark gray robes. His face was square, and his features were calm. Looking purely at his appearance, it was difficult for anyone to connect him with the Sect Master of the Myriad Manifestations Sect and the Peak Master of Heavy Formation Peak. He lacked an imposing edge, and even his breathing was very light, like a stone that had been submerged underwater all year round.

The formation map on the desk before him covered the entirety of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

On the map, crimson-gold dots of light were constantly increasing.

The outer ring Nine Palaces.

The Wood wall.

The Star restriction.

The Crimson Parasol earth veins.

The Sun-Supporting Light-Sealing Pavilion.

Lighting up bit by bit, closing bit by bit.

Dong Chen watched for a long time.

There was no wind in the quiet room, but the lamp flame flickered gently.

"Ah, Chunyangzi," Dong Chen sighed softly.

He hadn't expected Chunyangzi to be so decisive and bold.

After the five-win martial contest, the Naming Stronghold had only just moved into the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. People's hearts, resources, debt claims, and Cultivator deployments were still in complete disarray. According to common sense, they should have first sorted out their internal affairs, appeased their creditors, slowly laid down the basic infrastructure, and then gradually fortified everything layer by layer.

Yet, Chunyangzi directly threw a massive amount of wealth into rushing the work.

Ignoring material conservation, ignoring gradual progression, he went straight for closing up the defensive grand formation first!

The cost of such an approach was exceedingly high. The more they rushed, the more materials were wasted, the tighter the manpower deployment became, and the greater the wear and tear on formation artifacts. Many formation bases that could have been meticulously polished were forcibly stabilized using higher-grade precious materials just to meet the hurried schedule.

Dong Chen had investigated the matter very clearly.

Chunyangzi had even dug into his own pockets, taking out the heritage Pure Yang Palace had accumulated over countless years, and smashed it directly into the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

Only this way was the scale of the defensive formation forcibly elevated.

"Tonight is the last night."

Dong Chen watched the converging lines of light on the formation map and gently tapped his finger on the center of the Sun-Supporting Light-Sealing Pavilion.

"Once dawn passes, this place will be like an iron barrel. Wanting to make a move then will be doubly difficult."

His gaze was tranquil.

If this matter dragged on any longer, the arrangements Heavy Formation Peak had previously buried in the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar would be completely encased by this grand formation. To activate them in the future through the barrier of the Naming Stronghold's grand formation would multiply the difficulty exponentially.

What was even more troublesome was that the Crimson Parasol Meridian-Locking Formation that Chunyangzi had set up happened to suppress several Earth Qi nodes.

Chunyangzi might not have seen the full truth, but he had instinctively protected the most crucial locations.

As a result, Dong Chen's plans were going to be hindered.

"For this person to have commanded Pure Yang Palace for so many years, he ultimately has his own capabilities."

Dong Chen felt no anger in his heart.

This was simply the nature of the situation - a back-and-forth struggle.

"At worst, we must remove the hidden hands our side has placed, abandon this location, and consolidate our efforts elsewhere to realize our plans."

If the arrangements at the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar were left behind, they would instead serve as obvious clues that could implicate many more things.

Dong Chen grasped his Jade Token, infused it with his divine sense, and contacted his deathsworn.

Shi Qian immediately responded with his divine sense: "Peak Master."

Dong Chen said, "Move out tonight."

Shi Qian lowered his head. "Please give the order, Peak Master!"

"In the southwest corner of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, the Crimson Parasol Meridian-Locking Formation hasn't fully closed yet. Enter the ground through the third hidden vein. Do not destroy the formation, and do not kill. You just need to deliver these [Mountain-Sinking Formation Plates] into the old nodes."

Shi Qian: "If this operation is hindered and discovered by others, what should I do?"

Dong Chen: "Then, before you actively retreat, completely destroy all of our side's arrangements! Remember, leave no clues behind."

Shi Qian: "Yes, Peak Master."

Danxia Peak.

At night, the crimson clouds did not extinguish!

The entire mountain peak looked like a pill furnace held up by fiery clouds. Above the mountainside, crimson clouds swirled as the light from the clouds was absorbed and exhaled by countless pill rooms, condensing into a faint golden-red halo.

Wang Yu sat in front of a pill furnace, sipping tea while keeping an eye on the heat.

He wore a cloud robe, and the crimson clouds on the robe brightened and dimmed in rhythm with his breathing. A whisk rested in the crook of his arm. The fire of the Purple Yang Pill Furnace in front of him was gentle, and a thin wisp of medicinal fragrance drifted from the cracks in the furnace lid. It tasted bitter at first, then sweet, and left a refreshing sensation in the chest once inhaled.

A Jade Slip floated in front of him.

Within the Jade Slip hovered half of the formation map of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

The formation map was not complete.

Many areas were still blurry, but the rough outlines of the Crimson Parasol Meridian-Locking Formation, the Sun-Supporting Light-Sealing Pavilion, the Nine Palaces outer ring, and the Cyan Wyrm Wood wall could already be made out.

Wang Yu looked at it for a moment, his gaze pausing on several marked Earth Qi nodes.

"Tan Zhu should have already taken action, right?" he thought to himself.

Over these past few days, Tan Zhu had participated in the construction of the Naming Stronghold.

In name, he was setting up poison-art hidden troops and placing hidden hands for the Naming Stronghold to prevent future sneak attacks from the Flowing Cloud Peak faction. Given Tan Zhu's strength and status, this arrangement was perfectly reasonable. The Cultivators of the Naming Stronghold were wary of him, yet they also needed him.

In secret, Tan Zhu had been silently observing the formation, continuously memorizing the Earth Qi nodes, the direction of the formation bases, and the depths of the earth veins that had not yet been concealed during construction.

This half-completed formation map was the fruit of Tan Zhu's efforts over the past few days.

"The grand formation hasn't been completely finished yet. Once it is built and its power is fully activated, trying to destroy Dong Chen's arrangements will create unnecessary hurdles."

Wang Yu gently swirled the teacup in his hand.

The clear tea broth reflected his eyes.

Prioritizing the destruction of Dong Chen's plans, then collecting evidence to lay down groundwork for the future - this was Wang Yu's goal this time.
