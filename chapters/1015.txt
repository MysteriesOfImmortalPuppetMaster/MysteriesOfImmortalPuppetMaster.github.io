Chapter 1015: Dao Infusion into Lingtong

Ning Zhuo, however, was right by her side, spending day and night with her.

When she was confused, Ning Zhuo explained things to her. When she was injured, Ning Zhuo made arrangements for her. When she wanted to prove herself, Ning Zhuo let her try. And when she achieved results, Ning Zhuo would truly see them.

To Qing Chi, this closeness was far more real than her old memories.

Her growing attachment to Ning Zhuo became the most natural thing in the world.

Looking at her serious face, Ning Zhuo's heart softened, and he said gently, "Qing Chi, you are not my human pet."

Qing Chi blinked.

Ning Zhuo said tenderly, "You are my friend, and you are my comrade-in-arms. We have braved life and death together!"

Qing Chi froze.

Friend.

Comrade-in-arms.

These two words fell into her heart, making it grow even warmer.

She felt that Ning Zhuo was mostly just trying to comfort her.

But this comfort, she would engrave in her mind.

"Although the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect have their 'Live and Die Together' Divine Art, the bond of life and death between the Young Master and I is definitely above any Divine Art!" the Ghost Clan Girl thought in her heart, her eyes growing brighter.

Ning Zhuo coughed lightly. "Qing Chi, go rest for now. Build up your energy before the Troop Command Hall's Minor Trial."

Qing Chi complied, leaving with visible reluctance.

After the stone door closed, only Ning Zhuo and Sun Lingtong were left in the room.

The smile on Sun Lingtong's face faded a bit. He first took out a few storage bags from his waist and laid them out in front of Ning Zhuo.

"These are the things you specifically asked me to collect," Sun Lingtong said. "Cloud Beast cultivation, rearing, nurturing, taming, healing, as well as several Cloud Beast recipes and methods for building cloud nests. I took everything I could during the Minor Trial, and whatever I couldn't take, I found a way to memorize."

Ning Zhuo opened them to take a look, and indeed, they were packed to the brim.

Jade Slips related to Cloud Beasts, recipes for beast fodder, cloud qi conditioning methods, young beast soothing techniques, talisman diagrams for regulating the temperature and humidity of cloud nests, and a few small bottles of Medicinal Pills commonly used for Cloud Beasts.

These items were of great value.

Ning Zhuo formally accepted them. "Thanks for your hard work, Boss."

Sun Lingtong waved his hand. "We're bros. If you ask me to go, how could I not get it done for you?"

As he spoke, his gaze landed on the long box beside Ning Zhuo, and his eyes immediately lit up.

"The Dao-Bearing Jade Pages are here?"

Ning Zhuo nodded.

Sun Lingtong immediately sat up straight, the playful smile on his face retracting.

This was the true major event between the two of them.

Ning Zhuo pushed the long box to the middle between them. One by one, the Dao-Bearing Jade Pages floated up and hovered in midair. The jade pages were thin and warm, glowing with a white light like the moon, illuminating both of their faces.

Ning Zhuo first took out a Dao-Bearing Jade Page, pinching it between two fingers.

His divine sense sank into it.

The inside of the jade page was empty and clear. Ning Zhuo formed his true intent from his Yang Dao realm and infused it in.

Light and heat, ascension and emergence, initiation, illumination, and breaking filth.

Manifest, move, ascend, shine, emerge.

How Yang Qi expelled Yin cold, how it nourished the physical body, how it illuminated evil beings, and how it imbued dead objects with a trace of emerging vitality.

The true intent flowed out from Ning Zhuo's divine sea, not as words or images, but as thin, warm, golden streams. They entered the Dao-Bearing Jade Page, and faint golden patterns immediately surfaced on the page. The patterns spread from the edges toward the center, like sunlight slowly crawling across window paper.

Sun Lingtong sat opposite him, his expression gradually turning serious.

Ning Zhuo handed the jade page to him, and Sun Lingtong caught it to begin absorbing it.

The next moment, his eyes widened slightly.

He felt as though he were suddenly standing on a mountain ridge in the early morning.

The sunlight on the horizon had not yet fully risen, and a thin mist still lingered between Heaven and Earth. When the first ray of sunlight pierced through the mist, the chill retreated, and the dewdrops on the grass lit up. In the distance, there was cooking smoke, bird songs, someone pushing open a wooden door, and a hint of vigor re-emerging in a sick person's eyes.

It was worth noting that Sun Lingtong practiced the Virgin Boy Art of the Void Sect. It primarily cultivated the divine sea and essentially belonged to the path of Pure Yang!

Sun Lingtong's comprehension was no lower than Ning Zhuo's, but he had yet to reach the Golden Core stage. His years of Cultivation were too few, and since his interests lay elsewhere, his Yang Dao realm was not outstanding.

In the past, when he looked at Yang, he mostly just saw it as heat, fire, light, and a force to expel ghosts and break evil.

But at this moment, Ning Zhuo's true intent made him realize that Yang could also be "manifestation" - illuminating things hidden in the dark, awakening the slumbering, and suddenly giving shape to vague thoughts in the heart.

Sun Lingtong's throat bobbed, and fine sweat seeped from his forehead.

Ning Zhuo continuously handed over the second, third, fourth jade page...

Earth Dao realm!

Heaviness, bearing, returning, suppression.

Earth was not simply mud and stone. Earth could hold up all things and could also bury them. When mountains collapsed, they ultimately returned to the earth. No matter how high a stone flew, it must fall back to its foundation. When the true intent of the Earth Dao entered the jade page, the white jade surface turned slightly yellow, with patterns rising and falling like mountain ranges, or stretching across like field ridges.

After Sun Lingtong received it, he felt as if he were being held up by a vast expanse of land.

The land did not speak, but it was so stable it was almost unnerving. All footsteps from running, jumping, fleeing, stealing, and vaulting over walls ultimately landed on it. Throughout his life, Sun Lingtong had excelled at agility, transformation, and slipperiness, but at this moment, the Earth Dao's true intent told him that all variations required a landing point, required something to bear them.

Formation Dao realm.

Ning Zhuo's Formation Dao foundation came from a complex mix of sources. Sun Lingtong had originally lent him Formation Dao true intent; later, Ning Zhuo continually cross-referenced it with Shen Xi's insights, the Nine Palaces formation plate, the Rising Cloud Minor Trial, the repair of the Naming Fire Furnace, the Xu Earth Divine Domain, and the Artifact Forging Hall's grand formation to form his own understanding.

But basically ninety-nine percent of it was still Sun Lingtong's realm.

Rings of fine formation patterns surfaced on the Dao-Bearing Jade Page. At times, the Nine Palaces shifted; at times, the Five Elements rotated; at times, it resembled the fire veins interlinking within the Naming Fire Furnace, and finally, it transformed into a simple and unadorned formation diagram.

When Sun Lingtong received it, his reaction was the least pronounced.

This was, fundamentally, his own stuff.

This transfer of true intent lasted for the better part of a shichen.

Sun Lingtong's eyes were tightly shut, and his brows were locked in a frown. Clearly, receiving all this was not easy for him.

Ning Zhuo looked at Sun Lingtong, confirmed he was fine, and then withdrew his divine sense.

He put away the remaining Dao-Bearing Jade Pages, his mind recalling the Perfect Dragon King Qian Longzi mentioned by the True Monarch Earth-Forming Hell-Suppressing.

Qian Longzi was known as the Perfect Dragon King and left his name in the annals of history. He possessed Grandmaster-level realms in ten different paths: the Dragon Dao, Water Dao, Cloud Dao, Lightning Dao, Beast Taming... It was rumored that in his youth, his comprehension shocked the world; in his middle age, his might awed the Four Seas; but in his later years, he found it impossible to make any further progress.

Ten perfections was already the limit.

What caused this hindrance was the curse of knowledge.

The more one knew, the heavier one became. Every realm of understanding was like gathering sand to build a tower, consuming vast amounts of time and energy. And the more towers there were, the more prosperous one appeared. But when a Cultivator wanted to embrace a new truth, these towers would block out the sky.

Ning Zhuo now deeply understood this.

"I must pay attention to the layout of my Cultivation paths," he said silently in his heart.

"I cannot blindly expand."

"There's no need to expand. If the internal systems don't support each other, they will easily form obstacles."

Comprehensive realms like the Yin-Yang realm and the Five Elements realm were necessary.

Ning Zhuo felt increasingly fortunate that he possessed a profound Five Elements realm.

"When I return to the Ten Thousand Medicine Valley in the future, I must thank the Five Elements Divine Lord in person!"

At the same time, he was also thankful to have the Dao-Bearing Jade Pages.

These thin jade pages might, in the future, become crucial tools for him to manage his own realms, bless others, and balance the burden of his Cultivation.

Sun Lingtong slowly opened his eyes.

In his pupils, remnants of golden light, yellow light, and formation patterns - three distinct colors - still lingered.

Right after, his upper body swayed, and he nearly fell backward.

Ning Zhuo reached out and supported him.

Sun Lingtong grabbed Ning Zhuo's sleeve, his voice floating: "I can't, I can't. Receiving so much true intent all at once, I feel like I'm going to turn into an idiot."

Sun Lingtong rubbed his temples vigorously.

His head was a complete mess right now, and he felt extremely dizzy.

After recovering for a long while, Sun Lingtong finally stood up. Not wanting to delay Ning Zhuo's Cultivation, he got up to take his leave.

He walked to the door, then suddenly turned his head: "Right, Little Zhuo."

Ning Zhuo looked up. "Hmm?"

Sun Lingtong rubbed his temples and chuckled, "I just got my hands on a poison-mixing technique during the Minor Trial. It's not just simply mixing a few poisons together; that would be too crude. This focuses on the mutual generation, excitation, and concealment of toxicities. It can even turn multiple non-toxic substances into poison under specific heat and auras."

Sun Lingtong continued, "I heard you mention that Tie Kuang extracted the pill toxins from the furnace, right? The pill toxins of the Naming Fire Furnace rightfully belong to us. If you ask for them back, they'd be perfect for me to use."

These pill toxins had accumulated over a long period of time, originating from countless Medicinal Pills, medicine dregs, deviations in heat, and the baleful energy of furnace ash. While ordinary alchemists would avoid them like the plague, to Sun Lingtong, they were premium materials.

Ning Zhuo nodded. "Given our current relationship with Tie Kuang, this small request is just a matter of a single sentence."

"Alright, give them to me once you get them." Sun Lingtong's steps were still a bit weak, but as he walked out, he couldn't help humming a little tune.

A day later.

Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

When night fell, this treasured land of feng shui still lacked even a sliver of peace.

Mountain winds blew past from on high, causing the sea of clouds to surge like the tide. Deep within the earth, however, a blazing Yang Qi continuously rose, like an unborn sun held up beneath thick mountain rocks.

In the center of the Cloud Altar, that natural high platform was red-gold in color. The surface was wide and flat, and its edges featured rings of natural fire patterns spreading outward, like the halo of the grand sun falling upon the stone.

Seen in the daytime, this place was bright and majestic. But at night, its uniqueness became even more apparent.

In ordinary mountains late at night, Yin Qi would seep out from the crevices of earth veins, plants would drip with dew, and stones would grow cold. The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, however, constantly surged with hidden heat. The crimson patterns on the ground glowed faintly, like many charcoal fires buried in the dirt, dotting and stretching across the area. When the Artisans stepped on it, the soles of their shoes were baked warm.

Chunyangzi stood at the highest point of the Cloud Altar.

He wore a red-gold Daoist robe. The three thousand lines of scriptures on it had returned to their original state, though a few faint scorch marks remained on the edges.

The night wind fluttered the hem of his robe, and the fine light of the scriptures flickered with it. His face was still pale, and a hard-to-conceal weariness lay between his brows.

He stood perfectly straight.

His spine was like a sword.

As long as the surrounding Cultivators looked up and saw his figure, their hearts would feel a bit more at ease.

The great battle was a tragic victory, and his severe injuries had not yet healed, but the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar was already in their hands.

The gazes of everyone from the Naming Stronghold as they looked at him were no longer the same as before.

Chunyangzi knew very well that this was reputation.

The sacrifices made in the previous battle and the risks taken had all borne fruit.

A massive increase in his reputation meant a massive increase in his power!

He looked down, his gaze sweeping inch by inch over the construction scene.
