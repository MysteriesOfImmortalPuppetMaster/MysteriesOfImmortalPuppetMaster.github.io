Chapter 975: Seeking Refuge and Recruiting

The shadow puppet was as thin as a cicada's wing, pale yellow in color, its edges finely stitched with blood-red silk threads.

A small armored figure was painted on its surface. Its facial features were simple, but its eyes were inlaid with two specks of black jade that gleamed faintly in the lamplight. Illuminated by the firelight, the shadow cast upon the stone wall was actually thicker and more substantial than the physical puppet itself, appearing just like a true armored soldier standing before the wall.

Pi Fujie reached out and tapped it, and the shadow puppet stood up.

The shadow of the small armored figure on the stone wall raised its arms in a defensive posture. Though the movement was subtle, it exuded a heavy, solid bearing.

"A Golden Core-level shadow puppet - the Calamity-Substituting Injury-Transferring Shadow," Pi Fujie said. "When an enemy attacks you, as long as you activate this shadow in advance, you can transfer a portion of the offensive force onto the puppet. No matter how fierce the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect are, their attacks remain at the Golden Core-level. This item should be quite helpful to Young Master Ning."

Seeing Ning Zhuo remaining silent, Pi Fujie smiled. "I am willing to gift this item to Young Master Ning."

The lamplight in the cave manor flickered slightly.

Ning Zhuo looked up at him, his gaze as calm as water. "I appreciate Daoist Pi's generous intentions, but I must politely decline."

Pi Fujie's eyebrows twitched slightly. Once the words "politely decline" came out, the underlying meaning was clear.

Ning Zhuo continued, "This item is too precious; I truly cannot accept it."

The smile on Pi Fujie's face faded slightly, though he showed no anger. His fingers slowly rubbed the cuff of his leather robe, and demonic patterns on the sleeve faintly surfaced as he did so. "Is Young Master Ning still minding my Pi clan's background?"

Ning Zhuo shook his head. "Daoist Pi misunderstands. The Nanming Stronghold is currently at the center of the storm. If I publicly declare my intent to save the Red-Robed Guest's demonic soul while simultaneously accepting a heavy treasure from the Pi clan, I'm afraid many will seize upon it to use against me."

Pi Fujie stared at him for a moment before suddenly bursting into laughter. "Young Master Ning is cautious indeed."

He retracted the shadow puppet but did not rush to put it away in his sleeve. Instead, he let it lie quietly in his palm. The small puppet's black jade eyes darted around, as if it were also sizing up Ning Zhuo.

Putting away his joking demeanor, Pi Fujie's voice deepened. "I truly wish to invest in Young Master Ning, and I truly do not want to be your enemy again. Previously, turning an enemy into a friend earned praise from my clan, but in my eyes, we can take it a step further. If Young Master Ning won't accept this shadow puppet, it matters not. If there is any support you desire, just say the word."

Ning Zhuo's expression finally changed. A sharp gleam flashed in his eyes, as fast as a blade skimming the surface of the water.

Pi Fujie suddenly felt a slight tension in his back.

Ning Zhuo sat up straight, his expression solemn. "I want to borrow a Nascent Soul-level shadow puppet to deal with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect!"

The cave manor suddenly fell quiet.

The smile froze on Pi Fujie's face.

His Adam's apple bobbed. "Young Master Ning, you really dare to ask."

Ning Zhuo replied, "Since Daoist Pi asked, I am answering seriously."

Pi Fujie smiled bitterly.

He rolled up the Calamity-Substituting Injury-Transferring Shadow again and placed it into his sleeve, his fingers lingering inside for quite a while.

"A Nascent Soul-level shadow puppet... I am only at the Foundation Establishment Stage, I cannot mobilize such an item." He looked up at Ning Zhuo, his expression uncharacteristically sincere. "I can report this to my family and will do my best to fight for it on your behalf. But as for the result, I cannot make the final decision, nor can I guarantee anything."

Ning Zhuo nodded. "I understand."

Pi Fujie stood up. When he arrived, he was full of smiles; as he left, his brows were slightly furrowed.

Ning Zhuo personally saw him out of the cave manor, watching as he transformed into a dark red streak of light and departed.

Just as Ning Zhuo was about to turn back to the cave manor, he caught sight of a figure out of the corner of his eye.

The figure was hiding at the corner of the mountain path. His fiery red short robe was half-obscured by the clouds and mist. His hair was tied up in a messy bun, and several small fire tubes hung at his waist.

He would peek his head out to look, then shrink back, scraping his toes back and forth on the stone steps, rubbing away a small patch of moss.

Upon seeing the person clearly, Ning Zhuo couldn't help but smile. "Huo Nian'er."

The figure instantly stiffened.

Huo Nian'er, also known as A'Huo, shuffled out from the mist, a flush of embarrassment on his face.

Normally, he was quick-tempered and loud-voiced, never backing down from an argument in the Artifact Refining Hall. But standing here at the entrance of the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor, he seemed not to know where to put his hands and feet, as if all his fiery temper had been blown away by the mountain wind.

"Y-Young Master Ning," Huo Nian'er cupped his fists.

Ning Zhuo walked down two stone steps. "Are you looking for me?"

Huo Nian'er glanced up at him, then quickly averted his eyes. "I heard you're going to face off against the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect."

As he spoke, he frowned, his cheeks growing redder with agitation. "Zhang Dadan and the others all said to wait and see, saying this matter is too big and we shouldn't recklessly get involved. Patch Sun also said to play it safe, and Jiang Xiaobian kept muttering about how we're too weak to interfere..."

Becoming increasingly frustrated, he reached up and scratched his hair, making his already loose bun even messier. "I thought about it again and again, and my chest just felt tight. Young Master Ning thought highly of me before, and even accepted the small mechanisms we offered. I, A'Huo, might not have any great abilities, but I can't just sit back and do nothing!"

Ning Zhuo looked at him.

In Huo Nian'er's eyes, there was opportunism, hot-blooded passion, fear, and a recklessness unique to young people.

Ning Zhuo understood in his heart. "So, you came?"

Huo Nian'er nodded forcefully. "Yeah!"

"Have you thought about what you can do?" Ning Zhuo asked.

Huo Nian'er opened his mouth, his face growing even redder. It took him a long time to squeeze out a single sentence: "Haven't thought of it."

"Just coming here is already good enough." Ning Zhuo walked up to Huo Nian'er and patted him on the shoulder.

Huo Nian'er froze.

Ning Zhuo continued, "I am currently short on manpower. If you're willing, you can stay and cultivate in the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor for now, and help me handle various chores related to artifact refinement, repairs, and firearms."

Huo Nian'er's eyes suddenly lit up. He cupped his fists and bowed deeply. "Thank you, Young Master, for taking me in!"

Ning Zhuo helped him up. "No need to be so formal. There aren't many rules in the cave manor, but you must be diligent in your work. Gongsun Yan will arrange a place for you to live and cultivate."

He called for Gongsun Yan, who quickly settled Huo Nian'er in.

Ning Zhuo felt moved; he could sense Huo Nian'er's genuine sincerity.

Zhang Dadan, Patch Sun, and Jiang Xiaobian had not come. Ning Zhuo didn't blame them at all.

Playing it safe and protecting oneself was inherently the most common choice in the Cultivation World.

There was indeed an element of opportunism in Huo Nian'er, a point Ning Zhuo saw very clearly. But for someone to wager themselves as a bet was already better than a million pretty words.

In the afternoon, a flying message arrived from Profound Armor Cave.

The flying message was not an ordinary paper crane, but a palm-sized bronze beetle. The beetle's six legs were as sharp as hooks, and its carapace was engraved with profound soldier patterns. It flapped its wings, flew into the cave manor, and landed steadily on the desk. Its carapace opened, spitting out a bronze-colored projection of light.

The light projection coalesced into the form of an envoy, who introduced himself as a deacon of Profound Armor Cave.

He first bowed to Ning Zhuo, his tone polite and attentive.

"Young Master Ning, Profound Armor Cave is willing to repair all the Profound Soldier Armor in your possession free of charge! Furthermore, several sets of Profound Soldier Armor have been sent along with this message. If anything catches your eye, they can all be sold to you at a discount."

Following the flying message, cultivators from Profound Armor Cave, who had arrived at the cave manor together with it, carried three large copper chests inside.

The lids of the chests were opened, and bronze radiance immediately flooded the entire cave manor, illuminating the stone walls with a bright cyan hue.

Piece after piece of Profound Soldier Armor floated up - bracers, heavy shields, long hooks, short axes, and more. There was a wide variety, a dazzling array of equipment.

Ning Zhuo's mind was crystal clear: The Shouzhuo Wheel Slash Formation had already proven that if Profound Soldier Armor was integrated into a system involving formation dao, mechanism dispatching, and close-quarters martial arts, it could exhibit excellent synergistic effects. Profound Armor Cave had clearly seen this as well. They wanted to use this opportunity to continue showcasing the value of their Profound Soldier Armor!

Because of this, the discount offered for this sale was astonishingly high.

Ning Zhuo immediately realized that this was an excellent opportunity; it was highly likely that if he missed this chance, he wouldn't get another one.

The transaction was quickly negotiated.

Ning Zhuo then asked, "Does your noble cave have any Nascent Soul-level Profound Soldier Armor for sale? The price is negotiable. Renting is also fine."

The smile on the deacon's light projection instantly faded slightly.

His expression remained polite, but his tone grew more strict. "Forgive me, Young Master Ning. Nascent Soul-level Profound Soldier Armors are all paramount treasures of our cave. For the time being, they are neither for sale nor for rent."

"I only need it for a single battle," Ning Zhuo said.

The deacon shook his head. "I have no authority to agree to this matter."

With the words spoken up to this point, the meaning was clear enough. Ning Zhuo did not continue to press the issue. He only nodded and said, "In that case, I will trouble your noble cave to repair my armor."

The deacon looked relieved and hurriedly cupped his fists. "Profound Armor Cave will certainly do its utmost."

After the light screen dispersed, Ning Zhuo looked at the numerous pieces of Profound Soldier Armor he had purchased, his eyes as calm as the night.

"Once a Nascent Soul-level Profound Soldier Armor is lent out, the nature of the act is completely different - it would mean Profound Armor Cave standing on the same line as me, jointly facing the deeply entrenched forces behind the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. They won't cross that line."

Towards evening, people from the Five Elements Peak's Rotation Hall arrived.

The visitor's status was extremely high, possessing Nascent Soul-level cultivation. He was none other than the dignified Vice Hall Master.

He arrived riding a five-colored cloud wheel. When the cloud wheel landed before the entrance of the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor, the five-colored spiritual light of metal, wood, water, fire, and earth spun in layers. The clouds and mist were forcibly pushed apart to the sides, opening like an invisible pair of massive doors.

The Vice Hall Master wore a five-colored Dharma robe. He had a gaunt face, a long beard hanging to his chest, and eyes as gentle as water. Yet, he possessed the composed bearing of someone who had long held a high position. Standing within the cloud light, he looked like an immovable mountain.

Ning Zhuo personally went out of the cave manor to welcome him. "For the Vice Hall Master to visit in person, this junior is extremely flattered."

The Vice Hall Master raised a hand to support him out of thin air and smiled. "Ning Zhuo, your previous performance was stunning, and your mastery of Five Elements Spells was particularly impressive. Countless people in the Rotation Hall have mentioned you, all full of praise. We, too, have been constantly thinking of you."

Ning Zhuo welcomed him into the cave manor.

After the two took their seats, the Vice Hall Master picked up a teacup. Just as the fragrance of the tea reached his lips, he slowly put it down, his gaze falling upon Ning Zhuo's face. "I have come to act as a peacemaker."

Ning Zhuo's expression remained unchanged.

The Vice Hall Master looked at him. "The fierce reputation of the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect is widely known. The various forces of Flowing Cloud Peak using the Red-Robed Guest's demonic soul to pressure you makes the current situation extremely perilous. If the Rotation Hall steps forward to mediate, we might be able to ease things for you. If the negotiations go smoothly, there might even be room to ransom back the Red-Robed Guest's demonic soul."

His voice was as gentle as a stream, but hidden beneath the waters were reef-like motives.

Ning Zhuo heard this very clearly. He first stood up and bowed. "This junior is grateful for the Vice Hall Master's profound kindness."

The Vice Hall Master looked at him with a smile. "Being grateful is good. You are a smart person, so you should understand that for some things, the price of fighting is too high. Through negotiation, you can actually preserve much more."

Ning Zhuo sat back down, his fingers lightly resting on the edge of his teacup. A few spirit leaves floated on the tea, their tips swaying slightly with his movement. "This junior understands."

The Vice Hall Master's gaze flickered. "Then what are your thoughts?"

Ning Zhuo lowered his eyes for a moment before looking up again, his gaze clear and upright. "I'm afraid this junior must disappoint the Vice Hall Master's good intentions."

The Vice Hall Master fell silent. The air in the cave manor seemed to grow a few degrees heavier.

He remained gentle, but there was a trace of seriousness in his tone. "Why?"

Ning Zhuo said, "This junior's situation is currently terrible. If I mediate now, I will definitely have to make many concessions."

The Vice Hall Master did not deny it.

Ning Zhuo continued, "For example, having the Nanming Stronghold abandon the fight for the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar."

The hand with which the Vice Hall Master was lifting his teacup paused in mid-air.

Ning Zhuo's tone remained as respectful as before, but the weight of his words was distinctly clear. "The Nanming Stronghold has already formed an alliance. Senior Chunyangzi sits in the seat of the Grand Stronghold Leader, and the various Nascent Soul and Golden Core cultivators all hold creditor rights. This junior only occupies the lowest seat in the Nanming Stronghold; I cannot give up such interests on their behalf."

The Vice Hall Master slowly set down his teacup. With a soft clink, tiny ripples spread across the tea in the cup. He looked at Ning Zhuo and didn't speak for a long time.

Outside the cave manor, dusk was deepening. The mountain mist was dyed a pale red by the sunset glow, looking like embers from a stove scattered at the edge of the sky, possessing a gentle warmth intertwined with a hint of fading chill.

Finally, the Vice Hall Master sighed softly. "For a young man to be able to apply rules to himself... it is very rare."

Ning Zhuo cupped his fists. "This junior is merely acting according to the rules of the Nanming Stronghold."

The Vice Hall Master stood up. "Since that is the case, I won't press you further. However, Ning Zhuo... you only have one life."

"This junior will remember it."

Ning Zhuo walked him out of the cave manor.

The Vice Hall Master departed on his cloud wheel. The five-colored cloud light streaked across the sky, leaving behind a faint spiritual glow, like a rainbow plunging into the twilight.

Ning Zhuo stood at the entrance of the cave manor until the cloud light had completely vanished into the horizon before turning back inside.

When night fell, Kong Ran arrived.

He wore an apricot-yellow short jacket. The baby fat on his cheeks hadn't yet faded, and his irises were as pitch-black as lacquer. As the night breeze blew by, the short jacket on his body puffed up slightly, making him still look like a playful child.

"Brother Ning." Kong Ran cupped his fists in greeting, his movements rigid and precise, as if he had practiced them many times in advance.

Ning Zhuo smiled and returned the greeting. "Brother Kong visiting late at night - there must be urgent business."

After taking his seat, Kong Ran didn't start by joking around as he usually did. He placed a letter on the table, pressing his fingers against the corner of the envelope, his knuckles turning slightly white. "My father is very worried about your life."

Ning Zhuo looked at the letter.

Kong Ran continued, "My father said that Brother Ning is currently too much in the limelight, entangled with the grand scheme at every turn. To rashly risk life and death for the sake of an unfamiliar demonic soul is truly unnecessary. Why not temporarily slow down, take a detour, and plan it out slowly?"

As he said these words, his brows were slightly furrowed, and a solemnity unfitting for his age showed on his small face.

He himself was worried about Ning Zhuo. Moreover, his visit this time also carried the calculations and considerations of Kong Zhaoming in his capacity as Prime Minister.

Ning Zhuo asked, "Prime Minister Kong is willing to help?"

Kong Ran nodded. "He is willing. But my father also made a demand - my Kong clan wishes to have a slice of the pie regarding the Naming Fire Furnace's creditor rights. The specific price can be negotiated in detail."

Ning Zhuo wasn't surprised. During their previous conversation, Kong Ran had already hinted at Kong Zhaoming's intentions; this was simply the second time.

Ning Zhuo picked up his teacup and blew away the hot steam floating on the surface of the tea. "I need Nascent Soul-level assistance. Preferably mechanical constructs."

Kong Ran's eyes widened slightly. "Brother Ning's Mechanical Arts can already control Nascent Soul-level mechanisms?"

Ning Zhuo only possessed Foundation Establishment Stage cultivation. Although his abilities regarding divine sense had been showcased quite a bit in the previous few contests, proving to be far superior to ordinary Foundation Establishment cultivators, it was still far from enough to control Nascent Soul-level mechanical constructs.

Kong Ran knew this, and a look of confusion appeared on his face.

Ning Zhuo shook his head. "I cannot control it. Even if I can barely force it to activate, I won't be able to unleash its full power."

Kong Ran became even more puzzled. "Then what do you need mechanical constructs for?"

Ning Zhuo looked at him. "In fact, I need an autonomous Nascent Soul-level mechanical construct capable of fighting on its own."

Kong Ran drew in a sharp breath of cold air.

His chubby cheeks puffed out and his eyes went round. "Brother Ning, this request of yours is way too frightening."
