Chapter 1025: Newcomers Dilute Old Power

Ointment-Boiling Granny reached out and gently stroked the ears of the iron pot. Her movements were familiar and tender, like an old woman touching the lifelong partner who had accompanied her for many years.

"This old woman thought about it for a long time, and I still decided to come."

Ning Zhuo asked, "Granny, why do you want to join the Nanming Stronghold?"

Ointment-Boiling Granny smiled.

When she smiled, the wrinkles on her face smoothed out, carrying a warm, earthly aura.

"Because this old woman wants to boil better medicinal ointments and cure more illnesses," she said. "When an ointment is applied to a person, what is applied is medicine, what is cured is the illness, and what is warmed is the heart. If the Vermilion Bird Fire can be used to boil ointments, the medicinal properties will be mild and the efficacy will increase. Applied to a wound, it would slowly seep in like a spring breeze."

"Your willingness to come is a blessing for the Nanming Stronghold," Ning Zhuo said. "We are currently lacking people skilled in medicine, ointments, and post-injury recovery. After joining, you can take charge of the external wound ointments. Panacea Ointment, Tendon-Connecting Ointment, Cold-Pain Ointment, and Sore-Ulcer Ointment can all be listed as standard provisions in the stronghold."

"I will arrange for someone to tally the necessary medicinal ingredients. If we are short on herbs, the stronghold will purchase them. If they can be grown ourselves, we will coordinate with Old Man Baicao and Lin Jinglong to open up a medicinal garden on the outer ring of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar."

Ointment-Boiling Granny nodded. "Good, good. I will listen to your arrangements, Young Master."

On the day Ning Zhuo arrived at the Nanming Stronghold, he did nothing else but recruit newcomers.

Liu Jinke, Old Chef, Gongsun Yan, Lady Spiderweb, Bold Zhang, Stone-Boiling Elder, Ointment-Boiling Granny...

The scale of the Nanming Stronghold continued to expand. It was no longer just a temporary framework propped up by a few Nascent Soul creditors.

It began to have true flesh and blood.

It had become a faction that could survive, expand, and absorb all sorts of characters.

Many cultivators in the hall watched Ning Zhuo preside over these matters, witnessing all of this with their own eyes.

Gradually, two common realizations dawned on them. First, for a portion of the Nanming Stronghold, Ning Zhuo had the final say. Second, the door to the Nanming Stronghold was truly open.

Over the next few days, the Nanming Stronghold's reception hall was hardly ever quiet.

Someone came bearing mechanism boxes.

It was a tall, thin young man with sunken cheeks but bright eyes, carrying seven or eight wooden boxes on his back. The lids of the boxes were all carved with small flying birds. After entering, he looked at Ning Zhuo and said he was skilled in mechanical birds. Having been fortunate enough to see the recording of Ning Zhuo's previous battle with Liu Jinke, he wanted to ask for Ning Zhuo's guidance on the operation and development of mechanical flying birds.

Someone came bringing spirit plant seeds.

A round-faced female cultivator wore a light green dress, with a few fresh, tender leaves tucked into her hair. She held a clay pot in her hands, within which a few pale purple grass sprouts swayed gently. She said she could cultivate Yang fields, raise Fire Grass, and also repel insects for spirit plants.

There was also an old talisman master whose white eyebrows drooped to the corners of his eyes and whose fingers were as thin as twigs. His body was covered in talisman pouches. As he walked, the talisman paper rustled like a cluster of autumn leaves blown by the wind. Upon meeting, he didn't say much; he merely spread out a stack of Evil-Warding Talismans, Fire-Stabilizing Talismans, and Meridian-Suppressing Talismans for Ning Zhuo to inspect, validating his own worth.

Ning Zhuo received them one by one.

He didn't put on airs, nor did he feign false intimacy.

For every person who entered, his attitude was earnest.

For example, to the tall, thin young man skilled in mechanical birds, he asked how far the birds could fly, whether they could stably transmit messages within crimson-gold formation light, and how to correct their direction if they encountered turbulent cloud qi.

To the round-faced female cultivator, he asked which side of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar was most suitable for opening a Yang field, and since Fire Grass loved the sun but detested dryness, how cloud qi could be used as a buffer.

To the old talisman master, he didn't just look at the quality of the talismans; instead, he asked about the source of the Talisman Paper, the ratio of Cinnabar, and the burden on a cultivator's spiritual energy when activating them.

These questions were detailed and precise.

Many cultivators who originally just wanted to try their luck had their depths plumbed by him in just a few words, and immediately put away any contempt.

Some were nervous, some were delighted, and some were secretly alarmed.

They gradually discovered that Ning Zhuo didn't just have great fame, a deep background, and Righteous Path tactics. This youth truly understood many things. Mechanisms, formations, the Fire Element, the Yang Path, the Pill Dao, talismans, spirit plants... Even if he only touched upon them superficially, he gave people the feeling that he knew what he was talking about and would absolutely not be easily deceived.

Over the next few days, the pages of the Nanming Stronghold's roster increased one by one.

Sun Lingtong and Qing Chi continued to participate in the Rising Cloud Minor Trial and gained quite a bit.

Naturally, they were also paying attention to Ning Zhuo and the situation at the Nanming Stronghold.

Qing Chi couldn't help but worry for Ning Zhuo, saying to Sun Lingtong, "Big Brother Sun, taking in so many people all at once, can the Young Master remember them all?"

Sun Lingtong sat cross-legged on the windowsill, pinching a spirit fruit in his hand and taking a crisp bite.

He grinned and said, "Others might not remember, but my Young Master definitely can. That big brain of his didn't grow for nothing."

Qing Chi nodded seriously, as if she had heard some very reliable logic, but then she stiffened her face, showing displeasure. "You're just a little study boy, how can you casually discuss your Young Master like that?"

Sun Lingtong paused for a moment, then immediately giggled, "My mistake, my mistake."

He took another bite of the spirit fruit.

Sun Lingtong recalled his most recent conversation with Ning Zhuo. He had expressed to Ning Zhuo that by recruiting newcomers so broadly, there would likely be many spies among them.

At the time, Ning Zhuo had put down his teacup, his expression calm. "I know."

Sun Lingtong raised an eyebrow. "You know, and you're still accepting them?"

A faint smile floated onto Ning Zhuo's lips. "Boss, was the original Nanming Stronghold clean to begin with?"

Sun Lingtong blanked for a moment, then let out a heh.

From the very beginning, the Nanming Stronghold wasn't a pure Sect, nor a clan, much less a faction united by the inheritance of a specific Cultivation Art.

It was an organization bound together by debt.

The earliest batch of people - Nine Fires Dragon Lord, Chunyangzi, Tan Zhu, Tu Yuanzi, and the Red-Robed Guest - each had their own origins and their own motives. Those who joined later, like Situ Xing, Shen Xi, and Lin Jinglong, all had super clans behind them. The Zhu Clan, Confucian cultivators, Iron Madman, and Danxia Peak - the hidden hands of various factions had long since reached in.

The Nanming Stronghold had always been a hodgepodge.

In a pot of soup that already had many flavors, adding a few more wasn't anything strange.

"They send people in because they want to watch us."

"And I accept them so that they too will be bound by the Nanming Stronghold and used by me."

Sun Lingtong rolled his eyes in realization, completely understanding.

Ning Zhuo wasn't afraid of complexity, nor was he afraid of internal traitors.

He was actively creating complexity; actively recruiting internal traitors.

In the previous structure, the Nanming Stronghold had few people, making the authority of Nascent Souls like Chunyangzi and Tan Zhu exceptionally heavy. Tu Yuanzi almost completely stood on Ning Zhuo's side, but his combat strength and prestige weren't enough to suppress the situation.

With a massive influx of newcomers, the situation would be different.

The power and influence of those Nascent Souls would inevitably be diluted.

And newcomers needed arrangements.

Newcomers needed resources.

Newcomers needed rosters, merit systems, positions, and rules.

Who would distribute these?

It was Ning Zhuo, currently sitting in the seat of honor, holding the Naming Fire Furnace, and in charge of internal affairs!

The more people there were, the more matters there were to handle.

The more matters there were, the more places Ning Zhuo could reach his hands into.

His power would grow greater, and his prestige would rise higher.

As Sun Lingtong figured this out, he couldn't help but laugh out loud. "Good grief, you're using the newcomers to dilute the power of the old guard."

Ning Zhuo's expression was serious. "Boss, this is called expanding the organization and perfecting its structure."

Sun Lingtong rolled his eyes. "Fine, fine, fine, you're Righteous Path, you have the final say!"

In the hall of the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor, light and shadows flickered. Ning Zhuo's youthful face was illuminated warm and clear by the furnace light. Yet, his gaze was not immature in the slightest; it was like a deep well, reflecting the light of the sky but also hiding the undercurrents beneath the water.

Looking at Ning Zhuo, Sun Lingtong suddenly felt somewhat emotional.

He had known Ning Zhuo for far too long!

He still remembered when this kid was neglected by the Ning Clan as a child, keeping his head down, silently enduring, quietly studying mechanisms, figuring out human nature, and imitating high-level figures like the City Lord of the Meng Clan.

Now, this youth, facing Nascent Souls, noble clans, Demonic Cultivators, rogue cultivators, merchants, and spies, could already calmly incorporate them all onto his chessboard.

Sun Lingtong felt a warmth in his heart and an inexplicable, profound sense of pride.

"Leaving the mountain to travel the world, Little Zhuo has progressed too fast. He has already become extremely reliable, especially when it comes to the Righteous Path; there are many things that I wouldn't even think of."

The Internal Affairs Hall was holding a Rising Cloud Minor Trial this time, one that was hosted in every iteration, called the "Golden Coffer Theft Trial."

The Myriad Manifestations Sect's Internal Affairs Hall was in charge of the Sect's treasury, account books, rewards and punishments, stipends, allocation of spiritual materials, and the calculation of mission merits. It could be said to be the veins and stomach of the behemoth that was the Myriad Manifestations Sect.

The content of the Golden Coffer Theft Trial was to invite cultivators participating in the Flying Cloud Assembly to come and attempt to steal from the Treasure Pavilion.

The person in charge was one of the Deputy Hall Masters of the Internal Affairs Hall - Ruan Hengqiu.

Ruan Hengqiu was a mid-stage Nascent Soul cultivator, whose Dao title was "Fairy Box-Locker".

She was no longer young, but she did not appear old. She had a thin face and long, slender eyes, with a few strands of silvery-white hair mixed into her temples. She usually wore a dark cyan Dharma robe with narrow sleeves.

Her Dharma robe was not embroidered with dragons, phoenixes, or auspicious clouds; it only had tiny copper buttons adorning the cuffs and hem. Those copper buttons were not decorations, but miniature locking talismans. As she moved, they would occasionally emit very soft click sounds.

At her waist, she perennially hung a bunch of small keys.

There were only seven keys, varying in size and made of different materials. There was one of white jade, one of profound copper, one of black wood, and even one that seemed carved from translucent cloud crystal.

She stood on the high white jade steps outside the Treasure Pavilion, her hands tucked in her sleeves. She looked at the cultivators who had come for the trial today with a calm expression. "Everyone coming to attempt this is not considered to be violating a taboo. The Internal Affairs Hall needs to know exactly how reliable the Treasure Pavilion's defenses still are. If anyone maliciously injures another, they will be immediately expelled from the minor trial and strictly disciplined. If anyone can test out and find a loophole, the Internal Affairs Hall will offer an additional reward."

In the crowd, Sun Lingtong's eyes shone.

Today, he was disguised as an inconspicuous, short cultivator with a slightly sallow complexion, a fake mole glued to the bridge of his nose, and wearing dusty gray clothes.

"This minor trial is great," Sun Lingtong licked his lips, his heart itching hotly.

Ever since arriving at the Myriad Manifestations Sect's main mountain gate, he had long felt stifled. Upon learning that there was such a minor trial, he immediately rushed over to participate, determined to thoroughly indulge himself.

Ruan Hengqiu made way for the passage, and the main doors of the Treasure Pavilion behind her slowly opened.

Rumble.

Those doors were not ordinary wooden doors, but two massive slabs of Sinking Gold Stone. There were no carvings on the door's surface, only countless crisscrossing formation runes.

The cultivators swarmed inside.

Sun Lingtong was mixed in among them, rubbing his hands together in eager anticipation.

After entering the Treasure Pavilion, there was a straight, long corridor.

Thirty-six small copper mirrors hung from the ceiling of the corridor. Each copper mirror was only the size of a palm, with sun patterns carved on the back and a mirror surface as clear as water. They did not emit a blinding, intense light, but merely sprinkled down a thin layer of pale golden radiance.
