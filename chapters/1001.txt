Chapter 1001: It's All Ning Zhuo's Fault

Here, cloud qi surrounded the area on all sides, rolling in overlapping layers around the mountainside like white silk wrapping around a pillar.

Fire veins ran hidden underground; occasionally, a speck of crimson would seep through the crevices of the mountain rocks. At first glance, it looked like magma embers, but upon closer inspection, it was an extremely gentle and warm earth fire spiritual light.

This place was exactly the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

The altar's surface was broad and octagonal in shape, paved with ancient stones that had turned a crimson-gold color after years of sun exposure.

The waist of the altar swallowed the clouds.

Wisps of cloud qi flowed up the stone steps, and upon reaching the altar's waist, they were gathered by an invisible force, transforming into an extremely faint, white-gold cloud ring.

The base of the altar suppressed the fire.

The deep fire veins coiled along the terrain, like a slumbering crimson dragon whose scales were still glowing. It only emitted a very faint rumbling sound from underground. That sound transmitted through the rock layers, deep and heavy, like the crimson dragon slowly breathing deep beneath the earth.

Beside the cloud ascending altar, there was also a Yang-Locking Well.

The well opening was not large. Its rim was cast from ancient bronze, mottled in color, and one could vaguely see three vein-suppressing bronze wedges driven deep into the well wall. One bronze wedge faced the sunlight, one faced the fire vein, and one faced the cloud root. The three nodes each had their own light and color: the sunlight node was pale gold, the fire vein node was crimson, and the cloud root node was milky white. They converged deep within the well wall, forming an extremely fine, bright swirling current.

There was no water in the well.

Looking down, one could only see the sun's brilliance, the cloud qi, and the earth fire firmly locked together, neither leaking nor dissipating, neither clashing nor turning chaotic. That ball of light slowly rotated at the bottom of the well, like an unhatched sun, and also like a furnace core deeply hiding the essence of heaven and earth.

In the past, White Cloud Village had been stationed here for many years, managing the surface-level Cloud Ferry for a long time, while the Earth Expansion Alliance was the true aggrieved party who had cultivated this treasured land of feng shui.

Tan Zhu exposed the secrets of this place, triggering a conflict between White Cloud Village and the Earth Expansion Alliance.

Nanming Stronghold forcefully intervened afterward, triggering the five-win martial contest.

Now that the dust had settled on the contest, the news of Liang Ming's defeat and death spread faster than the mountain wind, quickly reaching this place.

The stationed cultivators of White Cloud Village were originally still maintaining the formation flags; their leader was a Golden Core cultivator.

This person had a skinny figure and wore a cloud-white Daoist robe. There were already a few strands of frost at his temples.

He stood beside the Yang-Locking Well. When he received this news, his body swayed. He reached out and pressed the rim of the well, his knuckles turning white from exertion.

"Everyone, stop what you're doing. The five-win martial contest has a result." He was silent for a few breaths before finally speaking.

The group of cultivators gathered around. After learning the result from their leader, their expressions changed drastically.

Some turned pale, some gritted their teeth, and some gripped their magic artifacts, their limbs trembling slightly.

A young White Cloud Village cultivator couldn't help but ask in a low voice, "Martial Uncle, are we really giving it up?"

The skinny Golden Core cultivator's Adam's apple bobbed, and he squeezed his voice through his teeth, "The five battles are decided, and the entire sect knows. Since Liang Ming has been defeated, this place belongs to Nanming Stronghold."

The young cultivator's cheeks flushed red. "But this place, we've guarded it for so many years!"

Just as he spoke, high in the sky, the cloud qi suddenly split apart.

Firelight arrived first, crimson like dragon scales, rolling and pressing over the cloud layer.

Nine Fire Dragon Lord arrived, treading on flames. He was tall, with crimson hair draped over his shoulders and deep brow bones. His fire-patterned robe fluttered and billowed wildly high in the sky.

Beside Nine Fire Dragon Lord, Tan Zhu slowly appeared.

He wore a dark grey robe. His figure was emaciated, his cheekbones slightly protruding, and his eye sockets deep and dark. Faint purple-black smoke coiled around his cuffs; with every breath, the smoke slightly contracted, sliding along his wristbones like a living creature.

Finally, Tu Yuanzi appeared.

He looked like a youth, wearing a loose earth-yellow robe, appearing quite honest and straightforward.

The three Nascent Soul cultivators stood shoulder-to-shoulder high in the sky.

The cloud ring on the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar abruptly stagnated. The white-gold cloud qi at the altar's waist was compressed inward, as if pressed down by an invisible giant hand.

The expressions of the White Cloud Village cultivators changed drastically.

The skinny Golden Core leader's face twitched, but he eventually cupped his hands in greeting.

Nine Fire Dragon Lord looked down expressionlessly. "The five battles have ended; Liang Ming is defeated and dead. From now on, the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar belongs to Nanming Stronghold."

The skinny Golden Core cultivator's eyes twitched. The fingers hidden in his sleeves clenched, then helplessly relaxed. "My White Cloud Village knows the agreement and will keep our promise."

Tan Zhu: "In that case, let us proceed with the handover. The earth veins, well opening, altar foundation, and cloud root nodes must all be verified on the spot."

As they spoke, several more streaks of escape light rushed over from the distance.

Shen Xi, Lin Jinglong, Situ Xing, and the other follow-up cultivators also hurried over.

"Begin packing up." The Golden Core leader let out a long sigh and issued the order.

The cultivators of White Cloud Village sprang into action.

Cloud cottages were dismantled one by one.

Several cloud beasts, entirely snow-white with fine scales on their backs, were led out. The animal pens were also taken away.

Pots of spiritual plants and nursery beds were all collected.

Those spiritual plants had absorbed the sun's brilliance and cloud qi for many years; the edges of their leaves shimmered with a faint gold-white luster. The White Cloud Village cultivators moved them, mud and pot together. Someone even scraped clean the crushed cloud moss stuck to the bottom of the pots.

Beside the Yang-Locking Well, several White Cloud Village formation cultivators lowered their heads and began dismantling the formation plates.

White Cloud Village took away all facilities. Even the cloud ladders propped against the cliff face were retracted section by section.

The wind blew over, sweeping through this former ferry crossing, making it look empty and desolate.

The Nanming Stronghold cultivators watched this scene with varying expressions on their faces.

Some were excited, their eyes shining, feeling that they had finally secured a foothold on Flowing Cloud Peak.

Some were vigilant, constantly observing, afraid that White Cloud Village would secretly leave behind traps or backdoors as they withdrew.

Some felt disappointed, looking at the emptied cloud cottages and spiritual plants, muttering, "They really didn't leave a single blade of grass."

Finally, White Cloud Village's skinny Golden Core leader, wearing an expression as if he had lost his parents, cupped his hands to Nine Fire Dragon Lord, Tan Zhu, and Tu Yuanzi.

"The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is handed over to Nanming Stronghold today, according to the agreement," he enunciated clearly. "The altar foundation is intact, the Yang-Locking Well is undamaged, and the three nodes of the fire vein, cloud root, and sunlight are all present. As for White Cloud Village's private property, it has all been recovered."

Nine Fire Dragon Lord crossed his arms over his chest, his crimson hair flying in the wind. "Good. You hand it over straightforwardly, and everyone saves face."

The skinny Golden Core cultivator's gaze turned cold as he looked up at Nine Fire Dragon Lord. "Nanming Stronghold has gained power today, so naturally, the Dragon Lord can say whatever he pleases. My White Cloud Village has managed this place for many years; even if the sentimental attachment is shallow, there was still hard work involved. How long Nanming Stronghold can hold onto it will depend on your future abilities."

The firelight in Nine Fire Dragon Lord's eyes surged.

Tan Zhu: "Whether we can hold it or not naturally depends on our abilities. If White Cloud Village has the capability in the future, you can also compete for it according to the rules."

The skinny Golden Core cultivator glanced at Tan Zhu, a chill rising in his heart.

He knew that continuing to speak today would only bring humiliation upon himself, so he raised his hand and waved. "Let's go."

Under his lead, the White Cloud Village cultivators retreated in utter gloom.

White Cloud Village withdrew.

Only the people of Nanming Stronghold remained on the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

Nine Fire Dragon Lord landed in the center of the altar, his crimson boots stepping onto the ancient stones. He looked around, an uncontrollable smile appearing on his face.

"A good place."

Tan Zhu slowly landed beside the Yang-Locking Well, his emaciated fingers gently tapping the rim of the well. The swirling light at the bottom of the well reflected in his gloomy eyes. The interlacing of gold, crimson, and white actually added a rare trace of brightness to his face, which was shrouded in poisonous gas year-round.

"No wonder Chunyangzi fought with his life on the line," he said in a low voice.

Tu Yuanzi crouched down, pressing his palm against the altar surface, emitting a burst of earth-yellow light.

The earthen light spread along the patterns of the stone slabs. A moment later, Tu Yuanzi nodded. "The altar foundation is stable. White Cloud Village didn't dare touch its roots."

Shen Xi suggested, "White Cloud Village even moved away their formations; we need to set up defenses immediately."

Situ Xing looked at the empty mountain ridge. "My family can provide some funding."

Lin Jinglong: "My clan is willing to plant spiritual vegetation."

Nine Fire Dragon Lord laughed heartily. "Good, let's do it this way first. Our Nanming Stronghold has successfully charged the peak and established our fortress. From now on, the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is our stronghold's base!"

His voice rolled across the mountain ridge and fell into the sea of clouds.

The Nanming Stronghold cultivators were quiet at first, but then a fervent heat surged in the eyes of many.

Some clenched their fists, some cheered in low voices, and some looked toward the Yang-Locking Well with dry throats.

They finally had a true foundation on Flowing Cloud Peak!

-----

Earth Expansion Alliance Base.

Several Nascent Soul cultivators gathered in the hall.

At the head was the Alliance Leader of the Earth Expansion Alliance. He was a middle-aged cultivator with a broad forehead and a square face, wearing a thick brownish-yellow robe. An earth seal hung at his waist, and his brow was heavy.

"We lost this martial contest," the Earth Expansion Alliance Leader said slowly.

A tall, thin elder's Adam's apple moved, his voice hoarse. "True Monarch Liang Ming fought all the way to the sixth day. The Seven Jades Corpse Sacrifice was only a hair's breadth away, yet it still ended up in such a state."

The Earth Expansion Alliance Leader said, "A miss by a hair's breadth is a miss by a thousand miles. Chunyangzi chose to strike second to gain the upper hand, making it impossible for Liang Ming to recover from his accumulated injuries; he was indeed a cut above. It's just a pity that even The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook was blocked by him."

The crowd fell silent for a while.

In the battle against Nanming Stronghold, the Earth Expansion Alliance suffered heavy sacrifices.

True Monarch Shi Heng was recuperating from injuries, Yue Qingshan was killed in battle, and although Liang Ming got his physical body back, his Nascent Soul had collapsed, meaning his cultivation base was practically crippled.

"It's Ning Zhuo," Qiu Lei spoke up, breaking the silence.

"We invested heavily, crowdfunding to have the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect take action, specifically targeting Nanming Stronghold's core members. As a result, Ning Zhuo was detained and taken away."

"This is just like our side throwing a heavy punch that hit nothing. Nanming Stronghold lost a mid-stage Foundation Establishment weak link and instead pushed up Chunyangzi. Meanwhile, our Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect were dragged down and actually couldn't participate in the fifth battle."

"And at the final crucial moment, it was still the Void-Embrace Blade-Snapping Guards that blocked the residual might of The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook."

"Liang Ming lost to Chunyangzi, but we all lost to Ning Zhuo!"

The crowd fell silent once again.

-----

White Cloud Village Base.

The cloud qi of Flowing Cloud Peak swept past the eaves, emitting low wailing sounds.

The atmosphere here was incredibly oppressive.

The Golden Core leader who retreated from the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar completed his debriefing and slowly stepped down with a heavy expression.

"In the end, we couldn't hold onto this treasured feng shui land."

"It's more than that. Previously, this place was a Cloud Ferry, bustling with traffic. Having lost this territory, we've lost a major source of revenue."

"The agreement of the five-win martial contest is known to everyone. Now that we've lost, we naturally have to hand it over. Could it be that we could renege on it?"

"Let's consider the future instead. With this victory, Nanming Stronghold has successfully charged the peak. From now on, there is another major faction on Flowing Cloud Peak. How should we face this powerful new neighbor?"

The cultivators of White Cloud Village each fell deep in thought.

The five-win martial contest already had a result. Because of Nanming Stronghold's forceful arrival, Flowing Cloud Peak's old status quo would inevitably be affected in the future.
