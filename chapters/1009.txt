
Chapter 681: Chunyang's Heavy Bet
Nine Fires Dragon Lord was building an alchemy chamber on the south side.
Nine small furnaces hovered in midair, each with a different shape. Some resembled dragon heads, some fire urns, and others round crimson-copper cauldrons.
Standing in the center, Nine Fires Dragon Lord waved his sleeve, and nine types of flames flew out from his palm.
Earth fire was thick and heavy, wood fire verdant green, lightning fire bright white, heart fire formless, true fire violently crimson, pill fire gentle and nourishing, spirit fire light and agile, demon fire darkly beautiful, and dragon fire the most domineering. The nine fires entered the small furnaces respectively, burning silently and casting a red glow on the clouds halfway up the mountain.
Chunyangzi looked at Nine Fires Dragon Lord's back, his emotions complex.
He could already tell that this demon dragon was hardly of any great use on the battlefield. His dragon bloodline was so extraordinary, yet when fighting, he always reverted back to alchemy. What a total waste of a demon dragon's body.
But a Nascent Soul was ultimately a Nascent Soul!
Nine Fires Dragon Lord was indeed a master at alchemy. His control over heat, his use of pill furnaces, and his application of dragon-race precious materials often possessed ingenious subtleties others could not fathom.
The figure of the Red-Robed Guest flashed across Chunyangzi's mind.
The Red-Robed Guest was dead, his soul having fallen into enemy hands.
For Chunyangzi, this was actually a good thing.
"Don't just look at Ning Zhuo's astonishing combat strength; his shortcoming in his cultivation base is still right there. Everyone thinks his future is promising and bright, but that future is ultimately not now."
"If the Red-Robed Guest were still alive, he would be an extremely strong fulcrum in Ning Zhuo's hands. But alas..."
Even though Nine Fires Dragon Lord disappointed him, Chunyangzi was clear on one point: he still had to win him over to deal with Ning Zhuo.
Tan Zhu was also lending a helping hand.
His figure was gaunt and withered, and he wore a grayish-black robe.
As he walked past the edge of the newly laid foundation, wisps of extremely faint poisonous smoke drifted from his sleeves.
The poisonous smoke fell into the crevices of the stones and vanished without a trace.
Several cultivators of the Naming Stronghold, seeing him approach, quickly avoided him, their eyes filled with awe and wariness.
Tan Zhu didn't care in the slightest.
He squatted down, pinched a bit of crimson-gold sand with his withered fingers, and lightly sniffed it near his nose. He then took out a small black jade bottle. The bottle opened, and a drop of dark purple poisonous liquid fell, seeping into the soil.
Tan Zhu silently circulated his cultivation art.
A moment later, a nearly imperceptible thread of purple poison talisman emerged deep within the rock layer.
The talisman continuously multiplied and sank, lurking deep underground.
Watching from afar, Chunyangzi's eyes slightly darkened.
Regarding Tan Zhu, he had already seen through a part of him.
"There must be someone behind him!"
Chunyangzi was secretly wary of Tan Zhu.
A dying person was the hardest to control. If pushed to the brink, they could do anything.
Furthermore, how could the strength of a faction capable of dispatching a cultivator like Tan Zhu be ordinary?
One could figure that out using only their toes.
"At a critical moment, Tan Zhu could be won over to deal with Ning Zhuo. But it's even more likely that Ning Zhuo and I will join forces to eliminate this variable, Tan Zhu, first." Chunyangzi continuously envisioned future scenarios.
Chunyangzi and Ning Zhuo had previous experience cooperating, and their foundation for cooperation was very strong.
Political matters emphasized continuity. Easily switching factions would leave outsiders with the impression of being two-faced. Once tainted with such a reputation, commanding people's hearts in the future would cost several times the effort.
Therefore, Chunyangzi knew that the best plan was still to unite with Ning Zhuo against Tan Zhu.
"First, sweep away the hidden danger that is Tan Zhu, and then carefully cross swords with Ning Zhuo... But I wonder if Ning Zhuo will share this tacit understanding."
Thinking of this, the "performance" Ning Zhuo put on beneath the Truth Bell suddenly surfaced in Chunyangzi's mind again.
"Brilliant." The more Chunyangzi thought about it, the more brilliant it seemed.
In front of the Truth Bell, Ning Zhuo publicly admitted his faults, was placed into the Rising Cloud Minor Trial, and taken away. Under the watchful eyes of the masses, he accomplished an extremely beautiful escape.
"The chess piece that was Qinghuangzi was also used very well."
Like Tan Zhu, he was a dying man.
Yet when the public looked at Tan Zhu, they felt fear, wariness, and suspicion. When the public looked at Qinghuangzi, it was pity, empathy, and the helplessness of seeing an old man at the end of his life protecting his only inheritor.
Ning Zhuo used Qinghuangzi to take a step back into the vast sea and open sky, and then sat on the mountain to watch the tigers fight.
If he had actually fought a grand battle against the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, even while holding the Yang Fish, he might have been defeated and killed.
But by not fighting, he was inherently undefeated.
"From this matter alone, one can see that the strength of Ning Zhuo's political maneuvering might even surpass the Spirit Treasure Yang Fish. Pity that my sect's Yang Fish is already damaged and needs repairing; its spiritual energy needs even more nourishment before it can be used again."
Thinking of this, the wound on Chunyangzi's chest throbbed with a slight pain. He calmly adjusted his breathing, suppressing the ache.
Tu Yuanzi was currently organizing the foundation.
An earth-yellow halo diffused from his palm.
The originally chaotic and clashing yang-fire earth veins underground were slowly supported by his Earth Element magical power.
With every step he took, the rock layer beneath his feet grew a fraction more stable.
There were a few hollows left behind where the White Cloud Township had removed the formation pillars, originally showing signs of collapsing. Tu Yuanzi squatted there, pressing his palm to the ground for the duration of half an incense stick at a time.
Sweat rolled down his cheeks and dripped onto the crimson-gold rocks, quickly evaporating from the geothermal heat.
Chunyangzi observed him from afar, analyzing in his heart once again.
"It's almost certain that this Tu Yuanzi was dispatched by Ning Zhuo's clan."
The five-win martial contest had revealed many things.
"Tu Yuanzi's combat strength is not great, and whether he is truly a Nascent Soul is even up for debate."
Ning Zhuo helping Tu Yuanzi avoid the martial contest might not seem like much to outsiders. But in the eyes of someone like Chunyangzi, it held far too much information.
Ning Zhuo wouldn't lie about such a small matter.
The political cost of doing so would be too high!
Since that was the case, Tu Yuanzi's combat strength was limited. Ning Zhuo keeping him off the field was to protect him, and also to protect a banner his own clan had placed within the Naming Stronghold.
Tu Yuanzi was putting in brute effort and hard work. And judging from daily interactions, he was indeed an honest man.
"Why did Ning Zhuo's clan send such a person?"
Chunyangzi's thoughts stirred slightly.
"Perhaps because he's easy to control."
"Ning Zhuo himself is proficient in mechanism arts, and has dabbled in the Five Elements, Formation Dao, and Alchemy, but he doesn't excel in beast taming. Tu Yuanzi's cultivation base is high enough, his temperament is grounded, and he is loyal and reliable. Placing him in the Naming Stronghold makes him perfectly suited to keep up appearances for Ning Zhuo."
"But if an honest man can be utilized properly, he can also become the flaw I use to defeat Ning Zhuo."
Chunyangzi once again thought of the Red-Robed Guest.
"The Red-Robed Guest died at a truly good time!"
"Ning Zhuo has lost a major fulcrum because of it."
At this moment, a trusted subordinate came to ask, saying that preparations were complete and wondering if construction should begin.
Chunyangzi nodded.
He had injuries and couldn't personally take action, so he let his subordinates handle it.
Before long, the Golden Core cultivators who had come from Chunyang Peak all sprang into action.
There were seven of them in total. Some were elderly, some middle-aged, and some still appeared young, but all wore crimson-gold Daoist robes with the fire crest of Chunyang Peak embroidered on the cuffs. The seven had different expressions; some were invigorated, some solemn, and some hid hesitation in their eyes.
They brought out tightly sealed crimson-copper chests one by one.
The chest lids were opened, revealing the sparse remaining assets of Chunyang Peak. Crimson-gold fire bricks, sunrise jade bottles, sun essence stone slabs, old palace formation flags, and a few pure yang spirit seeds wrapped in layers of talismans...
Not even during Chunyang Palace's most difficult times had many of these items been used.
Now they had just joined the Myriad Manifestations Sect, and their submission was considered a success. But why use Chunyang Palace's accumulations to build the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar?
By doing this, Chunyang Palace's assets would be mostly depleted. The next step would be for everyone on Chunyang Peak to tighten their belts and start accumulating anew.
A few Golden Core cultivators couldn't help but feel hesitant.
Was it worth laying down such a heavy bet?
An elderly, white-haired Golden Core cultivator with a gaunt frame and sunken eye sockets felt his fingers tremble slightly as he held a crimson-gold fire brick. He glanced back at Chunyangzi. Seeing him standing up high, pale-faced but with an aura like the midday sun, the old man's feelings grew even more complicated.
In the past, Chunyang Palace often had multiple Nascent Soul cultivators. But after the current Chunyangzi took office, he gradually became the only Nascent Soul left in Chunyang Palace.
Whether it was Chunyang Palace or Chunyang Peak, it was entirely Chunyangzi's dictatorship!
No matter how hesitant the Golden Core cultivators were, an arm couldn't wrestle a thigh; they had no choice but to carry out the task.
The seven Golden Core cultivators cast their spells simultaneously.
Crimson-gold fire bricks were embedded one by one into the outer ring of the ancient altar. The sunrise jade bottles poured out faint purple-gold morning light, and the sun essence stone slabs were laid into the core earth vein nodes. The old palace formation flags unfurled in the wind; though the fabric was old, pure yang scriptures still lit up character by character.
The sun patterns of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar were triggered, suddenly flaring up into a halo of light.
Waves of heat surged at the mountain peak.
Chunyangzi narrowed his eyes, estimating the progress in his mind.
"I have already put in all my effort. This construction speed is acceptable."
"I should be able to make it in time... right?"
"Actually, the crux of this matter still depends on Ning Zhuo's side—how long will it take to repair the Naming Fire Furnace."
The longer it dragged on, the more advantageous it was for him.
"The worst-case scenario is that Ning Zhuo successfully repairs it in a single day." Just as this thought arose, Chunyangzi couldn't help but feel tense.
Because this involved the secret opportunity for Soul Formation, even though Ning Zhuo wasn't present, Chunyangzi's thoughts were completely focused on him.
Artifact Refinement Hall.
Inside the earth fire chamber, the firelight was still vigorous.
The Naming Fire Furnace floated quietly above the black iron circular platform, its furnace body now complete.
On the dark red furnace walls, vermilion feather-like fire veins connected one by one, and the fine root sinews transformed from crimson paulownia roots were faintly visible within the formation patterns at the furnace's base. The gentle cloud qi left by the warm cloud marrow acted like a thin milky-white halo, nourishing the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit within the furnace.
The little Vermilion Bird rested deep in the heart of the furnace. Its feathers were fiery red, and a tuft of golden down on its head swayed slightly.
It was still somewhat timid but already far more stable than it had been initially.
After the final inspection showed no errors, Tie Kuang nodded to Ning Zhuo.
Ning Zhuo reached out and beckoned, storing the Naming Fire Furnace into his pouch.
The two walked out of the earth fire chamber. Along the way, Tie Kuang received a flying message and read the latest intelligence: "Oh? Over at the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, heavy construction is already underway."
Ning Zhuo's eyelids twitched slightly.
Tie Kuang continued, "Chunyangzi is using the prestige of his grand victory to project his influence over the entire Naming Stronghold. Shen Xi is setting up formations, Lin Jinglong is planting trees, Situ Xing is installing formation equipment, Baicao Peak has also extended a hand, Nine Fires Dragon Lord is building an alchemy chamber, Tan Zhu is burying poison, and Tu Yuanzi is working himself to the bone even more, having been sent to smooth out the foundation."
Tie Kuang grinned, revealing his white teeth: "I heard Tu Yuanzi is so tired he's sweating bullets. This friend of yours is quite unpretentious."
If these words fell on the ears of an outsider, they might just seem like casual chatter.
But Ning Zhuo heard the flavor of sowing discord.
"Senior Tu Yuanzi is willing to exert effort for the Naming Stronghold, and I, Ning Zhuo, am nothing but grateful in my heart," Ning Zhuo said. "His temperament is grounded and steady, and he is most skilled in matters of earth qi. The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is a new addition to the Naming Stronghold, and the preparation of its foundation will have long-term impacts. Leaving it to him puts everyone in the stronghold at ease."
A hint of a smile flashed through Tie Kuang's eyes.
Slippery little fellow.
An airtight response.
He clasped his hands behind his back. "The furnace is repaired, and it only took a day. Chunyangzi is riding high right now. If you don't return soon, many things over there will be set in stone."
"Chunyangzi is so hasty, I'm afraid he just wants to beat you to the punch, lay down the defensive formation, and seize the initiative in this regard."
Ning Zhuo slightly lowered his eyes.
The firelight in the corridor fell on his face, illuminating the youth's profile so that half was warm and gentle, and half deep and profound.
"I will return to the stronghold in three days," Ning Zhuo said.
Tie Kuang raised an eyebrow: "Oh?"
Ning Zhuo replied, "Senior estimated three days and has already announced it to the public, so there can be no mistake. Furthermore, although the repair was successful, the furnace body, fire veins, and artifact spirit have only just connected. Two days of warm nourishment is the most appropriate."
Tie Kuang laughed heartily and patted Ning Zhuo's shoulder: "Since you have made arrangements, I won't hold you back."
Ning Zhuo bid farewell to Tie Kuang.
Watching Ning Zhuo's departing back, a deep interest sprouted in Tie Kuang's heart.
The situation in the Naming Stronghold had suddenly changed.
Outside, various factions from Flowing Cloud Peak were glaring like tigers eyeing their prey. Inside, Chunyangzi was using his victory to expand his influence. After the completion of the Naming Fire Furnace's repairs, Ning Zhuo's momentum would also greatly surge. There was bound to be a brilliant clash between him and Chunyangzi.
Tie Kuang looked forward to watching a good show.
Chunyangzi was one thing, but Ning Zhuo was even more interesting.
Young in age, yet profound in various realms, but most importantly, he possessed a talent for political maneuvering. Such a person in the Myriad Manifestations Sect's future, provided he didn't die prematurely, would definitely become a pillar.
Understanding more, interacting more, and building a good relationship was the path Tie Kuang chose.
Tie Kuang muttered to himself, "So, do not hesitate for too long, Ning Zhuo."
"The matter of the Roosting Flame Cloud Nest is a win-win situation; it brings benefits to both you and me!"
At the same time, in a certain Rising Cloud Minor Trial, things had progressed to the final trial.
