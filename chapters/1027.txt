Chapter 1027: Geniuses

Troop Leading Hall’s Rising Cloud Minor Trial [Snapping the Flag to Test the Formation].

Pang Qingwu, an executive of the Troop Leading Hall, had a tall and slender figure with a cold and hard expression. A thin, long, old scar on her left cheek slanted from the corner of her eye down to her jaw. Rather than marring her beauty, it added a chilling temperament to her whole person, one forged on the battlefield.

Her voice was crisp, like clashing metal: "In 'Snapping the Flag to Test the Formation,' we do not ask about your background, nor do we ask about your cultivation base. We only ask if you can understand the momentum of war. After entering the formation, you will each lead fifty Cloud Soldiers and face the alternating pressure of the Black, Crimson, and White Armies. The flag commands of the three armies contain both truth and falsehood, and the drumbeats have both reality and illusion. If you only know how to charge and kill, you will definitely lose. If you only know how to defend to the death, you will also lose."

She raised the command flag in her hand, and every cultivator was assigned to an individual arena.

Rumble.

The clouds and mist in the center of the cloud terrace suddenly sank, like a giant piece of white cloth being pulled from the sky by invisible hands. The white cloth spread out and immediately transformed into valleys, shallow rivers, low slopes, wooden stockades, and three military camps.

The sound of war drums subsequently rang out.

Thump.

Thump.

Thump, thump, thump!

The drumbeats were initially steady but quickly became rapid. Within the cloud curtain, the Black Army formed a shield wall at the front, the Crimson Army readied their bows and crossbows in the back, while the White Army was erratic and unpredictable, slowly slithering along the edge of the valley like a long, thin snake.

As soon as Qing Chi entered the formation, fifty Cloud Soldiers materialized beside her.

These Cloud Soldiers wore snow-white armor and had blurred faces, acting only on the orders of the trial-takers. If the orders were correct, the Cloud Soldiers' military morale would rise. If the orders were mistaken, they would hesitate, scatter, and ultimately be swallowed by the three armies.

The other trial-takers had already started issuing commands.

Someone shouted, "Raise shields! Seize the high ground!"

Someone waved a flag: "Archery formation, retreat! Lure the enemy in deep!"

Still, others gritted their teeth: "Everyone, charge with me! Break the White Army first!"

For a time, killing shouts arose from all across the Cloud Battlefields, and flag shadows fluttered in the air.

Qing Chi, however, was in no rush.

She stood in front of her fifty Cloud Soldiers and slightly tilted her ear. The mountain wind blew the hair at her temples, but her eyes gradually settled into a deep calm.

Sun Lingtong was not by her side; this time, she was acting alone.

Today, the Ghost Clan Girl wore a set of short, tight-fitting clothes suitable for movement. Her dark blue skin emanated a faint, cold, and moist luster under the cloud light. The scale-fin-like keratinous growths on the sides of her elbows and knees laid slightly flat, like hidden pieces of armor. The two short horns on her forehead were curved like small bronze blades. Her eyes were bright, and her demeanor carried a feral focus.

Some things seemed to be carved into her blood and bones.

For instance, military formations.

For instance, Azure Flame.

For instance, the moment she stood at the edge of a battlefield, she could hear the rhythm between each breath and exhalation of a marching army.

Qing Chi observed calmly.

An army was not just a pile of people.

An army was a massive, living creature.

The flags were its eyes.

The drums were its heart.

The supply routes were its stomach and intestines.

Morale was the fire in its chest.

"The White Army is a fake," Qing Chi murmured softly to herself.

The Cloud Soldiers behind her waited in silence.

Qing Chi's eyes lit up slightly, and she suddenly raised her command flag: "Left wing, hold position. Right wing, advance diagonally. Forward fifteen paces, stop. Back ten paces, slow. Central army, turn after three breaths."

The fifty Cloud Soldiers were split up very meticulously by her, like casting a small net diagonally. They didn't immediately occupy the high ground, nor did they rush to rescue the river mouth. They even looked somewhat sluggish.

But a moment later, the White Army's flag commands suddenly changed.

The White Army's flag rolled up first, then folded back three times. Qing Chi's fifty Cloud Soldiers were perfectly positioned to jam the White Army right in the middle of their change of direction.

The White Army's vanguard stalled.

A sharp gleam flashed in Qing Chi's eyes.

She pressed the command flag in her hand downward. The fifty Cloud Soldiers simultaneously dropped to one knee, raised their shields diagonally, and thrust their cloud spears through the gaps between the shields.

Rip!

The White Army's vanguard formation was torn open, revealing a gap.

The battle continued...

The Ghost Clan Girl's performance gradually attracted Pang Qingwu's attention.

She watched Qing Chi alter her commands time and time again within the formation.

Qing Chi's orders were not flashy, and in some places, they even seemed unpolished. She was not very familiar with the flag signaling regulations of the Troop Leading Hall, causing her to be half a beat slow during a few transitions.

Yet, she could always preemptively catch that slight deviation in breathing before the enemy's flag commands changed.

Pang Qingwu was astonished inwardly: "What keen insight this Ghost Clan girl possesses!"

Qing Chi spun her command flag.

The fifty Cloud Soldiers no longer met the enemy head-on; instead, they split into two streams, like two hands pressing down on the Black Army's shoulders from the sides. The more urgent the drumbeats became and the more the Black Army tried to raise their shields to advance, the more skewed their shoulder line was pressed.

Over thirty shields in the Black Army's front row clashed together simultaneously, shattering the cloud shields into a sheet of white mist.

The brilliance of this move lay not in its lethality, but in using the enemy's drumbeats to counter their own movements. It was like grabbing a dislocated joint in the opponent's body and giving it a gentle twist, causing their entire arm to go numb and lose strength.

Qing Chi herself was stunned for a moment.

She didn't know why she had done that.

It was as if she had done it before.

Her eyes went distant for a split second, then quickly refocused.

The Crimson Army's archers and crossbowmen began a volley.

A sky full of crimson cloud arrows rained down with a sharp whistling sound. Every cloud arrow trailed a thin red line in midair, like meteors on an autumn night.

Qing Chi's palms were sweating slightly, but her chest grew hot.

Under her command, the fifty Cloud Soldiers darted left and right, neither completely scattering nor tightly bunching up. The crimson arrows fell among them, shattering puffs of cloud qi, but they consistently failed to completely tear apart the formation.

At the final moment, the three armies closed in to encircle her.

Many trial-takers had already been defeated.

Some were crushed by the Black Army, some were flanked by the White Army, and some had their Cloud Soldiers scattered by the Crimson Army's arrows.

Qing Chi only had twenty-one Cloud Soldiers left.

"Continue!" Qing Chi's battle intent, like fire, had been thoroughly ignited; her morale was at its peak.

After the minor trial ended, Qing Chi received her reward.

The reward was not a weapon, but a thin Military Manual - Record of the Illusions and Realities of the Three Flags.

This book specifically discussed the truth and falsehood of flag commands, the breathing of military morale, as well as decoy orders and counter-decoy orders. To many Military Strategist cultivators, it was merely an advanced basic text. But to Qing Chi, it was like a key, gently inserted into a fog-shrouded door deep within her mind.

Pang Qingwu personally handed the Military Manual to her. A rare trace of gentleness appeared on her cold and hard face: "Have you led troops before?"

Qing Chi blanked for a moment.

She looked down at the Military Manual, her fingertips gently rubbing the cover, and her voice was very soft: "It seems... I have."

"Study it well," Pang Qingwu said. "You aren't a little girl who is only suited to follow behind others."

Qing Chi looked up.

Pang Qingwu continued, "Among all the cultivators so far, you are ranked second. Above you is Huang Dang of the Diyuan Kingdom; he forcibly used military courage to stabilize the routing formation three times, which is quite impressive. You win in terms of spiritual energy, but your shortcoming lies in the regulations. Our Troop Leading Hall has other Military Path minor trials, and I look forward to your future performances."

Qing Chi nodded earnestly: "Thank you, Milady!"

Rising Cloud Minor Trial [Repairing the Network of Broken Mechanisms].

When Ban Ji walked into the testing ground, many cultivators consciously made way for him.

The venue for the 'Repairing the Network of Broken Mechanisms' minor trial was a giant circular workshop.

The center of the workshop was piled high with broken Mechanisms. Mechanical birds with broken wings, mechanical beasts missing legs, cloud chariots with half their formation plates burned away, puppet heads charred black by lightning... Various mechanical remains were piled into small mountains, emitting a mixed odor of machine oil, scorched wood, spirit copper, and old Talisman Paper.

The assessment rules were simple.

Within the time it takes an incense stick to burn, without using any complete materials, one could only take parts from the pile of broken mechanisms to assemble a mechanical construct capable of moving, fighting, and executing complex commands.

As soon as many people entered the venue, they lunged toward the mechanical remains that looked the most intact.

Ban Ji stood in front of the scrap pile, sweeping his cold gaze over it once. Suddenly, a Gold-Silk Silver-Shuttle Plate slid out from his sleeve.

Ban Ji reached out and tapped.

Mechanism Art - Twisted Roots and Intertwined Branches Weave!

Buzz.

The Gold-Silk Silver-Shuttle Plate emitted an extremely fine, light hum.

The sound wasn't loud, but it was incredibly dense, like countless tiny insects crawling on silver leaves, or like someone strumming a thousand guqin strings at breakneck speed.

From the pile of broken mechanisms, a severed spider-shaped mechanism abdomen, a pair of mechanical eagle wings, four cloud chariot axle arms, half a lightning-patterned formation plate, and seventeen old copper gears flew out simultaneously.

His fingers twitched slightly, and the Gold-Silk Silver-Shuttle Plate separated into thirty-six threads, each wrapping around a broken part. Those remnants were not crudely spliced together; rather, they slowly adjusted their angles under the guidance of the silk threads.

The mechanical eagle wings were too light; attaching them to the spider abdomen would cause an imbalance in the center of gravity.

The cloud chariot axle arms were too stiff; using them directly as legs would result in a loss of climbing ability.

The lightning-patterned formation plate was more than half destroyed; it could not serve as the core and could only act as a secondary core for a one-time burst.

Various calculations brewed in Ban Ji's mind, and the light in the depths of his eyes grew increasingly sharp and cold.

Mechanism Art - Borrowing Life Through a Hundred Bones!

Clack, clack, clack, clack.

A series of mechanical components biting together rang out.

Those broken pieces were like the corpses of dead insects twitching their limbs anew, gradually assembling into a bizarre mechanical spider-eagle amidst the gold and silver threads. It had a spider-like abdomen, six long legs, a pair of tattered eagle wings on its back, and a lightning-patterned formation plate embedded beneath its belly. It didn't look aesthetically pleasing - in fact, it was somewhat hideous - but it was incredibly practical.

The moment the mechanical spider-eagle landed, its six legs simultaneously nailed into the ground.

Bang!

It sprang up fiercely, its eagle wings flapping as it actually executed a short-distance glide in midair. When the lightning-patterned formation plate lit up, a blue-and-white electric arc sprayed out from beneath its belly, striking a ripple across the testing puppet's shield.

The highest evaluation!

The other cultivators exclaimed in shock one after another, but Ban Ji's face bore no signs of joy.

Because Ning Zhuo did not participate in this minor trial.

"Ning Zhuo is already in charge of the Naming Stronghold, yet I am still here participating in minor trials!"

Rising Cloud Minor Trial [Linked Calamity Cave].

It was Pi Fujie's turn to follow the crowd and step into the stone grotto together.

The first Calamity was the cold calamity.

The cold energy within the cloud grotto suddenly erupted, and white frost crawled along the ground accompanied by endless cracking sounds. Many trial-takers immediately urged their flames to resist, but Pi Fujie merely lifted his sleeve.

The second Calamity was the poison calamity.

Dark green poisonous mist seeped out from the cloud membrane, carrying a cloying, sweet scent. Before the poisonous mist could even approach, many people felt the roots of their tongues go numb.

Pi Fujie narrowed his eyes and, on the contrary, opened his mouth to inhale a little.

The third Calamity was the calamity of the heart.

The cloud grotto abruptly fell silent.

Ning Zhuo's figure surfaced before Pi Fujie's eyes.

That big-headed youth stood deep within the clouds and mist, his expression gentle, yet his eyes were so clear and bright that it made Pi Fujie's heart run cold.

The smile on his face froze, and his previous calm and composure were swept away...

Ultimately, Pi Fujie secured first place.

The reward was an "Ash-Molting Heart Protection Talisman," which could endure the initial erosion on the cultivator's behalf when a Heart Demon, curse, or soul technique struck. Pi Fujie pinched the talisman, a hint of satisfaction flashing in the depths of his eyes.

Purple Thunder Peak Minor Trial [Thunder Trace Severing Mechanism].

Xiao Caiting stood before the Seven-Fold Thunder Talismans.

She was the daughter of a mountain rogue cultivator. Her face was delicate and pretty, and between her brows was an ethereal quality brought about by a natural spiritual perception.

When the Seven-Fold Thunder Talismans lit up simultaneously and the purple-white lightning split into countless thin threads in midair, she merely extended a single finger and gently tapped the void.

Lightning Method - Single Thread of Origin Qi Guidance!

A single, purest wisp of aura was drawn out from the chaotic web of lightning by her, swimming around her fingertip like a tiny purple snake. Behind her, the phantom of a thunder beast faintly materialized, raising its head in a silent roar.

The eyes of the cultivator on duty lit up as he watched, and he immediately bestowed upon her the Notes on the Seven Thunders Discerning Mechanisms.
