Chapter 1010: Poisoning the Great Army

War drums were the first to sound from deep within the Cloud Sea.

Thump.

Thump.

Thump!

The drumbeats were heavy, each echoing with escalating pressure.

Sun Lingtong had just stepped into this trial realm when the drumbeats shook his figure, making him sway.

The usual playful smile on the young boy's face instantly receded by a fraction. His eyes darted around, looking first at the sky, then at the ground, and finally at the dense, dark mass of the great army in the distance.

It was a Cloud Shadow Army of ten thousand strong!

The Azure-Armored Left Army was as swift as the wind, the White-Armored Central Army was as steady as a mountain, and the Black-Armored Right Army was as impenetrable as a city wall. Behind the three armies, smoke rose from a hundred large cauldrons. Ten grain-transport cloud paths coiled like white pythons. Three spirit springs shimmered with clear light, and in front of two medical tents, white-robed military medics had already ignited the Miasma-Cleansing Fire. Further away, Cloud-Horned War Beasts dragged heavy supplies, snorting white steam from their noses as their hooves caused the cloud ground to tremble slightly.

At the end of the White Cloud Plain, a massive cloud gate hung in the air.

If the main flag of the Central Army reached the cloud gate within six shichen, the trial-takers would be considered completely defeated.

In the previous minor checkpoints, the numerous cultivators had already learned various poison arts, such as Chaotic Flag Poison, Misbeat Drum Miasma, Cold Hoof Powder, Sweet Sleep Water, Copper Cauldron Purge Potion, Medic's Misjudgment Counter-Incense, Slow Blood Poison, and Miasma-Cleansing Reverse Ash.

Upon reaching this stage, everyone had to bring out their true skills - using poison arts to take down the great army before them!

Qing Chi stood beside Sun Lingtong. The Ghost Clan girl had her long hair tied up, her cheeks slightly pale from the cold wind of the Cloud Plain, yet her eyes were astonishingly bright.

Her gaze was fixed intensely on the distances between the three armies, their banners, and the positions of the drum carts.

"The Left Army will move first," Qing Chi said suddenly. "They are fast, and their drum cart is positioned in the middle-rear. Their military qi is spreading close to the ground; this is a trap. Whoever hurls a strong poison at them first will be the first to be eliminated."

Sun Lingtong rubbed his nose. "Go on."

Qing Chi gave an affirmative hum and raised her hand to point at the Right Army. "The war beasts over there might be a breakthrough point. If the war beasts fall into chaos, the supplies will halt. If the supplies halt, the medical tents and grain paths will all be blocked."

As they spoke, from all directions on the Cloud Plain, the trial-takers had already attacked with full force.

Liu Wenyan was the first to slip beneath the cooking smoke.

She was thin, her gray-green short robe swept up by the cloud qi as she glided close to the ground like a withered leaf. Her narrow, elongated eyes were half-squinted. Her nostrils flared slightly as she took a few sniffs of the steam coming from the cauldrons and made her judgment.

She lightly flicked her fingertips.

Three streams of medicinal powder followed the cooking smoke and flew into the copper cauldron, cloud cauldron, and bone cauldron.

The fire beneath the copper cauldron was the strongest. The powder reacted to the heat, transforming from a harmless stomach-fortifying powder into a mild laxative poison. The medicinal mist steamed from the cloud cauldron was mixed with her Medic's Misjudgment Counter-Incense; if the military medics used mind-clearing medicines, it would instead cause the soldiers' hearts to race, leading to a misjudgment of the poison's severity. As for the bone cauldron, a bit of cold-natured grass ash fell in. After the Cloud-Horned War Beasts drank this broth, the bottoms of their hooves would gradually turn cold until their legs grew weak and their muscles turned soft.

"Oh, this female cultivator has some ideas." Sun Lingtong's eyes lit up. "She's poisoning the pots."

Qing Chi nodded. "She's dragging down their logistics. The larger the scale of an army, the greater the role logistics play."

On another side, Qi Mangu had already laid a worn leather drum across his knees.

He had broad shoulders, a thick back, and a dark complexion, with a corner missing from his left ear. This young man was usually sluggish, but at this moment, he sat among the cloud grass like a silent black stone, eyes closed, listening to the drumbeats of the great army.

The Azure-Armored Left Army launched its charge.

Long spears stood like a forest, military qi surged like a tide, and the sound of cloud boots stepping on the ground fell as dense as rain.

Qi Mangu raised his hand and lightly tapped his worn drum.

Thump.

That single sound blended into the war drums, completely inconspicuous. Yet, on the membrane of the azure-armored drum cart hundreds of zhang away, a fine layer of Misbeat Drum Miasma had quietly adhered. Fang Zhaoqi also made his move.

He stood perfectly straight, possessing a square face and a tattered flag manual hanging at his waist. Two fingers on his right hand were missing, hidden within his sleeve. He looked up at the banners. A moment later, he crushed the Chaotic Flag Poison into scentless micro-dust and used a crosswind to blow it toward the Central Army's main flag.

The flag cloth fluttered.

The palm of the army's flag officer turned slightly blue, and the blue and black colors in his eyes began to misalign.

Ruan Qingxian crouched by the edge of the cloud path near the war beasts.

She was not tall, her lips were tinged blue, and fine vines dangling several small medicine pouches hung from her wrists. Her smile was sweet and soft, but her fingertips were extremely steady. She pinched the Cold Hoof Powder delicately like pollen and let it naturally sprinkle by the puddles the Cloud-Horned War Beasts were about to pass.

These individuals each displayed their own brilliance.

Several small groups were also not inferior in the slightest.

A team of yellow-robed cultivators buried Ambush Poison Stones beneath the cloud plain, preparing for them to self-activate when the military qi advanced over them.

A pair of twin female cultivators - one releasing sleep poison and the other releasing waking poison - deployed the two toxins one after the other, specifically aiming to disrupt the military medics' diagnoses.

There was also a team of rogue cultivators who released a large number of transparent poisonous spiders, which quietly crawled toward the scouts.

Qing Chi's brow furrowed tighter and tighter as she watched.

"There are too many people," she said in a low voice. "They all have their own schemes and act independently. If the toxicities interfere with each other, I'm afraid we will ultimately fail."

In the previous checkpoints, everyone was a competitor. Now, in this final checkpoint, while competing with each other, they also had to consider cooperating.

Sun Lingtong crouched on the ground, grabbed a handful of cloud grass, and sniffed it under his nose. "I've figured it out! I know what to do. Watch me, hehe."

He quietly sneaked forward, leading Qing Chi to approach the army, and scattered a handful of Slow Blood Powder.

When the Azure-Armored Army stepped over it, the poison did not seep into their bones; it merely made their breathing slightly heavier. But don't forget that Qi Mangu had previously deployed his poison art. Thus, with the drumbeats mistimed and their breaths delayed, the Left Army's charge immediately shifted from a swift wind to a chaotic gust.

Having accomplished this, the two then headed toward the Right Army.

Sun Lingtong took out a light yellow pellet, crushed it into powder, and blew it toward the intersection of the ten grain-transport cloud paths.

The toxicity of this poison powder was not strong; it could only make the cloud qi become viscous. Ordinarily, it wouldn't be of much use. But just earlier, Liu Wenyan had poisoned the cauldrons, and Ruan Qingxian had poisoned the war beasts.

Under the combined effects of Sun Lingtong's poison mixed in - coupled with the war beasts' cold hooves, the slowed supply wagons, and the sticky cloud paths - the wagon wheels acted as if they were rolling through half-dried malt sugar, creaking and growing heavier with every rotation.

Ruan Qingxian immediately glanced at them from afar, a flash of surprise in her eyes. Then she smiled and proactively drove the war beasts toward the edge of that specific cloud path.

The two sides had previously competed against each other, but now they were cooperating.

Within the great army, a military medic's expression suddenly changed. "The medicinal scent is wrong!"

Three Cloud Shadow Army medics acted simultaneously.

The Miasma-Cleansing Fire flared up with a roar. Pale blue flames surged like waves, sweeping toward the military camp, cloud paths, drum carts, and main flags.

The expressions of many trial-takers shifted slightly.

The sleeping and waking dual poisons of the twin female cultivators were mostly burned away by the Miasma-Cleansing Fire. The transparent poisonous spiders of the rogue cultivator team were exposed by the firelight, dropping to the ground with pattering sounds. The Ambush Poison Stones buried by the yellow-robed team were preemptively crushed by the military qi; their poisonous smoke had barely risen before it was scorched into ashen wisps by the Miasma-Cleansing Fire.

Qing Chi's gaze sharpened. "The military medics have begun to clear the field."

Sun Lingtong's fingers flew as he pulled out a bag of Miasma-Cleansing Reverse Ash.

The Miasma-Cleansing Fire was influenced and first burned toward the non-toxic areas, missing the crucial heated mild laxative powder beneath the copper cauldron. Seeing this, Liu Wenyan immediately added a wisp of medicinal smoke, completely maturing the toxicity in the copper cauldron.

The Central Army began to change its formation.

The main flag lowered, and the left and right flank flags flipped simultaneously. The White-Armored Central Army's military qi condensed into a thick cloud wall, intending to suppress both the Left Army's chaotic momentum and the Right Army's delayed logistics in one fell swoop.

Fang Zhaoqi let out a muffled groan, his mutilated fingers tightly curled inside his sleeve.

His Chaotic Flag Poison was heavily suppressed by the military qi, making it extremely difficult to maintain.

Qing Chi stared at the flag formation. A map of the military dynamics, invisible to others, surfaced in her mind. The chaos of the Left Army, the congestion of the Right Army, and the stability of the Central Army transformed into three streams of different colored qi in her eyes.

She suddenly noticed that every time the Central Army's main flag lowered, twenty-four shield-bearers in the front row of the white-armored soldiers would simultaneously change their footing. During this step change, their military power would pause for a fraction of a moment.

Without a doubt, this was a flaw!

She immediately told Sun Lingtong this precious intelligence.

"Watch me!" Sun Lingtong's eyes burst with brilliant light, and he launched into full action...

Time ticked by, bit by bit.

The ten-thousand-strong Cloud Army was still advancing.

Only their speed was getting slower and slower, like a massive mechanism jammed with countless fine grains of sand. The Left Army's drumbeats were misaligned, their breathing delayed. The Central Army's flag signals were crooked, their military qi fractured. The Right Army's war beasts were drowsy, their supplies gridlocked. The military medics were busy distinguishing symptoms, and the Miasma-Cleansing Fire scrambled to put out fires everywhere, but it always fell just a bit short.

Trial-takers from all paths went all out, resulting in a spectacular scene.

In the final half shichen, the three armies merged their formations.

Military qi pressed down like an iron wall, causing the flora of the Cloud Plain to bow low, as the poisonous mist was pulverized into thin smoke.

The cloud gate was a mere three li away.

The Central Army's main flag was still slowly moving forward.

All the trial-takers had taut nerves; they had all reached their limits.

Sun Lingtong also felt a sense of despair. "Are we going to fail?"

Qing Chi closed her eyes for a moment. When she opened them again, her gaze fell upon the space between the drum carts behind the Central Army's main flag and the Right Army's supplies.

"There!" she said in a low voice. "The main flag can move, but the drum cart cannot afford to stop. That is the biggest flaw!"

Sun Lingtong didn't hesitate; he exhausted his final poison art at that exact spot.

The drum cart stopped.

The drumbeats missed a beat.

The White-Armored Army formation stalled in unison.

Then, a chain reaction occurred. It was like a neatly arranged row of dominoes - Sun Lingtong had pushed over the first block, and the countless subsequent blocks cascaded down one after another!

Ultimately, this great army on the Cloud Plain was brought down by poison. The trial-taking cultivators had all successfully cleared this stage.

Sun Lingtong let out a long breath and plopped down onto the cloud grass. He laughed, the beads of sweat on his forehead shaking. "That was close, way too close. Any worse, and we would've only gotten second place."

Qing Chi also sighed in relief. She clenched her fists, her eyes shining brightly.

Divine sense descended, distributing the rewards.

"This Mixed Poison Method seems pretty good." Sun Lingtong selected one of the options.

He shuddered slightly, receiving a Poison Scripture.

Browsing it roughly, he saw the contents: poison could induce warm poison, sweet poison could conceal miasmic poison, mind-clearing medicine could reverse-catalyze sleep poison, and a detoxification primer could even serve as a bridge for the next dose of poison.

"Good stuff." The corners of Sun Lingtong's mouth slowly widened into a grin.

Qing Chi also made her selection, ultimately choosing a set of military flags.
