Chapter 1023: Widely Recruiting Newcomers

Chunyangzi revealed a thoughtful expression on his face, "The Yang Fish was previously injured by The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook and needs to recuperate. Now that the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar has been destroyed, the environment for nurturing the Yang Fish has also been greatly affected. If this place can be repaired early, the Yang Fish can naturally be lent to you sooner."

Ning Zhuo smiled bitterly, "Repairing such a Feng Shui treasured land requires one's Feng Shui Cultivation arts to be extremely profound, at the very least. This junior is willing but lacks the ability in this aspect."

Chunyangzi said gently, "Your attainment in the Formation path is profound, and you have the foundation of the Five Elements and the Yang path; it's not impossible for you to understand what is required to repair the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. Moreover, repairing this place requires more resource investment and does not necessarily rely on Feng Shui skills."

These words had already been spoken very clearly.

Chunyangzi was encouraging Ning Zhuo to invest resources!

He let out a sigh, his voice carrying a bit of exhaustion, "This time, to stabilize the foundation of the Naming Stronghold, I have already mobilized the pure Yang Palace's years of accumulation. It was not easy for this place to fall into our hands. If it cannot be restored early, it would be a true pity."

Hearing this, Ning Zhuo silently criticized in his heart, "Old fox!"

"If I didn't know this was your Soul Formation opportunity, I might have actually been moved by your words."

His face, however, instantly became solemn. "This junior has seen all the contributions Senior has made to the Naming Stronghold. All of your investments will definitely be calculated into the debt. I will not let Senior's sacrifices be in vain!"

Chunyangzi secretly pursed his lips.

He was already certain that Ning Zhuo wouldn't deny this debt.

He wanted Ning Zhuo to take out real gold and silver, to take out tangible resources to repair the treasured land!

But Ning Zhuo simply didn't take the bait.

After a moment of silence, the two exchanged a smile.

One smiled with gentle warmth.

One smiled with respectful humility.

In the hall, the Sun Crystal Lamp shone warmly, its light falling on their faces, creating a bright and majestic atmosphere of harmony.

Ning Zhuo said, "There is another piece of good news I must inform Senior about."

Chunyangzi looked at him. "Oh?"

"This time, Hall Master Tie Kuang gained a full twenty percent of the contribution for repairing the Naming Fire Furnace. However, he will not personally participate in the Naming Stronghold and will instead find someone to hold his shares by proxy. Calculated according to the current debts, this person's contribution still cannot compare to Senior's."

Ning Zhuo cupped his hands. "Therefore, Senior is still the Grand Chief of the Naming Stronghold."

Chunyangzi smiled and waved his hand. "I do not care about these hollow titles. Managing the Naming Stronghold involves a multitude of trivial matters; I even find it bothersome. If there is someone capable, I am willing to abdicate and yield the position."

Ning Zhuo immediately revealed a look of admiration. "This junior admires Senior's broad-mindedness."

But in his heart, he thought, "You can try fooling ghosts with that. Not even a ghost would believe those words!"

The two chatted for a few more sentences before Ning Zhuo actively stood up to take his leave. "This junior will not delay Senior's recuperation any longer. Everyone in the Naming Stronghold is looking forward to Senior's early recovery so you can return to take charge of the overall situation!"

Chunyangzi nodded. "Go. You must also make early preparations. The Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect cannot be taken lightly."

"This junior understands."

Ning Zhuo withdrew from the guest hall.

After the hall doors closed, the smile on Chunyangzi's face faded bit by bit.

He leaned against the back of his chair and let out a long breath. The old wound on his chest vaguely began to feel cold again, and the damage to the earth veins of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar also felt like a thorn stuck in his heart.

"Fortunately, I still managed to fool Ning Zhuo."

"The greatest secret has been kept."

"The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar has ultimately fallen into my hands. The meat is still in the pot; it just needs to be cooked slowly for a bit."

Thinking of this, Chunyangzi's eyes darkened again.

Tan Zhu!

And that mysterious Earth Element cultivator.

"An originally perfect situation was ruined to this state by those two!" Chunyangzi wished he could pull out their tendons and peel their skin!

As for Ning Zhuo.

Chunyangzi didn't blame him. In fact, he still had to rely on him to maintain the situation at the Naming Stronghold.

"And I must recuperate. The sooner my injuries are healed, the better!"

Many things did not need to be said out in the open. After their conversation, Ning Zhuo didn't obtain the Yang Fish, but Chunyangzi also didn't get Ning Zhuo's resource investment.

However, he had given Ning Zhuo his implicit approval. With that, Ning Zhuo immediately let go of all restraints.

He arrived at another guest hall.

This guest hall was more spacious than the one deeper inside. It was located on the middle-level cloud platform of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. Outside the hall, one could see vast expanses of surging clouds. The morning light pierced through the clouds and shone upon the crimson gold stone steps, as if paving them with a thin layer of golden sand.

Ning Zhuo didn't sit in a secondary seat. He went directly to the main seat, gently lifted his robe, and sat down.

He gathered everyone and stated that he had been entrusted by Chunyangzi to take charge of the stronghold's internal affairs for a period of time.

Among the cultivators, some thought 'as expected,' having anticipated this all along. Others sent flying messages to contact Chunyangzi and received affirmative replies.

Ning Zhuo had returned with the repaired Naming Fire Furnace, successfully succeeding Chunyangzi and gaining actual power.

"Since the Grand Formation has already closed, there's no need to rush the subsequent clean-up work; we can just take our time."

"The primary matter right now is to open our doors wide and recruit newcomers!"

Facing everyone, Ning Zhuo immediately put forth a new measure.

The first to join was Liu Jinke.

Today, he had changed into a clean, gold-patterned long robe. His pale golden eyes had lost their former arrogance, replaced by a layer of complex melancholy.

His waist was empty, no longer adorned with the magic treasure he used to favor. After being defeated by Ning Zhuo in three battles, his sharp edge had been greatly blunted. He now looked like a golden blade that had been sheathed.

He walked into the hall and stopped in his tracks.

Besides Ning Zhuo in the main seat, there were other cultivators in the hall.

Some were watching with the intention of enjoying a spectacle, some had complicated looks in their eyes, and a few were secretly conversing using their divine sense, judging Liu Jinke.

Liu Jinke felt these gazes, and his Adam's apple bobbed slightly.

To say it wasn't awkward would be a lie.

He had once represented the various factions of Flowing Cloud Peak in battle, only to be defeated three times by Ning Zhuo and ultimately surrender in public. Formally joining the Naming Stronghold now meant completely casting aside what little face he had left from the past.

But he also understood that he had long been out of better options. Since he had surrendered in public, he had to walk this path to the very end.

Ning Zhuo looked at him and smiled faintly, "Senior Brother Liu Jinke, have you thought it through?"

Liu Jinke took a deep breath, cupped his hands, and said, "I am willing to join the Naming Stronghold!"

Faint murmurs of discussion immediately rang out in the hall.

"Liu Jinke really joined."

"They fought so fiercely back then, and now he's become one of us."

"Young Master Ning Zhuo defeated him and then took the opportunity to subdue him. This kind of tactic, tsk."

Ning Zhuo nodded. "The Naming Stronghold has just been established, and our rules are still simple. Liu Jinke joins the stronghold today; from now on, he shall share in the stronghold's rights, honors, and disgraces, advancing and retreating together with us. If there are merits, the stronghold will record them. If there are faults, the stronghold will judge them. Everyone here can bear witness."

The ceremony was very simple, even a bit crude.

There was no high altar, no drumming or music, and no complex contract documents. There was only Ning Zhuo as the host, the newly joining cultivator, and a hall full of witnesses.

Direct and highly efficient!

Next were Old Chef, Gongsun Yan, and Lady Spiderweb.

Old Chef walked at the very front.

He wore a dark long robe. His face had dark traces from being smoked by year-round cooking fires, and the wrinkles at the corners of his eyes were deep.

Gongsun Yan followed behind him.

He was originally a guest elder of the Lin Clan but was taken away from Lin Jinglong by Ning Zhuo. When he stepped into the hall, Lin Jinglong's expression became somewhat complex.

Lady Spiderweb walked at the very back.

She had a graceful figure and wore a dark purple long skirt with spiderweb patterns. The layers of her skirt hem were like spiderwebs unfurling in the shadows. Yet, hidden in the depths of her eyes were caution, fear, and a trace of unwillingness.

She was originally a Demonic Cultivator.

After falling into Ning Zhuo's hands, she was imprisoned and forced to help set up the Void Traversing Dragon, completely lacking freedom.

Now, she felt both fear and admiration toward Ning Zhuo.

She feared this youth who appeared gentle but actually possessed incredibly deep methods. She feared the complex network of connections behind him; even though his cultivation was merely at the mid Foundation Establishment Stage, he could command Nascent Soul cultivators and mobilize various factions.

At the same time, Demonic Cultivators often greatly admired the strong.

Lady Spiderweb was very clear that Ning Zhuo was a strong backer, and his future was bright; this backer would only grow stronger.

Under normal circumstances, for genius cultivators to reach the level of Situ Xing, Zhu Fenxiang, and Ban Ji, they were already at the very top. But looking at Ning Zhuo, right in the Rising Cloud Minor Trial phase, he had pulled together a group of people, established the Naming Stronghold, and seized a place on Flowing Cloud Peak.

This kind of ability to stir up the winds and clouds was extremely rare even when looking across all past Flying Cloud Assemblies.

Previously, Lady Spiderweb had been extremely resentful of Ning Zhuo for imprisoning her and wanted to break free. But after learning about the feats Ning Zhuo had accomplished, she changed her mind.

As she entered the hall, her gaze swept over Tu Yuanzi, who was sitting among the audience. Many people had concluded that the latter was Ning Zhuo's family guard.

Ning Zhuo looked at the three of them. "Old Chef, Gongsun Yan, Lady Spiderweb; today you join the Naming Stronghold."

Old Chef immediately bowed in salute, his voice slightly hoarse, "This old man is willing to serve the Young Master and the Naming Stronghold."

Gongsun Yan closely followed. "Gongsun Yan is willing to join the Naming Stronghold. Should the Young Master command, I will definitely do my utmost."

Lady Spiderweb lowered her brow and said, "This concubine is willing to join the Naming Stronghold and heed the Young Master's orders."

The people in the hall communicated via divine sense, continuously discussing the matter.

It was very obvious that Ning Zhuo had intentionally arranged this to slot his own people in.

Some talked about Ning Zhuo's retinue; how could an ordinary cultivator traveling outside bring a chef along? Adding Tu Yuanzi into the mix further confirmed their belief that Ning Zhuo possessed a noble status.

Others were a bit clearer on the situation, detecting the subtlety of Ning Zhuo's move, especially when Chunyangzi was heavily injured and recuperating, thus unable to manage secular affairs.

Outside the hall, a cultivator suddenly came to report, "Young Master, there is another person outside requesting an audience, saying he is willing to join the Naming Stronghold."

"Oh?" Ning Zhuo's brow twitched slightly.

Many cultivators in the hall also immediately looked toward the doorway.

Today, right after Ning Zhuo announced they were widely recruiting newcomers, people joined the stronghold one after another. But everyone knew: these people were either Ning Zhuo's former subordinates or those who had submitted after defeat; there were reasons for all of them. For another person to arrive right now, taking Ning Zhuo by surprise, meant it was very likely someone outside of his arrangements, which naturally made others feel curious.

Ning Zhuo said, "Please, invite him in."

Not long after, a male cultivator walked into the guest hall.

He wore a light gray long robe made of a material that was not particularly luxurious. The edge of the robe was lined with a pale blue thread, resembling the thin ice that first forms on a lake's surface in winter.

This person's face was like cold porcelain, his skin so pale it was almost entirely lacking in color. His eye sockets were slightly deep, his nose bridge was very straight, and his lip color was faint. On his earlobe was a small, pale blue cold spot, like a fleck of ice embedded in his flesh.

His hands were hidden within his sleeves, and his footsteps were very light.

As he walked into the hall, he did not project any imposing aura from his body, yet everyone could still feel a faint, subtle chill. That coldness was not piercing to the bone; instead, it carried a quietness like the morning frost.

When the warm light of the Sun Crystal Lamp fell on him, it seemed to be thinned out by a layer by the chill.
