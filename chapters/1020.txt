Chapter 1020: An Even Bigger Leverage!

Ning Zhuo’s eyes narrowed slightly.

Before this operation, Sun Lingtong had already obtained the complete text of the Pure Yang Pill Scripture from Ning Zhuo.

"The earth vein structure of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is like a natural grand furnace. If Chunyangzi enters secluded cultivation here to attempt a breakthrough to Soul Formation, he can place his excessive pure Yang energy into the altar. This geomantic treasure land will bear the backlash of 'isolated Yang fails to grow' on his behalf. Once the Yang Qi is locked, supported by the cloud Qi, and nourished by the earth fire, that crucial spark where extreme Yang gives birth to Yin will very likely appear."

"This is what Chunyangzi is desperately fighting for!"

"This is his Soul Formation opportunity!!"

Realizing this truth, Sun Lingtong’s heart trembled fiercely.

He and Ning Zhuo both completely understood now: this was why Chunyangzi had been so urgent recently, even willing to empty the Pure Yang Palace's treasury and work day and night.

Just as Sun Lingtong was about to retreat, a muffled explosion suddenly erupted underground.

The Mountain-Crushing Dust-Returning Method had been activated.

The entire southwestern earth network was violently yanked. The phantom roots of the Green Horned Dragon where Sun Lingtong was hiding snapped abruptly, and the rock layer beneath his feet caved in. His body dropped, nearly sweeping him into the fissure.

Crap! he cried out inwardly, but his body reacted faster than his thoughts.

Thieving Art - Empty Sleeve Flip!

Sun Lingtong flicked his sleeves, and two gray cloth talismans, as thin as cicada wings, flew out from his cuffs. The cloth talismans burned rapidly, turning his body into a blur as they carried him away.

Tapping his toes continuously against the air, his figure floated lightly over the edge of the fissure.

But before he could land steadily, a plume of grayish-white poisonous gas spewed from underground.

This was the residual poison from Tan Zhu's Withered Flower Three Lives Seal mixing with the earth vein's Yin fire. Stirred up by the shattered Earth Element spiritual energy, it formed a temporary toxic tide.

When the toxic tide rushed in front of Sun Lingtong, it still carried the lingering traces of bone-green, blood-gray, and soul-purple.

Sun Lingtong's expression changed. "What potent poison!"

In this critical moment, he couldn't think of a defensive method.

"I have some too!" Sun Lingtong pulled out his dagger and slashed fiercely in front of him.

Mixed Poison - Naming Pill Poison!

The two poisons collided, using poison to combat poison.

Sizzle sizzle...

A teeth-grinding corrosive sound echoed in the air.

"Little Zhuo!" Having bought himself half a breath of time, Sun Lingtong shouted through his divine sense.

Ning Zhuo made his move at almost the exact same time.

Spirit Communication Art, Life Hanging by a Thread!

The strings of Life Hanging by a Thread abruptly pulled taut.

Inside the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor, a mirror-like gleam surfaced deep in Ning Zhuo's eyes. His right hand pressed against the edge of the Spirit Communication Mirror, and his left hand gently hooked a finger.

The scene that had once occurred in White Paper Immortal City replayed itself within the Myriad Manifestations Sect.

Sun Lingtong suddenly vanished into the distance, successfully escaping.

In almost the very next breath, Chunyangzi appeared at the exact spot where Sun Lingtong had been.

"Who was that?! Which faction sent him?"

Fury.

Apprehension.

And a trace of irrepressible alarm.

"Stronghold Lord!" On the surface, a trusted subordinate ran over.

The confidant reported urgently, "The southwestern earth network is damaged, and three fire eyes of the Sun-Patrolling Night-Illuminating Ring are unstable. The Grand Formation Master says the Nine Palaces Formation can temporarily contract inward, but it won't hold for long. We've already activated the Green Horned Dragon roots to fill the gaps and are currently using shadow reflection to capture traces."

"Lock down the information," Chunyangzi ordered. "For tonight's events, publicly announce only that the formation foundation experienced an accidental tremor. Not a single word otherwise is to be leaked."

"Yes, sir!"

Chunyangzi continued his commands, "Have the formation masters contract the Nine Palaces and protect the Yang-Locking Well first. Order our men to force all the Green Horned Dragon roots into the southwestern earth network; even if we lose thirty percent of them, we must stabilize the fire vein. Finally, secure the formation shadows - I want all the Qi signatures from tonight preserved!"

"Yes, sir!"

A fierce glint flashed in Chunyangzi's eyes. "Summon Nine Fire Dragon Lord, Tu Yuanzi, and Old Man Baicao here. If Tan Zhu returns, have him come too. Do it immediately!"

The subordinate didn't understand the deeper meaning behind this but quickly retreated to execute the orders resolutely.

Chunyangzi stood alone above the southwestern earth network.

The night breeze blew, causing the crimson-gold formation lanterns to sway. The light and shadow danced across his face, flickering dimly.

His injuries hadn't healed in the first place, and now, having forcibly used his pure Yang spiritual energy to suppress the earth vein, his chest felt a faint chill. The scar left by The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook felt like a micro-thin needle of ice piercing deep into his glabella, sending out intermittent stabs of pain.

But all this physical pain paled in comparison to the shock, fury, hatred, and anxiety boiling in his heart.

Situ Xing was the first to arrive!

The star-patterned jade pendant at his waist swayed gently, and his eyes were calm. Raising a hand, he scattered seven silver-white chess pieces. They chimed as they hit the ground, transforming into a small starry scale.

Technique - Seven Stars Shadow Reflection!

Starlight cascaded down, illuminating the fissure in the ground and revealing the details within.

Situ Xing drew a sharp breath, his expression rapidly turning grave. Gritting his teeth, he immediately began to salvage the situation.

Soon after, Lin Jinglong also arrived.

After exchanging words via divine sense, he waved his hand, tossing three Green Horned Dragon Armor-Clasping Seeds onto the edge of the fissure.

The seeds sprouted the moment they hit the soil.

Crack, crack, crack.

Green roots wildly burrowed out. Scale patterns lit up on their surfaces, looking like small dragons coiling into the earth. They entangled the severed fire veins, forcibly pulling several dislocated earth networks back into place. However, the crimson-gold earth fire was too intense; as soon as the Green Horned Dragon roots probed in, they were scorched until they hissed and smoked.

Lin Jinglong frowned but continued to urge them on.

Shen Xi arrived next.

His face was solemn as he held the Nine Palaces Misstep formation plate. The nine palace light dots on its surface were jumping erratically, clearly indicating that the damage to the southwestern earth network had already affected the formation he had laid down.

"Senior Chunyangzi." Shen Xi cupped his hands. "The Nine Palaces Formation can contract inward three times. After three times, if the earth network remains unstable, the formation plate's foundation will be damaged."

Chunyangzi said, "Contract it once for now."

Shen Xi didn't ask further questions, pressing his finger onto the central palace crystal of the formation plate.

Formation - Nine Palaces Centering Lock!

The Nine Palaces formation plate emitted a clear hum as nine formation lines retracted from all directions, like nine thin, silver-white snakes burrowing into the southwestern earth vein. The distorted sense of space was forcibly suppressed, and the expansion of the ground fissures came to a halt.

Nine Fire Dragon Lord, clad in red-gold scale armor with sparks dancing across his dragon horns, arrived with an expression that shifted from impatience to confusion.

When he saw the southwestern fissure, his expression immediately changed.

"Who did this?"

Chunyangzi didn't answer, merely directing him to start repairing it immediately.

Nine Fire Dragon Lord nodded and promptly set to work.

Tu Yuanzi, wearing a loose earthy-yellow robe, hurried over.

Chunyangzi asked him to step in and specifically assess the extent of the damage.

Tu Yuanzi crouched down and pressed both hands to the ground. After sensing for a moment, he said deeply, "The earth network was tampered with, very deep down. The tearing right now is extremely severe; it requires a long period of recovery."

Chunyangzi looked at him. "How long?"

Tu Yuanzi hesitated for a moment. He wanted to offer some comfort but ultimately decided to tell the truth. "If you only want to close the formation, we can barely manage it tonight. But if you want full recovery, it will take at least three to five years, and up to ten years or more. Furthermore, since there's poison mixed in, the time required will be even longer."

Chunyangzi felt a sweet taste in his throat and nearly spat out a mouthful of blood.

But he quickly and resolutely swallowed the blood back down!

By the time Tan Zhu arrived unhurriedly, the danger at the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar had temporarily been resolved, and the situation had stabilized.

Chunyangzi stared fixedly at Tan Zhu with a gaze as sharp as a carving knife.

Sensing the anomaly, everyone cast their eyes toward Tan Zhu.

Tan Zhu kept his expression cold and hard, pretending to know nothing about what had happened, and even openly asked Chunyangzi what exactly occurred.

Consumed by a mix of anger and hatred, Chunyangzi remained as cold as ice on the surface, merely shaking his head and stating that an accidental tremor in the formation foundation had caused all this.

Tan Zhu sneered inwardly. I didn't think you'd dare speak the truth anyway.

Speaking the truth would only make things worse and result in a greater negative impact.

Chunyangzi possessed profound political acumen; he would never make an unwise move like confronting or accusing someone right then and there.

Chunyangzi stared at Tan Zhu and suddenly smiled faintly.

He took out a small vial. "The formation foundation shook accidentally, leaving behind inexplicable Qi signatures. I acted in time to gather all these signatures in my hands. It is only a matter of time before the truth is uncovered."

Behind his words lay a heavy threat.

A sharp glint flashed through Tan Zhu's eyes. "Very well! If an enemy caused this sabotage, I will strike with full force to severely punish the culprit!"

He sneered inwardly again. Chunyangzi has run out of tricks and lost his mind! Although I used a poison method, it was based entirely on external toxic materials. With that barrier, and given that the poison method was specially created, it absolutely won't leave behind any of my own Qi signatures.

He has zero evidence on me.

Shi Qian... That man is Dong Chen's capable deathsworn; he wouldn't leave any obvious clues either.

Only the last person, the one with the weakest cultivation, will leave Chunyangzi with some leverage.

That's fine too.

Tan Zhu was also very curious about who, or which faction, was secretly participating in this matter.

Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor.

Sun Lingtong plopped his butt onto the ground, gasping heavily for air. His forehead was covered in cold sweat, and his clothes were soaked through at the back.

The young boy's brows furrowed tightly. "Crap, I left behind a little bit of pill poison."

Ning Zhuo shook his head. "The situation was critical. Boss, as long as you didn't get poisoned, it doesn't matter if you left a clue."

He smiled faintly. "The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is Chunyangzi's Soul Formation opportunity. We've already seen through this huge secret. Even if he figures out that it was us spying, what can he do about it?"

"Three days have passed. I can hardly wait to head to the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar tomorrow and have a nice, long chat with Chunyangzi."

"Hahaha."

So what if you have leverage over me?

The leverage I hold over you is even bigger!
