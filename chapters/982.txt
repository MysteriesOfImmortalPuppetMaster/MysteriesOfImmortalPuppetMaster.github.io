Chapter 982: Yang Fish

When Ning Zhuo expressed that he had absolutely no confidence in winning and solely wanted to preserve his own life, Chunyangzi's fists clenched hard. He truly wanted to give the big-headed youth a severe beating right then and there to vent the stifled frustration in his heart.

"It is quite good that you possess such self-awareness," Chunyangzi spoke, his tone stiff.

"However, our Nanming Stronghold charged the peak with all our might, stirring up the winds and clouds with immense momentum. In this five-win martial contest, our stronghold has given its all, even suffering a casualty."

"Under such circumstances, if the fifth match is lost, all our previous efforts will come to naught."

"As military strategy dictates: 'The first drumbeat rouses the spirit, the second weakens it, and by the third, it is exhausted!'"

"Looking at our opponents, their morale is undoubtedly soaring. The next time they attempt to block our charge up the peak, they will definitely be even more united and willing to invest their resources."

"Therefore, absolutely do not be careless. Do not assume there will be many more opportunities. In the future, will we ever have such a good chance again?"

"Ning Zhuo, you are a smart person. Use your brain and think carefully!"

"Nanming Stronghold was formed out of mutual benefit and is extremely loose. If we suffer a crushing defeat and the people's hearts scatter, the stronghold will exist in name only. Thus, this first attempt is very likely our last chance!"

Chunyangzi spoke with righteous indignation, his words resonating with sonorous power.

Ning Zhuo revealed a look of shame. "Senior Chunyangzi, you are absolutely right. But I am still young, and my cultivation level is low - merely at the mid Foundation Establishment Stage."

"Even if I receive some sponsorship, I won't be able to familiarize myself with it in such a short time. I also very much wish to defeat the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, but under these circumstances, I simply have no confidence."

Bang.

Chunyangzi slapped the table, using the action to vent a fraction of his anger.

He knew full well that Ning Zhuo was extorting benefits from him, and he knew the boy had likely been waiting for him to step forward all along, yet he had no choice but to play along.

Furthermore, he even had to cooperate and act out this charade with him.

This kid is truly full of wicked schemes!

Chunyangzi gritted his teeth. "Good. It is enough that you possess the desire to defeat the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. It makes my special trip here worthwhile."

"Since I am the Grand Chieftain of the Nanming Stronghold, how could I sit by and watch you face formidable foes alone?"

"Although the Red-Robed Guest was my enemy, he died in battle for our stronghold. I deeply admire that. Let past grudges scatter with the wind."

"This time, I will do everything in my power to help you secure victory!"

Ning Zhuo wore a look of pleasant surprise. "Really? Senior Chunyangzi, I knew you were a paragon of the Righteous Path! Your magnanimity is boundless and far beyond what ordinary people could ever hope to achieve!"

"But Senior, do you truly have a method to make my combat strength skyrocket enough to rival the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect?"

Ning Zhuo opportunely revealed a trace of doubt.

Chunyangzi quietly watched Ning Zhuo's performance. The fiery glint in his eyes sank to its deepest depths, like molten lava solidifying deep underground - hiding a mountain-incinerating power beneath a calm surface.

He coldly snorted, "After pondering for a long time, I have thought of a miraculous solution for you."

"However, before that, I have an important question to ask you."

"Ning Zhuo, do you still retain your virgin body?"

"Ah?!" This question was asked so abruptly that even Ning Zhuo was momentarily stunned.

Ning Zhuo wanted to answer, but then fell into hesitation.

Physically speaking, he was naturally pure and untainted. However, since beginning his cultivation, he had experienced many bizarre events and was entangled in various matters. For a moment, he wasn't sure if the Pure Yang Palace's definition of a "virgin body" tested the physical body, Primordial Yang, or something else.

Chunyangzi was already losing his patience.

He reached out and grabbed, his five fingers locking around Ning Zhuo's wrist like iron pincers. He said in a deep voice, "Do not resist. If you want to defeat the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect instead of being beaten to death by them, let this seat test you."

Ning Zhuo's expression changed. He immediately prepared to activate his spiritual energy to protect himself and drive out Chunyangzi's spiritual energy and divine sense.

Rashly probing another person's cultivation status like this was a massive taboo. Even against a Nascent Soul cultivator like Chunyangzi, Ning Zhuo was ready to turn hostile.

However, Chunyangzi did not use his spiritual energy or divine sense. Instead, he activated a unique minor art that seemed designed solely to detect Ning Zhuo's virginity.

In the very instant Ning Zhuo wanted to stop him, Chunyangzi was already finished.

"??!!!" Chunyangzi's eyebrows abruptly shot up, his brow peaks pulled high as if by invisible strings. His expression was almost exaggerated.

"Your Yang Qi... is actually this abundant!" He still held onto Ning Zhuo's wrist, looking as though he had caught a glowing, rare, and precious jade. His fingertips even felt slightly hot.

"A virgin body. The purest virgin body!" His gaze toward Ning Zhuo completely changed. The scripture characters on his crimson-gold Daoist robe couldn't help but swim around, moving faster and faster, revealing his suddenly surging emotions.

"Ah!" Ning Zhuo was stunned once again.

Chunyangzi looked Ning Zhuo up and down. In his eyes, there was astonishment, envy, annoyance, and an uncontrollable surge of yearning. "Ning Zhuo, are you willing to switch to cultivating our sect's Pure Yang Pill Scripture?"

At this moment, his tone softened, and his perception of Ning Zhuo completely shifted.

Ning Zhuo revealed a strange expression. He gently pulled his wrist free from Chunyangzi's grasp and rubbed the reddened skin.

"Senior, there is no longer an independent mountain gate for the Pure Yang Palace in this world. Today, there is only the Chunyang Peak of the Myriad Manifestations Sect."

"Furthermore, this junior comes from a Righteous Path family and still bears the responsibility of producing offspring. If I were to maintain my pure Yang virginity for my entire life just to cultivate the Pure Yang Pill Scripture, I'm afraid it would violate filial piety."

The fervor on Chunyangzi's face stalled. He subconsciously picked up his teacup, wanting to use the act of drinking tea to cover up his gaffe, but the moment he smelled the tea's aroma, he found it extremely ordinary!

Chunyangzi picked up the teacup and set it back down. The base of the cup clicked lightly against the table. "This seat... has lost his composure."

Even so, when he looked at Ning Zhuo, the embers of fiery light in his eyes had yet to fade.

He sighed in admiration, "This seat has seen countless people, but I have never seen such dense and pure Pure Yang Qi as yours. If you were to cultivate the Pure Yang Pill Scripture, your future achievements would definitely far surpass mine, and there is even a sliver of a chance that you could stand shoulder-to-shoulder with our founding patriarch."

Ning Zhuo also felt astonished, not expecting Chunyangzi to think so highly of him.

However, his path of cultivating the Three Sects' Supreme Methods had long been set in stone, and his greatest interest had always been Mechanism Arts. In his heart, no matter how good the Pure Yang Pill Scripture was, it couldn't compare to the Three Sects' Supreme Methods.

Ning Zhuo wisely remained silent, not taking the bait.

Chunyangzi's regret was too genuine, so genuine that for a moment, Ning Zhuo didn't know how to respond without losing his sense of propriety.

Fortunately, Chunyangzi quickly reined in his thoughts. A figure capable of presiding over the Pure Yang Palace for many years naturally wouldn't let momentary emotions delay important matters.

"Since you possess such Yang Qi and have a virgin body, you are suited to use our Pure Yang Palace's sect-guarding spiritual treasure." When he said the words "Pure Yang Palace," his tone noticeably deepened, like taking a firm, heavy step in a vast expanse of snow.

Ning Zhuo's heart stirred, catching the underlying implication - Chunyangzi still viewed himself as the master of the Pure Yang Palace. Though the Myriad Manifestations Sect had given him a new title, it had not truly erased the old mountain gate residing in his heart.

Chunyangzi raised a hand and gave a brush. A burst of golden light erupted within the cave manor. A small golden fish materialized out of thin air, hovering above his palm.

The small fish was only three inches long, yet its body seemed forged from solidified sunlight. Every scale resembled gold leaf as thin as a cicada's wings, and along the edges of the scales flowed lines of crimson-gold fire, as fine as strands of hair.

The moment it appeared, the temperature inside the cave manor abruptly spiked. Even the Three Lights formation patterns on the stone walls were dyed with a warm hue, akin to the morning dawn.

The small fish gently flicked its tail, and a delicate, luminous chiming echoed in the air, as if countless minuscule golden bells were simultaneously rustled by the morning breeze.

The most bizarre feature was its eyes. While its entire body was pure gold, its two eyes were the color of black jade. The blackness was as deep as the endless night and as still as the freezing water of an ancient well. Not a single trace of cold Yin Qi leaked out; it was entirely contained within those two dots of ink-black pupils.

As the golden fish swam, its black jade eyes would occasionally shift, and a hair-thin strand of Yin Qi would fleetingly flash amidst the room full of golden light.

"Yang Extremity Generating Yin!" Ning Zhuo's pupils slightly shrank at the sight.

The Yang Attribute of this Yang Fish had grown so intense that it reached the absolute extreme, yet right at that peak of intensity, it had nurtured those two black jade Yin eyes. It swam above Chunyangzi's palm like a blazing sun harboring two black stars.

Chunyangzi looked at the Yang Fish in his palm, a look of profound pride rising on his face. "This is our sect-guarding spiritual treasure - the Yang Fish. It has the effect of amplifying one's destiny and nourishing one's Dao Foundation."

"It has followed the successive sect masters of my Pure Yang Palace, undergoing a thousand years of refinement. Its spiritual nature is deeply rooted, and it is fiercely loyal to the Pure Yang Palace - it is my Pure Yang Palace's greatest trump card!"

The Yang Fish seemed to have heard its master's praise. Its tail gently patted Chunyangzi's palm, instantly splashing a speck of golden light onto the floor tiles, which actually caused the icy stone surface to radiate a faint warmth.

Chunyangzi said, "Although the Yang Fish is a spiritual treasure, it is a pity that its sentience is as ignorant as a newborn child. Its spiritual intelligence hasn't fully opened, making it difficult to communicate with. It naturally gravitates towards places brimming with abundant Yang Qi. The Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect are advanced in age, and their cultivation methods lean heavily towards Netherworld Death Qi. The Yang Fish will innately loathe them. If you take the martial arena to fight the Three Valiants, it will naturally favor you."

Ning Zhuo stared at the Yang Fish, still remaining silent.

Just by observing it from a distance, he could faintly sense that the Yang Fish's tiny body contained a magnificent, immeasurable power!

Chunyangzi continued, "Of course, a mere inclination is not enough. I want it to truly acknowledge you, treating you just like the successive sect masters of the Pure Yang Palace!"

After he finished speaking, he flipped his left sleeve and took out a medicinal pill.

The pill was entirely unremarkable. It was grey and round, looking at first glance like a pebble casually picked up from the side of the road. Its surface had no pill patterns, no treasure light, nor did it emit a strong medicinal fragrance.

However, when Chunyangzi took it out, his hand visibly sank by half an inch. This medicinal pill was clearly incredibly heavy!

Chunyangzi's expression was far more solemn than at any prior moment. Even his breathing became much more restrained. "This pill is called the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill. Its value is even higher than that of the Yang Fish."

Ning Zhuo's heart jolted.

Chunyangzi placed the pill on the table between them. "In their youth, every generation's Chunyangzi swallowed this pill to comprehend the True Intent of the Yang Dao within it. Before stepping down, they would then use the fruits of their many years of cultivation to replenish the pill, allowing the True Intent inside to be passed down endlessly through the generations. It has been passed down for many eras, and it contains the complete understanding of the Yang Dao, the Pill Dao, and the Fire Dao from every successive sect master of the Pure Yang Palace!"

Ning Zhuo could not conceal his shocked expression. "Senior, what are you doing?!"

It was too precious, far too precious.

This was actually the legacy treasure of the Pure Yang Palace.

No wonder its value far exceeded the spiritual treasure, the Yang Fish.

This Pure Yang Patriarch Pill could be called the very foundation of the Pure Yang Palace, the absolute essence of its accumulated heritage!

It was strictly meant for the successive leaders of the Pure Yang Palace, yet now, Chunyangzi was offering it to Ning Zhuo, an outsider, to use!

Chunyangzi stared fixedly at him, his gaze like two golden branding irons. "I want you to sign a soul contract and swear an oath beneath a divine statue!"

Ning Zhuo did not hesitate for a second. "This junior complies!"

Chunyangzi's gaze remained imposing, as if trying to see right through Ning Zhuo.

Ning Zhuo added, "We can finalize the contract right now. As for swearing the oath - this junior is willing to do it in broad daylight, in front of the public, facing the Truth Bell!"

The golden light in the depths of Chunyangzi's eyes abruptly surged.

Ning Zhuo's expression was completely candid. His eyes were clear and unclouded, without the slightest evasion.

The atmosphere between the two grew increasingly grave.

"Good!" Chunyangzi finally spoke, breaking the heavy tension. "With the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill protecting you, the Yang Fish can be used by you. You won't need to expend any spiritual energy; the Yang Fish possesses its own abundant might."

"With our sect-guarding spiritual treasure protecting you, you will stand in an invincible position."

"Ning Zhuo!"

Ning Zhuo cupped his fists. "This junior is here!"

Chunyangzi said, "You must win!"

Ning Zhuo took a deep breath, bowed respectfully, and declared, "With Senior's help, this junior will definitely win!"

Chunyangzi gazed deeply at Ning Zhuo.

After a moment of silence, his cuff shifted slightly. The Yang Fish in his palm gave a gentle flick of its tail fin and leisurely swam out from between his fingers. Like a golden leaf held up by the morning light, it gracefully floated into the midair space between them.

Instantly, golden light spread out silently like the tide.

The Yang Fish was merely three inches long, its entire body as bright as molten gold, yet it illuminated every corner of the room like a newly risen morning sun.

The intricate formation patterns on the stone walls lit up one after another. The originally deep and quiet cave manor was painted in a warm, gentle golden hue. Even the cold remnants of tea in the cup on the table emitted a very faint wisp of white steam, like a nearly imperceptible sigh of early snow about to melt.

Ning Zhuo did not reach out to touch it.

He only lowered his eyelids, quietly watching the golden fish swimming in the air. Its movements were nimble and innocent, much like a young fish playing in a shallow stream. It first completed a circle around Chunyangzi's palm, like a child playfully running around their closest elder.

"Go ahead," Chunyangzi smiled as he looked at it.

Grasping Chunyangzi's meaning, the Yang Fish slowly shifted its black jade-like eyes toward Ning Zhuo, carrying a somewhat ignorant yet focused expression.

Ning Zhuo felt a slight flush of heat, much like the sensation of the first ray of sunlight on a winter morning piercing through thick clouds and landing on his chest.

The Yang Fish swam slowly through the air, gradually approaching Ning Zhuo.

The Yang Dao spiritual treasure's most primal and purest perception of untainted Yang Qi made it develop a slight interest in Ning Zhuo.

Ning Zhuo slowly raised his right hand.

Chunyangzi watched all of this. A sliver of golden light wavered faintly in the depths of his eyes, but he did not stop him.

Ning Zhuo's hand stopped three inches in front of the Yang Fish, palm facing up. He didn't activate the slightest bit of spiritual energy, nor did he release his divine sense to probe it. Instead, he made the aura around his body exceptionally calm and incredibly docile, like the tranquil surface of a lake without a breath of wind.

The Yang Fish paused in midair for a single breath.

The tiny golden scales on its body opened slightly, and the crimson-gold lines of fire flowing between them abruptly lit up like small streams. Within the cave manor, an incredibly light and delicate luminous chiming echoed - ding-dong, ding-dong - resembling countless miniature golden bells hidden deep within the morning mist, gently swayed by the very first breeze.
