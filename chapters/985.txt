Chapter 985: Under the Truth Bell

Liu Jinke looked at Ning Zhuo, a wry smile pulling at the corners of his mouth. "I'm currently staying with you, eating your food, living under your roof, and receiving the protection of your Nanming Stronghold. Logically speaking, I shouldn't be saying these discouraging words."

He paused. "You've defeated me three times. But although I, Liu Jinke, am down and out, I'm not so petty as to watch the person who defeated me walk towards a dead end and still clap my hands in cheer from the sidelines."

A trace of self-mockery appeared in his eyes. "I joined you previously because I thought you, kid, were deep-scheming and ruthless enough to live a long time in a place like the Myriad Manifestations Sect. Now it seems my judgment might not have been accurate."

"To force yourself to face the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect for the sake of a demonic soul - is it worth it? If you wanted to establish your reputation, you've already done enough. The Nanming Stronghold has just been established, five Nascent Soul cultivators have joined, and you've even beaten me into submission. The reputation of you, Ning Zhuo, is already loud enough. So what if it gets a bit louder? Loud enough to cost your life?"

For a moment, complete silence fell among the few of them.

Ning Zhuo said, "I am very grateful that Senior Brother could say these things to me. But this time, I must go."

Liu Jinke's frown deepened.

Ning Zhuo slowly said, "Reputation is of course important. But my going today is only partly for reputation."

Liu Jinke stared at him. "What else?"

Ning Zhuo's gaze was as clear as a mirror. "My Dao Path."

Liu Jinke furrowed his brows even tighter. "Dao Path? You call this a Dao Path? Seeking your own doom can also be called a Dao Path?"

Qing Chi immediately glared at him. Huo Nian'er also burned with anger upon hearing this, but Gongsun Yan reached out and pressed down on his shoulder.

Ning Zhuo did not get angry. "This is my Righteous Path!"

Liu Jinke's chest heaved several times. He couldn't understand it!

But seeing Ning Zhuo's firm gaze and genuinely feeling his resolve, his frustration ultimately turned into a low sigh. "People like you are truly a hassle."

Ning Zhuo smiled. "The fact that Senior Brother is worrying about me shows that sparing your life back then wasn't for nothing."

Liu Jinke's face darkened. "Stop flattering yourself. I'm just afraid that after you die, Nanming Stronghold won't be able to protect me, and I'll be dragged out to settle old scores again."

He shook his head. "I can't persuade you."

Ning Zhuo nodded. "Senior Brother has already done his best."

Liu Jinke let out a sigh and stepped aside to clear the way. "Then go."

Ning Zhuo stepped forward.

Old Chef hurriedly took a step to follow, then stopped, his lips moving. "Young Master..."

Old Chef ultimately just bowed in a salute, his voice as hoarse as grinding gravel. "This old one will wait in the cave manor for Young Master's return. Today's Spirit Food will always be kept warm for you."

Gongsun Yan's eyes were slightly red as he lowered his head and said, "Young Master, I will continue to inspect the Mystic Iron Armor and the mechanical materials. With just a word from you, I can start working on them at any time."

Huo Nian'er: "I will follow Senior Brother Gongsun's arrangements."

Ning Zhuo nodded. "Thank you for your hard work."

Seeing everyone's expressions, Qing Chi walked up to Ning Zhuo's side. "I will go with the Young Master. Wherever the Young Master goes, I will go. Whether it's the Truth Bell, the martial arena, or the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, I will follow."

Ning Zhuo smiled faintly. "Qing Chi, you will stay in the cave manor today."

Ning Zhuo raised his hand, gently patted her shoulder, turned around, and walked out of the cave manor.

Outside the cave manor, the daylight was at its peak.

A molten-gold sun hung high in the sky, dyeing the thousands of clouds into layered, warm hues.

Verification Peak was located deep within the Myriad Manifestations Sect.

The terrain of this peak was not beautiful. Its entirety was as pitch-black as ink, and from afar, it looked like a chunk of cold iron forcibly pulled from deep underground - sharp-edged and starkly rugged.

The mountain walls were covered with scorch marks from raging flames, heavy and black, exuding a withered, charred aura that hadn't dissipated in a millennium. It was as if the entire peak had once survived a heaven-incinerating fire but refused to fall, merely standing there in silence, using its battle-scarred body as its own epitaph.

From the foot of the mountain to the summit, there were exactly 9,999 stone steps. Each step was broad and coarse, with uneven edges, transmitting a fine, gritty sensation through the soles of one's shoes when stepped on.

Mountain gusts blew up from the surging sea of clouds below, carrying the unique crisp chill and vast emptiness of high altitudes. There were no railings on either side of the stone steps, only profound cloud vapor rolling and undulating like white waves. As the clouds parted and closed, the silhouettes of the Myriad Manifestations Sect's various peaks flickered in and out of view, like a long scroll unceasingly flipped by the wind.

Standing on this mountain path, one would naturally give rise to a certain feeling - the only way forward is up; there is nowhere else to retreat.

When the cultivators saw Ning Zhuo, a commotion immediately broke out.

"He actually came!" someone exclaimed in a low voice.

"This kid actually dared to come to Verification Peak. Taking an oath under the Truth Bell... once the words are spoken, he is completely cutting off his own retreat."

"For the Red-Robed Guest's demonic soul? The Red-Robed Guest originated from the Demonic Path after all. Even if he joined the Myriad Manifestations Sect for many years, why would Ning Zhuo go to such lengths?"

"What do you know? The Nanming Stronghold was just established, and the Red-Robed Guest died because of it. If he just abandons him, the Righteous Path reputation he built up earlier would immediately suffer a massive blow."

"Reputation is reputation; life is life. What kind of figures are the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect? Those are fierce individuals who can hunt down Nascent Soul cultivators!"

"Actually, at this point in time, it would be extremely difficult for Ning Zhuo to back down even if he wanted to. Who told him to shamelessly boast about wanting to rescue that demonic soul?"

The voices in the crowd were kept very low, yet they intertwined densely, like the mountain wind scraping through thousands of stone crevices.

Some felt pity, some were excited, some thought Ning Zhuo was utterly foolish, and some hid respect in their eyes. But even more people held the mindset of coldly watching the situation unfold from the sidelines.

Situ Xing stood next to a protruding black rock halfway up the mountain.

He watched Ning Zhuo climb the steps one by one, his gaze flickering slightly.

Beside him, Shen Xi and Lin Jinglong stood with their arms crossed, watching.

Shen Xi sighed softly, his brows furrowed with worry.

Lin Jinglong, on the other hand, said, "Regardless of whether he can leave the arena alive from the hands of the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, for this courage alone, I acknowledge him!"

Chunyangzi stood at a high vantage point.

His long hair was bound in a crown, and his face was gaunt yet pristine like an ancient pine, but a faint golden light swirled in his eyes.

As he watched Ning Zhuo climb the stairs, there was worry in his eyes, as well as burning anticipation.

Many cultivators from Flowing Cloud Peak had also arrived. They stood further away, their robes of various colors, yet their auras subtly merged into a united chill, like the turbulent undercurrents beneath a frozen winter lake.

Among them, some hoped Ning Zhuo would die, some hoped the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect would be injured, and others simply wanted to see the Nanming Stronghold and the several major factions fight until both sides were mutually destroyed. Everyone wore a different expression, hiding their own calculations in their eyes.

It wasn't just Flowing Cloud Peak; Heavy Array Peak, Danxia Peak, Ten Thousand Beasts Peak, the Demon Subjugation Hall, and others had all dispatched their respective envoys specifically to observe the ceremony.

As Ning Zhuo walked higher and higher, the voices on the mountain path gradually died down. In the end, only the sound of the wind remained.

This wind blew from deep within the sea of clouds beyond the cliff, passing through the crevices of the black rocks and brushing past thousands of robes. It let out a low, drawn-out whimper, like the only unsuppressed breath left between heaven and earth.

Ning Zhuo stepped onto the final stone step.

No-Retreat Square suddenly unfurled before his eyes.

The peak's summit had been sheared flat as if by a single blade. Three sides were bottomless abysses, and the cloud vapor churned endlessly beyond the cliffs, like a white tide unceasingly crashing against an unseen shore.

The square was massive, paved with black stone. Each slab was as thick and heavy as a tombstone. As soon as one stepped onto it, it felt as though an invisible weight pressed gently against their chest. The initially drifting thoughts would also settle down, like reefs revealed after the receding tide.

There were no walls or railings on the square - only endless wind and the churning sea of clouds further in the distance.

A large number of cultivators were scattered around the perimeter of the square, their robes snapping in the wind like countless battle flags fluttering in the gale.

Despite so many people gathered, there was no real clamor. All the sounds seemed to be sucked in by those black stones, dissipating heavily upon hitting the ground, like drops of ink in water, leaving no trace.

The Truth Bell was situated at the very front of the broken cliff.

There was no bell tower, no canopy, no jade pillars, nor any incense altars. That matte-black iron bell sat heavily on a low stone foundation - stout, deformed, and completely devoid of luster, like a piece of scrap iron abandoned by heaven and earth at the edge of the cliff.

The bell's body looked like solidified night, and also like the charred core left behind after a massive fire. Behind it was the boundless sea of clouds, the white clouds churning like waves, which only served to make its oppressive, silent blackness stand out even more. It was as if the millennium-long weight of the entire Verification Peak rested upon this single bell.

The moment Ning Zhuo laid eyes on it, his mind sank slightly. He felt the Yang Fish in his sleeve pause as well. That small spiritual treasure fish seemed to have also sensed the ancient, eternal silence deep within the Truth Bell. Its golden scale-light retracted considerably within his sleeve, leaving only a hint of warmth pressing against his wrist like a heartbeat.

Under the watchful eyes of the crowd, Ning Zhuo walked up to the front of the Truth Bell.

The black stones beneath his feet were as cold as iron, and a chilling sensation even slowly seeped in through the soles of his shoes.

The mountain wind swept past his cheeks, blowing the hem of his dark cyan robes so that they fluttered wildly.

Countless gazes landed on him simultaneously. Some were as fervent as fire, some as hostile as a blade, some as scrutinizing as a ruler, some as expectant as the morning light, and some held the malice of waiting to see him plunge into the abyss.

Ning Zhuo raised his hands and first offered a proper, formal bow to the Truth Bell. Then, he turned around, facing the crowd from afar.

The youth's features were still handsome and delicate, his forehead full like the moon, and his eyes as clear as if freshly washed.

At this moment, standing on this coarse, black, and icy Verification Peak, amidst the howling cold winds of the No-Retreat Square, his figure couldn't be considered tall, yet it showed not the slightest intent of dodging or shrinking back.

Ning Zhuo spoke.

His voice was not loud, yet it was steadily supported by those silent black stones on the square, transmitting clearly in all directions like a stone tossed into a tranquil lake, its ripples spreading out layer by layer.

"Seniors, Fellow Sect Members. Today, I, Ning Zhuo, have ascended Verification Peak and stepped onto the No-Retreat Square, not to make a name for myself, nor to show off my bravery, but to fulfill a promise and shoulder a responsibility."

The sound of the wind paused slightly, as if even the mountain gusts were listening to him continue.

Many people's brows twitched slightly, their gazes becoming even more focused.

Ning Zhuo continued, "The Nanming Stronghold was just established, originally intended for fellow Daoists to support one another in seeking a place to stand. Although Senior Red-Robed Guest came from the Demonic Path, he had been part of the Myriad Manifestations Sect for many years and was bound by the sect's rules. While his actions still bore some old habits, he ultimately operated within the bounds of the sect's laws."

On the side of Flowing Cloud Peak, someone let out a cold laugh, the corners of their mouth curving into an acerbic arc. Yet, they did not speak up to interrupt - in front of the Truth Bell, no one dared to easily cause trouble.

Ning Zhuo's gaze swept over the crowd, his expression as pure and upright as a stream flowing over white stones. "This time, Senior Red-Robed Guest perished because of matters concerning the Nanming Stronghold, and his demonic soul has fallen into the hands of others. Since this matter arose due to my Nanming Stronghold, I, Ning Zhuo, cannot simply stay out of it."

He paused. "There is a saying: to establish oneself in the world, one must have integrity; to walk the Dao, one must bear responsibility. I, Ning Zhuo, have shallow cultivation and am still young; I dare not claim to be able to turn the tide. But as the founder of the Nanming Stronghold, since I have received everyone's trust, I must shoulder your hardships."

His voice was deep and steady. "Today, I am willing to take an oath under the Truth Bell." He raised his head, looking at that matte-black iron bell. "In this life, I, Ning Zhuo, will do everything in my power to track down the demonic soul of the Red-Robed Guest. If his soul still exists, I will absolutely find a way to rescue him!"

The golden light in Chunyangzi's eyes suddenly flared.

Situ Xing, Shen Xi, and Lin Jinglong remained silent.

Tuyuanzi, hiding at the back of the crowd, felt his nose tingle with sour emotion upon seeing this scene and quickly lowered his head to rub his face.

The expressions on the cultivators of Flowing Cloud Peak clearly turned quite a bit uglier.

Ning Zhuo turned back to look at the Truth Bell. "These words come from the bottom of my heart. I ask the Bell Spirit to testify."

The next moment, the black bell trembled slightly and let out a toll.

Clang...

The bell's chime was clear and melodious, rapidly spreading across the entire peak, proving that what Ning Zhuo said came entirely from his heart - they were true words!

The crowd's expressions became increasingly solemn.

Ning Zhuo turned back around and looked at the various cultivators in the square. "Now, Senior Red-Robed Guest's soul is trapped in the hands of the various factions of Flowing Cloud Peak. Because this involves many parties, I, Ning Zhuo, am willing to establish a martial duel agreement with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect - a battle of life and death!"

"In this battle, life and death are not forbidden!"

"If I lose, I, Ning Zhuo, will suffer the consequences myself. If I win, I ask everyone here to bear witness - Senior Red-Robed Guest's soul must be returned to my Nanming Stronghold. Once his soul is stabilized, I will then return his bodily remains and relics together."

The youth's voice spread steadily in the wind, like an invisible sword plunged into stone.

Open and aboveboard!

Clang...

The Truth Bell echoed once more, verifying that Ning Zhuo's words came entirely from his heart.

Just then, a light cough came from the crowd. "Well said."

The crowd looked toward the source of the sound, only to see a gaunt, withered old man slowly walking out from among them.

He wore an old cyan robe with a few frayed spots at the corners, and his fingers were long and withered like bamboo joints that had been air-dried for years.

Qinghuangzi.

Quite a few cultivators recognized this old man.

Although his days were numbered, he still possessed a bearing that made it impossible for people to ignore him. The wrinkles on his face were as deep as gullies, but his eyes were as clear as a spring, carrying a lucidity settled by the passage of time.

He looked at Ning Zhuo. "For this old man's [Empty Valley Syllable Azure Mechanism Cylinder] to have a successor like you... very good."

Ning Zhuo hurriedly bowed. "Senior."

The youth exhaled a breath of turbid air. "I originally thought that Senior would..."

Qinghuangzi waved his hand. "Relax, this old man has no intention of stopping you."

Taking advantage of the motion, his withered, raised hand gestured lightly behind him. "Not only that, but this old bag of bones has also invited people to support you this time."

As soon as his voice fell, a surge of boundless righteous aura welled up on the other side of the square.

A group of Confucian cultivators arrived side by side. Their robes and crowns were meticulously neat, and their auras were pure and upright, like a breeze brushing across book pages.

Walking at the very front was Duanmu Zhang. His face was square and proper, not a single hair of his beard or head was out of place, and his gaze was steady. The several Confucian cultivators behind him each had their own distinct bearing - some held scrolls in their hands, some had jade brushes hanging from their waists, and the fragrance of ink wafted from between the sleeves of others.

The Confucian cultivators approached Ning Zhuo and came to a halt.

Duanmu Zhang looked at Ning Zhuo, his expression stern and solemn. "Little Friend Ning Zhuo, there is naturally no need to speak much about the dangers of what you are doing today. However, your ability to publicly shoulder this responsibility on Verification Peak already embodies the integrity of a gentleman. We are willing to compose a poem to serve as a testimony for this matter, and use our boundless Literary Qi to wish you instant success."
