Chapter 979: The Three Valiants Life-Claiming Nail

Upon learning the news, the Confucian cultivator group of the Myriad Manifestations Sect could finally sit still no longer.

Duanmu Zhang, Chu Xuangui, Nangong Zhi, Li Guanyu, and the others urgently gathered in a study.

The study was brightly lit like broad daylight. The portraits of sages hanging on the walls appeared solemn and heavy in the firelight, as if the gazes of these ancient sages were traversing through the long years to fall upon this group of descendants.

Chu Xuangui's brows were deeply furrowed: "This kid Ning Zhuo is blowing things more and more out of proportion. Swearing an oath under the Truth Bell means this matter is set in stone."

Li Guanyu said in a low voice, "His journey has been a bit too smooth; it's inevitable that he would become radical."

Nangong Zhi sighed softly, "Is there still no reply from Fellow Daoist Wen Ruanyu?"

Duanmu Zhang shook his head.

They had already transmitted Ning Zhuo's latest situation over through hidden channels, but Wen Ruanyu remained silent; the messages were like stones sinking into the ocean.

The Confucian cultivators looked at each other, sharing the same heavy feeling in their hearts.

What they didn't know was that at this moment, Wen Ruanyu was immersed in the deepest state of Cultivation.

Inside a secluded small courtyard, the doors and windows were tightly sealed shut.

Outside the courtyard, two Cultivators stood guard. In name, they were looking after him, but in reality, it was house arrest.

The courtyard was elegantly arranged. An old plum tree leaned diagonally in front of the window. There were no flowers on its branches, only a few unripe fruits hidden among the leaves. As the night wind blew past, the branches and leaves gently brushed against the window paper, making a soft rustling sound, like time slowly flowing in the dark.

Wen Ruanyu sat cross-legged inside the room.

His Confucian robes were as clean as snow, and his features were as gentle as jade. An unrolled bamboo slip was spread out in front of him. The text on the slip emitted a gentle Literary Light; every word seemed to be breathing quietly.

Wen Ruanyu was completely oblivious to the special messages transmitted from the outside.

He had been immersed in his own world of Cultivation for a long time.

For a very long time, he had been rushing about and toiling for the affairs of the White Paper Immortal City, mediating between various factions, sorting out city disputes, pacifying the public, and handling conflicts of interest...

He was practicing the Confucian cultivator's path of entering the world, walking amidst the mundane smoke and fire of the mortal realm, never having a moment of leisure day after day.

This time, returning to the Myriad Manifestations Sect to report, he originally thought it would just be a formality. He never expected to be quietly placed under house arrest here.

House arrest brought restrictions, but it also brought a rare peace and quiet.

During these idle days, he gradually received good news about Ning Zhuo, and he also slowly felt at ease.

After his mind calmed down, he reviewed the affairs of the White Paper Immortal City from the very beginning.

He reflected on every one of his decisions: where he was selfish, where he was biased, where he considered the big picture, where he ended up hurting the common people, where he lost his manners due to impatience, and where he missed opportunities due to being soft-hearted.

He dissected his own heart layer by layer like peeling an onion. The deeper he peeled, the clearer he saw.

Within his Literary Palace, a speck of Pure Light quietly floated up, like a lone lantern on a night sea, gentle yet firm.

Wen Ruanyu's breathing gradually slowed, and his expression became increasingly peaceful.

The characters on the bamboo slip floated up one by one, transforming into gentle Literary Qi that slowly revolved around him, like stars surrounding the moon.

He felt that the bottleneck that had trapped him for so long was loosening, like the first trickle of clear water seeping through a crack in a dam.

A gentleman reflects upon himself three times a day.

This was inherently the most fundamental cultivation method of a Confucian cultivator.

The night wind brushed past the courtyard wall, carrying a faint charred fragrance.

The courtyard wall was not high. Several small vermilion lanterns hung above the wall. The flames were protected by a layer of invisible formation runes. Even when the mountain wind blew through the forest, it could not extinguish them; they merely swayed gently within their thin lampshades.

In the courtyard stood an old osmanthus tree. Its branches slanted horizontally, and its leaves were as dark green as if soaked in old ink. The tips of the branches were adorned with dense, tiny golden flowers that trembled slightly in the night wind, like a handful of crushed gold scattered under the dark canopy of the sky.

Under the osmanthus tree, Zhu Fenxiang sat by a stone desk. She wore crimson-patterned battle armor, her cuffs tightly bound, and a fire-patterned jade pendant hung at her slender waist.

Her features were as bright and beautiful as autumn maple fire, with firelight flowing in the depths of her eyes. Her usual blade-like confident aura was currently suppressed by a layer of solemnity.

This was all because she had just learned the news: Ning Zhuo had publicly announced that he would swear an oath under the Truth Bell.

Her mother, Zhu Guizhi, was also sitting opposite her at this time.

Zhu Guizhi had a full, mature figure and wore luxurious clothing. A crimson jade osmanthus branch hairpin in her hair gave off a warm, fat-like luster under the light.

"This time, he is walking too dangerous a path," Zhu Guizhi's voice was slow and measured.

Zhu Fenxiang looked up at her: "Mother, you've never thought highly of him."

"We both know exactly who the Death Facing Sect's Three Valiants are," Zhu Guizhi's voice was slow and deep, like an old millstone slowly turning.

"Their reputation isn't just empty talk blown up by storytellers in taverns. No less than ten Nascent Soul cultivators have died at the hands of those three. They have experienced countless life-and-death battles, and behind them, they have the colossal backing of the Death-Facing Sect. No matter how smart Ning Zhuo is, how exquisite his mechanical arts are, or how good his luck is, he is ultimately just at the mid Foundation Establishment Stage."

She stared intently at her daughter: "This time, the possibility of him dying in the martial arena is extremely high."

Zhu Fenxiang's expression tightened: "Ning Zhuo is no ordinary Foundation Establishment Cultivator. He still has many hidden methods in his hands that he hasn't revealed. However, this is indeed a difficult hurdle. Yet, he is stubbornly crashing headfirst into it, almost as if on purpose."

Zhu Guizhi looked quietly at her daughter, sighing softly in her heart. Her daughter was still unconsciously speaking up for Ning Zhuo; that stubborn sense of protectiveness could be felt even across the stone desk.

Zhu Fenxiang lowered her eyelashes, her voice softening slightly: "If he really swears an oath under the Truth Bell, there will be absolutely no room to turn back on this matter."

Zhu Guizhi's thoughts spun rapidly. After a moment of silence, she finally spoke up: "Fenxiang, this is a peerless opportunity."

Zhu Fenxiang was puzzled: "What opportunity?"

Zhu Guizhi leaned forward slightly, lowering her voice even further, as if afraid it would be overheard by the night wind outside the wall: "An opportunity to take the initiative and get close to Ning Zhuo. From now on, you can directly stand by his side!"

Zhu Fenxiang's expression froze slightly, followed immediately by astonishment: "Why?"

"Ning Zhuo and I currently exchange information and borrow each other's momentum in secret. Both sides have room to maneuver, and our relationship is just right."

"If I stand by his side in public, the space we have to maneuver around each other will become much smaller."

"Moreover, we would also have to face competition from the Situ Clan, and might even get swept into the waves stirred up by the Naming Stronghold."

"Mother, you have always acted comprehensively and prudently; why are you being so radical today?"

Zhu Guizhi listened quietly, but felt a bit of gratification in her heart. Her daughter hadn't let her emotions cloud her judgment and could still logically analyze the pros and cons - this was very good.

However, the calmer Zhu Fenxiang was, the more Zhu Guizhi felt pity for her. She felt that her daughter had not yet realized her own true feelings for Ning Zhuo, and that she as a mother should give her a push from behind.

"Daughter, if you just gaze from afar across a distance like before, you will never have a chance. The pavilion closest to the water enjoys moonlight first; you must stand by the water's edge before you can scoop up that moon." Thoughts surged like a tide in Zhu Guizhi's mind, yet her face remained as steady as ever.

"Whether publicly or privately, this matter is justifiable," Zhu Guizhi said slowly.

"Externally, we can claim that the Zhu Clan wants to lay its hands on the Naming Fire Furnace. The Naming Fire Furnace is a numinous treasure artifact. The Naming Stronghold has only just been established, and its creditor structure is not yet completely stable. By getting close to Ning Zhuo at this time, it is to intervene in the power dynamics of the Naming Stronghold and seek long-term benefits for the clan."

"Fenxiang, if the Zhu Clan can truly secure a place regarding the Naming Fire Furnace, those elders in the clan will also be moved."

Zhu Fenxiang: "Then what about privately?"

Zhu Guizhi sighed again in her heart - To pave the way for you, of course. You silly girl, your heart is entirely focused on him, but you stubbornly refuse to admit it.

She swallowed these words back into her stomach and simply said softly, "Ning Zhuo is worth investing in! The path of Cultivation is long and endless. If you meet an outstanding person with great prospects, merely walking alongside them for a stretch of life's journey will be of immense benefit to us. Isn't that right?"

Zhu Fenxiang immediately thought of how highly the Wutu Prison-Suppressing True Lord regarded Ning Zhuo. She nodded sincerely, her face filled with agreement.

She thought about it seriously: "I will contact Ning Zhuo first and hear what he has to say."

Zhu Guizhi smiled: "That is fine."

Zhu Fenxiang stood up and walked into the inner room. The furnishings in the inner room were simple. In the center, a divine statue of the Wutu Prison-Suppressing True Lord was enshrined.

The statue was only about a foot tall, its entire body radiating a dense, heavy, earth-yellow divine light. It sat on the table like a miniature mountain; just looking at it made one's spirit feel grounded.

Zhu Fenxiang stood before the divine statue, formed hand seals with both hands, prayed silently, and sent out an inquiring message.

-----

Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor.

Gongsun Yan was by the side, head lowered, inventorying materials. Fire Twister Ah Huo squatted next to the large wooden chests, hugging his knees, his eyes staring blankly wide.

These chests were packed full of various mechanical materials.

Three hundred Azure-Patterned Bamboo Cores, which could be used to repair the skeletons of Mechanical Birds.

Twenty boxes of Cloud Silk Soft Copper, light yet tough, suitable for refining mechanical feathers.

One thousand taels of Fine-Toothed Silver Springs, capable of filling the gaps in every rotating pivot of a Mechanical Bird's joints.

Eight large boxes of Lightning-Patterned Crimson Rivets. When integrated into an automaton, they could instantly enhance a burst of explosive power.

Five pecks of Floating Sound Jade Dust. They could be fused into the matching mechanisms of the Empty Valley Syllable Azure Cylinder to make the transmission of musical rhythms smoother and more harmonious.

...

The sheer quantity of these materials was massive. Their precious light overlapped layer upon layer, almost turning the entire cave manor into a glazed secret chamber. Even someone like Gongsun Yan, who was used to seeing grand scenes, felt his breathing tighten under the pressure of the room full of spiritual radiance.

Ning Zhuo remained unmoved.

While the scale of these sponsorships was indeed astonishing, they could not bring him a qualitative change.

They could expand the size of his Mechanical Bird flock, replenish his previous mechanical battle losses, and allow Ning Zhuo to fight longer and more steadily. However, when facing the Death Facing Sect's Three Valiants, their significance was minimal.

Right at that moment, Ning Zhuo's heart twitched slightly as he sensed a commotion from the Wutu Prison-Suppressing True Lord statue within his Storage Belt.

He probed with his divine sense and received Zhu Fenxiang's message.

Ning Zhuo frowned slightly.

"Zhu Fenxiang is testing me. Do she or the Zhu Clan want to directly intervene in the Naming Stronghold?"

This ally, who had always remained hidden in the shadows, suddenly had new schemes.

Ning Zhuo had originally been waiting for a reaction from Wen Ruanyu's side. Ning Zhuo foresaw Wen Ruanyu's obstruction. Seeing him walk an increasingly dangerous path, the Confucian cultivator group should be brewing an intervention to dissuade him. But the prerequisite was to reach an agreement with Wen Ruanyu.

Ning Zhuo was somewhat surprised; he hadn't expected Zhu Fenxiang to be the first one to surface.

A trace of a bitter smile rippled in Ning Zhuo's heart.

By establishing the alliance and setting up the stronghold, he had stirred up the winds and clouds, triggering even more chain reactions.

This ally, Zhu Fenxiang, was bringing him trouble. Beneath her simple inquiry lay an ambition that made Ning Zhuo vigilant!

Just as Ning Zhuo was carefully deliberating on how to respond to Zhu Fenxiang, a visitor arrived outside the cave manor.

The newcomer was masked and wore a grayish-black cloak. His aura was faint and elusive.

He knocked on the door but did not report his name: "It's all fake names and false identities anyway, so we might as well save the useless effort. I have come solely for your Young Master's battle against the Death Facing Sect's Three Valiants."

After the masked Cultivator entered the cave and saw Ning Zhuo, he looked him up and down: "Young Master Ning, I brought an item."

Ning Zhuo invited him to take a seat, but the masked Cultivator shook his head and remained standing.

He took out a long black box from his sleeve.

The box was pieced together from unknown black bones. Dark red bloodlines seeped through the bone seams, resembling long-dried bloodstains.

Three twisted humanoid figures were carved onto the lid. The three figures stood back-to-back, their postures hideous, as if they were tearing at each other or struggling in despair.

Ning Zhuo's eyes narrowed slightly at the sight.

The masked Cultivator opened the long box. Inside lay a rusty-black long nail.

The seven-inch body of the nail was covered with dense barbs. The tail end of the nail was embedded with half a shattered soul crystal. Curled up within the soul crystal was a transparent human figure with blurred features. Its chest bore three overlapping scars, as if three different weapons had pierced its body simultaneously.

The moment the long nail was revealed, an extremely low and shrill soul shriek echoed through the cave manor. It emitted a hatred so intense it was almost palpable, like cold air seeping out from thousands of feet underground. In a single breath, the air in the entire room became bone-chillingly cold.

"This item is named the Three Valiants Life-Claiming Nail," the masked Cultivator's voice was deep.

"Its core is the remnant soul of our clan's Nascent Soul ancestor. Back then, our ancestor was slain by the joint forces of the Death Facing Sect's Three Valiants. When his Nascent Soul was destroyed, only a wisp of his soul remained undissipated. Sworn to revenge, he exhausted the entire clan's foundation, set up a grand formation, and used his soul as a refinement material. With endless, undying resentment, he locked all his hatred onto the Death Facing Sect's Three Valiants. Hateful as it is, we descendants are incompetent and unable to exact vengeance on behalf of our ancestor."

"This treasure is a single-use item. Once it strikes its target, it can temporarily nullify the Shared Life and Death Divine Art!"

"Oh?" In that instant, a sharp glint flashed in Ning Zhuo's eyes.

The assistance capable of bringing him a qualitative change - he had finally waited long enough for it!
