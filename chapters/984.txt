Chapter 984: Renowned Artisan Realm!

Yang Qi!

Yang Qi favors high places, bright places, circulation, and places brimming with vitality.

Yang Qi can dispel cold and shatter impurities, bring back spring and restore life, and reveal forms while illuminating objects.

If Yang Qi is too intense, it can scorch the Divine Soul like a raging fire burning paper.

If Yang Qi is too superficial, it cannot retain vitality, like flowing water passing over stones.

If Yang Qi is too scattered, it merely has the appearance of light and heat, making it difficult to form a solid foundation.

The Apprentice-level of the Yang Path allowed Ning Zhuo to become familiar with the circulation of Yang Qi and to understand the various Yang-attribute aspects within spiritual materials.

Ning Zhuo was not the least bit surprised, nor did he feel any sense of complacency.

The Pure Yang Patriarch Pill continuously emitted its medicinal power, transforming into vigorous Pure Yang True Intent within his Upper Dantian's divine sea.

Scene after scene seemingly floated before Ning Zhuo's eyes.

A scorching sun hung high in the sky, and all creation bowed its head to receive its light. Furnace fire rolled like a dragon within the hearth, and liquid medicine surged and boiled within a cauldron. Crimson gold slowly melted on the forging anvil, then rapidly solidified in cold water, emitting plumes of white steam...

A warm breeze blew past frozen fingers, and blood color once again surfaced on the fingertips, like dead wood meeting spring. Spring thunder rolled from the depths of thick clouds, muffled and distant, and a tender green bud burst forth on the branch of a withered tree after the thunder subsided. Blood Qi surged and churned within a patient's chest, causing him to suddenly stand up from the edge of his sickbed, his dim eyes once again shining with compelling brightness!

Scorching sun, furnace fire, crimson gold, warm breeze, spring thunder, blood Qi - these were all the Dao of Yang!

Ning Zhuo advanced by leaps and bounds. Before long, he officially broke through from the Apprentice-level and arrived at the Artisan-level.

Ning Zhuo's mind quickly became clear: If given a pill furnace right now, he could rely on his senses to mobilize Yang fires like Earth Core Fire and Sun True Fire, mastering basic temperature control.

If asked to handle a Magic Artifact tainted by Yin corrosion, he could slowly wash it with Yang Qi, dispelling the entrenched gloomy cold and making the spiritual runes within the artifact flow as smoothly as before.

If he encountered a Cultivator suffering from depleted Yang Qi, he could discern whether the depletion was in their flesh and blood, their Divine Soul, or their lifespan and Qi mechanics.

An Artisan's view of Yang is concrete and practical. It lands in every control of the fire's heat, every handling of materials, and every line of a rune.

Continue.

Ning Zhuo continued his triumphant advance, greedily absorbing the Pure Yang True Intent.

This time, the difficulty sharply increased!

The temperature in his Upper Dantian's divine sea seemed to soar. Countless small mirror surfaces were unable to bear it, rapidly melting away under the washing of the true intent.

Ning Zhuo frowned slightly, his glabella burning hot.

Above Artisan was Renowned Artisan. Not even one in a hundred could reach this level.

Ning Zhuo's attempt to break through to this point in such a short time felt like crashing headfirst into a massive, invisible wall.

At this moment, his Five Elements realm provided assistance!

Wood generates Fire, Fire generates Earth, Earth generates Metal, Metal generates Water, and Water generates Wood.

The Five Elements cycled!

The true intent of the Five Elements spread out within his divine sea-

Azure Wood light emerged from the edges of the mirror sea, spreading upward like early spring vines. That was the Wood element's power of growth, supporting the initial rise of Yang Qi, much like spring vegetation breaking through the soil, giving Yang its initial impetus.

Crimson Fire light bloomed immediately after, the Fire element's light and heat illuminating all directions, vividly revealing the external manifestations of the Yang Dao.

Light is manifestation, heat is movement, and upward-reaching tongues of flame are ascension and growth!

Yellow Earth light descended heavily, spreading like the ground, steadily catching the wildly darting golden radiance. Pressed down by the Earth nature, the Yang Qi instantly gained roots, no longer floating and scattering like ownerless dust.

White Metal light then converged, like a sword returning to its sheath or a pill solidifying. It compressed the overly scattered light and heat within the Yang fire into a single point, allowing it to form pills, talismans, and artifacts without being wasted into nothingness.

Black Water light flowed through last, gentle and accommodating. It softly restrained the Yang's agitation. The golden light that had nearly burned through the mirror surfaces was immediately soothed when enveloped by the Water intent, becoming enduring, dense, and controllable like a spring stream.

Using the Five Elements as a bridge, Ning Zhuo dismantled, diverted, and digested the tyrannical and peerless Yang Dao true intent within the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill one by one.

A great sun was disassembled by him into spring thunder, furnace fire, warm breeze, crimson gold, and blood Qi. From these concrete objects, he re-summarized its internal laws.

The manifestation of Yang is bringing hidden things into the light.

The movement of Yang is pushing Qi mechanics to awaken from silence.

The ascension of Yang is making low-lying Qi rise and grow.

The illumination of Yang is touching shadows with light, leaving evil spirits, disease Qi, and turbid thoughts nowhere to hide.

The emission of Yang is stimulating vitality, causing medicinal properties to rise, reviving blood Qi, making artifact embryos heat themselves, and giving runes the momentum to operate actively.

Manifestation, movement, ascension, illumination, emission!

An Apprentice merely recognizes Yang, and an Artisan can utilize Yang, but a Renowned Artisan must understand the internal laws of the Yang nature, knowing why Yang manifests, how it moves, on what basis it ascends, and how it illuminates and stimulates all things.

This was no longer a simple accumulation of experience, but a touching of the deep veins and textures of the Yang Dao, like an artisan moving from carving wooden tools to glimpsing the growth rings and posture of the wood itself.

If Ning Zhuo did not have the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill and relied solely on his own Cultivation, it would take him at least years, if not decades, passing through countless pill furnaces, formations, sickbeds, and battlefields, to slowly grope his way to this threshold.

Ning Zhuo let out a muffled groan, a thin line of dark red blood seeping from his nose.

The thousands of small mirrors that were originally densely packed were now almost completely exhausted. Even the Heaven and Earth twin giant mirrors were gradually melting, showing obvious signs of severe strain.

Ning Zhuo overcame his greed and decisively paused!

Once, in the Two Note Kingdom, he had suffered a small loss by being too greedy for progress within the Ten Thousand-Li Traversing Dragon. Now, having learned from his mistakes, he did not commit a similar error.

Half-step Renowned Artisan-level!

Carrying this tremendous achievement, he slowly opened his eyes.

He "spat out" the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill, and it reverted to its original, ordinary, gray stone-like state.

As Ning Zhuo opened his eyes, the golden light within the Cave Manor gently swayed, like the surface of a lake rippled by the wind.

At some unknown point, the Yang Fish had swum right in front of him, barely three inches away from his glabella.

This was an unprecedentedly close distance!

And those black jade-like fish eyes looked at him fixedly. Deep within the gloomy coldness of its eyes, a wisp of golden light that had not yet dissipated from Ning Zhuo's glabella was reflected, like a morning star on the verge of falling reflected in the night sky.

It gently flicked its tail fin, its golden scales spreading a circle of fine ripples in the light. This time, it did not retreat.

...

Today was a momentous day.

Today, Ning Zhuo was going to the location of the Myriad Manifestations Sect's Truth Bell to take a public oath - to rescue the Red-Robed Guest's Demonic Soul, and to officially set the martial exhibition pact with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect.

Deep within the Cave Manor, Ning Zhuo stood alone before a bronze mirror.

He first tied his inner lining. His movements were unhurried, his knuckles weaving through the sashes like flowing clouds and moving water. Then he took out the Great Thousand Mechanical Sound Garment and gently draped it over his body.

The robe's color was a profound azure. At a glance, it looked gentle and elegant like distant mountains after rain. Upon closer inspection, however, layers of extremely fine mechanical runes flickered faintly deep within the fabric, crisscrossing like the fibers inside bamboo joints, or like threads woven from the wind echoing in empty valleys.

As Ning Zhuo raised his arm, an extremely faint mechanical sound chimed from within his sleeves - ding... dong... It was like the wind sweeping through spiritual bamboo in a distant empty valley, the bamboo joints knocking against each other, transmitting intermittent clear notes, distant and lingering.

The moment his belt tightened, the Great Thousand Mechanical Sound Garment clung to his figure like a living thing. The dense mechanical runes on the clothes lit up for a moment, then quickly sank back deep into the fabric, like ripples settling on the water's surface.

The robe's sleeves were wide but not messy, and the hem was long but didn't drag on the floor. Paired with Ning Zhuo's youthful face, it exuded a special aura.

The youth's eyes were clear and bright, his lip color slightly pale from days of continuous Cultivation, and his forehead was as full as a full moon. Yet, within his eyes, there was a calmness that far exceeded his age, like a bottomless deep pool.

The Great Thousand Mechanical Sound Garment suppressed the residual childishness about him, yet did not conceal the unique brightness of a youth.

Ning Zhuo reached out to adjust his collar, but his brow was slightly furrowed.

The Yang Fish, meanwhile, swam slowly beside him.

Chun Yangzi's sponsorship had come at too crucial a moment.

The Yang Fish and the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill - one was the Pure Yang Palace's sect-guarding spiritual treasure, and the other was the pill passed down through generations of Pure Yang Palace sect masters. Only with their arrival did Ning Zhuo's chances of winning against the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect truly gain a foundational support.

However, over the next few days, although others still sent sponsorships, they were not ideal. Most of those items were merely icing on the cake; they couldn't change the overall situation or support him in truly holding his ground against the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect.

The Three Valiants' Life-Claiming Nail and the Yang Fish were the true heavyweights that could be brought to the table. The former was sinister and freezing, its sharp edge pointed directly at the ironclad life-and-death bond of the Three Valiants. The latter was majestic and bright, perfectly countering the netherworld death Qi, like a rising sun piercing through cold mist.

"Tribulation and fortune, tribulation and fortune," Ning Zhuo muttered in his heart as he looked at himself in the mirror.

Tribulation is disaster, and fortune is vitality.

This time, it seemed that because Chun Yangzi's gift was so precious, no new fortunes manifested.

Ning Zhuo glanced at the Yang Fish in the mirror.

Over the past few days, he had been constantly contemplating the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill, using the true intent within it to comprehend the Yang Dao.

Ning Zhuo had now officially crossed that threshold and become Renowned Artisan-level. However, upon reaching this layer, his progress suddenly slowed and steadied.

The true intent within the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill was still abundant, the Yang Fish was growing closer, and his Five Elements realm could still help him dismantle and digest those tyrannical principles. But every time he delved deeply into his comprehension, it was as if the youth encountered an invisible barrier - it didn't block him forcefully, but it constantly dragged his pace, like an undercurrent lurking beneath still waters.

What was bizarre was that the closer he investigated it, the more untraceable the obstruction became.

"At this rate, it drastically falls short of my expectations," he sighed softly.

The Yang Fish flicked its tail and swam to his cuff, seemingly attracted by the faint, clear sounds within the sleeve of the Great Thousand Mechanical Sound Garment. It gently bumped its forehead against the mechanical runes on the sleeve's edge, causing the runes to ripple with a warm golden hue, like a gentle star cast onto the surface of the water.

Ning Zhuo looked at it, his gaze softening slightly. Raising his hand, he guided it, and the mechanical runes on the cuff opened slightly.

The Yang Fish flicked its tail and swam inside, its golden scales flashing before disappearing.

From outside the Cave Manor door came Old Chef's lowered voice: "Young Master, it's about time."

Ning Zhuo acknowledged the call, turned around, and walked out.

In the front hall of the Cave Manor, several people were already waiting.

Old Chef stood to the side, dressed much more neatly today than usual. His slightly dark face, perennially smoked by cooking fires, lacked its usual warm smile. The wrinkles around his eyes were drawn deep, and his hands were tucked into his sleeves, though his cuffs rubbed together lightly from time to time, revealing the unease in his heart.

Gongsun Yan was on the other side, the bloodshot redness in his eyes not yet faded. These past few days, he had been helping Ning Zhuo process mechanical materials, inspect the Mystic Soldier Armor, and adjust various auxiliary Magic Artifacts, barely getting any shut-eye.

Seeing Ning Zhuo dressed neatly right now, his youthful aura clear and solemn, a flash of amazement crossed his eyes, only to be suppressed by an even deeper worry.

Behind Gongsun Yan stood Huo Nian'er.

He was currently serving as an assistant by Gongsun Yan's side, doing odd jobs like transporting materials, tending the furnace fire, running errands, and delivering messages. Gongsun Yan had a serious personality and arranged things meticulously. Initially, Huo Nian'er had been extremely impatient and grumbled about it secretly several times.

However, after these past few days, seeing with his own eyes how Gongsun Yan worked tirelessly without sleep to adjust one mechanical contraption after another for Ning Zhuo, that little bit of dissatisfaction in his heart was slowly pushed down.

Seeing Ning Zhuo come out today, his Adam's apple bobbed. His eyes were a bit fervent, yet also a bit nervous.

Qing Chi was also there.

The Ghost Clan Girl stood the straightest, her hands behind her back, her eyes sparkling as she looked at Ning Zhuo.

In her eyes, Ning Zhuo seemed omnipotent. The Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, the Heaven-Dangling Single Line Hook, the life-and-death bond - she probably knew how formidable they were, but she still felt that Ning Zhuo would definitely be able to pull through.

"Young Master, you look really good today!" Qing Chi blurted out.

Ning Zhuo smiled slightly at Qing Chi: "I'm going to take an oath beneath the Truth Bell today, how could I have the leisure to compare looks?"

Qing Chi nodded, her expression serious and full of anticipation: "I want... I want to go with you, Young Master."

"You stay in the Cave Manor," Ning Zhuo said.

Qing Chi pursed her lips, and the joy on her face immediately collapsed by half.

"Oh," she replied softly, gently scuffing her toe against the floor tiles. There was still some unwillingness in her eyes, but in the end, she didn't speak again.

Ning Zhuo stepped out of the front hall. Liu Jinke stood halfway along the path, having waited for a long time.

Recently, he had been boarding at the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor. After all, having surrendered in public, the rumors and scrutiny outside were too intense.

Compared to the ostentatious sharpness of a Golden Core cultivator during their first meeting, Liu Jinke had noticeably restrained himself quite a bit now.

Liu Jinke's voice was hoarse: "Young Master Ning Zhuo, are you truly going to go?"

Ning Zhuo nodded: "Naturally."

The muscles on Liu Jinke's face twitched. He seemed to have many things he wanted to say, but ultimately only squeezed out one sentence: "Do you know that this time, you will very likely fall by the hands of the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect and pay with your life?"

Old Chef's expression abruptly changed slightly, Gongsun Yan's fingers tightly clenched, and fiery anger flared up on Huo Nian'er's face as the veins on his forehead throbbed.

Qing Chi's gaze instantly sharpened, locking onto Liu Jinke like two unsheathed daggers.

Liu Jinke ignored the others. He stared at Ning Zhuo, his pale golden eyes holding irritation and an indescribable anxiety: "I lost to you; there's no disputing that. But the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect are completely different from me."

"Those three old codgers have killed Nascent Souls on more than one occasion. Once their life-and-death bond is active, hitting one is equivalent to hitting all three. If you injure one, the other two will sustain his life. If you kill one, the remaining two can still drag him back. You are only at the mid Foundation Establishment Stage. Ah, Ning Zhuo, these actions of yours might earn you the title of carrying the banner of the Righteous Path, but once you step onto the martial arena, you are just delivering your head beneath someone else's blade!"

Qing Chi couldn't help but take a step forward: "How are you speaking? How could a defeated opponent like you possibly fathom my Young Master?!"

Ning Zhuo raised his hand, gently stopping her.
