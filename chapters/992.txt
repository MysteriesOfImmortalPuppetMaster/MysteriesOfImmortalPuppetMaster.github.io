Chapter 992: Preparations for Battle

The news that Ning Zhuo was trapped in the Rising Cloud Minor Trial blew like an invisible mountain wind across the various peaks of the Myriad Manifestations Sect.

Chunyangzi returned from the Artifact Refining Hall, silent as a stone along the way.

The afternoon sunlight fell on Chunyang Peak. The pill aura at the summit was as faint as sheer gauze. Crimson-gold twilight slowly seeped through the gaps in the clouds, illuminating the stone steps in front of the hall until they were as bright as if freshly washed.

Chunyangzi stepped up the stairs. His crimson-gold Daoist robe fluttered in the wind like an unyielding battle flag. The scriptures of the Pure Yang Pill Sutra on his robe flickered, resembling a quietly burning sea of fire silently exhaling its radiance.

At this moment, the Yang Fish had returned to his sleeve, swimming gently in the dark folds of the cuff, occasionally leaking a thread of warm golden light.

The elders of the Pure Yang Palace came out to welcome him.

"I will go into battle for the fifth match," Chunyangzi declared.

The elders were shocked. One of them said, "Sect Master, this battle is of great importance. Your personal intervention will naturally boost our momentum. However, you are the Grand Chieftain of the Nanming Stronghold and the master of Chunyang Peak. If you were to suffer any injuries in the martial contest..."

Another elder interjected, "Why not let Tan Zhu..."

Chunyangzi raised a hand, stopping their words. "Ning Zhuo maintaining his arranged duel with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect is equivalent to drawing away a wave of powerful enemies for the Nanming Stronghold. Although Tan Zhu is strong, his intentions are unclear. Moreover, if we let him fight, once the creditor rights are calculated, my position in the Nanming Stronghold would be in grave danger."

"The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is of great benefit to our faction."

"If I can preside over the Nanming Stronghold and occupy this place, it will be beneficial for everyone top to bottom on Chunyang Peak."

The elders were deeply moved.

Chunyangzi pointed at one of them, instructing him to pass on a message: "Next, I will enter secluded cultivation for a few days to prepare for the fifth battle!"

The elders' expressions changed slightly. An elder who understood the inside story breathed a sigh of relief. "This old man hasn't seen you, Sect Master, make such solemn pre-battle preparations in over ten years."

Chunyangzi smiled faintly. "Remember, I have already stepped down from the position of Sect Master."

The elders hurriedly waved their hands and spoke, demonstrating their loyalty.

Chunyangzi shook his head, not in the mood to bother with these trifles. He waved his hand to dismiss the elders, then strode alone to the quiet room at the peak's summit.

The four walls of Chunyangzi's quiet room were all made of crimson-gold rock, which naturally contained Yang fire patterns.

A single futon was placed in the center.

Chunyangzi sat down cross-legged and first took out the Clear Heart Evil-Reflecting Mirror, placing it in front of him.

The surface of the Clear Heart Evil-Reflecting Mirror was as clear as freshly condensed morning dew. The four characters on its rim, "Yang Illuminates, Nothing Hidden," glowed quietly in the dim light of the room.

Chunyangzi raised a hand and tapped. His own reflection surfaced on the mirror.

The figure's demeanor was as majestic as a mountain, yet hidden in the depths of its eyes were a few wisps of impure fire surging like undercurrents. There was anxiety like a scorching wind igniting a prairie, doubt like a thin mist obscuring the eyes, and a desire for the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar that burned the heart like raging flames. Moreover, there was an extremely thin but persistent thought coiled in his mind - the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill. This medicinal pill was still in Ning Zhuo's hands!

Ning Zhuo's innate talent was simply too good. In particular, his aptitude for the Yang Dao was so exceptional that just recalling it brought a complex mix of feelings to Chunyangzi's chest.

"With his youthful virgin body, his Primordial Yang is as abundant as water stored in a deep pool. The sheer vigor of his pure yang qi makes him akin to an uncarved piece of morning sun gold-jade. If he relies on the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill to comprehend too deeply, hollowing out the true intent of the Yang Dao accumulated by past sect masters, what then?"

Chunyangzi's eyelids twitched slightly, and his reflection in the mirror also frowned. The moment this thought was born, it was like a speck of dust falling into a pill furnace - though tiny, it was an eyesore.

He remained silent for a moment before suddenly exhaling softly. "I'm overthinking it."

As his voice fell, the Clear Heart Evil-Reflecting Mirror lit up slightly.

Staring at himself in the mirror, Chunyangzi slowly said, "The Pure Yang Patriarch Pill is a medicinal pill passed down through generations of my Pure Yang Palace. Its true intent is as profound and heavy as a mountain. How could it be drained in just a few days? Even with Ning Zhuo's innate talent, he is only a Foundation Establishment cultivator. Being able to taste a bit of its benefits already speaks of his profound serendipity. After this battle, I will reclaim the medicinal pill. Then, when I discuss Pure Yang Dao methods, the Nanming Stronghold, and the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar with him, I will actually have more leeway."

At this point, the wisp of impure thoughts in his heart finally faded away like smoke.

By temporarily lending out the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill, Chunyangzi naturally had a scheme in mind: let Ning Zhuo taste the sweetness first. That way, when Chunyangzi goes to negotiate in the future, he will certainly have greater assurance of success!

Once this line of thought became clear, Chunyangzi tossed Ning Zhuo to the back of his mind and faced his other various impure thoughts.

In the mirror, the impure fire in the depths of his eyes gradually dissipated strand by strand, like ashes swept clean by the wind.

Chunyangzi activated the Three-Period Sundial Plate.

The Three-Period Sundial Plate floated above his head. The three markings on the circular dial - Morning, Noon, and Dusk - lit up in sequence. Three colors of sunlight intertwined like three silk threads of varying temperatures, landing on Chunyangzi's body.

The morning sunlight was as gentle as a stream, washing away fatigue. The noon sunlight was as fierce as a furnace fire, refining away cowardice. The dusk sunlight was as deep as the earth, restraining impetuousness.

The Yang Fish also began to swim slowly around him. Its golden scales scattered rings of warm light that spread like ripples, while the speck of Yin chill deep within its ink-jade-like eyes was as still as a deep well.

Chunyangzi closed his eyes and regulated his breathing. The impure thoughts in his body were washed away layer by layer, like water filtered through sand. His heart lake was as if illuminated by a blazing sun, becoming so clear that it felt almost boundless and empty.

He gradually reached a certain state of perfection - the Pure Yang Spotless Body!

The simultaneous illumination of the Three-Period Sundial Plate and the Clear Heart Evil-Reflecting Mirror allowed his control over the Three-Period Yang Body Method to stabilize rapidly.

The phantoms of the Morning Yang Body, Noon Yang Body, and Dusk Yang Body appeared one after another behind his back, then successively dispersed. Every time they surfaced, they were more ethereal than before, and every time they dissipated, the energy consumption was lower than before.

By the second day of his secluded cultivation, when Chunyangzi executed the Three-Period Yang Body Method again, the three Yang bodies manifested almost at a single thought. The Morning Yang Body cradled a speck of vitality in its palm like holding an opening bud. The Noon Yang Body was surrounded by a fierce, blazing intent, like glowing iron in a furnace. The Dusk Yang Body bore a calm expression like an ancient bell in the night, with the light of restraint and sealing floating within its palm prints.

Chunyangzi's True Form opened his eyes, his gaze serene. At this point, his battle preparations were largely complete. All that remained was to quietly await the time of battle.

On Heavy Formation Peak, formation clouds hung low like a curtain of lead.

Inside the main hall at the peak's summit, Dong Chen sat in the master seat, with Yin Changgeng standing below.

Yin Changgeng said in a deep voice, "To think it would actually be Chunyangzi."

Dong Chen did not speak immediately. Formation lines flowed through the main hall like an inverted river of stars, casting flickering light and shadows across his face.

The fact that the Nanming Stronghold's side had Chunyangzi personally taking action was somewhat surprising to both of them.

Originally, they had prepared to use Tuoba Pan and employ a special combat tactic to deal with Tan Zhu. But they never expected that the strongest Nascent Soul cultivator of the Nanming Stronghold wouldn't even take the field.

A trace of deep contemplation, like faint ripples on a deep pool, surfaced in Dong Chen's eyes: "Chunyangzi is more anxious than you or I thought."

Yin Changgeng's brow twitched.

Dong Chen said, "The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar holds extreme significance to him - so much so that he would rather take the field personally than hand the battle merit over to Tan Zhu."

Yin Changgeng fell silent for a few breaths, then cupped his hands. "In that case, it is not suitable to use Tuoba Pan."

"Swap him out," Dong Chen said flatly.

This time, without waiting for Yin Changgeng to make a recommendation, he stated directly, "To counter the Pure Yang cultivation method, have Liang Ming enter the fray."

Yin Changgeng hesitated slightly. "Peak Master, Liang Ming is a trump card the Earth Expansion Alliance has painstakingly hidden until now. To use him here..."

Dong Chen merely raised his eyes and shot him a glance.

Yin Changgeng hurriedly nodded. "Yes, this subordinate will arrange it immediately."

In another cave manor.

Tan Zhu sat on a stone bed, draped in a dark robe. The furnace fire on the wall flickered with a gloomy green light, like the blinking eyes of a ghost.

"Chunyangzi..." he muttered in a low voice.

He had been waiting - waiting for Chunyangzi to come and invite him. But now that Chunyangzi had taken the field personally, it completely severed this prospect.

He could only continue to wait.

Only this time, what he was waiting for was a new order from Wang Yu.

Danxia Peak.

In the sky above the mountain peak, crimson pill clouds surged like a tide.

In front of the pill furnace, the firelight reflected on Wang Yu's face, as if coating it in a layer of flowing crimson gold.

After listening to the deacon's report, he rested his horsetail whisk lightly in the crook of his arm, a hint of surprise appearing in his eyes. "Chunyangzi is fighting personally?"

The deacon bowed low like a bending willow. "Exactly. It has been confirmed that Chunyangzi will fight the fifth battle for the Nanming Stronghold, and the Earth Expansion Alliance and White Cloud Township factions have both been notified."

Wang Yu fell into silent thought.

His plan had originally relied on Tan Zhu.

If Tan Zhu took action, there was a high probability he would win, thereby acquiring a massive amount of creditor rights.

With Chunyangzi's shares weakened, the shares Zhongli Mei possessed from repairing the furnace would have been enough to allow the pawn Wang Yu had arranged to immediately vault into the position of the Nanming Stronghold's Grand Chieftain.

At that time, it would have been perfectly justifiable for Danxia Peak to intervene with the Naming Fire Furnace, as easy as sailing a boat with the current.

But with Chunyangzi's move, the situation had suddenly changed.

Wang Yu reached out and cracked open the furnace lid a sliver. Pill aura rose from the furnace like mist, and a medicinal fragrance that was first bitter, then sweet, spread out.

He gazed at that wisp of pill aura, much like looking at a river that had suddenly changed course.

"It seems the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar holds extraordinary significance and immense value for Chunyangzi."

Pill clouds flowed in the depths of Wang Yu's eyes, resembling a river of stars entering his vision. His realm in the Pill Dao was quite profound, but his realm in the Yang Dao was limited, so he still couldn't see through to the sliver of truth hidden at the deepest level.

Wang Yu sighed softly. "Keep a close eye on this battle. Especially on who the Earth Expansion Alliance will send out."

According to his understanding of the Heavy Formation Peak side, since the outcome of this fifth battle would determine the final result, Heavy Formation Peak would definitely make a move and bring out their trump card!

Ning Zhuo sat cross-legged in the center of a bizarre field.

The Pure Yang Patriarch Pill hovered within his divine sea, transforming into a great sun.

As the golden light shone down, the waves of the divine sea were dyed entirely crimson gold, and the countless mirror surfaces condensed by the Mirror Platform Spirit Communication Manual were illuminated brilliantly.

The Hair Tied to the Beam and Awl Piercing the Thigh manifestations, the winds of civil and martial arts, and the pages of the Luoshu Tome swirled around.

His Master-level Yang Dao realm continuously climbed, and his Pill Dao realm rose along with it by a substantial margin.

Sun Lingtong and Qing Chi were also in the midst of a Rising Cloud Minor Trial.

A rhinoceros-like cloud beast had already charged right up to them, closing its two arms and pressing down like a set of iron doors.

The wind howled dully, and the mist on both sides of the cloud path was squeezed into white waves.

Sun Lingtong slipped his toes, and his entire body, like a piece of paper blown away by the wind, slid right under the cloud beast's crotch. Along the way, he slapped Qing Chi on the lower back, pushing her to the other side: "Three steps left, duck!"

Qing Chi's body reacted faster than her brain, and she immediately complied.

Boom!

The cloud beast's two feet smashed into thin air, striking the cloud path so hard that a corner of it collapsed. Crimson iron sparks scattered in all directions, flying right past Qing Chi's cheek, the heat turning the tips of her ears slightly red.

Qing Chi turned her head back, her eyes turning fierce.

She didn't charge blindly this time. Spinning the short blades between her fingers, she spread her ghostly aura close to the ground. A bluish-black shadow slithered along the crevices of the cloud path, entangling the cloud beast's ankles.

As the cloud beast paused its leg, earthen-yellow divine runes lit up on its chest, shattering the ghostly aura with a crackling sound.

Sun Lingtong, however, took advantage of this brief pause and had already vaulted onto the cloud beast's back.

His stature was small and his movements were swift. Three slender needles flew out from his sleeves, piercing into certain critical points on the cloud beast's back with a series of clinking sounds. The moment the fine needles pierced the body, an ear-piercing ring immediately resounded, like a small knife scraping against an iron surface.
