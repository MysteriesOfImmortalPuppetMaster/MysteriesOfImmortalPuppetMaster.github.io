Chapter 996: A Solitary Yang Cannot Grow

The martial arena.

Chunyangzi suddenly intensified his offense once more.

Nine Yang Gate-Knocking Finger!

This time, however, Chunyangzi’s attack was not aimed at Liang Ming or the Yin Tombs, but pointed directly at the sky.

The light from his finger shot into the flying carriage in the air, instantly activating it to reflect a magnificent golden pillar of light downwards.

The pillar of light accurately enveloped the Eclipse Mirror Underworld Official, striking its body until it cracked. Its chest's black mirror, in particular, shattered outright, losing nearly half of its mass.

The observing cultivators had been watching continuously for several days and nights. Seeing this scene, some of their eyes lit up and their hearts stirred: "So Chunyangzi still has such a coordinated tactic."

The Nine Yang Gate-Knocking Finger needed to be executed continuously, with the power of each subsequent strike growing stronger.

Previously, Chunyangzi's accumulation for this move had always been interrupted by Liang Ming. Now, by borrowing the power of the patrolling carriage, he could chain his strikes much more easily, instantly causing the combat value of the Nine Yang Gate-Knocking Finger to soar.

Liang Ming attempted to stop him, but failed the first time.

Chunyangzi consecutively tapped out five fingers. The second finger broke through the Yin mud like a blade snapping vines; the third finger pierced the mirror surface like an awl into wood; the fourth finger shattered the Underworld Official's right arm like snapping a branch; the fifth finger collapsed the front half of the Yin Tomb like a toppling wall.

As the sixth finger fell, half of the Eclipse Mirror Underworld Official's body was blown apart, and the fifth tomb consequently collapsed by more than half!

Liang Ming's chest shuddered, and he coughed up a mouthful of blood. Suffering the backlash exacerbated his injuries.

Soft sighs of admiration spread among the spectators.

Chunyangzi, however, showed not a hint of joy.

Although the fifth tomb was mostly destroyed, it hadn't been completely uprooted.

Fortunately, Liang Ming was heavily injured, yet he still hadn't entered the triggering condition for The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook.

Chunyangzi could control the battlefield and was extremely focused on evading The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook!

Breathing heavily, Liang Ming didn't hesitate to consume a precious talisman to launch a sudden burst of offense, which finally managed to interrupt Chunyangzi's combo.

His explosive power was inferior to Chunyangzi's, but his sustained endurance was slightly superior.

This was the mutual torment between two Nascent Soul cultivators; anyone could see it.

On the fifth night, the Sunset-Gathering Yang-Replenishing Lamp lit up for the fifth time. This crimson jade lamp was already dim and lusterless, its purple-gold radiant glow almost depleted.

Chunyangzi cradled the lamp in his palm. The warmth entering his body slightly alleviated the burning pain in his meridians, but it was a drop in the bucket.

Liang Ming looked at the lamp, his voice hoarse. "Chunyangzi, I'm very curious... how much longer can you sustain this?"

"Earth's belly swallows the fire; the Yin cellar hides the Yang. I invoke the Earth-Belly Fire-Swallowing Lord to guard my sixth tomb!"

Even though Liang Ming's injuries were much heavier now, establishing the sixth tomb was far easier than the previous ones.

The reason was simple. The previous five Yin Tombs had already taken root on the Dawu Heavenly Terrace, forming a continuous chain between the mounds that created a terrain of Yin soil. The Yin Qi was continuously eroding the surroundings. Half of the underground Crimson Yang vein had already been suppressed, like a wild beast locked in a cage.

Only the other half of the Crimson Yang vein remained, still serving as Chunyangzi's geographical advantage, coordinating with the Noonday Sun-Patrolling Carriage.

This mechanical vehicle was still in the sky, constantly patrolling like a massive lamp that refused to extinguish.

The ground caved in to form a circular pit, from which rose a Yin Tomb as round and bulging as a belly.

The sixth Yin Tomb looked like an upside-down black pottery furnace buried in the earth.

The phantom statue of the Earth-Belly Fire-Swallowing Lord sat up from the tomb, rapidly expanding until it was ten zhang tall, resembling a giant tower.

The statue quickly solidified, using the earthen tomb as a stool to sit on.

The Earth-Belly Fire-Swallowing Lord's stomach was round and massive. Suddenly, its navel expanded, forming a bottomless sinkhole. The edges of the hole quickly stretched open, almost covering its entire belly.

Within the bottomless sinkhole, layers of Yin soil spun inwards like a vortex.

The Earth-Belly Fire-Swallowing Lord bared its belly, swallowing the surrounding blazing sun and flames like a massive whale opening its maw, with exceptional results.

The battlefield noticeably darkened at a speed visible to the naked eye.

Chunyangzi's face paled.

"Yang Intent!" His attainment in the Dao of Yang was extremely profound, so he immediately noticed that what the Earth-Belly Fire-Swallowing Lord was devouring wasn't just superficial flames and sunlight, but also the permeating Dao Intent of Yang!

Chunyangzi launched another fierce offensive.

The more Yin Tombs there were, the more targets he had to attack.

Liang Ming had to protect six Yin Tombs, putting him in a very passive position.

He barely managed to survive this round of attacks, gaining a terrifying new wound in the process. His Grey Fetus Breathing Soil Garment was sliced open with a long gash, disemboweling Liang Ming and exposing the eerie white ribs within.

The key issue was that when Liang Ming used methods to heal himself, the sword wound was filled with Yang intent and Sword intent, making his healing efforts yield minimal results.

What Liang Ming gained in exchange was the expansion of the Yin soil.

The establishment of the sixth tomb brought about a qualitative change.

The Yin soil linked together and the Yin Tombs relied on one another. The resulting terrain crossed the final line of stalemate, overpowering the Yang Flame vein.

The audience all witnessed this scene: the Yin soil rapidly spread, covering a vast area, quickly blanketing eighty percent of the entire battlefield.

Only twenty percent of the surface remained dry, but the surging Yang Qi that was originally radiating from it had also mostly dissipated, unable to compare to its peak condition.

"Perhaps the turning point of this battle is right now."

"To think Liang Ming actually managed to seize the terrain advantage?"

"Six Yin Tombs, six deities... Liang Ming is not fighting alone. If it were me, I'm afraid I would have completely lost control of the battlefield after the third Yin Tomb was established."

"It's tough for Chunyangzi. He is wary of The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook, so he can't use his full strength. Liang Ming exploited this mindset to play it safe and steadily advance, leading to the current situation."

"Liang Ming is going to turn the tables! Since the battle began, he has never held the upper hand, but things will be different from now on."

"Exactly. Chunyangzi's other weakness has also been exposed - he is not adept at protracted battles! Or rather, he has never experienced such a unique war of attrition."

The cultivators outside the arena discussed animatedly.

Chunyangzi's face was ashen.

His confrontation with Liang Ming had already lasted five or six days.

The prolonged attrition had made it impossible for him to maintain his past grace and composure.

The Sunset-Gathering Yang-Replenishing Lamp was now a dried-up spring, unable to provide him with continuous replenishment. The Noonday Sun-Patrolling Carriage had been cruising at a high altitude for so long, constantly consuming his spiritual energy and divine sense.

The Three-Period Yang Body Method had been executed several times, with the avatars repeatedly perishing.

Although the Burning Night Returning Light Sword was incredibly powerful, its consumption was also massive. The longer Chunyangzi fought, the more cautious he became with each use.

His most frequently used technique was the Nine Yang Gate-Knocking Finger.

This finger technique consumed relatively little energy.

Moreover, the secret behind chaining its attacks had already been seen through by everyone. Liang Ming had suffered heavily from the sixth and seventh fingers multiple times, and had grown extremely cautious. Reaching this stage, Liang Ming would definitely go all out to interrupt it.

Ironically, this allowed Chunyangzi to retain the initiative.

Golden Crow Pill-Holding Ring!

Chunyangzi took out an explosive Medicinal Pill, letting the Golden Crow hold the pill and fly out to detonate. It shattered a Yin Tomb and successfully returned.

Helpless, Liang Ming sighed bitterly in his heart.

Every time this spirit treasure held a pill, its power and wondrous uses would vary drastically depending on the type of pill.

This made it very difficult for Liang Ming to defend against it effectively.

This spirit treasure made Chunyangzi's methods highly diverse, covering areas such as offense, healing, recovery, and more, elevating his overall combat strength by a large margin across the board.

But the problem remained the same: every activation of the Golden Crow Pill-Holding Ring would drain a massive amount of Liang Ming's spiritual energy.

Coughing blood, Liang Ming sneered, "Chunyangzi, I'm going to win."

"The Cultivation Art you practice, the Pure Yang Pill Scripture, is far too pure. It is entirely Pure Yang, to the point that a solitary Yang cannot grow."

"Only when Yang reaches its extreme and births Yin can there be circulation. You only have intensity, only brightness, only ascension; you have no concealment, no rest, no reflux."

"If you could comprehend the state where Yang reaches its extreme and births Yin, wouldn't your sustained power be more than ten times what it is now?"

"What a pity, truly a pity. The Dao Path you walk is far too extreme."

Liang Ming once again used words, attempting to shake Chunyangzi's fighting spirit.

Hearing this argument, most of the audience fell silent.

The cultivators of the Nanming Stronghold all revealed worried expressions.

Everyone knew that Liang Ming's words hit the nail on the head and were being validated by the current state of the battle.

Chunyangzi coldly snorted, but a bitter taste welled up in his mouth.

Having cultivated for so many years, how could he not understand the principle of Yin and Yang being rooted in one another? As a Nascent Soul cultivator who had cultivated for a thousand years, his horizons were already broad, and he understood his own limitations.

But understanding his limitations didn't mean he could break through them.

His main cultivation art was the Pure Yang Pill Scripture!

In his lifetime, he had already walked the path of Pure Yang too deeply and too far, like being on a suspended single-plank bridge. He had already reached the absolute limit; relying solely on himself, turning back was simply impossible.

That was why he needed a blessed land of feng shui like the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

This place wasn't meant for him to turn back, but to continue ascending along his Dao Path! Thus, giving him the hope to charge into Soul Formation.

Golden light gathered once more in Chunyangzi's eyes, like a furnace fire reigniting.

Three-Period Yang Body Method!

The Three-Period Sundial Plate flew out once again. The three temporal markings of morning, noon, and dusk lit up like lamps, immediately manifesting three Yang avatars.

The Morning Yang Avatar held the Sunset-Gathering Yang-Replenishing Lamp in its hand, attempting to consume itself to replenish the lamp oil.

The Noon Yang Avatar charged towards the sixth tomb and smashed down with a punch, triggering a burst of flames and frequent thunderous explosions.

The Earth-Belly Fire-Swallowing Lord was beaten back, forcing the giant maw on its abdomen to shrink.

Meanwhile, the Dusk Yang Avatar dashed towards the fourth Yin Tomb, attempting to seal it.

Chunyangzi himself whipped up a massive blazing sun. Roaring Yang fire covered the entire Yin soil, shrouding all six Yin Tombs within.

Protected by their respective deities, the Yin Tombs struggled to resist.

These deity avatars were also in a miserable state, a battle result accumulated from Chunyangzi's previous offensives.

Amidst the searing flames, the tomb soil continuously collapsed and shrank. However, the deities each took action to replenish it.

Liang Ming sighed bitterly. Controlling his Netherworld Shovel, he took the initiative to attack Chunyangzi while simultaneously scattering Nascent Soul-level Yin soil to constantly replenish the six Yin Tombs.

Both sides fell into a bitter struggle once more.

A bizarre domain.

Ning Zhuo sat cross-legged within, his eyes tightly closed.

A crimson-gold pill-sun hung in the sky above his clear mirror spiritual light dantian.

Massive, heavy radiance spread out layer by layer. Every pill pattern was like the meridian network of an ancient scripture, and with every flicker, a vast Pure Yang true intent surged out.

The true intent was simply too abundant!

Yet, Ning Zhuo sensed something was wrong.

Strands of Pure Yang true intent descended, transforming into threads of crimson-gold light, but they merely hovered among the countless small mirrors without advancing. They illuminated the mirror surfaces, warmed his divine sense, and deepened Ning Zhuo's bodily perception of the heat control for the Dao of Alchemy. However, they never truly broke through that crucial bottleneck.

His attainment in the Dao of Alchemy was still rising, but his attainment in the Dao of Yang remained stagnant, even though it was clearly only a single door away from the Grandmaster realm.

As long as he gently pushed the door open, he would be able to step into a new stage, but... it simply wouldn't work.
