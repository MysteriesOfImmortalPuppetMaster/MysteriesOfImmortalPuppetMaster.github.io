Chapter 995: Stalemate

This step of progress yielded immediate results.

When Ning Zhuo recalled the Yang fish, he no longer just felt the vast pure Yang foundation within that little fish's body. He immediately understood why that wisp of golden light, emitted when the Yang fish flicked its tail, made one's heart feel warm. It wasn't just emitting light and heat; it was also radiating Yang intent outward.

Ning Zhuo thought back to when Chunyangzi checked his pulse. At the time, he had wondered how Chunyangzi could see through the details of his Pure Yang Virgin Boy Body so quickly. Now, Ning Zhuo somewhat understood. Chunyangzi relied on Yang intent to make his judgment. It was like using divine sense to flip through a book - yielding a massive harvest in an instant.

Applying it to himself now, he could also observe a person's breathing, the color of their skin and blood, and the divine light in their eyes to comprehend the abundance or scarcity of their Yang intent, thereby seeing the surplus or deficit of their Yang Qi.

The intelligence regarding the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar surfaced in Ning Zhuo's mind again, and a look of deep thought appeared in his eyes: "This geomantic treasure land holds a distinct profundity of the Yang Dao. It is truly indescribably wonderful!"

Ning Zhuo had already completed his journey through the Master-level realm of the Yang Dao. Next was the Grandmaster level!

He could already faintly see the door ahead.

"My Alchemy Dao realm has also reached the Renowned Artisan level."

Successive generations of Pure Yang Patriarchs all exclusively cultivated the Pure Yang Alchemy Scripture. This Cultivation Art cultivated the Dao of Pure Yang, and simultaneously, the Dao of Alchemy!

Therefore, the true intent contained within the Pure Yang Patriarch's pill encompassed the Yang Dao, the Alchemy Dao, and the Fire Element Dao.

Originally, Ning Zhuo's Alchemy Dao was only at the Artisan level.

He could complete standard procedures according to a pill recipe like following a blueprint, stably refine qualified Medicinal Pills, watch the fire, wash materials, and control the furnace, all while rigidly adhering to the rules.

But now, if asked to refine Yang-attribute Medicinal Pills, he could modify originally rigid recipes to make them much smoother to execute.

"The transformation of medicinal properties is like the changes in heat control. The key lies in the focal point of the heat, much like the point of a sword..." A look of enlightenment surfaced in Ning Zhuo's eyes, like the moon emerging from layered clouds.

The true value of a Renowned Artisan of the Alchemy Dao lies in their physical intuition, much like a musician distinguishing tones.

Whether the medicinal properties inside the furnace are stifled, hollow, rising, clashing, or blending - like a complex mixture of flavors - they must all be perceived through the sounds of the furnace, the fragrance of the pill, the color of the fire, and the hue of the smoke, much like a physician's four methods of diagnosis.

A Renowned Artisan optimizes processes and adjusts pill recipes. Though still bearing traces of artisanship, they can give birth to marvels through sheer skill, much like a poem achieving its rhythm.

Ning Zhuo felt a faint joy in his heart, but his brows slightly furrowed at the same time.

"My rate of progress is slowing down more and more."

The Pure Yang Patriarch's pill hung in his body like a sun in the sky. Its vast true intent was as boundless as the sea, showing no signs of drying up or exhausting.

Ning Zhuo clearly felt that his speed of comprehension was becoming slower and slower.

The magnitude of his progress, if made known, would be enough to shock everyone in the world. However, Ning Zhuo had absorbed true intent many times before and had never encountered a situation like this.

"What is the reason for this?" the youth wondered.

The battle between Chunyangzi and Liang Ming had already reached its third day.

The Cultivators observing the battle were not surprised.

They had seen it coming since the first day of the clash.

Liang Ming's offensive wasn't strong; he had been fully executing some sort of tactic. Chunyangzi, on the other hand, was wary of The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook. He was clearly holding back his strength, continuously probing Liang Ming's methods.

Outside, the sun rose, the moon set, and the stars rotated. But inside the battlefield, the Noon Heaven Sun-Patrolling Dharma Carriage hung perpetually, trapping both combatants in a high noon that had been stretched to its absolute limit.

The Cloud-Gathering Yang-Replenishing Lamp was lit once again by Chunyangzi. The crimson jade lamp bowl was as thin as cicada wings, and the purple-gold cloud thread within it flickered weakly like a candle in the wind.

The dawn-like light shone on his face, bringing a slight warmth back to his pale lips.

This lamp was being used for the third time, and every time it was lit, it consumed the morning rosy clouds gathered within. If it were a normal, short battle, the Cloud-Gathering Yang-Replenishing Lamp would be enough to allow Chunyangzi to erupt with power for several consecutive rounds. But this battle had dragged on for too long - so long that even the reserves of morning clouds in the lamp appeared meager.

Liang Ming saw this and said in a low voice, "Chunyangzi, how much longer can you hold on?"

Chunyangzi's voice was indifferent: "How much blood can you bleed?"

Liang Ming chuckled softly, his laughter sounding like sandpaper scraping against an iron mask.

He raised his hand and pressed it against his chest, and his magic treasure, the Void Return Heart-Shielding Cover, floated out.

The small black earth cover, resembling the lid of an alchemy furnace, hovered in front of his heart.

Immediately after, Liang Ming fiercely struck his own chest with a palm and spat out a mouthful of dark yellow vital essence blood.

As the vital essence blood hit the ground, it was rapidly drawn in by the remnant roots of the three Yin tombs. Like tree roots absorbing water, the Yin tombs immediately recovered significantly.

The Nascent Soul reappeared above Liang Ming's head, raising an earth tablet for the fourth time: "Ash placenta wraps the fire; mature earth nourishes the tomb. I invite the Ash Placenta Corpse Mother to guard my fourth tomb!"

The stone platform on the south side suddenly bulged, the ground seemingly going into spasms.

Chunyangzi snorted coldly. Already prepared, he immediately activated Techniques and magic treasures, whipping up a frenzy of attacks.

This time, it was extremely difficult for Liang Ming to form the tomb!

The newly gathered ash placenta tomb soil was continuously scorched and churned by the flames.

The Ash Placenta Living Soil Garment on his body was already battered and torn. This grayish-white long robe was woven from old graveyard soil and placental membranes. Its surface was cold, clammy, and dull like an aged burial shroud, and at this moment, it was a ghastly sight.

Not hesitating to sustain injury, Liang Ming personally stepped into the tomb soil, using his own body as the foundation of the tomb.

The light of the Nascent Soul's earth tablet faded, and finally, a round, bulging Yin tomb rose up.

The mound of the tomb was covered in a grayish-white placental membrane, expanding and contracting like a fetus breathing in the womb.

The Ash Placenta Corpse Mother stood up from behind the tomb. This minor god of the Underworld had a highly swollen belly like a hanging cauldron and a slender, elongated body like a withered vine. She had no eyes on her face - only a giant mouth that split horizontally all the way to the roots of her ears.

Her hair dragged on the ground like a bundle of cold, wet black threads. With every step she took, a layer of grayish-white placental membrane spread out beneath her feet like shed snakeskin.

The next moment, she opened her mouth and wept! At first, the crying sounded like an infant, then it shifted to an old woman, and finally, it sounded like the agonizing screams of countless women in labor. The sound was extremely penetrating.

Chunyangzi hurriedly sealed his ear apertures. The scriptures on his crimson-gold Daoist robe ignited one by one like sparks, suddenly bursting into flames and making him look as if he were bathing in fire.

Three Thousand Scriptures Flourishing Fire!

Any attack that struck his Dao robe would be met with a backlash of Pure Yang flames.

The sonic wave attack from the Ash Placenta Corpse Mother immediately triggered the flame backlash. However, She did not interrupt Her sonic wave assault, acting like a puppet that felt no pain.

Chunyangzi quickly noticed something amiss: "Hmm? You can actually borrow my fire to nourish your earth!"

Liang Ming coughed twice, blood spilling from the corners of his lips: "This is generating earth with fire. Since you took the initiative to fight, I naturally made ample preparations. The Ash Placenta Corpse Mother is specifically meant to counter you!"

Chunyangzi decisively stopped the Three Thousand Scriptures Flourishing Fire Technique.

He had no choice; the more intensely the flames burned, the more soil accumulated on the Yin tomb. This would instead become an act of aiding the enemy.

Suffering from a lack of intelligence, Chunyangzi shifted his thoughts and activated the Clear Heart Evil-Illuminating Mirror.

The mirror's light shone onto the Ash Placenta Corpse Mother, revealing a lump of blue-black dead fetus within her belly. Outsiders could see a curled-up infant shadow inside, surrounded by a dark yellow aura of grave soil.

The mirror's light continued to shine toward Liang Ming, but it was blocked and deflected by the Void Return Heart-Shielding Cover.

This magic treasure clearly had a countering effect as well.

The Clear Heart Evil-Illuminating Mirror was counteracted by it; the colors within the mirror became muddy like stirred mud water, and the black spots on its edges expanded a little more.

Chunyangzi turned the mirror toward himself - illuminating himself to steady his heart.

The mirror's light fell on his face and shone into his heart, clarifying and dispersing all irritability, anxiety, and other distracting thoughts.

The golden light in Chunyangzi's eyes became even more condensed. With thoughts as fast as lightning, he made an important decision.

He took out the Three-Phase Sundial Plate and suspended it in front of himself.

On the face of this small golden plate, the three markings of Morning, Noon, and Dusk lit up simultaneously.

Chunyangzi's spiritual energy, divine sense, and even his vital essence blood rapidly and drastically plummeted. Almost in the next moment, three Yang Avatars walked out from behind him.

The Morning Yang Avatar had a gentle expression, its palms brimming with vitality. Wielding the Cloud-Gathering Yang-Replenishing Lamp, it could actually replenish the morning rosy clouds within it.

The Noon Yang Avatar had the most flourishing fire intent. Wreathed in golden-red flames, it pounced straight towards the Ash Placenta Yin Tomb.

The Dusk Yang Avatar had a calm expression. Sealing talismans floated out from its palms as it attacked Liang Ming.

Liang Ming retreated again and again. The crying of the Ash Placenta Corpse Mother suddenly rose sharply, and the two previous minor Underworld gods attacked simultaneously!

Boom, boom, boom...

Both sides once again plunged into a bitter struggle.

In this manner, another two days and two nights passed.

The burn wounds on Liang Ming's body had doubled. His Nascent Soul was listless and dispirited, and he had scattered who knew how many pounds of Nascent Soul-level precious material Yin soil.

Chunyangzi's Morning Yang Avatar dispersed into golden clouds. The Noon Yang Avatar charged into the Ash Placenta Tomb in a suicidal attack, exploding with a boom and blowing a massive hole in the tomb mound.

The Dusk Yang Avatar, entangled with Liang Ming, was directly sliced in half at the waist by a ferocious horizontal sweep from Liang Ming's Netherworld Shovel.

Up until this point in the battle, Chunyangzi's three Yang Avatars had already perished several times.

His Cloud-Gathering Yang-Replenishing Lamp was nearly depleted to the bottom. The black spots on the Clear Heart Evil-Illuminating Mirror deepened as if dyed in ink. The Three Thousand Scriptures Flourishing Fire had been ignited repeatedly, causing even the scripture characters on his crimson-gold Daoist robe to noticeably dim.

Liang Ming continuously used the Ash Placenta Corpse Mother and the Lamp-Hiding Tomb Woman to exhaust the Cloud-Gathering Yang-Replenishing Lamp in a war of attrition. He also had the Sun-Subduing Old Official shoot out Yin nails to disrupt the light path of the Noon Heaven Dharma Carriage.

The Nascent Soul reappeared above his head, holding up the earth tablet once more. His voice was like snapping dry twigs: "The mirror illuminates a wicked heart, and the wicked heart corrodes the mirror. I invite the Mirror-Corroding Netherworld Official to guard my fifth tomb!"

On the stone platform to the southeast, a black earth mirror rose from a fissure. The mirror's surface was rough like an unpolished stone. It couldn't reflect a clear human face, but it could reflect the muddiness, heaviness, and secrets hidden at the bottom of one's heart.

From within the mirror surface, a Netherworld Official with no facial features slowly walked out. His face was as smooth as a mud slab, and an even smaller black earth mirror was embedded in his chest. His robes looked as if they had been soaked in mud, sticking heavily to his body.

The Mirror-Corroding Netherworld Official silently raised his hand, and the light of the Clear Heart Evil-Illuminating Mirror was immediately absorbed without causing the slightest reaction.

On the contrary, the black spots on the surface of the Clear Heart Evil-Illuminating Mirror rapidly expanded, as if black ink were soaking through a whole sheet of rice paper.

The mirror's body trembled slightly, almost as if letting out a mournful wail.

Chunyangzi had no choice but to put away the precious mirror and summon the Night-Incinerating Light-Returning Sword. At the same time, he executed the Nine Yang Gate-Knocking Finger technique.

Liang Ming squandered his spiritual energy as if it cost nothing, and the five Yin tombs followed suit, launching a pincer attack against Chunyangzi.

The Bone-Hugging Boy threw bone-fragment hidden weapons. The Lamp-Hiding Tomb Woman shook her black lantern to steal sunlight. The Sun-Subduing Old Official flipped through a tattered Underworld registry to control the broken nails. The Placenta Corpse Mother spread out a thick placental membrane to absorb the Yang fire.

Finally, the Mirror-Corroding Netherworld Official continued to shine his black mirror upon Chunyangzi, causing his Soul to sway and filling him with endless anxious thoughts.

The augmentation of the five tombs made Liang Ming's offensive become magnificent and imposing as well. At the very least, in terms of momentum, he was no longer being firmly crushed.

Chunyangzi once again activated the Golden Crow Pill-Holding Ring.

The precious ring left his body and transformed into a three-legged Golden Crow.

Chunyangzi took out a Medicinal Pill. Its entire body was clear and bright as ice, and its outer shell had a ring of crimson gold encircling it.

It was the Clear Heart Yang-Stabilizing Pill.

The three-legged Golden Crow held the pill in its beak and charged straight into the fifth Yin tomb. This triggered a thunderous explosion, blasting the Mirror-Corroding Netherworld Official until he was covered in dirt and ashes.

Chunyangzi seized the opportunity to activate the second patrol of the Noon Heaven Sun-Patrolling Dharma Carriage.

Rolling waves of heat pressed down, and the sunlight was so blinding that one couldn't open their eyes. The grave soil in the arena was baked until it cracked and popped like a dried-up field. The Mirror-Corroding Netherworld Official was the first to show signs of failing to hold on; fine, dense cracks appeared on the surface of the black mirror on his chest.

"An opportunity!" Chunyangzi seized this flaw. The Night-Incinerating Light-Returning Sword flew out, and a line of bright light sliced straight through, cutting the Mirror-Corroding Netherworld Official into two halves.

The Night-Incinerating Light-Returning Sword looped around in a small half-circle and charged toward Liang Ming.

Liang Ming had long been on high alert. He immediately tossed out a clay doll to block this strike for him, avoiding being heavily injured by the flying sword.

"That's the second one," Liang Ming sighed in his heart.

Even after accumulating them until now, he only had five such clay dolls in total.

Chunyangzi's combat strength was majestic and tyrannical. Even with Liang Ming going all out, fighting up to this point, the depletion of his accumulated wealth was already making his heart ache.
