Chapter 1028: Verifying Information

Purple Thunder Peak Minor Trial [Nine Tribulations Lightning Charge].

Huang Dang forcefully withstood up to the sixth heavenly lightning strike!

A descendant of a renowned general from the Earth Wall Kingdom, his stature wasn't particularly tall, but his shoulders and back were thick and broad. Standing there, he looked like an iron stele weathered by wind and rain. When the lightning struck down, his entire body was charred black and his teeth ground together with a cracking sound, yet he never fell.

Rising Cloud Minor Trial [Chaotic Sound Formation].

In the arena, a hundred chaotic sounds rang out simultaneously. They sounded like crying and laughing, like drums and bells, disturbing the mind and making one irritable.

Gu Tinglan entered carrying a zither.

He was a zither cultivator from a humble background. His clothes were plain and clean, his features were gentle, and his fingertips bore thin calluses left by years of plucking strings.

Gu Tinglan closed his eyes for a moment, then plucked a single string.

Twang.

The zither note was like a clear spring striking a rock, instantly finding the tiniest flaw among the hundred chaotic sounds. Picked at lightly by the zither's melody, that single flaw caused the entire Chaotic Sound Formation to tear open like an overly taut piece of cloth.

Success.

Rising Cloud Minor Trial [Hundred Injuries in One Furnace].

A hundred cloud-shadow wounded figures materialized in the arena simultaneously. Some had severed tendons, some were suffering from blood loss, some were poisoned, and some had their Divine Souls shaken.

Mei Zhaoxue only observed for three breaths of time before immediately making her move.

Coming from a branch family of a medical cultivator clan in the Northern Territory, she wore a pale blue medical robe with only a large silver needle pinned in her hair.

She struck with three needles. The first needle secured the life force of the most critically injured; the second stabilized the spreading momentum of the afflictions; the third borrowed the returning medicinal efficacy of the lightly wounded to temporarily soothe the auras of the severely injured.

She continued applying needles.

A moment later, she claimed first place!

Rising Cloud Minor Trial [Nine Games of Chaotic Tallies].

Pei Wusuan intentionally lost two games.

"It's only interesting if there's a bit of difficulty."

A moment later.

"Ah, first place again. The matches here are getting boring too."

Rising Cloud Minor Trial [Dream Lantern Shadow Reflection].

Luo Mianhua gently blew out a lantern.

She was a rogue cultivator. Her dress was as soft as mist, and her eyes always carried a hint of drowsiness.

In the trial grounds, dreamscapes were layered upon each other, and the more many people struggled, the deeper they sank. Luo Mianhua, however, split into three dream bodies within the dream, counter-suppressing the dreamscape and leaving everyone utterly astounded.

Rising Cloud Minor Trial [Hundred Damaged Artifacts].

A hundred damaged Magic Artifacts were laid out in the arena, requiring the cultivators to rapidly identify and repair them.

Wei Qingcheng came from a mortal workshop. When he participated in the minor trial, his sleeves still bore tiny holes burned by old sparks.

"I didn't expect this guy to get first place!" Many cultivators cast their gazes over.

The cultivators from the Artifact Refining Hall actively rushed over. "Little brother, are you interested in joining the Artifact Refining Hall?"

Talisman Making Hall Minor Trial [One Mistake in a Thousand Talismans].

Liu Jianwei had a slender figure and extremely bright eyes. She always held a short jade brush in her hand.

A thousand talismans unrolled simultaneously, their runes fluttering like snowflakes. She took only a single glance before bringing her brush down on the bottom right corner of the 379th talisman.

That talisman emitted a soft pop. The single wrong stroke hidden among the tens of thousands of runes had been picked out by her.

First place!

Ten Thousand Beasts Peak Minor Trial [Hundred Beasts Startling the Camp].

Wu Zhaoye stood in the center of the agitated herd.

He possessed a half-demon bloodline; his pupils were slightly slitted, fine black scales grew behind his ears, and he had a silent disposition. The hundred beasts were startled, roaring incessantly, forcing many beast-taming cultivators to retreat again and again.

Yet Wu Zhaoye squatted down and pressed his palm against the ground.

Beast Taming Art - Shared Breath, Subdued Heart!

His breathing slowed, and the breathing of the hundred beasts was slowly guided to follow suit. The first to quiet down was a cloud leopard, followed by a horned deer, an iron-feathered eagle, a green-backed wolf... In less than half an incense stick's time, the hundred beasts' camp transitioned from frenzy to tranquility.

Countless cultivators cast shocked gazes at him.

Rising Cloud Minor Trial [Yin-Yang Harmony].

Inside the trial grounds, there was crimson fire on one side and a freezing spring on the other. When ordinary cultivators participated, the slightest carelessness would result in their spiritual energy being thrown into chaos, torn apart by the two forces.

Xie Hanchu simply stood still with his eyes closed. A chill seeped out from deep within his bone marrow, yet outside his skin, it transformed into a faint layer of morning light.

Innate Talent - Cold Marrow Dawn!

Cold yet concealing warmth; Yin yet containing Yang.

The crimson fire and freezing spring simultaneously bowed low at his sides, like two beasts that were meant to fight, now held back by an invisible leash.

Truly producing instant results!

Rising Cloud Minor Trial [Flying Sword Asking the Rainbow].

Ning Zhuo appeared, and the crowd was in an uproar.

"Ning Zhuo is here too?"

"Isn't he a Mechanism Master?"

"He also knows the Sword Dao?"

"Is there anything he doesn't know? He even repaired the Naming Fire Furnace. Yang Dao, Pill Dao, Formation Dao, Mechanism Art... Him participating in the Sword Dao minor trial now isn't completely incomprehensible."

"Practicing the sword is one thing, but getting a ranking in the Sword Dao minor trial is another entirely."

"That's true, but when it comes to Ning Zhuo, it might not apply."

The Sword Trial Cloud Platform was suspended in mid-air, surrounded by twelve silver-white sword pillars. The pillars were covered in sword marks, each left by outstanding sword cultivators who had participated in previous minor trials.

A transparent sword screen floated in the center of the cloud platform. Behind the sword screen lay four layers of trials.

The first layer: Sword Qi.

The second layer: Sword Light.

The third layer: Sword Momentum.

The fourth layer: Sword Intent.

Ning Zhuo's every move attracted countless gazes.

When he stepped onto the Sword Trial Cloud Platform, his expression was peaceful, entirely devoid of tension. He was simply here to review his lessons.

The first layer of Sword Qi was Severing the Cloud Threads.

One hundred and eight white cloud threads emerged on the platform. Each was as fine as a hair, yet exceptionally tough. Challengers had to use their flying swords to channel Sword Qi and sever the threads without damaging the bells located behind them.

Ning Zhuo used the White Porcelain Sword.

The second layer of Sword Light was Piercing the Mirror Rainbow.

Twelve cloud mirrors floated in mid-air, each only permitting a specific type of sword light to pass through. If the sword light was impure, it would be rebounded by the mirror.

Ning Zhuo used the Mechanical Seven Rainbow Sword.

The third layer of Sword Momentum was Defending Ten Feet.

A ring of silver thread appeared in the center of the platform. The challenger stood within the ring and had to face cloud swords flying from all directions. Being forced out of the silver ring meant failure.

Ning Zhuo used the Edge-Folding Ruler.

The fourth layer of Sword Intent was Reflecting the Heart Shadow.

The mist on the platform dispersed, revealing three silhouettes.

The first figure was an armored sword cultivator, exuding a murderous aura.

The second figure was a white-robed sword cultivator with a cold and detached expression.

The third figure had no face, only a hovering sword.

The challenger had to use their Sword Intent to shatter the shadows.

If their Sword Intent was impure, they would be counter-suppressed by the heart shadows. At best, their divine sense would sting with pain; at worst, they would faint on the spot.

Ning Zhuo used the Crimson Feather Sword.

An elder specifically rushed over to personally announce: "Ning Zhuo, first place."

When Ning Zhuo left the Sword Trial Cloud Platform, countless gazes followed his every move.

At the foot of Verification Peak, howling winds raged.

Nine thousand, nine hundred and ninety-nine black stone steps stretched from the base of the mountain all the way into the depths of the sea of clouds. The stone steps were rough, their edges uneven, sending the sensation of grinding gravel through the soles of anyone who stepped on them.

At the peak, on the No-Retreat Plaza, a large number of Myriad Manifestations Sect cultivators had already begun setting up the venue.

The black stone plaza faced the abyss on three sides. Gusts of wind surged up from the sea of clouds, flapping everyone's robes loudly. The Truth Bell still sat heavily on the low stone foundation at the front edge of the cliff. That matte-black iron bell reflected no light; it was thick, squat, and slightly deformed, remaining as silent as a solidified block of the night.

It did not speak.

Yet, whenever anyone passed by it, they subconsciously lowered their voices.

Cultivators from the Internal Affairs Hall brought over dark copper pillars one by one. The surfaces of the pillars were carved with extremely fine Myriad Manifestation patterns, emitting dull thuds when they hit the ground.

Disciples of the Formation Setting Hall laid down runic bricks. After the runic bricks were embedded into the black stone ground, faint silver lines spread outward.

Cultivators from the Demon Subjugation Hall were in charge of inspecting the Truth Bell.

……

This was an important stage as the Flying Cloud Assembly entered its midpoint - any cultivator holding a token in this assembly had to undergo information verification beneath the Truth Bell.

Wooden tokens, stone tokens, copper tokens, iron tokens, silver tokens, golden tokens, and jade tokens - each tier represented the Myriad Manifestations Sect's varying levels of validation regarding a cultivator's present and future.

Low-grade tokens only recorded name, clan, origin, or Cultivation Art, cultivation level, and primary cultivation dantian.

The higher the grade, the more information was recorded: cultivation skills, realms, combat methods, Magic Artifacts, taboos, age, bone age, lifespan status, Innate Talent, Dao Path tendencies, and even battle records, achievements, and contributions.

Upon reaching this stage, the Myriad Manifestations Sect would employ even stricter detection methods.

Lies, disguises, aliases, and concealments - all would be stripped bare!

A young disciple of the Formation Setting Hall carried a dark copper pillar, sweating from the exertion. He set the pillar down, caught his breath, looked at the black bell by the cliff, and whispered, "After tomorrow, a lot of people's true backgrounds won't be able to stay hidden."

An older Internal Affairs Hall cultivator nearby, sporting a square face and short beard, was crouching on the ground verifying the runic bricks. He pressed his finger against a silver line and spoke without looking up. "Every Flying Cloud Assembly is pretty chaotic. There are plenty of geniuses, but the waters are deep. Without a proper check, who knows what's real and what's fake?"

The young Formation Setting Hall disciple smiled. "Then tell me, who do you think is the most dazzling new genius this time?"

As soon as he said this, several cultivators working nearby pricked up their ears.

The square-faced cultivator chuckled. "That's really hard to say. Situ Xing comes from a super clan; his foundation is thick and his demeanor is steady. Zhu Fenxiang excels in the Divine Dao and can invite numerous deities, giving him a lot of weight. Although Pi Fujie has a Demonic Cultivator background, his Three Tribulation Molting Rebirth is truly troublesome."

Another female cultivator carrying a talisman box chimed in, "Xiao Caiting is also very eye-catching. The daughter of a rogue cultivator from the wilderness, she hasn't studied any profound lightning arts, yet she can guide the qi dynamics to control lightning. Huang Dang's recent performance has also been quite good. It's rumored he can forcefully withstand three heavenly lightning strikes; he's unimaginably tough."

"In the past two days, quite a few cultivators have also emerged. I heard there's a Ghost Clan Girl who is highly favored by the higher-ups of the Troop Leading Hall."

"But if you ask me, the strongest must be Ning Zhuo!"

The cultivators fell silent; no one objected.

"Speaking of Ning Zhuo..." The female cultivator carrying the talisman box stopped, her expression subtle. "His token grade isn't considered the highest, is it?"

"It isn't." The square-faced cultivator pressed the last runic brick firmly into place, and the silver line flashed brightly with a pop. "His main focus is no longer on the Rising Cloud Minor Trials anyway."

The crowd went silent for a moment.

Some among them had witnessed multiple Flying Cloud Assemblies, and in their hearts, they all felt that a figure like Ning Zhuo was exceedingly rare and hard to come by.

The wind blew in from beyond the cliff, and the sea of clouds churned.

The Truth Bell stood there in silence, its black body swallowing the last remnants of the setting sun.

The young Formation Setting Hall disciple looked up at the No-Retreat Plaza, at the Grand Formation that was gradually taking shape, and said softly, "With tomorrow's public verification, there will be quite a spectacle to watch."

The square-faced cultivator stood up and dusted the black stone dust from his hands, gazing toward the distant sea of clouds. "This Flying Cloud Assembly is already past the thirty-percent mark, but the real excitement has just begun."

No one spoke again.

Only the sound of the wind swept across the No-Retreat Plaza, fluttering the newly erected formation flags one by one.

The shadows of the flags swayed on the black stone ground, like countless names yet to be unveiled, waiting for the light of tomorrow.
