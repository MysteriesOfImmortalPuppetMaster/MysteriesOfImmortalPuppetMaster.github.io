Chapter 1014: Human Pet

A Grandmaster had already mastered the essential principles of a certain field, able to create new sub-categories under the old framework, and even invent methods on the battlefield. Such a realm was enough to establish a sect and be remembered by future generations.

Further up were the Great Grandmaster, Quasi-Supreme Grandmaster, and Supreme Grandmaster realms, with each tier growing increasingly distant from ordinary cultivators. A Great Grandmaster could comprehend by analogy, almost thoroughly understanding a certain field. A Quasi-Supreme Grandmaster exhausted the mysteries of that field, their skill approaching the Dao. A Supreme Grandmaster, on the other hand, could create entirely new major categories, pushing the upper limits of the entire school.

Which of these realms did not require years and months of accumulation?

Which tier did not demand repeated failure, summarization, and enlightenment?

Ning Zhuo already felt that his comprehension of the Demonic Contamination Blood Meridian Art was slow, but that was ultimately still Cultivation Art practice. The elevation of one's realm was longer and more profound than Cultivation Art practice. It was like sunken sand at the bottom of a river, accumulating day after day, waiting for a torrent to wash over before a new riverbed could be revealed.

The most distinct example right before his eyes was the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill.

The successive sect masters of Pure Yang Palace would use this pill to open their path in their youth, and before stepping down, they would replenish it with their own true intent of the Yang Dao. How many generations of Chunyangzi, how many years of inheritance, and the lifelong comprehensions of how many Nascent Soul cultivators regarding the Pure Yang Pill Scripture did it take to accumulate into this single legacy pill?

Even as Ning Zhuo used the Myriad Manifestations Cradle to almost completely hollow out the true intent within it, it merely pushed his Yang Dao realm to that of a Grandmaster.

This was already shockingly horrifying.

But looking at it from another angle, it also meant that the Yang Dao realm of the successive generations of Chunyangzi basically did not exceed the Grandmaster level by much.

Who was Chunyangzi?

The leader of Pure Yang Palace.

A cultivator with a Nascent Soul cultivation base.

His main cultivation method was the Pure Yang Pill Scripture.

He held the Yang Fish, a sect-pacifying spiritual treasure, and bore the hundreds or even thousands of years of Dao inheritance of Pure Yang Palace.

For such a Nascent Soul expert, the path they primarily cultivated had only accumulated to the Grandmaster realm. If it were an ordinary Nascent Soul cultivator, even if they cultivated for over a thousand years, they might not necessarily reach the Grandmaster realm in the principles corresponding to their main Cultivation Art.

Because a Grandmaster could already establish a sect!

Ning Zhuo could do so now as well.

Of course, this kind of establishing a sect meant creating a small branch under the mainstream framework of the Yang Dao. It still belonged to the old framework.

As for truly jumping out of the framework and forging a new path, there was a name in the contemporary era that was so resounding it was almost ear-piercing.

Wang Xinyue.

The School of Mind!

Wang Xinyue's realm in traditional Confucianism must have been profound. Without a foundation, how could one create? Without deep knowledge of the old studies, how could one pioneer a new one? But as for exactly what level she had reached in Confucianism, everyone speculated endlessly, and no one could say for sure. Some said she was already a Great Grandmaster of the Confucian Dao; some said she used the School of Mind to open another school entirely, rendering the old realms incapable of measuring her. Still others said that her true brilliance lay in walking the old studies to their absolute depths, before walking out of them with the ruthlessness to discard everything.

Thinking of this, Ning Zhuo's gaze toward Yuan Dasheng became even more solemn.

"To deal with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, it would be best if both Yuan Dasheng and Meng Yehu could recover."

Individually, the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect were already quite tyrannical. Each of them was a Golden Core expert with unique skills. When fighting together, they even possessed the "Live and Die Together" Divine Art!

If Ning Zhuo could restore these two great battle powers, the situation would be entirely different.

Meng Yehu's problem had now been half-solved.

The yellow dust divine power on his body came from the Forgetful River Prefecture Lord's Divided Spirit, and in essence, it involved the Divine Art [Filthy Earth Placenta]. Through Zhu Fenxiang, Ning Zhuo had summoned the Earth Prison Suppressing True Lord, finally uprooting the yellow sand divine power. Meng Yehu was no longer shackled by divine power and had regained his freedom.

However, Meng Yehu's spiritual energy was dim and weak; it was difficult to even get a response from Life Hanging by a Thread. His old Mechanical body was severely sandified and could no longer be used. In the future, Ning Zhuo would have to forge a new body for him and slowly nourish his spiritual energy.

"I need Profound Yellow Mother Qi." Ning Zhuo's heart stirred.

He now held the complete Naming Fire Furnace in his hands.

This spiritual treasure had been completely repaired and integrated flawlessly, with the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit sleeping peacefully. Danxia Peak would definitely be interested in the Naming Fire Furnace. If he used a portion of the Naming Fire Furnace's future pill-refining quota to negotiate with Danxia Peak for Profound Yellow Mother Qi, he might just have a chance.

Of course, this matter could not be rushed.

Ning Zhuo gently tapped his fingers against his knee.

Could Meng Yehu's path to success be replicated on Yuan Dasheng?

The Yin-attribute power inside Yuan Dasheng originated from the methods of the Imperial Guard Commander under the Forgetful River Prefecture Lord. If Ning Zhuo asked Zhu Fenxiang again to summon a suitable god, it might just be resolved.

But this thought had surfaced before, only to be immediately suppressed by Ning Zhuo.

Ning Zhuo would not take that path!

Meng Yehu had already exposed his spiritual energy.

Zhu Fenxiang knew.

The Earth Prison Suppressing True Lord also knew.

If Yuan Dasheng was exposed too, things would be different. One Mechanism possessing deep spiritual energy could still be passed off as a serendipitous coincidence. But two Mechanisms having such spiritual energy would lead to associations and suspicion. If investigated further, the risk of exposing the Cremation Prajna Spirit Release Sutra would increase correspondingly.

That was his true foundational secret. Ning Zhuo would not jeopardize his long-term foundation just for a Golden Core battle power right in front of him.

"I'm in no rush anyway."

"Yuan Dasheng's spiritual energy has stabilized. Snow-Colored Maiden Hui's icy suet-jade hands can continuously nourish it. As long as I don't act recklessly, he won't break down in the short term."

"It's better to solve this matter myself."

He stored away Yuan Dasheng, his internal need for the Yin-Yang realm growing somewhat stronger.

Right at this moment, a light knock came from the stone room's door.

"Young Master," Old Chef reported, bringing a letter and a long wooden box.

Ning Zhuo dismissed Old Chef. Raising his hand with a beckoning motion, the flying letter landed in his palm.

The letter came from the Confucian cultivator group.

Chu Xuangui, Duanmu Zhang, Li Guanyu, and the others had, according to Ning Zhuo's request, mobilized the Confucian cultivator group overnight to produce a batch of Dao-Bearing Jade Pages, delivering them to the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor. The tone of the letter was quite earnest, and it mentioned one matter - Wen Ruanyu was currently coming out of seclusion, and her emergence might come with a breakthrough.

Ning Zhuo put down the flying letter and turned his gaze to the long box.

The long box was pure white overall, its surface lacking any gold or jade decorations. It only had three Confucian Sect seals affixed to it. The ink on the seals was upright and pure, every stroke carrying Literary Qi, securely protecting the items within the box.

Ning Zhuo unfastened the seals.

As the lid of the box opened, a faint ink fragrance mixed with the aura of jade wafted out.

Neatly laid inside was a stack of thin pages.

Dao-Bearing Jade Pages!

They looked like paper, yet also like jade.

Every single one was as thin as a cicada's wing, its edges shimmering with a warm, gentle white light. When held in the hand, one could barely feel their weight, yet they paradoxically possessed the cool, moist texture unique to jade. The body of the pages was translucent, with extremely fine veins swimming inside them, like the water veins of mountains and rivers, or like the fibers of book pages. If one injected their divine sense into them, they could feel an empty and pristine space within, like a tiny literary palace specifically waiting for true intent to take residence.

Ning Zhuo picked one up.

The jade page bent slightly between his fingers, not breaking, instead resembling a flexible piece of moonlight.

Not long after, Sun Lingtong and Qing Chi returned to the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor.

Sun Lingtong still had the appearance of a young boy, his eyes highly lively. He had just returned from the Rising Cloud Minor Trials, with several small pouches hanging from his waist. The mouths of the pouches faintly emitted medicinal qi, cloud qi, and the aroma of beast fodder, jingling as he walked. Obviously, over the past few days, he had conquered multiple Rising Cloud Minor Trials and gained quite a harvest.

Qing Chi stood by his side. The Ghost Clan Girl was in great spirits today, her black hair tied behind her head, revealing a fair yet slightly wild face. Her eyes sparkled, and the moment she saw Ning Zhuo, joy immediately surfaced on her face, and even her chest subconsciously straightened up a bit.

"Young Master is back?" Qing Chi stepped forward briskly, her voice crisp. "Congratulations to the Young Master for repairing the Naming Fire Furnace!"

Sun Lingtong added, "Young Master, we already heard the news on our way back."

In front of outsiders, he always assumed the identity of Ning Zhuo's pageboy.

Ning Zhuo nodded slightly. "Although it's repaired, it is like a sick patient and still needs to rest and recover for a period of time."

Ning Zhuo's gaze turned toward Qing Chi. "I heard from Lingtong. Your performance in these recent Rising Cloud Minor Trials has been very good."

She had originally just been happy for Ning Zhuo, but to her surprise, the first thing he did upon opening his mouth was praise her. The Ghost Clan Girl's eyes grew even brighter, the corners of her lips turning up uncontrollably. She wanted to pretend to be a bit more composed, but the joy on her face was like spring water overflowing its banks, impossible to hide no matter how hard she tried.

"It... it wasn't that great," Qing Chi said softly. "It was all Big Brother Sun's good commanding."

Sun Lingtong immediately put his hands on his hips. "Hey, hey, hey, I love hearing that. Say a few more words like that."

Qing Chi shot him a glance, her expression serious. "But I was the one who spotted a few variations in the army formations."

Sun Lingtong immediately understood that Qing Chi wanted to show off more in front of Ning Zhuo, so he said with a grin, "Yes, yes, yes, Miss Qing Chi's eye for the military path is exceptionally good. Without your reminder, my little bit of poison powder would have probably been scattered right into a fake flag formation."

Looking at the two of them, Ning Zhuo felt somewhat gratified in his heart.

He said, "I have been in the limelight too much recently; moving a single hair affects my entire body, and the impact is too great. If I went to many of the Rising Cloud Minor Trials, the situation would immediately change its flavor, and I fear people would target me. With the two of you hiding your identities and acting freely, you are unconstrained."

He encouraged Qing Chi again, "Especially you. You have great talent in the military path, so continue to forge ahead. The Rising Cloud Minor Trials are inherently the Myriad Manifestations Sect's way of screening talent. As long as you can pass, you can obtain inheritance, resources, and even prestige."

Qing Chi nodded vigorously.

Sun Lingtong chuckled, "I was just about to tell you about this. Young Master, after this minor trial ended, on our way back, someone invited Qing Chi to participate in the Troop Leading Hall's Rising Cloud Minor Trial."

Ning Zhuo's eyes slightly lit up. "The Troop Leading Hall?"

One of the sixteen halls of the Myriad Manifestations Sect - the Troop Leading Hall!

This hall researched the military path and had extremely profound accumulations. The military path emphasized leading troops in combat, using Military Might to substitute for spiritual energy and Divine Power, leveraging army formations, flag commands, military qi, and the soldiers' will to pry out higher-level combat strength. Although the Myriad Manifestations Sect was a great cultivation sect, its internal factions were spread across various lands, and externally, it faced complex situations such as the Flying Cloud Kingdom and White Paper Immortal City. Thus, the status of the Troop Leading Hall could not be slighted.

Ning Zhuo himself had made progress in the military path. After Young Master Qingwu imparted his true intent, he had also reached the peak of the Apprentice realm. However, unlike Mechanisms and Artifact Refining, his talent in the military path couldn't be considered particularly outstanding.

Qing Chi, however, was different.

In her observations of armies, she had already displayed a natural acuity. This ability wasn't just intelligence; it was more like she had a connection to the organization of army formations themselves, or more accurately, it was comprehension!

Ning Zhuo said sincerely, "This is a good thing. Qing Chi, you should go."

Qing Chi's breathing hitched slightly. "The Young Master also thinks I can go?"

Ning Zhuo nodded. "Of course. If you can gain something in the Troop Leading Hall's minor trial, it will be helpful to both the Nanming Stronghold and me in the future."

Qing Chi's heart instantly warmed.

To be personally acknowledged by Ning Zhuo, to have him look forward to her help...

This feeling made her even more joyful and expectant than winning the minor trial just now. The Ghost Clan Girl quietly clenched her fists in her sleeves, her knuckles turning slightly white from the force, yet she tried her best to maintain a calm expression on her face.

"I will definitely do my absolute best." She gazed at Ning Zhuo, her eyes fiery. "Young Master, in the future when you fight against the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, I can also join the battle. You can just say that I'm a human pet subdued by the Young Master."

Ning Zhuo was struck dumb.

Sun Lingtong also froze for a moment, then clutched his stomach and laughed, "Qing Chi, what exactly is stuffed inside that head of yours?"

Qing Chi, however, was very serious.

She had lost many memories. Her former family, her father, her childhood sweetheart, her past life... When she first thought of them, they also brought her many intense emotions. But as time passed, these things became vague and drifted further away, as if separated by many layers of veil.
