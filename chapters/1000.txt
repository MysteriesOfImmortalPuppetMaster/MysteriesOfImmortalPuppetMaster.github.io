Chapter 1000: This Battle, I Win!

On the side of the Naming Stronghold, a look of shock flashed across Tan Zhu's eyes.

He could see it: "Liang Ming has no way to execute this Divine Art entirely on his own. However, by relying on his main Cultivation Art and combining it with the seven minor Underworld gods, he managed to construct a Divine Art. Calling himself a True Lord is quite fitting."

"A Divine Art is hard to match; Chunyangzi is in a precarious situation now."

The seven tombs trembled simultaneously.

The thin and small figure of the Bone-Embracing Boy crouched on the first tomb. The shattered bones in his arms crackled, transforming into a blackish-yellow Physical Body Tablet.

The Lamp-Hiding Tomb Woman stood before the second tomb. Her white mourning clothes had been scorched black and curled at the edges by the Yang fire. She cupped the cracked lamp with both hands; the last wisp of black light flickering in the lampshade transformed into a spiritual energy Tablet.

The Sun-Concealing Old Official flipped through a fragmented tome. Dipping a broken brush into black blood, he wrote bizarre talismans in front of the third tomb, forming a Golden Core Tablet.

The Ash-Fetus Corpse Mother knelt beside the fourth tomb. Her massive abdomen split open, and grayish-white amniotic membranes spread out like a tide, wrapping around a lifespan Tablet.

The Mirror-Corroding Nether Official stood upon the fifth tomb. The shattered mirror on his chest reflected Chunyangzi's face, harboring a hidden Dao Heart Tablet.

The Earth-Belly Fire-Swallowing Lord's ruined abdomen rumbled. It used its remaining half-arm to embrace a Divine Soul Tablet.

Finally, the Sun-Burying Tablet Lord personally presided over the Fate Tablet!

The seven Tablets hummed in unison.

Blackish-yellow brilliance rose from the seven Underworld tombs, converging above Chunyangzi's head.

In an instant, an immense, gloomy, and chilling intent of death suppressed the Great Noon Heavenly Plateau!

Streaks of blackish-yellow tablet marks surfaced on Chunyangzi's body.

Those tablet marks were like branding irons pressed against his skin. The moment they appeared, his chest turned cold, as if freezing ice had been stuffed into his ribcage, or as if he were being buried layer by layer beneath Underworld soil.

Liang Ming glared at Chunyangzi with ruthless ferocity, his mouth splitting into a grin. "A lone Yang cannot grow! Chunyangzi, lasting this long is already your limit. Surrender and concede, and you might just save your precious life."

Many spectators couldn't help but hold their breath.

Having fought for six days and six nights, Chunyangzi's aura was weak. The sutras on his crimson-gold Daoist robe were dim and lackluster, and its hem was stained with Underworld soil and blood.

With his opponent's tactical setup complete and himself trapped in dire straits, Chunyangzi suddenly laughed.

"Hahaha, of course I know a lone Yang cannot grow."

"But similarly, when Pure Yang reaches its absolute extreme, it becomes the utmost strength and the utmost ferocity!"

Chunyangzi raised a hand and pressed it against his chest.

Forbidden Spell - Pure Yang Extreme Manifestation!

Purging impurities to keep the one, burning the body to manifest Yang. Once this method was executed, the Cultivator would rapidly burn away all impure qi and residual Yin within their body over a short period. Leaving absolutely no room for buffer or maneuver, it left behind only the purest and fiercest Pure Yang spiritual energy.

And since it was a forbidden spell, the price was naturally devastating.

Once unleashed, the Pure Yang body acted like a furnace refining impure iron; even one's own flesh and blood, lifespan, meridians, and so on would be burned as firewood.

Chunyangzi was putting everything on the line!

A booming explosion echoed within his body.

Pure Yang Immaculate Body!

A layer of incomparably clear and pure golden-white brilliance surfaced from deep within his bones and flesh, like spring water melting snow, or like the blazing sun washing over a mountain.

The bloodstains on his body were washed away, the Underworld soil was eradicated, and the aura of decay vanished into nothingness. The seven streaks of blackish-yellow tablet marks sizzled under the intense heat but still remained rigidly branded onto his body, like seven gloomy, freezing chains binding a great sun.

Though the crimson-gold Daoist robe was tattered, its three thousand sutras ignited once more.

Three Thousand Sutras Flourishing Fire!

The ruined robe fluttered and billowed. Flames rolled upward along the torn edges, much like a half-burned scripture that insisted on reciting its final chapter amidst the embers.

The Clear Heart Evil-Revealing Mirror was densely covered in black spots and was no longer usable.

The Three-Period Sundial immediately flew out. After enduring six days and six nights of fierce battle, the edges of its dial were covered in scorch marks and tainted with an indelible layer of black.

On the golden dial, the three marks of Morning, Noon, and Twilight lit up simultaneously.

Three Sun Bodies stepped out from the light of the dial.

The Morning Sun Body carried a gentle dawn light. Its face was youthful, and its brows bore the aura of burgeoning life. Taking a single step, it directly transformed into a ray of morning light and charged into the Cloud-Gathering Yang-Nourishing Lamp.

As the Morning Sun Body dove into it, the entire lamp flared brightly. Fine cracks appeared on the crimson jade walls of the lamp, feeding a surge of incredibly pure Yang energy back into Chunyangzi.

This was his final replenishment!

The Twilight Sun Body, on the other hand, turned silently and threw itself directly at the Sun-Burying Tablet Lord.

The twilight glow on its body retracted, looking like the very last golden rim of the setting sun before it sank behind the mountains.

Liang Ming tried to intercept it, but the Twilight Sun Body displayed unprecedented speed at that moment and struck directly into the Fate Tablet.

Its body rapidly dimmed and, a few breaths later, dissipated entirely. However, it left behind a seal, doing everything in its power to delay the might of the Fate Tablet.

The Noon Sun Body strode forward and charged into Chunyangzi's true form.

The two merged into one, like the convergence of two mighty rivers!

In a split second, Chunyangzi's aura skyrocketed, like a sudden blast of wind into a furnace. Golden-red veins of light surfaced from deep within his flesh and blood, akin to subterranean fire piercing through stone, manifesting on the surface of his robust physique.

The Golden Crow Holding Pill Ring transformed into a Golden Crow and opened its eyes for the fifth time!

Its spiritual energy was already exhausted, but the brilliance deep in its eyes remained razor-sharp.

It opened its beak and caught a crimson-gold Medicinal Pill.

Ancestral Fire Unity Pill!

The pill was a deep gold, akin to aged copper. It had no patterns on its surface, but at its core, a wisp of Ancestral Fire burned quietly.

The moment the Golden Crow Holding Pill Ring took the pill into its mouth, the manifestation of the Three-Legged Golden Crow became more solid and lifelike than ever before.

Flapping its wings, it soared straight toward Liang Ming.

Liang Ming was greatly startled. The Golden Crow Ring, after consuming the pill, actually gave him the sensation of facing a true Three-Legged Golden Crow.

Feeling immense pressure, Liang Ming hastily defended himself.

Chunyangzi stepped forward.

High Noon Shadowless Step!

There wasn't a single trace of a shadow beneath his feet. His entire being seemed to have been completely swallowed by the midday light. With one step, the stone plateau beneath his feet exploded with a bang. When he reappeared, his figure had already dashed right in front of the Underworld tombs.

Nine Yang Gate-Breaching Finger, unleashed once more!

The first finger ray, golden-white in color, scoured away the Underworld soil, sending clods flying and turbid qi churning.

The second finger ray, filled with Pure Yang, drilled into the ground, ruthlessly destroying the foundations of the first and second tombs.

Meanwhile, the Three-Legged Golden Crow shrieked, tearing through Liang Ming's Ash-Fetus Breathing Earth Garment with its claws.

The third finger ray slashed horizontally.

The fourth finger ray cleaved furiously downward.

The Bone-Embracing Boy let out a piercing scream. The shattered bones in his arms, along with the Physical Body Tablet, were overturned by the force of the finger. The ghastly white shattered bones exploded into powder, and the boy's tiny divine body was also torn apart by the golden light, scattering into a gust of bone ash.

Liang Ming, meanwhile, was still struggling to fend off the Three-Legged Golden Crow. The Void Return Heart-Shielding Canopy flew up to protect its master, while the Underworld Rampart Yellow Springs Shovel blocked horizontally in front of him.

The fifth finger ray pressed down - heavy and resolute!

The Lamp-Hiding Tomb Woman held up her cracked lamp to meet it, her mourning clothes fluttering fiercely in the flames.

Boom!

The Lamp-Hiding Tomb Woman, together with the spiritual energy Tablet, was snuffed out completely.

The sixth finger - its light surged like a tide.

Rolling waves of Pure Yang fire spread out, as if the great sun were pouring down a sea of golden flames.

The Ash-Fetus Corpse Mother threw out her amniotic membranes, and the Sun-Concealing Old Official frantically flipped through his fragmented tome. The two minor Underworld gods joined forces to block the front of the tombs.

The golden tide pressed down.

The amniotic membranes were burned through!

The fragmented tome was reduced to ashes!

The two divine phantoms melted in the fire, shattering simultaneously. Like paper tossed into a furnace, they were instantly turned to ash.

Unable to bear the immense burden, Chunyangzi's fingertips split open.

Golden-red beads of blood trickled down his finger bones. Midair, they were ignited by the Pure Yang Extreme Manifestation forbidden spell, transforming into tiny sparks that floated around him, burning fiercely.

The seventh finger descended.

The golden light at his fingertip condensed into a single line, so bright that it caused a stinging pain in the eyes of all the observing Cultivators.

The shattered mirror on the Mirror-Corroding Nether Official's chest reflected the light, the Earth-Belly Fire-Swallowing Lord's ruined abdomen rumbled, and the Sun-Burying Tablet Lord's sleeves billowed. The three remnant gods joined forces to intercept the strike.

The corroded mirror shattered.

The earthen belly exploded.

The Sun-Burying Tablet Lord's profound black formal robes burst into fierce flames, and the seventh tomb, which had been in the best condition, directly collapsed at one corner.

As for the other two tombs, they crumbled simultaneously!

The seal left by the Twilight Sun Body also dissipated at the exact same time. It had completed its most crucial mission, successfully stalling long enough for Chunyangzi to turn the tables.

Under Chunyangzi's final explosive burst, the seven Underworld tombs were obliterated until only the last, newest tomb remained.

The entire field of Underworld soil churned in chaos. Not even one in ten of the composite elements required for the Seven Tablets Death Sacrifice Divine Art remained, ultimately resulting in - 

A total failure on the verge of success!

The final Sun-Burying Tablet Lord was burned to ashes in the flames.

At this point, all seven minor Underworld gods had been annihilated. The residual Yin Qi instinctively burrowed into the ground, only to be melted away like frost and snow by the Crimson Yang Ore Vein.

The medicinal efficacy fueling the Three-Legged Golden Crow was rapidly depleted. It soon bottomed out, its imposing might gone.

Liang Ming successfully fended it off, but in the very next moment, he suffered a tragic backlash from his Divine Art!

His chest exploded on the spot, spraying blood and dirt several meters away.

His Nascent Soul let out a shrill, agonizing scream. Cracks had already been densely spread across it, running through its entire body. Now, reaching its absolute limit, the entire Nascent Soul shattered spectacularly, like porcelain crashing onto the floor.

With his Nascent Soul collapsing, Liang Ming nearly passed out on the spot; his lifetime of cultivation was completely gone!

The spectators were completely dumbfounded; a dead silence fell over them.

"So Chunyangzi was waiting all along!"

"He deliberately held back his offensive earlier. He wanted Liang Ming to reveal his trump cards first!"

"Observe clearly before making a move. By striking second to subdue the enemy, he actually seized the initiative. This level of courage and boldness is terrifying."

"He purposefully dragged out the bitter battle for six days and six nights, quietly waiting for his opponent's momentum to grow to a point of no return, waiting until the trump card of the Seven Tablets Death Sacrifice was finally revealed. Exceptional skill emboldens a person, truly exceptional skill emboldens a person!"

"I was wrong. Earlier, I only felt that Liang Ming was mysterious and unfathomable. Now, it seems the outside world's intelligence drastically underestimated Chunyangzi's true combat power!"

"With such combat power, how did the Pure Yang Palace end up in its current state?"

"Nonsense. When mingling in the Righteous Path, what use is it to just be good at fighting? Without management skills, the larger the scale, the harder it is to sustain. The Pure Yang Palace has been heavily ostracized by surrounding factions these past few years; they barely have any room to survive."

Liang Ming collapsed directly onto the ground, severely injured and on the verge of death.

Filled with a mix of fury and resentment, he roared hoarsely, "Chunyangzi, I haven't lost yet! I can still win!"

A Divine Art backlash, a shattered Nascent Soul, a decaying Physical Body.

Having reached the state of his life hanging by a thread, he successfully triggered The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook!

At this moment, every sound on the Great Noon Heavenly Plateau was cut off as if by an invisible hand.

The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook activated.

There was no earth-shattering momentum.

There was no majestic, vast magical light.

There was only a single line.

The line was incredibly thin - like spider silk, like a thread of rain, or like a speck of cold light dangling from the depths of fate. It descended from the void, so fine it was almost invisible, yet it caused the hearts of countless Cultivators to simultaneously stop beating. It felt as if a frost blade was pressed against their throats, slicing even their breaths in two.

Chunyangzi's pupils rapidly shrank to the size of pinpricks.

A flash of golden light emerged from his sleeve.

A Yang Fish flew out.

This small golden fish was about three inches long. Its scales resembled tiny flakes of gold leaf, and as it flicked its tail, it scattered soft and pure sunlight. Deep within its ink-jade eyes hid a hint of Yin coldness, like a phantom shadow in an ancient well, perfectly aligning with the principle of Yin Extremity Generating Yang.

This was a realm that Chunyangzi had bitterly sought for many years but had never managed to achieve.

The Yang Fish, however, was born close to the Dao.

It was a Spirit Treasure with its own spiritual energy. Without waiting for Chunyangzi to command it, it had already sensed the killing intent of The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook.

In an instant, the Yang Fish flicked its tail and rose to meet it, like a moth throwing itself into the fire.

Its golden body twirled midair, spilling out a golden sea of Pure Yang. The golden light, like the accumulated morning suns of ten thousand years, surged wave after wave toward that thread-like hook, like waves crashing against the shore.

The hook line trembled slightly.

It seemed as if it were going to be repelled.

But in the next breath, the hook's tip bent, unexpectedly snagging the Yang Fish!

The Yang Fish struggled violently. Its golden scales burst with blinding brilliance before it violently exploded midair.

With just a light poke, the hook's tip pierced right through its dazzling radiance.

The small golden fish let out a mournful cry, ultimately unable to withstand the might of a Dao Artifact.

The hook line's residual power had not yet exhausted; it continued its thrust down toward Chunyangzi!

Chunyangzi gritted his teeth, his entire body feeling hollow and powerless.

He forcefully separated the Noon Sun Body, which had previously merged into his form.

The moment the two separated, the aura of Chunyangzi's true form drastically plummeted.

The Noon Sun Body roared and lunged forward. Its Pure Yang True Fire erupted like an exploding pill furnace, whipping up waves of golden-white flames several meters high into the sky.

The hook's tip met resistance, yet it still pierced through the fire!

A small hole appeared on the Noon Sun Body's chest, as tiny as a needle piercing through paper.

The next moment, its entire body disintegrated into smoke. Despite giving it its all, it only managed to shave off a tiny fraction of the Dao Artifact's residual might.

The hook's tip fell straight toward the center of Chunyangzi's brow, like an arrow aiming to pierce his forehead.

Chunyangzi's face was deathly pale, nearly devoid of any color.

At the final juncture, a Spirit Bamboo Puppet blocked the path in front of Chunyangzi.

Void-Harboring Blade-Snapping Guard!

Forcefully activated, it valiantly and fearlessly went up to meet the attack.

Harboring the Void within its body, it enveloped the descending Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook.

The hook plunged into the Void. Then, the crowd of Cultivators watched as the Void-Harboring Blade-Snapping Guard cracked inch by inch. Its mechanical fasteners snapped and flew off one by one, shooting out erratically like shattered stars.

In the end, unable to bear the heavy burden, it exploded with a boom, shattering into pieces and dying on the spot.

The Void detonated, swallowing the final shadow of the hook. The gap was then quickly mended by the reality of Heaven and Earth, restoring everything to its original state.

Ultimately, it was blocked!

Liang Ming's eyes widened intensely. Upon witnessing this scene, he finally passed out from sheer anger.

Chunyangzi stood in place, like a lone peak standing amidst the snow.

A tiny trail of blood slowly slid down from the center of his brow, like vermilion traced by a red thread.

The seven blackish-yellow tablet marks were deeply branded into his flesh and blood, currently emitting wisps of cold, gloomy cyan smoke.

The skin and flesh on seven of his finger bones were completely split open, the bones themselves shattered, dripping with scalding hot blood.

His crimson-gold Daoist robe was mostly destroyed. The three thousand sutras were as dim as ash, like lamps that had completely burned out.

The Golden Crow Holding Pill Ring kept its eyes closed; its spiritual energy was subdued, incredibly weak and feeble.

Only the Noon Heaven Sun-Patrolling Carriage remained suspended high in the sky. Over the course of six days and six nights, it had worked conscientiously, holding down the final geographical advantage of the Great Noon Heavenly Plateau for Chunyangzi. Yet, it had also endured countless attacks; its wheels turned sluggishly, its body was covered in scars, and it looked as though it could shatter at any moment.

Chunyangzi suddenly opened his mouth and spat out a large mouthful of blood.

He raised his sleeve and slowly wiped away the blood trace at the corner of his mouth.

His movements were very slow.

And extremely difficult.

Chunyangzi lifted his eyes, sweeping his gaze over the many Cultivators who were spectating the battle.

"This battle."

His hoarse voice echoed across the Great Noon Heavenly Plateau.

"I win!"
