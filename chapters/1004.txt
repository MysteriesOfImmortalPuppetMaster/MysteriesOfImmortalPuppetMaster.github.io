Chapter 1004: Where is My Medicinal Efficacy?!

The giant statue on the dome slowly became expressionless. The True Lord of Earth Prison Suppression had already given his advice; at this moment, he withdrew his divine power and silently retreated.

The sound of hammering in the domain remained dull.

Ning Zhuo stood at the formation core. His sleeves hung down lightly, his face slightly pale from just waking up after a coma, but his eyes were clear and bright.

With a wave of his hand, the accumulated flying letters and intelligence jade slips flew over from afar.

Most of these flying letters were talking about one thing: the battle between Chunyangzi and Liang Ming!

"In the fifth battle, Chunyangzi indeed took action personally."

"Truly not bad, he won the most crucial battle. Oh? The Nanming Stronghold has already occupied the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. This charge to establish a stronghold has succeeded!"

Ning Zhuo smiled faintly and immediately watched the combat recordings in detail.

The scene of the Great Noon Heaven Terrace Martial Arena unfolded with a boom.

The Noon Sky Sun-Patrolling Chariot hung high in the sky, like a great sun forcibly held aloft. For six days and six nights, it had remained almost entirely in the sky, shedding exhausted yet resilient sunlight to contend against the Nether Earth Tomb Nation that Liang Ming was slowly spreading out.

Liang Ming used the Divine Summoning Art to erect tombs.

The Bone-Embracing Boy, the Lamp-Hiding Tomb Woman, the Sun-Subduing Old Official, the Ash-Fetus Corpse Mother, the Mirror-Eroding Nether Official, the Earth-Belly Fire-Swallowing Lord, and the Sun-Burying Jade Lord... Minor gods of the Underworld manifested one by one. Yin Qi surged like a tide, and tomb earth rolled like waves.

Chunyangzi, on the other hand, repeatedly utilized the Three Times Yang Body Art.

Morning Sun Body, Noon Sun Body, Twilight Sun Body.

The Rosy Cloud Yang-Replenishing Lamp was lit multiple times.

The red jade lamp basin was as thin as cicada wings. The rosy cloud wick-line burned from its initial bright purple-gold to an exhausted crimson, and finally to a single, tottering glimmer.

The Golden Crow Pill-Holding Ring opened its eyes time and time again.

That red-gold armband transformed into a three-legged golden crow, holding different medicinal pills in its beak. As the pills varied, the power changed accordingly. It either replenished pure yang qi, suppressed yin poison, enhanced the Nine Yang Gate-Knocking Finger, protected the Dao Heart, or forcibly elevated his magical power mastery.

The Clear Heart Evil-Revealing Mirror was eventually covered in dense black spots.

The Three Thousand Sutras Blazing Fire burned until the scriptures on his Daoist robe grew dim, almost obliterated.

The Night-Burning Light-Returning Sword remained consistently sharp, as if cleaving the dark night over and over again.

When the Nine Yang Gate-Knocking Finger was unleashed up to the seventh finger, Ning Zhuo saw that the flesh and skin on Chunyangzi's fingers were completely torn, and even his finger bones were shattering.

-----

Ning Zhuo watched it three times consecutively.

"Senior Chunyangzi's tactic was one of the correct choices."

"Liang Ming held The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook in his hand. If Senior Chunyangzi had gone all out with fierce attacks from the very beginning, forcing him into a situation where his life hung by a thread, it would have triggered the Dao-level artifact ahead of time. By then, if Liang Ming threw out brand-new methods, such as a rapid recovery technique, Chunyangzi would have had a hard time."

"Therefore, he had to fight a war of attrition. He had to probe out Liang Ming's methods as much as possible so that he could be prepared."

"Though it seemed like he was being led by the nose by Liang Ming, in reality, Chunyangzi was forcing Liang Ming to erect the Seven Tombs one by one and reveal his trump cards in the open."

Ning Zhuo's eyes narrowed slightly.

"This is just like two people fighting in close quarters. The enemy's fist hidden behind their waist is the hardest to guard against. When they truly throw a heavy punch, their shoulders, waist, legs, and breathing all exert force together. At that instant, they no longer have any room to kick, jump, or change direction."

"The moment the heavy punch lands hardest is also when the situation is clearest."

"The strongest moment is also the weakest moment."

"When the Seven-Jade Death Sacrifice was on the verge of completion, Liang Ming placed all his mental energy into the consolidation of his divine ability. Chunyangzi countering at that exact moment presented the greatest chance of winning and the highest certainty."

"However, the might of The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook was so immense that not even the Yang Fish could block it."

"My previous layout was constrained; taking a step back indeed opened up the vast sea and sky. If I were alone facing the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, and the other side still possessed this Dao-level artifact..."

Ning Zhuo shook his head with a bitter smile.

"The Void-Embracing Blade-Breaking Guard is destroyed?"

"Tsk."

Ning Zhuo's fingertips gently brushed over his Mechanism Ring, a trace of pity flashing in the depths of his eyes.

"Fortunately, it can be mass-produced."

"Sacrificing this Void-Embracing Blade-Breaking Guard in exchange for invisible political gains is a profitable trade."

The mechanism he lent out blocked the final residual might of The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook for Chunyangzi in front of everyone's eyes.

Even if Chunyangzi refused to acknowledge it, others would remember.

Ning Zhuo's attention ultimately focused on the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

He read through the information regarding the handover of this treasured fengshui land again and again.

"Chunyangzi's Yang Dao is a school of its own, but it is too extreme. Liang Ming criticized him during the battle, saying that a solitary Yang cannot give birth to life. Though the words were harsh, the reasoning hit the nail on the head."

"Chunyangzi is under the curse of knowledge. The Yang Fish has been by his side for years, yet he has never been able to touch the realm of 'Yang Extremity Generating Yin'."

"But the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar... The altar's surface receives the sun, its waist swallows the clouds, and its base suppresses the fire."

"The three vein-suppressing copper wedges in the Yang-Locking Well correspond to sunlight, fire veins, and cloud roots."

"Sunlight enters the clouds, fire veins generate Yang, and cloud qi supports the sun."

Ning Zhuo's expression became increasingly solemn.

He now possessed the realm of a Grandmaster in the Yang Dao, and the medicinal power of the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill contained the complete text of the Pure Yang Pill Scripture. Ning Zhuo understood it thoroughly!

"To Chunyangzi, this place is definitely not just a simple Yang Dao treasured land."

"What Chunyangzi lacks is harmony."

"He needs to lock in his excessively blazing Yang."

"He needs to make his Yang neither dissipate nor become volatile, so that it can be hidden, nurtured, and transformed, ultimately giving birth to a speck of Yin at its absolute peak."

Chunyangzi's situation was like hopping on one thick leg, unable to jump over a towering threshold. He needed assistance to help him cross this level.

"But after crossing this level, exactly how much benefit will he reap?"

Ultimately, Ning Zhuo had no way to make a relatively specific estimate.

"Chunyangzi fought bitterly against Liang Ming to this extent, and his actions have already deviated from the duties of a Great Stronghold Leader. This exactly proves his yearning for this place!"

"The true answer won't be hard to find. As long as I clearly explore the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, I'll be able to figure out the true significance of this place to Chunyangzi!"

Right at this moment, Tie Kuang's voice echoed from above the domain.

"Little Friend Ning Zhuo, if you're awake, don't keep lazing around. If you can come out, hurry up and come out! The Naming Fire Furnace is still waiting to be repaired!"

Ning Zhuo's thoughts were interrupted.

Tie Kuang's voice continued, "There's one more thing. Chunyangzi has sent an envoy over to demand the return of the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill."

Ning Zhuo's heart skipped a beat.

He reached out and took the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill from his robes.

The medicinal pill still had that inconspicuous appearance.

Ordinary and plain, like a spherical pebble.

But as Ning Zhuo held it in his hand, he was very clear in his heart: this inheritance pill was now nothing but an empty shell. Its internal medicinal power was almost entirely gone, leaving only an empty pill husk!

Although the Myriad Phenomena Cradle was only an early-to-mid stage technique, the effect of casting it right now was truly formidable - through it, Ning Zhuo directly swallowed and absorbed the true intent within the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill until it was completely clean.

Looking at the pill shell, a hint of awkwardness finally surfaced on Ning Zhuo's face.

"If Senior Chunyangzi sees this, he might just go crazy, right?"

He coughed lightly.

Even though Ning Zhuo had a thick skin, he felt somewhat embarrassed at this moment.

His original plan was to absorb as much medicinal power and extract as much true intent as possible.

According to his estimation, the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill carried the realizations of successive sect masters; its true intent was like a sea, and its medicinal power was abundant. Even if he went all out, he would only be able to absorb a portion of it.

There would definitely be residual medicinal power left, making it justifiable when returning it.

Who would have thought he'd encounter an obstacle halfway? The curse of knowledge lay squarely in front of him, forcing Ning Zhuo to find another path. Once the Myriad Phenomena Cradle was successfully cast, the infant opened its mouth, heaven and earth fed it, and the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill was sucked until only its shell remained.

Ning Zhuo stared at the pill shell for a few breaths, and the awkwardness on his face slowly receded.

"No, he won't."

"Senior Chunyangzi has recently won and is still injured; he won't lash out immediately."

"Besides, even if he makes a fuss, I have a legitimate reason."

Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect.

These words lit up in Ning Zhuo's heart.

This layer of Righteous Path reputation he had meticulously plotted and cultivated was truly useful!

The Truth Bell had already verified his intentions.

His desire to rescue the demonic soul of the Red-Robed Guest was real, and his intent to fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect was also real. The Pure Yang Patriarch Pill was borrowed for this battle, and sucking its medicinal power dry was also to quickly elevate his realm to face strong enemies.

Everyone in the world would be able to accept this explanation.

If Chunyangzi wanted to lash out, he would first have to consider whether he could break through this layer of righteous justification.

The difficulty of doing so would be exceedingly high!

Ning Zhuo put away the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill, his expression calm.

"Chunyangzi winning and his prestige greatly increasing was also one of the possibilities I estimated earlier."

"Based on his attainment in the Righteous Path, his next move should be to deal with me and sweep away internal obstacles."

Within the entire Nanming Stronghold, looking at the performance of all members up to now - the only two who were politically matched opponents were Ning Zhuo and Chunyangzi.

Power cannot be divided. Even if temporarily divided, it will inevitably trend towards unification.

Someone like Ning Zhuo, who single-handedly built the Nanming Stronghold, linked the creditors, and influenced various factions, was the core.

Having defeated Liang Ming this time, captured the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, and being publicly recognized as number one in combat power with the greatest contribution, Chunyangzi's prestige had skyrocketed.

One mountain cannot house two tigers!

"Fortunately, I am well-prepared and have been taking precautions against this matter all along."

Ning Zhuo pondered for a moment and began to write letters.

He first wrote to the Confucian cultivator group.

In the letter, he asked Confucian cultivator seniors such as Duanmu Zhang, Chu Xuangui, and Dong Nishang to assist in mass-producing Dao-Bearing Jade Pages. Ning Zhuo promised to repay all required expenses and owed favors one by one in the future.

Subsequently, he wrote letters to Sun Lingtong, Old Chef, Gongsun Yan, and others, stating his condition and arranging upcoming matters.

The letter to Chen San was even more detailed.

He was to continue returning sponsorships, continue apologizing, and continue registering favors. For anyone unwilling to take back their funds, record their name, origin, sponsored items, and requested matters. There must be no negligence or embezzlement. The Three Valiants Life-Claiming Nail was still not included in the return list.

After that, he wrote to Shen Xi, Situ Xing, Lin Jinglong, and others.

The general idea was: Ning Zhuo remembers the deep affections of all fellow Daoists. Having just passed the Rising Cloud Minor Trial this time, Ning Zhuo has missed the agreed date and feels ashamed in his heart. Joyously, the Nanming Stronghold has obtained the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, so everyone's prior investments have finally heard an echo. Next, I will work together with Senior Tie Kuang to repair the Naming Fire Furnace and will temporarily not return to the stronghold.

Finally, he wrote a letter to Chunyangzi.

To be opened personally by Senior Chunyangzi. Ning Zhuo deeply inscribes the grace of the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill in his heart. Junior borrowed this pill to comprehend the Yang Dao, Pill Dao, and Fire Element, reaping massive harvests, all thanks to Senior's assistance. This pill's true intent is now heavily depleted, and Junior feels ashamed in his heart. I am willing to use the pill profits after the Naming Fire Furnace is repaired to compensate the Pure Yang Peak.

"Senior has recently won and is heavily injured; convalescence should be the priority. The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is newly secured, so I request Senior to preside over the overall situation. Ning Zhuo will temporarily follow Senior Tie Kuang to repair the Naming Fire Furnace. Once the furnace is complete, I will definitely apologize to Senior in person."

Ning Zhuo lowered his head, giving Chunyangzi plenty of face while weaving the repair of the Naming Fire Furnace, the battle against the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, and the overall situation of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar all into the letter.

Chunyangzi would be furious.

But Chunyangzi would also endure it.

-----

Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

Chunyangzi sat in the hall.

Standing below the hall was a middle-aged cultivator.

This person was tall and thin with slightly reddish skin and thick black eyebrows. He wore a visibly old brownish-red magic robe, and a copper gourd hung from his waist. The mouth of the gourd occasionally spat out specks of warm white luminous dust.

His name was Zhao Wangyang, originating from the Morning Glow Daoist Temple. He cultivated the Lesser Yang Breathing Art, possessed a mid-stage Golden Core cultivation base, and was most adept at using Yang Qi to warm and nourish injured bodies and dispel Yin coldness.

Zhao Wangyang held up a pledge of allegiance with both hands and offered it.

Taking a deep breath, he bowed and said, "Lord Chunyangzi, Junior Zhao Wangyang is willing to lead the thirty-seven remnants of the Morning Glow Daoist Temple to join the Nanming Stronghold. If Senior does not abandon us, Junior is willing to open a Morning Sun Medicine Garden on the periphery of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar to cultivate Yang-attribute spirit herbs for the Nanming Stronghold, and is also willing to follow the army and obey orders."

Chunyangzi looked over the pledge of allegiance, his gaze falling onto Zhao Wangyang.

After a great victory, the taste of prestige lay in such subtle details.

"Morning Glow Daoist Temple, I've heard of it," Chunyangzi said slowly. "Back in the day, the patriarch of your temple erected a sundial at the Eastern Ridge to dispel a cold disaster for the commoners of three counties, manifesting the might and righteousness of the Yang Dao. The fact that you have come to join us and are willing to open a medicine garden is very good."

Zhao Wangyang felt his heart relax, and his eye sockets grew somewhat hot. "Senior actually heard of the deeds of my sect's patriarch?"

Chunyangzi smiled faintly. "The Pure Yang lineage shares the same spirit across the world. Regarding the Morning Sun Medicine Garden, submit a charter report later. If you can produce results, the Nanming Stronghold will naturally not treat you unfairly."

Zhao Wangyang immediately bowed down, his forehead almost touching the ground. "Many thanks to the Great Stronghold Leader for taking us in!"

Chunyangzi: "As for your ranking and seating in the stronghold, there is no rush to decide. Do your work first..."

Chunyangzi paused mid-sentence and looked outside the door.

The confidant he had dispatched had returned, bringing back Ning Zhuo's letter and the wooden box containing the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill.

Chunyangzi's gaze instantly landed on the wooden box.

Zhao Wangyang was curious and watched silently.

The confidant approached Chunyangzi and presented the wooden box and letter.

The hall fell silent.

Chunyangzi reached out and took the wooden box.

"The Pure Yang Patriarch Pill has returned!"

This rock had actually been weighing on his heart the entire time.

This medicinal pill carried the Yang Dao true intent of successive sect masters; its value and significance in inheritance were much higher than even the Yang Fish!

It was good that it had returned.

Chunyangzi raised his hand and uncovered the sealing talisman.

The vermilion-lacquered wooden box opened silently.

Inside the box, that Pure Yang Patriarch Pill lay quietly, its exterior still plain and unadorned like a round pebble. The pill shell was grayish-white and entirely inconspicuous; one couldn't even see much spiritual light on its surface.

However, Chunyangzi knew best that the more this pill appeared this way, the more profound and restrained it was.

The majestic true intent of the patriarchs was all integrated into the medicinal power, deeply hidden within the pill.

Chunyangzi extended two fingers and carefully picked up the pill.

The pill fell into his palm, its touch still heavy.

A flash of pride quickly flashed across the depths of Chunyangzi's eyes.

He sent out a wisp of divine sense, wrapped and mixed it with pure yang magical power, and slowly seeped it into the pill shell.

Using this method to explore, he could instantly determine its truth or falsehood, reality or illusion.

The next moment, his brow twitched slightly.

The medicinal power...

The mountain wind blew past outside the hall, and the window lattice rattled softly.

Chunyangzi's divine sense roamed around inside the pill.

The pill shell was intact.

The pill patterns were still there.

But the inside was completely empty.

That expanse of pure yang true intent, which should have been as vast as a great sun sinking into the sea, was now only a thin layer of residual warmth.

Chunyangzi blinked.

"Hmm?" His expression was somewhat doubtful.

He thought that his injuries were too severe, his divine sense was damaged, and his perception was mistaken.

Thus, he sat up straight, and Nascent Soul aura slowly drifted from within his body.

This time, Chunyangzi held nothing back.

Divine sense like golden threads, and spiritual energy like sunbeams, seeped layer by layer into the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill.

The inside of the pill was empty, empty, empty.

Utterly empty.

Chunyangzi's heart turned cold.

It was as if someone had left the gates, pillars, and plaques of a treasure vault completely untouched, but had completely emptied out the mountains of gold and seas of silver inside, leaving only a bit of aged gold dust on the floor.

He looked down at the pill in his palm, the muscles around his eye sockets twitching slightly.

His originally grand and bright gaze first turned blank, then shifted to alarm and doubt, and afterward, that alarm and doubt was like oil ignited by fire, abruptly transforming into an uncontrollable wave of shock and horror.

Pure Yang Patriarch Pill!

Is this still my Pure Yang Patriarch Pill?

Where is the medicinal power?

Where is all my medicinal power?!!

What happened?

What did that little thief Ning Zhuo do?!

Could it be that he actually sucked away all the true intent inside it?!
