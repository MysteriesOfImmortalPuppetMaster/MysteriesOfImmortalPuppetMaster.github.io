Chapter 1006: You Should Join My Artifact Forging Hall

Tie Kuang raised a hand, and another layer of translucent fire curtain rose around the black iron circular platform, isolating him, Ning Zhuo, and the Naming Fire Furnace inside. The roaring sound of the furnace fires outside instantly quieted down significantly, as if blocked by a thick wall.

The inner formation was activated.

A flash of silver light darted from Ning Zhuo's sleeve as the Spirit Communication Mirror flew out, hovering before his palm.

The mirror's surface was clear as water, reflecting a spot of fiery red shadow deep within the belly of the Naming Fire Furnace. That was precisely the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit.

It was much stronger than it was initially. The tiny bird's body now possessed bright fiery feathers, but it still curled up deep within the furnace's core, its head buried under its wings, revealing only a tuft of fine golden feathers.

Ning Zhuo's expression softened, and he kept his voice extremely light: "Little darling, don't be afraid. Once today passes, you will be completely repaired."

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit curled its body up tightly, just about to shiver.

Life Hanging by a Thread shifted slightly.

Pulled by that invisible thread, the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit could only slowly raise its head. It looked at the Spirit Communication Mirror and let out a low chirp, as if cooperating.

Tie Kuang cast a sideways glance, uttering another sound of praise in his heart.

He grabbed a handful of Profound Obsidian Spirit-Fixing Sand and relaxed his five fingers.

The black sand did not fall to the ground; instead, it transformed into a ring of fine, dense sand mist that slowly spread out, clinging to the outer wall of the Naming Fire Furnace. Every grain of sand seemed to have eyes, specifically landing on the nodes where the furnace wall trembled the most and where spiritual energy was most easily disturbed.

The body of the Naming Fire Furnace trembled gently.

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit immediately let out a panicked chirp.

Ning Zhuo was quick-witted and deft; the radiance of the Spirit Communication Mirror draped down softly, and his divine sense seeped into the furnace core along with the mirror's light. His voice was like warm water washing over the bottom of the furnace: "Don't be afraid, don't move."

At the same time, Life Hanging by a Thread tightened by a fraction.

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit froze.

Tie Kuang's hands did not stop. The Profound Obsidian Spirit-Fixing Sand continued to spread, eventually forming a layer of black-gold sand coating outside the furnace, as thin as a cicada's wings. Once the sand coating was formed, the chaotic, subtle tremors across various parts of the furnace body were instantly suppressed significantly.

"Good," Tie Kuang said with some delight.

He had originally thought this step would take at least half a shichen (an hour), requiring him to repeatedly soothe the artifact spirit halfway through. He hadn't expected Ning Zhuo's methods to be so outstanding, vastly exceeding Tie Kuang's expectations.

Ning Zhuo, however, stared at the Profound Obsidian Spirit-Fixing Sand, his mind rapidly deducing. The sand coating didn't completely seal off the furnace spirit; instead, it resembled the Earth Element's Dao of load-bearing - suppressing restlessness but not life, settling panic but not vitality. The Earth Element was thick and heavy, capable of bearing all things. The Profound Obsidian Spirit-Fixing Sand used earth to support fire and used darkness to conceal light. It inherently had the effect of suppressing the furnace spirit's terror.

Tie Kuang then picked up the Warm Cloud Marrow.

That mass of warm, soft cloud marrow was lifted by his spiritual energy and slowly guided toward the furnace opening. As the Warm Cloud Marrow approached the Naming Fire Furnace, it suddenly acted like a little bird sensing the scent of its nest, automatically dispersing into threads of warm clouds and flowing in through the opening.

When the warm clouds entered the furnace core, the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit first shrank back, then its eyes shifted slightly. The warm clouds didn't touch it; they merely spread out slowly deep within the core, as if building a soft cloud nest for it.

Chirp.

This time, the chirp contained less terror and more curiosity.

Ning Zhuo felt relieved and said, "The Warm Cloud Marrow nourishes the artifact spirit's mind and supplements its sense of returning to the nest. During this major overhaul, the Naming Fire Furnace is undergoing a massive change. The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit is unfamiliar with the new environment. If it refuses to treat the new furnace as its nest, no matter how perfectly the furnace body is repaired, there will ultimately be a barrier."

"Lord Tie Kuang, is my analysis correct?"

Tie Kuang's movements paused for a moment.

He took a deep look at Ning Zhuo and suddenly smiled, "You kid really should come to my Artifact Forging Hall. With your level of insight, staying outside and messing around is a waste."

Ning Zhuo smiled, "This junior also greatly admires the Artifact Forging Hall. It's just that I am currently bogged down by debts and truly have no free time. If there is a chance in the future, I will definitely seek your guidance, Lord."

Tie Kuang scoffed, "Slippery kid."

After the Warm Cloud Marrow completely lined the furnace core, the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit also exhibited a positive reaction - it slightly uncurled from its contracted state. Its tiny claws stepped on the warm clouds, and its fiery feathers fluttered gently, resembling a long-frightened nestling tentatively standing in a soft nest for the first time.

The first hurdle had been secured.

Next came the most dangerous step.

Extracting the pill toxins!

Tie Kuang raised his hand, and a short, stout black clay smoke censer appeared in his palm. The censer had nine holes, and no fire emerged from them; only faint purple threads of smoke spiraled within. There were no ornate decorations on its body, only a series of old scars.

Ning Zhuo's gaze focused.

He remembered that Tie Kuang had once directly extracted faint purple pill toxins from the Naming Fire Furnace right in front of him. That method was wondrous and rare, leaving a deep impression on him to this day.

"You protect the artifact spirit," Tie Kuang lowered his voice. "This time, the extraction of the pill toxins will be fierce, unlike before. Although it is entirely beneficial to the Naming Fire Furnace, the toxins have accumulated for too long, and the root of the sickness is deep. When uprooting them, it will cause pain akin to having cramps and being skinned alive. The furnace itself feels nothing, but the artifact spirit will suffer agonizing pain."

Ning Zhuo took a deep breath, pressing his hands on both sides of the Spirit Communication Mirror: "I am ready. Please proceed, Senior!"

Tie Kuang held the smoke censer in his left hand and took out the Hundred Forgings Thousandfold Hammer with his right.

This hammer was merely one foot and seven inches long, completely pitch-black. Its striking surface was covered with dense indentations, and the handle was wrapped in dark red iron wire. As soon as it appeared, the surrounding firelight dimmed, as if all the furnace fires were bowing their heads to it.

Tie Kuang struck his first blow.

He did not strike the Naming Fire Furnace.

The hammer's shadow landed in the empty space three inches outside the furnace.

Dong.

The sound was deep, like a muffled clap of thunder. The outer wall of the Naming Fire Furnace trembled slightly, and a trace of the old cracks, hidden cavities, and toxic crevices concealed throughout the furnace body were shaken out.

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit let out a sharp shriek, its fiery feathers bristling.

Ning Zhuo immediately shone the Spirit Communication Mirror upon it: "Little darling, endure the pain. It's better for the toxins to come out. Hold on."

With Life Hanging by a Thread secretly manipulating it, the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit trembled ceaselessly, but did not fly around wildly.

Tie Kuang struck his second blow.

Dong.

From deep within the furnace belly, faint purple toxic smoke surfaced.

The moment the toxic smoke appeared, the air was instantly filled with a bitter, sickly sweet medicinal scent. It started as a thread of smoke, then in a blink, transformed into a wisp of turbid mist, twisting as it tried to drill back into the furnace wall.

Tie Kuang coldly snorted, and the nine holes of the black clay smoke censer simultaneously inhaled.

The faint purple pill toxin was sucked into the smoke censer.

Third blow.

Fourth blow.

...

The hammer blows fell one after another, each striking the most hidden old crevices of the furnace body. Numerous microscopic blemishes appeared on the surface of the Naming Fire Furnace. Gray-black, turbid gold, reddish-brown, pale green, dark purple - pill toxins of various colors drilled out from deep within the furnace wall bit by bit, like venomous snakes forced out of their dens.

The formation masters monitoring the furnace in the outer formation continuously reported the situation.

"Attention, on the southeast furnace wall, there is a leakage of turbid gold pill toxin."

"In the fire veins at the bottom of the furnace, reddish-brown fire toxin has been triggered and is rising."

"Pill qi and toxic qi are entangled in the furnace belly! Requesting the inner formation guards to quickly separate them!"

Beads of sweat seeped from Tie Kuang's forehead. The Hundred Forgings Thousandfold Hammer in his palm moved so fast it nearly blurred into a black shadow.

Every hammer blow had to be accurate to the extreme. A deviation of an inch would damage the furnace veins. A fraction too light, and the toxins wouldn't be forced out. A fraction too heavy, and the Naming Fire Furnace itself would be damaged.

Ning Zhuo was not having an easy time either.

While maintaining the Spirit Communication Mirror, he relied on Life Hanging by a Thread to control the artifact spirit. The artifact spirit was so terrified it was on the verge of rioting, but Ning Zhuo's spirit communication arts continuously soothed it, which was not entirely ineffective.

On the other hand, the youth was resisting the pill toxins in the air while carefully observing and distinguishing them. As he continuously learned, he also remained on guard against Tie Kuang, preventing the latter from laying down any hidden tricks.

The youth's face gradually turned pale, sweat formed on his forehead, but his eyes grew brighter and brighter.

He was gradually beginning to understand.

Tie Kuang's extraction of the toxins was absolutely not a brute force uprooting. Within it, there was the ascending of the Fire Element, the stripping of the Metal Element, the bearing of the Earth Element, the washing of the Water Element, and the generating and repairing of the Wood Element.

The Five Elements rotated endlessly between each strike of the hammer and each inhalation of the censer.

Ultimately, his Five Elements realm was the most profound. Ning Zhuo used his Five Elements realm to understand it, and then drew upon his Yang Dao realm, Alchemy realm, and Artifact Forging realm, seeing deeper and deeper the more he watched.

Ning Zhuo's mind gradually became clear.

He took the initiative to speak up: "Senior, on the west side of the furnace bottom, the pill toxin is entangled with Senior Zhong's pill qi. I will separate the pill qi; you just need to extract the turbid toxins."

A look of surprise flashed in Tie Kuang's eyes: "You can separate them?"

Ning Zhuo didn't say much; he just pointed his fingertip.

His Alchemy Dao had already reached the Master level; his ability to distinguish medicinal properties, pill qi, and pill toxins far exceeded his past self. Looking at it now, he knew that the pill qi was like a spring breeze, while the toxic qi was like sunken mud. Although they were similar in color, their natures were vastly different.

He blended his spiritual energy with his divine sense and gave a gentle flick.

The warm, thick pill qi was drawn to the side, exposing the grayish-purple pill toxin.

Tie Kuang did not hesitate; the black clay smoke censer inhaled violently, sucking away that burst of toxic qi.

"Well done!" Tie Kuang couldn't help but cheer out loud.

This praise came completely from the bottom of his heart.

For a Foundation Establishment cultivator to intervene in such a situation was already astonishing. The fact that Ning Zhuo could also accurately separate the pill qi from the pill toxin - this level of insight, divine sense manipulation, and Alchemy Dao foundation truly exceeded Tie Kuang's estimates.

In the matter of repairing the Naming Fire Furnace, Ning Zhuo was one of the only two cores. He occupied the most critical position, and if he could play a greater role, Tie Kuang would be able to save a lot of energy.

The toxin extraction lasted for a full three shichen (six hours).

By the end, the black clay smoke censer was almost completely stuffed with various toxic smokes, and all nine holes on its body emanated a dark purple halo. The Naming Fire Furnace, however, appeared much lighter. The dark red luster of the furnace wall became purer, and some originally gloomy blemishes revealed their true hollows.

Once the toxins were removed, the furnace body's weakness was exposed.

Sweat rolled down Ning Zhuo's forehead and along his cheeks. Without bothering to wipe it, he focused solely on sensing the changes in the furnace body, involuntarily revealing a look of joy.

Tie Kuang shot him a glance: "This kid's Alchemy Dao realm is quite profound; he can distinguish pill toxins and sort out pill qi. Just how old is he? How did he cultivate?"

"Could it be that he absorbed the medicinal power of the Pure Yang Ancestor Pill, which caused him to advance by leaps and bounds in pill refining?"

Tie Kuang had also contributed his strength to mold the strange domain meant to capture Ning Zhuo, so he knew exactly what Ning Zhuo had been doing over the past few days.

It was just that he couldn't clearly distinguish exactly how much of the Pure Yang Ancestor Pill Ning Zhuo had actually absorbed.

Tie Kuang put away the black clay smoke censer. His voice was hoarse and laced with exhaustion: "The next steps will be much easier to handle."

Three segments of Crimson Paulownia Root floated up.

Tie Kuang pressed his five fingers together like a knife, splitting the Crimson Paulownia Roots into nine root veins. The root veins were crimson and supple, with rosy light flowing within them, as if the vitality of the fire paulownia was slowly breathing.

"Ning Zhuo, try to communicate with the artifact spirit. Tell it not to resist," Tie Kuang said.

Ning Zhuo placed his palm against the furnace wall, and the Spirit Communication Mirror shone downwards.

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit stood in the warm cloud nest, weak and agitated, but it remained under Ning Zhuo's control and cooperated.

The formation patterns at the bottom of the furnace lit up.

That was the most complete foundation remaining of the Naming Fire Furnace. The formation patterns were archaic, resembling a Vermilion Bird wrapping its wings to form a nest, and also like a volcano slumbering at the bottom of the furnace.
