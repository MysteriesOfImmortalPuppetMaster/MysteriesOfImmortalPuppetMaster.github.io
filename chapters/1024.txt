Chapter 1024: Continuous Enlistment

Someone recognized this person and whispered, "It's him?"

"The cold-faced male cultivator with the 'Cold Marrow Dawn' innate talent from the Danxia Peak minor trial."

"I remember he didn't gain the approval of the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit."

"Cold Marrow Dawn, huh. Adapting to cold and warmth with ease, simultaneously cultivating dual methods. This innate talent is very rare!"

The murmurs of discussion floated gently in the hall before quickly dying down.

The male cultivator arrived in the center of the hall, stopped, and cupped his hands in a bow. "Xie Hanchu greets Young Master Ning Zhuo."

Ning Zhuo's gaze fell upon him, his eyes lighting up slightly.

Of course, he remembered Xie Hanchu.

During the Danxia Peak minor trial, Xie Hanchu had displayed the innate talent [Cold Marrow Dawn]. Cold Marrow Dawn was Yin in nature but contained Yang, cold in essence but harbored warmth. It could harmonize conflicting cultivation arts, allowing a cultivator to adapt to cold and warmth and practice dual methods simultaneously. Such innate talent could already be considered excellent for an ordinary cultivator.

It was just that the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit had not chosen him at the time.

In Ning Zhuo's eyes, not being chosen by a sentient treasure didn't mean this person was useless.

"Daoist Friend Xie," Ning Zhuo nodded slightly, his tone gentle. "Have you come to join the Stronghold?"

Xie Hanchu raised his eyes.

His pupil color was rather light, like grayish-blue water beneath ice. When he looked at Ning Zhuo, a faintly imperceptible ripple seemed to stir within that layer of coldness.

"Yes," Xie Hanchu said. "I wish to join the Naming Stronghold."

The crowd in the hall instantly grew a bit quieter.

Xie Hanchu paused, then added, "During the Danxia Peak minor trial, I failed to gain the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit's approval and had no destiny with the Naming Fire Furnace. But now that the Naming Fire Furnace has been repaired by you, Young Master, and the Naming Stronghold has occupied the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, I have pondered this for a long time. I feel that if I want to establish a foothold in the Myriad Manifestations Sect, the Naming Stronghold is the most suitable place for me."

He spoke steadily, without flattery, without eagerness, and without any deliberately feigned heroic sentiment. It was as if he was simply stating the conclusion of his internal deliberations.

Ning Zhuo, however, heard the underlying meaning.

Xie Hanchu wasn't here just for the temporary excitement. He had seen the perfectly repaired Naming Fire Furnace, and he saw the structure and future of the Naming Stronghold.

The smile in Ning Zhuo's eyes deepened. "Daoist Friend Xie is quite candid."

Xie Hanchu lowered his head. "In front of a gentleman of utmost sincerity like you, Young Master, what need is there to beat around the bush?"

Ning Zhuo couldn't help but chuckle.

"Good," Ning Zhuo said. "Since the Naming Stronghold is broadly accepting new members, we won't only accept those who just add icing to the cake. Xie Hanchu, you possess the Cold Marrow Dawn innate talent, can adapt to cold and warmth, and can harmonize the conflicting principles of water and fire, Yin and Yang. Even if the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit didn't choose you in the past, I, the new master of the fire furnace, represent the Naming Stronghold in welcoming you to our ranks!"

Xie Hanchu bowed once more. "Thank you for taking me in, Young Master."

Ning Zhuo waved his hand. "It's not taking you in; it's joining the stronghold. Once you join, act according to the rules. Merits will be rewarded, and transgressions will be punished."

Xie Hanchu replied, "Understood."

Ning Zhuo looked Xie Hanchu up and down, feeling genuine happiness in his heart.

What did the Naming Stronghold lack the most right now?

It lacked people.

It lacked useful people!

The proactive joining of a cultivator like Xie Hanchu, who had special innate talent and a calm disposition, showed that the Naming Stronghold had successfully opened up its situation.

The most crucial point was that Xie Hanchu's appearance at this very moment also sent a strong political signal to the outside world!

The Naming Stronghold was truly opening its doors broadly to new members, no longer restricted only to those in debt. At the same time, it possessed a strong appeal to minor geniuses like Xie Hanchu!

This was what Ning Zhuo was most pleased to see.

-----

Around noon.

A stocky young man walked into the reception hall.

He had a broad chest and a thick waist, a solid back and shoulders, and his skin was dark and shiny like a piece of black iron freshly drawn from a furnace. He had a square face, thick eyebrows, and full lips, with a somewhat constrained expression. On his back, he still carried a square iron shield covered in many tiny scratches, clearly showing it saw year-round use and wasn't just for show.

This was Zhang Dadan.

The moment he walked in and saw the numerous cultivators in the hall, he immediately felt the pressure. His footsteps faltered, and his thick, dark fingers subconsciously gripped the strap of his iron shield.

His hands were large, with hard, thick knuckles and heavy calluses on his palms, evidently from year-round carrying and forging heavy mechanisms.

"Lord... Ning Zhuo," Zhang Dadan spoke in a muffled, booming voice, feeling extremely anxious in his heart.

When Ning Zhuo first arrived at the main sect of the Myriad Manifestations Sect, he had once hosted a banquet for him, and they had interacted. But soon, Ning Zhuo's fame skyrocketed and he climbed higher step by step, leaving Zhang Dadan with no choice but to look up to him.

Not long ago, during the incident with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, Ning Zhuo fell into a massive predicament. Huo Nian'er proactively went to seek refuge and was taken under Ning Zhuo's command.

Now, the Naming Stronghold had seized the territory of Flowing Cloud Peak, the Naming Fire Furnace was repaired, and Huo Nian'er was still working under Ning Zhuo. This left Zhang Dadan feeling simultaneously regretful and fiercely envious!

"I am merely a Foundation Establishment cultivator. I didn't seek refuge with him before, so if I go now... what will Lord Ning Zhuo think? Will he take me in?" He had tossed and turned in hesitation for a long time before finally stiffening his neck and deciding to come formally join the Naming Stronghold.

At this moment, he stood in front of everyone, facing Ning Zhuo.

Ning Zhuo stood up abruptly and proactively took two steps forward. "Hahaha, this is my friend Zhang Dadan! Have you also come to join my stronghold?"

Zhang Dadan froze in place, allowing Ning Zhuo to walk right up to him and grab his arm.

This intimate and enthusiastic attitude instantly made Zhang Dadan's eyes redden, and an infinite warmth surged in his heart.

During that small banquet in the bluestone cave manor in the past, Ning Zhuo had also approached them like this - amiable, natural, and without the slightest air of a young noble. And he was still the same today!

But Ning Zhuo was already different now.

Out of all the Rising Cloud Minor Trials he participated in, he almost always took first place. His fame grew greater and greater. He fought Ban Ji, seized inheritances, established the Naming Stronghold, repaired the Naming Fire Furnace... Every single event, every single achievement, made the sense of distance Zhang Dadan felt towards Ning Zhuo grow a bit heavier in his heart.

Zhang Dadan's eyes blurred.

He scratched his head, messing up his coarse, stiff hair. His voice was hoarse as he said softly, "Forgive me, Young Master. I came late."

Ning Zhuo laughed heartily and forcefully patted Zhang Dadan's back. "You and I are already friends, what's this about being late? The Naming Stronghold is broadly accepting newcomers today, and you coming today, Dadan, is right on time!"

Zhang Dadan felt that the harder Ning Zhuo patted him, the more comfortable he felt in his heart, as if it could smack away the pent-up frustration in his chest.

Ning Zhuo returned to the main seat and publicly announced, "Zhang Dadan joins the Naming Stronghold today."

No one voiced any objections.

Zhang Dadan's heart burned with zeal. He bowed his head and saluted, "I will definitely do my best!"

Ning Zhuo smiled faintly. "The Naming Stronghold is newly established, so there isn't so much red tape. Dadan, just cultivate and act according to the rules, that will be enough."

Zhang Dadan nodded heavily. "Understood."

Ning Zhuo tilted his head back and let out a hearty laugh. He was happy from the bottom of his heart.

Zhang Dadan's joining was also altering the image of the Naming Stronghold. The stronghold's entry threshold was undergoing a drastic shift; it wasn't just taking in geniuses like Xie Hanchu, but could also accommodate ordinary Foundation Establishment cultivators like Zhang Dadan.

A faction couldn't just rely on geniuses to hold up its facade.

Geniuses were like blades that could break through stalemates. However, the ones who truly stabilized a foundation were often the countless minor figures like Zhang Dadan, who were steadfast and hard-working.

The time came to the afternoon.

More cultivators proactively came to join the Naming Stronghold.

In the reception hall, an old man walked in slowly.

He was tall and thin with a slightly hunched back, and his grayish-white hair and bushy beard hung down to his chest. His face was tanned red and black from wind and snow, and the wrinkles at the corners of his eyes were as deep as crevices in a snowy mountain. He wore a thick, old leather jacket, the hem of which was still dotted with unmelted frost and snow.

The most eye-catching thing was the stone cauldron he carried on his back.

The stone cauldron had three legs and was carved from ten-thousand-year bluestone. Its body was mottled, displaying pale white textures worn down by wind and frost. Faint white steam rose from the cauldron's mouth. If one listened closely, a bubbling sound could be heard, as if something hard was still being boiled inside, emitting muffled clinking sounds as it bumped against the cauldron's walls.

Someone nearby whispered, "Stone-Boiling Elder?"

"He came too?"

"This old man's approach in the Alchemy Path is very unorthodox. He uses metals and stones as medicine, boiling stones to form pills. It's said he boiled stones on the snowy mountain for forty years."

"Does that even count as pill refining?"

"Why wouldn't it? His Stone Marrow Pills and Iron Bone Pills have a decent reputation. It's just that the orthodox alchemists of Danxia Peak don't think much of his path."

Stone-Boiling Elder ignored the discussions of those around him.

Carrying the stone cauldron on his back, he walked to the center of the hall and cupped his hands toward Ning Zhuo.

This bow was somewhat awkward because the stone cauldron on his back was too heavy. When his shoulders moved, the cauldron emitted a bubbling sound, as if speaking on his behalf.

"This old man is Stone-Boiling Elder, willing to join the Naming Stronghold," he said.

As Ning Zhuo observed, he communicated with others via divine sense to obtain information on Stone-Boiling Elder.

Ning Zhuo said, "Senior uses metals and stones as medicine and cultivates the Stone Boiling Sutra?"

Stone-Boiling Elder: "Yes."

Ning Zhuo smiled and commented, "Metals and stones enter the furnace, using snow water as liquid, cold wind as firewood, and heart fire for warmth. Stone Marrow Pills strengthen tendons and bones, Jade Liquid Pills nourish the skin and retain youth, and Iron Bone Pills can make bones as hard as iron and stone. Although this method is unorthodox, it is very interesting."

"Young Master Ning," Stone-Boiling Elder's voice lowered a bit. "This old man has refined pills for forty years and dare not claim any great mastery. It's just that my path requires borrowing the fires of heaven and earth. Earth fire, earth flame, cold wind, snow water, and stellar phenomena can all enter the furnace. Hearing that the Naming Fire Furnace had been repaired, and that the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit is slumbering to nourish it, this old man thought that if I could secure a tiny stone stove in the Naming Stronghold and refine some bone-and-tendon strengthening pills for the stronghold, it would not be a waste of what I have learned in my life."

He spoke plainly, but when mentioning the Naming Fire Furnace and the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit, the desire in his eyes could not be hidden.

Ning Zhuo nodded. "Since Senior is willing to come, the Naming Stronghold naturally welcomes you."

Not long after, an old woman carrying an iron pot walked into the hall.

She was short in stature with grizzled hair combed neatly. Her face had many wrinkles but was very clean, and her gaze was gentle with a hint of shrewdness. She wore a faded, dark-brown apron, its edges folded squarely, looking as if she had just stepped out of an apothecary and hadn't yet had the time to properly change.

She carried that iron pot with both hands.

The bottom of the pot was thick, the walls were smooth, and the handles were polished to a shine. There was no fire in the pot, but a layer of thick, dark, and shiny medicinal residue clung heavily to the bottom, emitting a rich aroma of a hundred boiled medicines.

"Ointment-Boiling Granny," someone recognized her.

"She's here too?"

"She's an expert at treating external injuries. I've used her Ten Thousand Cure Ointment once, and it's truly effective!"

She arrived in the center of the hall and set down the iron pot.

When the iron pot hit the ground, it let out a soft clang.

The sound was not crisp, but very thick and heavy.

Ointment-Boiling Granny bowed to Ning Zhuo. "This old woman, Ointment-Boiling Granny, greets Young Master Ning Zhuo."

Ning Zhuo returned the bow. "Senior is too polite."

"This old woman has boiled ointments my whole life, treating bruises, cold aches, rheumatism, and sores for a lifetime," Ointment-Boiling Granny said slowly. "When the Naming Fire Furnace was choosing its master, I also went to take a look. Without destiny, I returned to my shop. I thought I would have no affinity with this furnace for the rest of my life. I didn't expect that you, Young Master, would repair it and establish the Naming Stronghold."
