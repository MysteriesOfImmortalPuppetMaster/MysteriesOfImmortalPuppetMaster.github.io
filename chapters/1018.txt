
Chapter 690: Shi Qian vs Tan Zhu
"Fellow Daoist Shi Qian," Tan Zhu said softly. "It's late in the night, are you still helping Heavy Formation Peak clean up its old messes?"
Shi Qian's heart sank. Tan Zhu actually called out his name!
He immediately knew that his operation tonight had been leaked.
"Tan Zhu," Shi Qian slowly stood up, the Mountain-Sinking Formation Plate still clutched in his palm. "You shouldn't be here either."
Tan Zhu smiled. "I am now a member of the Nanming Stronghold. What's wrong with helping to inspect our new territory?"
A cold glint flashed in Shi Qian's eyes.
"Step aside," Shi Qian said in a low voice. "You can pretend you saw nothing."
A final trace of luck remained in his heart, and he had to make one last effort.
Tan Zhu had already discovered him; if he interfered, Shi Qian's mission would inevitably fail!
Tan Zhu shook his head gently. "Once I've seen it, it's hard to pretend I haven't. The eyes are funny things; once they remember the light, pretending to be blind becomes very painful."
Shi Qian didn't waste any more words.
With a flip of his right palm, the Mountain-Sinking Formation Plate was pressed against the ground.
Earth Element Spell - Hidden Peak Cavity Lock!
Thud.
A muffled sound echoed from beneath the ground, and the entire small cavity abruptly constricted. The rock walls on all four sides seemed to be pushed by invisible giant hands, squeezing toward Tan Zhu.
For a moment, dark yellow earth light surged up layer by layer, carrying the heavy momentum of a toppling mountain. The air was compressed until it let out dull explosive pops, and the fire patterns on the four walls were distorted from the pressure.
This place was originally a hidden trump card laid down by Heavy Formation Peak. Here, Shi Qian held the geographical advantage!
Tan Zhu flicked his sleeve.
A pitch-black little pouch slid out from it.
This pouch was the size of a palm, its surface resembling animal hide and dead leaves. Seven tiny bone buttons were sewn along its edge. It was named the [Seven-Fold Hidden Poison Pouch]. It could store seven types of toxins, each capable of being nurtured independently without conflicting in battle.
Tan Zhu flicked his finger, and the first bone button of the poison pouch snapped open.
In an instant, his spiritual energy was rapidly consumed.
Poison Technique - Dry Well Drifting Miasma!
A grayish-green poisonous mist rolled out of the poison pouch. Instead of drifting everywhere, it rose like well water, rapidly spreading close to the ground. As soon as the poisonous mist touched the earth light of the Hidden Peak Cavity Lock, it made a soft sizzling sound. The heavy earth light was corroded into tiny holes, like a fresh mud wall pierced by rain.
Shi Qian furrowed his brow.
He couldn't truly go all out.
This place was too small, and it was deep within the earth veins of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. If he really bombarded it with his Nascent Soul spiritual energy, the commotion would definitely be exposed. The Myriad Manifestations Sect rules forbade infighting, especially secret battles among fellow disciples that damaged the sect's spiritual lands. Whoever blew the matter up would be held accountable.
Tan Zhu was also restraining himself.
His poison techniques could originally spread for miles as soon as they were unleashed, turning the underground into a poisonous swamp. At this moment, he only released a thin layer of poisonous mist that flowed along the cavity without any intention of breaking through the rock walls.
Both of them knew in their hearts—this was not a life-and-death struggle.
Shi Qian pressed the five fingers of his left hand against the ground, and the Earth-Listening Seal in his palm suddenly lit up.
Earth Element Spell - Earth Vein Fastening Sound!
Six earth-yellow ripples spread from beneath his palm like six invisible iron rings, linking one after another to bind Tan Zhu's ankles, back of his knees, waist, shoulders, and throat. As the ripples tightened, there was no sharp killing intent, only a heavy sluggishness, as if trying to drag a person back into the earth.
Tan Zhu's figure paused.
Yet, there was no panic in his eyes.
"Good earth spell," he praised softly as the second bone button of the Seven-Fold Hidden Poison Pouch opened.
Poison Technique - Floating Life Phantom Smoke!
This time, the poisonous smoke was a pale white, thin as morning mist. It didn't corrode the earth rings; instead, it seeped into the ground through the gaps in the earth rings. In the next moment, Tan Zhu's aura split into five, and five shadows of Tan Zhu unexpectedly appeared in the cavity simultaneously.
Shi Qian was stunned.
The Earth Vein Fastening Sound missed; it didn't bind Tan Zhu's true body.
The next moment, the five shadows surrounded Shi Qian.
Shi Qian knew things were turning bad and stomped his right foot fiercely.
Earth Element Spell - Mountain Suppressing Echo!
Boom.
A low, muffled mountain rumble echoed underground.
This rumble was extremely short, like a giant striking a drum through thick earth. The five shadows of Tan Zhu swayed slightly at the same time. Four of them dissipated like smoke, leaving only the one on the left with its sleeve fluttering, a wisp of blue-black poisonous gas shaken out of it.
"Found you." A fierce look flashed in Shi Qian's eyes.
He stepped forward, boldly closing the distance. His five fingers pressed together like a shovel, and a brass-like light appeared on the edge of his palm as he slashed toward Tan Zhu's chest.
Martial Art - Hill Severing Palm!
The palm light was heavy, without any dazzling sharpness, yet it carried a blunt and fierce momentum capable of slicing a hill in half. The air let out a muffled hum, and the natural spiritual energy in the cavity was pressed down by the palm wind.
Tan Zhu didn't meet it head-on.
He pinched a Purple Nightmare Bone-Corroding Needle between two fingers and gently tapped the needle's tip against the palm light.
Poison Technique - One Needle Inquiring the Bone!
Ding!
The thin needle tapped against the Hill Severing Palm, producing a crisp, light sound. A dark green poisonous light crawled up the edge of the palm like a tiny venomous snake, its speed astonishingly fast.
Shi Qian's right arm went numb.
He made a prompt decision. With a shake of his shoulder, the surface layer of his entire right arm instantly turned to mud. The earthen skin that the poisonous light had crawled over fell off with a patter, turning into a puddle of blackened mud upon hitting the ground.
At this, both men paused for half a breath.
Shi Qian felt both shock and anger.
Tan Zhu's poison was too insidious!
If his reaction had been a hair slower just now, the poisonous light would have drilled into his true body's bone marrow. If that happened, even though he was a Nascent Soul cultivator, a portion of his energy would be tied down.
A trace of solemnity also appeared in Tan Zhu's eyes.
As the person sent by Dong Chen, Shi Qian was indeed no ordinary earth cultivator. This person's spells were heavy but not clumsy; his reactions were fast, and he was willing to shed skin and abandon armor. Trying to take him down without making a big scene would be extremely difficult.
"Tan Zhu," Shi Qian lowered his voice. "If you and I keep fighting here, neither of us will benefit. What do you want?"
Tan Zhu looked at the three formation nails by his feet. "I want those."
Shi Qian's face darkened completely. "You dare?"
Tan Zhu's smile deepened. "Then come and stop me."
Killing intent surged in Shi Qian's heart.
At this moment, the illusion of luck in his heart vanished completely.
The Mountain-Sinking Formation Plate suddenly flipped over, and the mountain patterns on its surface all lit up. At the same time, the Earth-Listening Talisman in his palm synchronized, urging a dark yellow spiritual light to press down on the top of the cavity like an inverted small mountain.
Earth Element Talisman Spell - Mountain-Sinking Inverted Seal!
Crack, crack, crack.
The entire cavity was sealed by a layer of earth-yellow light shield. The inside and outside of the shield were isolated, and even the sound of the fire veins was suppressed. Shi Qian knew in his heart that Tan Zhu's combat strength was formidable, and he was nearing the end of his lifespan, but carrying a heavy responsibility, he had to try to resolve Tan Zhu in an extremely short time before finishing his cover-up mission!
Tan Zhu looked up at the spiritual light resembling an inverted small mountain, the smile on his face fading.
He knew Shi Qian was getting serious.
The third bone button of the Seven-Fold Hidden Poison Pouch opened.
Poison Technique - Myriad Orifice Cold Sting!
This time, there was no thick fog, no poisonous light.
Only a very faint sound of buzzing insects.
Buzz, buzz, buzz.
The sound drilled in right against the eardrums, like countless tiny insects flapping their wings. Shi Qian's pupils shrank, and he immediately sealed his ears and the periphery of his divine sea. However, the insect buzz didn't come from the outside; it actually sounded from the puddle of black mud he had just shed.
That puddle of black mud exploded, transforming into hundreds of blue-black poisonous motes.
Every speck of poisonous light was as fine as a needle tip, specifically seeking out the gaps in the flow of earth element spiritual energy to drill into.
Shi Qian let out a low roar, heavily clapping his palms together.
Earth Element - Deep Earth Orifice-less Body!
The skin all over his body instantly turned dark yellow, and layers of rock patterns surfaced beneath his flesh. When the poisonous motes hit his body, they made fine crackling sounds, like raindrops falling on roof tiles. Even so, more than a dozen poisonous motes still managed to drill into his body by following the folds of skin at the corners of his eyes, between his fingers, and on his neck.
Shi Qian let out a muffled groan, a trace of black blood spilling from the corner of his mouth.
He suppressed the toxicity and fiercely smashed his right knee against the ground.
Earth Element - Earth Prison!
Rumble.
Three earth walls flipped up from the ground. The first blocked the poisonous mist, the second squeezed toward Tan Zhu, and the third directly smashed toward the formation nails.
This was originally an enemy-trapping method, but in Shi Qian's hands, it could substitute offense for defense, excelling in both.
Tan Zhu's expression changed.
He immediately realized: Shi Qian's attempt had failed, and he wanted to destroy the evidence!
Tan Zhu's figure flashed as a short staff slid out from his sleeve.
The short staff was only half a foot long and pitch-black all over, its head inlaid with a purple-gray poison bead. Inside the bead, tiny smoke shadows tumbled, as if a poison cloud was sealed within.
Ink Nightmare Poison Staff!
It could compress a poison technique into a heavy strike. When unleashed, it didn't seek area of effect, only penetration, often capable of piercing through a Nascent Soul cultivator's protective spiritual energy.
Tan Zhu gripped the staff and pointed.
Poison Technique - Ink Nightmare Mountain Piercing Curse!
Whoosh!
As the poison staff thrust out, the purple-gray poisonous light condensed into a sharp cone and crashed into the third earth wall. The surface of the earth wall first dimmed, then purple-black cracks surged from within. The cracks spread rapidly, and the entire earth wall collapsed with a crash, as if hollowed out by poisonous insects.
Tan Zhu's pupils shrank slightly, surprised by the earth wall's power.
According to his combat experience, the poisonous spike should have penetrated the earth wall and threatened Shi Qian. However, due to the geographical advantage, the earth wall's defense had skyrocketed, causing Tan Zhu's move to slightly miscalculate.
At this time, the combat environment for the two of them was different from their previous contest in the martial arena.
The space in the martial arena was vast. A cultivator's every move and stance had more time to brew and accumulate, often resulting in wide, sweeping attacks of majestic scale.
But now, confined to a narrow space, the two were fighting fast with fast, the pace of the battle incredibly swift.
Because Tan Zhu slightly misjudged just now, he instantly lost his rhythm.
Seizing this instant, Shi Qian threw the Mountain-Sinking Formation Plate from his hand.
Earth Element - Moving Mountains!
The earth plate spun, bringing up rings of dark yellow halo light that covered the three formation nails. Once the halo light fell, the traces of the old formation nails would be distorted. Even if they were destroyed later, it would be difficult to trace the original spellcaster's aura.
The method of Moving Mountains was thought to be grand and sweeping, yet Shi Qian could apply it to subtle and intricate details. From this single move alone, one could see his profound mastery of Earth Element Spells.
Tan Zhu coldly snorted, and the fourth bone button of the Seven-Fold Hidden Poison Pouch opened.
Poison Technique - Nasal Reversal Breath Stealer!
A wisp of colorless and odorless poisonous gas shot out, heading straight for Shi Qian's face.
In an instant, all the hairs on Shi Qian's body stood on end.
He had no choice but to withdraw half of his spiritual energy to protect his face.
It was exactly this half-measure of retreat that caused an extremely slight pause in the rotation of the Mountain-Sinking Formation Plate.
In Tan Zhu's eyes, this was an obvious opening!
In the next moment, Tan Zhu launched a fierce attack.
Shi Qian struggled to parry left and right, secretly exclaiming, "Not good!" Under the fierce assault, he lost his footing, and the light shield maintained by the Mountain-Sinking Inverted Seal suddenly developed a crack.
The outside world was already constructing a formation to unify all the power of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. Suddenly detecting a gap, the compressed power immediately found a new place to pour into.
In an instant, the roaring sound of the fire veins surged in.
"Hm?!" Chunyangzi's pupils suddenly shrank. "There really is an enemy!"
