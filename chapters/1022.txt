Chapter 1022: The Youth Returns to the Stronghold

The Grand Formation over the entire fengshui treasure land had successfully closed.

The radiance of the Three Luminaries Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Grand Formation shrouded the surroundings. Crimson-gold formation light pierced through the clouds, illuminating the altar grounds brightly and warmly. However, upon careful inspection, the light in the southwest corner was a shade dimmer than elsewhere, like a tiny hole picked by a needle on a piece of gorgeous brocade.

Many cultivators had gathered here early.

Among them were former subordinates of Chunyang Peak, helpers sent by Hundred Herbs Peak, the Shen Clan, the Lin Clan, and the Situ Clan, as well as Formation Masters whom Chunyangzi had spent a heavy sum to hire.

Although the news from last night had been suppressed by Chunyangzi's orders, the unexpected shaking of the formation base could not be concealed.

Many people still felt anxious and uneasy in their hearts.

They had just won the five-win martial contest and had only just acquired the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, yet a formation base tremor had already occurred. Chunyangzi's heavy injuries were unhealed, Tan Zhu came and went with a gloomy demeanor, and the combat power of the Nine Fires Dragon Lord left people feeling uncertain.

The Nanming Stronghold was, after all, a newborn faction, giving people the impression of having a shallow foundation.

"I heard a corner of the southwest formation platform directly collapsed."

"Don't spout nonsense, it was just the formation base shaking."

"Lord Chunyangzi personally suppressed the earth veins last night. I heard he coughed up blood again because of it."

"What should we do? Nanming Stronghold has just established its footing; could something be going wrong again?"

Facing sudden changes, people's hearts inevitably wavered.

At this moment, a youth arrived, riding the clouds.

Today, the youth was wearing the Great Thousand Mechanism Melody Garment. The long robes swayed gently in the morning wind, and faint mechanical sounds came from the cuffs. The tinkling sounds were like the wind passing through spirit bamboo in an empty valley.

"It's Ning Zhuo."

"Ning Zhuo has returned?"

"How is it? Has the Naming Fire Furnace been repaired?"

Ning Zhuo descended from his flying cloud and landed in front of the crowd. He deliberately chose a higher vantage point to directly display the Naming Fire Furnace!

The repaired Naming Fire Furnace was dark crimson all over. Its walls were warm and thick, with fine, dense Vermilion Bird feather patterns naturally spreading across its surface. Its handles were like slightly opened bird wings, and its legs were like crimson paulownia roots, firmly rooted in mid-air.

Hovering by Ning Zhuo's side, its aura was a seamless whole.

Gone was the scorched and dilapidated state of the former broken furnace, nor could any messy traces of repairs left by various parties be seen.

Nine fires, pure yang, blood path essence, residual pill qi, and silver traces of starlight had all been refined into a single entity. Those marks were hidden deep within the vermilion feather patterns, submitting to the furnace body itself, like a hundred rivers returning to the sea.

The crowd was first quiet, then broke into an uproar.

"The Naming Fire Furnace!"

"It's fixed, it's really fixed!"

"This aura... it's so complete!"

"Hall Master Tie Kuang personally took action, and Young Master Ning Zhuo also participated. It's truly extraordinary."

"With the furnace here, there's hope for our debts!"

Many people's eyes instantly lit up.

They had gathered here in the first place because of the debts, benefits, and future pill quotas associated with the Naming Fire Furnace. Chunyangzi had won the five-win martial contest and snatched territory for the Nanming Stronghold. But the true root of the Nanming Stronghold remained the Naming Fire Furnace.

Ning Zhuo smiled. He had seized the initiative, well aware that nothing could break the ice and boost morale better than displaying the Naming Fire Furnace!

The morning light fell upon him as the big-headed youth cupped his hands in a bow. "Seniors and fellow Daoists, Ning Zhuo has arrived late."

His voice wasn't loud, but it spread through a spell, falling clearly into everyone's ears.

"The Naming Fire Furnace has undergone a major repair led by Hall Master Tie Kuang of the Artifact Refining Hall. Now, the furnace body is restored, its fire veins are connected, and its artifact spirit is stable. However, after such major repairs, the furnace spirit needs to be nourished and warmed. For the time being, we cannot rush to open the furnace."

The crowd nodded one after another.

"It's only right, only right. One needs to rest and recover after a severe illness, let alone a spirit treasure."

"As long as the furnace body is intact, what does it matter if we wait a little while?"

Shen Xi stood on the right side of the crowd.

He wore a long robe with blue and white formation patterns. Exhaustion from last night left faint dark circles under his eyes. Seeing Ning Zhuo arrive with the furnace, his brow finally relaxed a fraction as he muttered, "He came right on time."

Lin Jinglong stood beneath a newly sprouted Azure Dragon Spirit Tree, the cuffs of his cyan clothes still stained with dirt. Looking at the Naming Fire Furnace, admiration surfaced in his eyes. "This is the true bearing of a spirit treasure."

Situ Xing stood to the side with his arms crossed, the star-patterned jade pendant at his waist swaying gently. He looked at Ning Zhuo, the corners of his mouth lifting slightly. "This guy..."

The crowd's low morale quickly rebounded, as if illuminated by the warm light of the Naming Fire Furnace.

The setback at the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar had suppressed their morale, but the restoration of the Naming Fire Furnace was a shot in the arm. The crowd had something to look forward to again.

Ning Zhuo briefly exchanged a few words with the crowd before taking the initiative to excuse himself, walking deep into the fengshui treasure land.

Next, it was time for him to meet Chunyangzi.

Deep within the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, many new rooms had been built, all in the style of the Chunyang Palace.

This reception hall was no exception.

Concise fire patterns were carved into the crimson-gold pillars. Twelve Sun Crystal Lanterns hung from the ceiling; their light wasn't glaring, but warm and clear. The floor was paved with black-red stone slabs, within which fine golden threads meandered, slowly drawing the heat of the underground fire veins into the hall.

Chunyangzi sat in the seat of honor.

He wore a crimson-gold Daoist robe and looked very neatly groomed. The bloodstain left by The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook on his glabella was obscured by a faint golden light, and his complexion was baked into a rosy hue by his pure yang spiritual energy. Judging solely by his appearance, he still maintained the mighty demeanor of a sect master.

Ning Zhuo stepped forward and bowed. "Greetings, Senior Chunyangzi."

Chunyangzi smiled faintly. "Ning Zhuo, you've come."

He smiled mildly, yet scrutiny was hidden deep in his eyes.

Ning Zhuo smiled respectfully, his expression holding genuine concern. "This junior heard that the formation base shook last night, and Senior personally suppressed it, aggravating your old injuries. How are Senior's injuries now?"

Chunyangzi waved his hand. "It's no matter. Just a few minor injuries; a few days of rest will suffice."

He said it lightly.

But in his heart, he gave a bitter smile.

A few minor injuries?

If they were truly minor injuries, how could it be that he didn't even dare to fully activate his Pure Yang Immaculate Body? The hook intent of The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook was still lodged in his glabella, Liang Ming's Yin Earth backlash remained deep in his organs, and last night, he had forcefully used his spiritual energy to suppress the earth veins. If it were an ordinary Nascent Soul cultivator, they would have long since gone into death seclusion.

Ning Zhuo's expression remained unchanged, as if he believed it. "Since Senior is unharmed, everyone from top to bottom in the Nanming Stronghold can rest assured."

Chunyangzi's heart stirred slightly.

Ning Zhuo knew how to speak; this sentence was pleasant to the ear.

On the surface, Ning Zhuo was showing concern, but in reality, he was giving him face as the Grand Stronghold Leader.

Chunyangzi looked at the Naming Fire Furnace behind Ning Zhuo. "How is the furnace?"

Ning Zhuo stepped sideways, displaying the Naming Fire Furnace once again.

Under the hall's lighting, the dark crimson furnace body glowed with a warm luster, its vermilion feather patterns slightly expanding. The furnace emitted no restless leaks at all; there was only a steadfastness that came from having its foundation freshly reshaped.

A flash of surprise passed through Chunyangzi's eyes.

He understood alchemy, and he understood the Pure Yang Fire Dao. With just a glance, he knew that the degree of restoration of this furnace was extremely high. Tie Kuang was truly worthy of being the Hall Master of the Artifact Refining Hall, and Ning Zhuo was actually able to handle this matter to such an extent.

Ning Zhuo said, "The furnace body is restored, and more importantly, the pill toxins have been cleared. The fire veins were also thoroughly connected by Senior Tie Kuang. It's just that the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit is currently slumbering and requires a long period of nourishment. In the short term, we cannot officially start the furnace for alchemy."

Chunyangzi's brow twitched imperceptibly.

Unable to perform alchemy in the short term.

For him, this was actually good news!

Once the Naming Fire Furnace started, Ning Zhuo's prestige would instantly skyrocket. The eyes of all the creditors would shift from the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar to the Naming Fire Furnace. Now that the furnace needed nourishment, Ning Zhuo would be unable to immediately overshadow him through alchemy.

The tension and vigilance in Chunyangzi's heart quietly lowered a fraction.

Ning Zhuo added, "This junior brought the furnace to make a public appearance today because I worried that last night's formation base tremor would cause the hearts of those in the stronghold to waver. Therefore, I deliberately let everyone know that the Naming Fire Furnace has been repaired and the Nanming Stronghold's foundation is secure."

Chunyangzi nodded, his expression softening a bit. "You're very thoughtful, kid."

Ning Zhuo was indeed reliable!

Without needing instructions, he was able to plug the loophole in their morale. With the complex state of minds in the Nanming Stronghold nowadays, while Chunyangzi was healing, he truly needed a sensible person like this to stabilize the situation.

But Ning Zhuo was also undoubtedly his greatest political rival within the Nanming Stronghold.

Chunyangzi's mood was somewhat complicated.

On one hand, he was glad that Ning Zhuo didn't seem to have discovered the true secret of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. He had inspected the residual qi from last night, and it lacked Ning Zhuo's personal aura. The person who escaped had evasive movement techniques and was not a subordinate by Ning Zhuo's side. The possibility of Ning Zhuo knowing couldn't be entirely ruled out, but in Chunyangzi's eyes, the probability was not high.

On the other hand, he had to admit that right now, Ning Zhuo was very important to the Nanming Stronghold!

Chunyangzi picked up his teacup. The tea was a crimson-gold color, brimming with yang qi, and had a warm, rich fragrance. He took a sip, suppressing the dull pain in his chest, and asked, "When do you plan to fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect?"

Ning Zhuo cupped his hands. "This is all thanks to you, Senior. Winning both of your battles, you achieved final victory on behalf of the Nanming Stronghold and seized this fengshui treasure land, the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar."

Chunyangzi's eyes flickered slightly.

Ning Zhuo continued, "It will be far easier for this junior to deal with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect now. This is all riding on your coattails, Senior."

Chunyangzi instantly understood and nodded slowly.

With Ning Zhuo expressing his stance like this, he felt reassured.

"To me right now, this is also a benefit," Chunyangzi thought to himself.

Next, he had to heal his injuries, shifting from a high profile to a low profile. The Nanming Stronghold needed someone to hold down the fort on the outside.

Tan Zhu was an internal traitor and was ruled out first!

The Nine Fires Dragon Lord was decent at alchemy but of little use in battle, and politically, he had the natural shortcoming of being a demonic dragon.

The Red-Robed Guest was already dead; only a remnant soul remained, and it was still in the hands of the enemy.

Tu Yuanzi was almost universally acknowledged to be in Ning Zhuo's faction.

Calculating everything, the invisible leader of the Nanming Stronghold was inevitably Ning Zhuo.

Handing things over to Ning Zhuo made Chunyangzi highly vigilant, but at the same time, he felt reassured.

Thinking of this, a block formed in his heart.

"Since it's no longer difficult for Ning Zhuo to deal with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, why did I hand over the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill in the first place?"

That was a supreme inheritance treasure of the Chunyang Palace!

And in the end, it was completely sucked dry by this brat.

The moment Chunyangzi thought of this, his state of mind inevitably fluctuated. The fingers gripping the teacup tightened slightly, sending a circle of fine ripples across the tea's surface.

He stared intently at Ning Zhuo. "The medicinal efficacy of the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill has already been completely absorbed by you. What do you plan to do about this matter?"

Apology surfaced on Ning Zhuo's face as he stood up and bowed. "This junior is ashamed to face Senior over this matter. The loss of the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill, this junior will definitely repay!"

Chunyangzi looked at him, remaining silent.

Ning Zhuo continued, "It's just that the battle against the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect has yet to be completed. This junior wishes to brazenly ask Senior for help - I wonder if I could temporarily borrow the Yang Fish?"

Chunyangzi let out a cold sneer in his heart: "Little fox!"

"You've devoured the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill, yet you still want to borrow the Yang Fish."
