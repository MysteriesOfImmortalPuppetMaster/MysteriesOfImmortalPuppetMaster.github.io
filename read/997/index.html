Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 997: The Curse of Knowledge

"Why won't it work?"

Ning Zhuo directed his divine sense toward that Pill Sun.

The Pure Yang Patriarch Pill still radiated a vast and majestic light.

Its true intent remained frighteningly abundant.

Within the Pill Sun, Ning Zhuo could also see the countless fragmented insights left behind by past Sect Masters. There was the joy of a young Sect Master practicing Qi Refining while facing the morning sun; the majestic righteous qi that rose in the heart of a Sect Master in his prime after slaying a ghostly creature; and the silent reluctance of an aged Sect Master returning his life's accumulation of Pure Yang true intent back into the pill as he neared the end of his days.

These true intents were laid out before Ning Zhuo, like a mountain full of spirit fruits bending the branches under their weight.

They were already delivered to his lips, but he could neither bite nor swallow them!

Ning Zhuo let out a muffled groan, fine beads of sweat seeping from his forehead. His hands were pressed against his knees, his knuckles turning slightly white from the force.

"Could it be that my comprehension is insufficient, so I cannot grasp it?"

As soon as this thought emerged, it echoed within his divine sea.

Ning Zhuo fell into vexation. "Stuck here, I can't continue to absorb the pill's true intent. And before long, the battle between Chunyangzi and Liang Ming will reach its conclusion."

Although Ning Zhuo was detained, he was not isolated from outside information. On the contrary, he maintained a high level of attention to the outside world, especially the fifth battle.

At this very moment, the battle between Chunyangzi and Liang Ming had already lasted for six days and five nights, and both sides were showing obvious signs of exhaustion.

Anyone could tell that this battle wouldn't last much longer. At most, in another day and night, a victor would emerge.

"No matter the outcome of this battle, Chunyangzi will definitely ask for the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill back."

Ning Zhuo put himself in Chunyangzi's shoes. If he were him, even if he wanted to lend it out again, he would definitely take it back first to check the progress of the pill's absorption.

Once Chunyangzi finished the fifth battle, the struggle over the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar would definitely come to an end.

No matter how much Chunyangzi supported Ning Zhuo in fighting the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, he wouldn't joke around with the foundation of his own sect.

In fact, the very fact that Chunyangzi continued to lend him the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill after reclaiming the Yang Fish - right after Ning Zhuo had been "apprehended" - already made Ning Zhuo feel incredibly lucky.

"I've already absorbed a massive amount of true intent, and I don't have the ability to reverse-pour my cultivation realm back into it to restore its medicinal efficacy. For someone like Chunyangzi, the moment he takes the pill back and takes one look at it, he will never give me a second chance!"

Ning Zhuo was adept at reading people; he knew how difficult Chunyangzi could be and clearly understood his current predicament.

His original plan was to use the time Chunyangzi was fighting to absorb the true intent with all his might, pushing his own realm up drastically. But upon execution, the plan encountered a massive obstacle.

"I've never encountered this kind of situation when absorbing true intent before."

"Could it be that Senior Chunyangzi tampered with the pill?"

Just as this thought arose, Ning Zhuo shook his head.

Another possibility made him even more uneasy.

"I haven't cultivated the Pure Yang Pill Scripture."

"This inheritance pill was originally meant to be used by the Sect Masters of the Pure Yang Palace."

"Past Sect Masters all cultivated the Pure Yang Pill Scripture. Their spiritual energy, Physical Body, Divine Soul, and state of mind were all highly compatible with the true intent within the pill."

"Although my Primordial Yang is extremely abundant, my virgin body is pure, and my Yang Qi is overflowing, my foundation is ultimately not of the Pure Yang lineage."

"Could the critical issue simply be that our Cultivation Arts are incompatible?"

Frustration gradually rose in Ning Zhuo's heart, soon affecting his ability to absorb the true intent and boost his realm.

He simply opened his eyes and terminated his comprehension session.

Ning Zhuo gently tapped his fingers against his knees, his brows tightly furrowed as he fell into deep thought.

Right at this moment, a magnificent strand of divine thought descended: "Little Friend Ning Zhuo, hehehe, I know of your current vexation. The true intent is clearly right at your lips, yet you can neither eat nor swallow it. Is that right?"

Ning Zhuo blanked for a moment, not expecting that Wutu Prison-Suppressing True Lord was paying attention to him.

However, on second thought, the big-headed youth felt it made sense: "This region's foundation is the Divine Domain of Wutu Prison-Suppressing True Lord. Furthermore, I successfully used the Divine Summoning Art long ago and invited Wutu Prison-Suppressing True Lord to become my protector god."

Ning Zhuo immediately responded with his divine sense, "Exalted God, that is exactly the case. I humbly ask for your guidance."

The magnificent divine thought arrived once more: "Hehehe. Little Friend Ning Zhuo, you know of the School of Mind among Confucian cultivators. This is a new path capable of revolutionizing the entire landscape of Confucian cultivators! But have you ever wondered why the School of Mind originated from an up-and-coming talent like Wang Xinyue, yet failed to be born from the hands of those Great Scholars with profound foundations?"

Ning Zhuo's gaze flickered.

Wang Xinyue.

Mind is principle, the unity of knowledge and action, realizing innate knowing.

Logically speaking, Great Scholars among Confucian cultivators possessed deeper accumulations, thicker scholarly learning, higher Literary Palaces, had read more classics, and had seen more of the world. In terms of creating a new school of thought, they should have been much more qualified.

Yet, the School of Mind came from such a young newcomer.

Ning Zhuo began to understand in his heart.

Wutu Prison-Suppressing True Lord continued, "Those Great Scholars have walked too deeply down their old paths. Their past learning has become the very eyes through which they view everything in the world. How is a new path carved out?"

"It is carved out by questioning step by step, by measuring step by step."

"But for these Great Scholars, every step they take lands on the old path."

"The old path is simply too easy to walk."

Wutu Prison-Suppressing True Lord's words were steeped in profound meaning.

Ning Zhuo understood. "Wang Xinyue didn't have the heavy burden of old academic teachings, so she could open a path from her own mind and thoughts. Lacking the entanglement of many old rules, she could instead point directly to her true heart."

Wutu Prison-Suppressing True Lord: "You are a teachable child. This is what is known as 'The Curse of Knowledge'."

Ning Zhuo's heart jolted.

Wutu Prison-Suppressing True Lord: "The more you know, the more you will carry your old experiences with you to understand new things - this is both an innate gift and an acquired shackle."

"You have studied the Five Elements. When you see 'Yang,' you subconsciously observe it through the eyes of the Five Elements. You have studied the Formation Path. When you see 'Yang,' you subconsciously scrutinize it with the gaze of a formation core, thinking about how it lays out a formation, how it builds momentum, and how it flows. You have studied Mechanisms. When you see 'Yang,' you wonder how to integrate it into an artifact, how to drive it, and how it can serve as a mechanism's spiritual intuition."

"You look at 'Yang' carrying so many old experiences that 'Yang' is no longer pure 'Yang' in your eyes - it becomes a subset of the Five Elements, a variant of the Formation Path, fuel for a Mechanism. You can always see the shadows of the old in the new. This allows you to draw on others' strengths and integrate everything comprehensively, but it also means that every time you face an entirely new realm, you first pull out the rulers and compasses from your old box to measure it, rather than first using an empty and clear mind to experience its original form."

"'Yang' is simply 'Yang'. It is not confined by the mutual generation and restriction of the Five Elements, not limited by the flow of the Formation Path, and not enslaved by the drive of a Mechanism. It possesses its own independent nature - a nature like the rising morning sun, which relies on no past experiences, yet can permeate all things, becoming another origin outside the Five Elements, another meridian network outside the Formation Path, and another vitality outside Mechanisms."

"But you cannot comprehend this."

"You have already been obstructed by your own achievements."

It was as if a bolt of lightning flashed across the lake of Ning Zhuo's mind.

He was right.

Previously, by relying on his profound realms in the Five Elements and other paths, he had understood the Dao of Yang by analogy, and the effects had been excellent.

But this was like wanting to see the scenery of another mountain while standing still on his original mountain.

Gazing from his original mountain, he naturally stood high and saw far, but there was a limit to this. When he wanted to see more, he had to truly set foot on the other mountain.

But Ning Zhuo hadn't done that.

He carried his past achievements to comprehend the Dao of Yang. Under such circumstances, his achievements became his prejudices.

Wutu Prison-Suppressing True Lord: "The further a Cultivator walks down a certain path, the easier it is for them to be shaped by that path. If you walk extremely deeply into the Fire Element, you will view everything through fire. If you walk extremely far down the Formation Path, you will treat heaven and earth as a formation diagram. The advantage is that all things can be used by you. The disadvantage is that only the side you are familiar with remains of all things."

"Past generations of Chunyangzi all cultivated the Pure Yang Pill Scripture. They all walked onto a pure, extreme path. Therefore, if they wished to change, it would be extremely difficult."

"Because of this, there is also a very crucial phrase in cultivation."

"Forgetting the Dao!"

Ning Zhuo's eyes flashed with a sharp light as he muttered, "Attain the Dao, and then... forget the Dao."

At this moment, he deeply understood a principle - for whatever is gained, a price must be paid! Hidden on the flip side of a strength is a massive weakness. On the flip side of a tremendous harvest lies an equally tremendous, yet invisible, cost.

"Thank you, Exalted God, for clearing my doubts!" Ning Zhuo expressed his sincere gratitude.

Wutu Prison-Suppressing True Lord laughed heartily. Knowing he had successfully handed out a massive favor and achieved his goal, he said no more.

Ning Zhuo fell into deep thought.

After understanding the root cause, he immediately knew how to break the deadlock.

Dao-Bearing Jade Pages!

"Dao-Bearing Jade Pages can temporarily carry my realms for me. If I can move a portion of my Five Elements, Formation Path, and Mechanism realms out, I'll be able to absorb the true intent of the Dao of Yang in a much purer state."

The next moment, the light in his eyes sank again.

There weren't enough Dao-Bearing Jade Pages.

The few pages Ning Zhuo had on hand couldn't hold this much true intent.

Ning Zhuo could also create Dao-Bearing Jade Pages himself; he could even ask the Confucian cultivator community to help him make them.

But...

Time waited for no man!

Chunyangzi and Liang Ming had already been fighting for six days and five nights. This battle could end at almost any moment.

"Taking ten thousand steps back, even if Chunyangzi falls in battle, given his personality, it's impossible that he hasn't made posthumous arrangements in advance."

"It is almost certain that he has already assigned personnel. If he sacrifices himself, someone from Pure Yang Peak will definitely come over to demand the return of the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill!"

Anxiety surged in Ning Zhuo's heart once again.

What should he do?

Was he just going to watch helplessly as this monumental opportunity slipped through his fingers?

Ning Zhuo was unwilling to accept this.

He knew all too well just how precious true intent was!

"Wait a minute." His eyes suddenly lit up. A flash of inspiration hit him, and he thought of another way to solve the problem.

"Perhaps, this method will work!"

Cradle of Myriad Phenomena!

This Technique revolved around the Early Wisdom Innate Talent. It allowed the caster to experience all things in the world as if they were a newborn infant.

Once the things before them felt like they were being encountered for the very first time, they would naturally gain more inspiration and insight.

Treating heaven and earth as my cradle, with all things and matters serving as tools of enlightenment - this was the Cradle of Myriad Phenomena!

Kong Ran had a lot of authority to speak on this subject. He often used it to read Confucian classics, benefiting every time by gaining entirely new impressions from a completely new angle.
