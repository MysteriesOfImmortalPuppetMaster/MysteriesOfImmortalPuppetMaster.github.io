Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 1012: Shentu Wuwei

Ning Zhuo's mind stirred slightly along with it.

An incredibly exquisite method of controlling flying swords seeped into his divine sea along the river water of the memories.

Shentu Wuwei nourished his sword, released his sword, retracted his sword, and concealed his sword.

After Light Illuminating Courage left his hand, it did not just fly blindly at high speed. Instead, it circled, paused, sank, flicked, folded, returned, and hid. Like a fine blade, with a light slice, it could cut open the weak points of an enemy's protective spiritual light.

Ning Zhuo's breathing slowed down.

He saw Shentu Wuwei practicing his sword day after day.

In the early morning, he practiced sword light.

At noon, he practiced sword speed.

At dusk, he practiced returning the blade.

Late at night, he practiced controlling the sword through the air.

He practiced relentlessly, taking almost no days off all year round, drowning in his obsession!

At first, the courtyard was filled with small metal bells. The flying sword would weave through the bells; the bells would not ring, and the thread would not break.

Later, the small bells were replaced with candle flames. The flying sword passed through the fire, and only a sliver of the flame was sliced off.

Even later, the candles were replaced with strands of living human hair hanging in mid-air. When Light Illuminating Courage swept past, it only severed the white hairs, leaving the black ones untouched.

The meticulousness of his flying sword control left Ning Zhuo in awe.

"Pill refinement, artifact refinement, formation setup, talisman creation, mechanism arts, and so on - all emphasize precision. But the precision of a flying sword has a different flavor. It is not the interlocking of mechanical structures, nor the rising and falling of fire control and pill energy, but rather elements like thought, spiritual energy, distance, speed, and sharpness."

With a slight deviation in thought, the sword would be half an inch too fast.

With a slight impurity in spiritual energy, the blade would be delayed by a breath.

Shentu Wuwei's sword was very thin.

So thin that it resembled a single line of white light.

It was also sharp to the extreme.

Shentu Wuwei had already formed his Golden Core.

He was pale and beardless, his face often bearing a gentle smile. Even for his enemies, he would personally brew tea. As the tea fragrance curled upward, his white sleeves with gold trim brushing across the table, he would gently ask the other party, "Sir, are you here to throw your life away today?"

As his combat experience continuously accumulated, Shentu Wuwei had a realization: his primary sword, Light Illuminating Courage, was overly sharp but weak in defense. Thus, he conceptualized a defensive measure.

His defense was still a flying sword!

His secondary sword, Golden White Blade, was of a crimson-gold hue, short and thick. Its flying speed was not as agile as Light Illuminating Courage, but it excelled at defense.

It often hovered three feet by Shentu Wuwei's side like a golden latch, specifically blocking hidden weapons, magic treasures, and sneak attacks. Light Illuminating Courage killed enemies externally, while Golden White Blade guarded his body internally.

One for offense, one for defense.

One white, one gold.

Ning Zhuo felt his heart burn as he watched. The lifebound sword focused on slaughter, while the secondary sword focused on defense. His divine sense split into two strands, and his spiritual energy divided into two paths. It looked like two swords flying simultaneously, but in reality, it was an orderly alternation of offense and defense, advancing and retreating.

The scene in the memory suddenly darkened.

A massive fire ignited in the mountain villa.

In the night, corpses lay strewn across the white jade steps. Servants, guards, and disciples - many died without a sound. The faint white light of the Life-Illuminating White Lantern shone across the courtyard. Everyone who tried to flee found their footsteps sluggish, their Divine Souls seemingly dragged down by muddy water.

The Dead Spring Jade Urn tipped over, and grayish-white spring water flooded the ground, washing away the formation runes until they were fragmented.

Xiang Zican, clad in the Three Disasters Body-Locking Armor, carried the Life-Weeping Long Spear slung over his shoulder, crushing the "White Blade Accounting Manor" plaque underfoot with a single step.

Shentu Wuwei stood in the firelight. His white robe was still pristine, save for a few spots of blood on his cuffs.

He still wore a smile on his face.

That smile was terrifyingly gentle.

"This debt, I, Shentu, will remember."

Xiang Zican grinned, his bloodstained teeth eerily white. "Remember? It's good that you remember. After you die and go to the Underworld, perhaps you can file a complaint with the gods of the Underworld. Hahaha!"

Before his voice had even faded, Light Illuminating Courage vanished from its spot.

The flying sword transformed into a thin, snow-white line.

Fast.

Too fast.

It first bypassed the blade of the Life-Weeping Long Spear, sliding up along Xiang Zican's pauldron. Avoiding the thickest part of the Three Disasters Body-Locking Armor, it drilled straight through the gap between the armor plates.

Squelch.

A line of white light pierced out from the back of Xiang Zican's neck.

A head flew into the air.

Blood spurted wildly.

Xiang Zican's body remained standing in place, his hands still maintaining their grip on the spear. For a brief moment, the courtyard's blazing firelight was suppressed by the arc of blood trailing his severed head.

Shentu Wuwei's eyes held not a shred of joy.

Golden White Blade had already returned to defend, hovering three inches in front of his brow.

The next moment, the dim white lantern-shaped mole between Xiang Hundeng's eyebrows suddenly lit up, and the flame within the Life-Illuminating White Lantern flickered. Before the flying head could even hit the ground, grayish-white vapor surged from Xiang Zican's severed neck.

Xiang Sishui unsealed the Dead Spring Jade Urn.

The grayish-white spring water wrapped around Xiang Zican's severed neck like a living creature.

A bloody line split open across Xiang Hundeng's chest.

A tiny slit also appeared on the side of Xiang Sishui's neck.

The three shared life and death.

The injuries were distributed among them.

Vitality was infused.

Xiang Zican's head was pulled back to his neck by an invisible force. With a crack, the bones locked back together, and his flesh and blood wriggled like melting wax.

He rolled his neck, and his sinister laughter rang out once more.

"Again!"

The chill in Shentu Wuwei's eyes deepened.

Light Illuminating Courage vanished for the second time.

This time, the flying sword didn't slash toward Xiang Zican; instead, it went straight for Xiang Hundeng.

Xiang Hundeng's eye sockets were sunken like deep wells, and the Life-Illuminating White Lantern hovered before him. The lantern light illuminated countless soul shadows, attempting to drag down Light Illuminating Courage's trajectory.

Light Illuminating Courage suddenly split into seven white shadows.

Sword Light Differentiation!

Six of them were illusions. The true blade swept close to the shadows on the ground. In the very next breath, it flipped up from the cracks in the stone beneath Xiang Hundeng's feet, pierced his lower jaw, and penetrated his divine sea.

The Life-Illuminating White Lantern violently dimmed.

Xiang Hundeng's eyes rolled back, and the lantern mole between his eyebrows cracked open.

His Divine Soul was heavily injured!

Ning Zhuo watched with a jolt to his heart. "Good sword!"

The flying sword entering the divine sea from the lower jaw - an angle so tricky it sent chills down one's spine.

But in the next moment, Xiang Sishui gave a low shout.

The spring water in the Dead Spring Jade Urn swept upwards, pouring over Xiang Hundeng's head.

Blood simultaneously seeped from the corners of Xiang Zican's eyes and his ear canals, sharing the Divine Soul trauma on behalf of Xiang Hundeng.

Xiang Hundeng's chest heaved violently. He held his jaw wound with one hand and lifted the lantern with the other, its flame lighting up once more.

"What a handsome sword technique," he spoke indistinctly, his voice sounding as if it were squeezed through blood. "Pity, it still can't kill me."

Shentu Wuwei remained expressionless, his heart sinking to rock bottom as he attempted to strike at Xiang Sishui.

Light Illuminating Courage flew into mid-air, its blade suddenly shining as bright as day. Carrying a piercing, clear whistle, it seemed to suck all the light of the entire battlefield into its edge before instantly turning and plunging downward.

Sword Light Turning into a Rainbow!

This rainbow trajectory could only be formed when a flying sword's speed, sharpness, spiritual energy purity, and divine sense guidance all reached an absolute peak.

Like a streak of white rainbow piercing into the grayish-white spring water, Light Illuminating Courage actually sliced right through the spring currents above the Dead Spring Jade Urn.

Xiang Sishui's expression drastically changed.

The flying sword passed through the spring water, piercing straight into the location of his Qi Sea. If this sword landed solidly, it would surely shatter his spiritual energy foundation.

However, Xiang Zican did not retreat; instead, he advanced.

He threw his body directly against Light Illuminating Courage.

The blade pierced his chest.

Straight through his heart.

Xiang Zican tightly grabbed the blade with both hands. The flesh and blood on his palms were sliced and curled outward, exposing white bone. Laughing wildly, he used his own flesh and blood to lock Light Illuminating Courage in place for half a breath.

Half a breath was enough.

Xiang Hundeng's lantern light shone over.

Shentu Wuwei's divine sense slightly stagnated.

Xiang Sishui retreated three steps, his Qi Sea injury shared among them through their shared life and death. The grayish-white spring water rejoined, and the Dead Spring Jade Urn let out a dull hum.

A moment later, Shentu Wuwei was already covered in wounds. His brows stood on end, revealing a profound resentment.

Severing the head. Piercing the divine sea. Breaking the Qi Sea.

Within the memory, Ning Zhuo felt Shentu Wuwei's emotions.

It was an extremely deep resentment.

He had repeatedly struck their vitals. Had it been any other Nascent Soul cultivator, they would have been dead and buried by now.

But the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect stubbornly survived time and time again. Injuries were shared, even death could be dragged back, and all his battle achievements were entirely wiped out!

Shentu Wuwei was heavily injured. His white robes fluttered in the wind, the gold trim of his collar catching the blinding glare of the firelight.

Finally, with a wave of his hand, Golden White Blade flew in front of him, while Light Illuminating Courage hovered above his head.

The two flying swords, one white and one gold, revolved around his body. His figure gradually became blurred.

Man and Sword as One!

The man was the sword, and the sword was the man. Shentu Wuwei completely dissolved into a sword escape art.

A white rainbow abruptly rose!

Together with Light Illuminating Courage, he instantly pierced through Xiang Zican's chest.

In the next breath, the white rainbow turned back and sliced through Xiang Hundeng's shoulder and neck.

In the third breath, the white rainbow slanted down, cutting closely past the edge of the Dead Spring Jade Urn and leaving a deep slash upon it.

Within three breaths, all three Valiants were simultaneously severely wounded.

A gaping hole opened in Xiang Zican's chest, his heart shattered into a bloody mist.

Half of Xiang Hundeng's shoulder slid off, and the Life-Illuminating White Lantern almost slipped from his grasp.

The Dead Spring Jade Urn at Xiang Sishui's waist cracked open, spraying grayish-white spring water all over the ground.

The blazing firelight filling the courtyard was carved to pieces by the sword rainbow, leaving crisscrossing, fine white sword marks on the stone slabs of the ground. Those sword marks were exceptionally narrow and so deep that the bottom could not be seen.

The 'Shared Life and Death' was still operating.

The trio's vitality, soul power, spiritual energy, and injuries were like three ouroboros serpents, intertwining with and consuming one another.

Very quickly, the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect recovered.

Shentu Wuwei fully realized that this battle could no longer continue.

He coughed up a mouthful of blood. The blood landed on his white robe, like a red plum blossom blooming in the snow.

Light Illuminating Courage abruptly shot higher.

Golden White Blade followed closely behind.

The two swords, one in front and one in back, tore through the suppression of the Life-Illuminating White Lantern. Shentu Wuwei's entire being transformed into a pale sword rainbow, dragging his heavily wounded body as he pierced the air and fled far away.

Xiang Zican roared furiously and hurled the Life-Weeping Long Spear. The long spear shot through the clouds and grazed past the tail end of the sword rainbow, tearing open half of Shentu Wuwei's waist and abdomen.

Shentu Wuwei didn't look back. His sword escape flew faster and faster. The battlefield behind him rapidly shrank in the memory.

The river of memory flowed into its final segment.

Inside a hidden chamber forged of metal.

Shentu Wuwei sat on the ground, his white robe already soaked in blood. A small tip of Light Illuminating Courage was broken off, and Golden White Blade was covered in cracks. His face was deathly pale, yet the corner of his mouth still carried a hint of a smile.

The Life-Illuminating White Lantern had injured his Divine Soul, the Dead Spring Jade Urn had tainted his spiritual energy, and the Life-Weeping Long Spear had shattered his Physical Body's foundation. His mid-stage Nascent Soul cultivation could not save him; his lifelong foundations could only delay the inevitable.

He stretched out his slender fingers and slowly took out a rusted, black long spike embryo.

Ning Zhuo saw that Shentu Wuwei's hands were very steady.

So steady that he didn't seem like a dying man.

Screams echoed in the hidden chamber.

Cold sweat rolled down Shentu Wuwei's forehead, and his fair skin twitched, but his eyes shone with a terrifying brightness.

"Shared Life and Death..."

"Good! Then I will forge a Life-Claiming Spike specifically to break your Divine Art!"

As he refined it, he coughed up blood. The blood fell into the furnace fire, turning the flames a ghostly white.

His Soul was torn apart, the pain so intense that Shentu Wuwei's vision went black.

Soon, Shentu Wuwei's head dropped powerlessly. His Physical Body had withered away, and his Nascent Soul was hopelessly shattered.

Ultimately, he sank completely into darkness.

Ning Zhuo's heart stirred.

"Judging from the memory, this spike was indeed aimed at the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. It is highly likely that the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect obtained this magic treasure afterward and turned the scheme to their own advantage."

"The battle prowess of the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect is too exaggerated. Taking the initiative to take a step back was the correct choice!"
