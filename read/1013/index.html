Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 1013: Rest and Reorganization

The long river of memories surged with a thunderous roar!

All the sword cultivation experience of Shentu Wuwei's entire life was absorbed by Ning Zhuo.

How divine sense tracked the flying sword when it left the hand.

How spiritual energy was compressed when the sword's speed increased.

How the blade slightly trembled right before the sword light transformed into a rainbow.

What the exact most secure distance was when the secondary sword protected its master.

What it felt like when a Sword Cultivator suffered a backlash from damage to their lifebound sword.

How to first align one's breathing with the sword's resonance before achieving unity between man and sword.

How to use the sword's edge to pierce through the void when executing sword evasion, how to hide the physical body within the sword's trailing momentum, and how to protect the divine sea from being torn apart while traveling as a streak of light.

These experiences and insights were detailed down to the most minute and trivial levels!

The Innate Talent "True Self" allowed Ning Zhuo to firmly guard his ego.

"I have my own Dao Path."

"No matter how sharp Shentu Wuwei's sword path is, it can only be used by me!"

Inside the stone chamber, Ning Zhuo slowly opened his eyes.

The Three Valiants' Life-Claiming Nail lay in his palm. Its rusty black hue had dimmed considerably, and large cracks had appeared on the soul crystal at its tail end. The transparent human figure inside had disappeared, leaving only a few faint white-gold sword marks wandering deep within the soul crystal.

Ning Zhuo let out a breath of turbid qi.

When this breath was exhaled, it surprisingly carried an extremely subtle hum of a sword.

He raised his right hand and gently tapped his fingertip.

A wisp of spiritual energy flew from his fingertip, condensing into an extremely faint white line in midair. The moment the white line appeared, it rapidly elongated, faintly revealing a sharp intent before quickly dissipating.

Ning Zhuo blinked.

"Just a little bit off."

He was not disappointed; instead, his eyes lit up.

Shentu Wuwei's lifelong experience with flying swords was indeed of top quality!

From the basics of flying swords to the nourishment of lifebound swords, from secondary sword defense to long-range slaughter, and to the unity of man and sword as well as sword evasion for escaping, almost everything was solidly grounded without any superficiality. They were like a treasure trove of metal and iron, forcibly cracked open by the Boat-Burning Soul Crossing Art and poured out before Ning Zhuo.

Originally, Ning Zhuo could only be considered to have vaguely heard of the sword path.

Now, things were different.

He had truly touched the threshold of the sword path and was only one step away from fully mastering it.

Sword light division, sword light transforming into a rainbow, the unity of man and sword - these classic technical achievements of the sword path. Ning Zhuo felt that if he properly digested these memories, he might be able to achieve at least one of them.

Of course, Ning Zhuo also knew.

These were only "skills."

His sword path realm did not advance by leaps and bounds because of this.

Shentu Wuwei's experience was a bridge, a boat, a path walked by someone else. Ning Zhuo could borrow this path to move forward, but he could not treat someone else's sword heart as his own.

He looked down at the Three Valiants' Life-Claiming Nail in his palm.

The soul crystal was already empty!

With the backlash restriction lacking the crucial support of the Nascent Soul's remnant soul, the power of this item was greatly reduced, and its danger was also mostly eliminated.

Ning Zhuo put this magic treasure away again.

"Previously, I made a contract with the masked cultivator to use this against the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. My absorption of the Nascent Soul soul is also to deal with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, so there's no conflict."

Ning Zhuo took out the sword embryo gifted by Situ Xing.

The sword embryo lay quietly in his palm. It was no more than a foot long and had not yet been truly sharpened, emitting a cold starlight throughout. Its body was forged from meteoric refined iron and its inside had been tempered by starlight for a thousand days. On the surface, it looked black with traces of silver, but upon closer inspection, one could see extremely faint starlight spots floating up and down, as if the night sky had been sealed deep within the iron.

The sword embryo had not yet become a proper sword.

It lacked long-term nourishment from an owner, lacked the daily permeation of spiritual energy, and also lacked that aura of mutual familiarity and synergy between a Sword Cultivator and their sword.

Ning Zhuo held the sword embryo in his palm as his divine sense gently swept over it.

Inside the sword embryo, the starlight iron was steady and delicate, proving it to be of excellent quality. If a Sword Cultivator were to nourish it day and night with their own spiritual energy, they would undoubtedly cultivate a highly satisfying flying sword in a few months at the shortest, or several years at the longest. If accompanied by sword arts, sword sutras, and heart-blood refinement, it would have even more room for growth in the future.

"It is indeed a good item," Ning Zhuo said softly.

If it were an ordinary cultivator who had obtained such a sword embryo and happened to acquire Shentu Wuwei's sword path experience, they would probably be ecstatic. They would immediately begin to nourish it, washing the sword with their spiritual energy every day, and sorting out the sword paths with their divine sense, wishing they could plant their entire heart into the sword embryo.

But after looking at it for a moment, Ning Zhuo placed the sword embryo back into its box.

"It's a pity, I lack time too much."

To properly utilize the sword embryo requires slow and meticulous effort. Nourishing a flying sword cannot be rushed. Three or five days will yield no results, and a week or two will only bring a slight sense of closeness. To raise a sword embryo to the point where it can truly kill enemies on the battlefield, it must undergo a long period of synchronization.

Although this process does not require entering secluded death-meditation, it must be continuous every day, permeated with one's mind and spirit.

For Ning Zhuo, it was still too slow.

What he lacked the most right now was precisely this kind of leeway to take things slowly.

"If I really want to quickly enhance my combat strength in the sword path, it would be better to purchase a finished flying sword."

A hint of a bitter smile appeared at the corners of Ning Zhuo's mouth.

He had clearly just obtained a Nascent Soul Sword Cultivator's lifelong experience and had the sword embryo gifted by Situ Xing in hand, yet he still had to temporarily set this path aside.

A conventional genius cultivator would build around their foundation, gradually refining their expertise. They would carefully select a sword path fortuitous encounter, and then delve deeply into that sword path. If they were lucky enough to obtain a precious flying sword material, they would grow alongside the flying sword. A few years later, their sword techniques, flying sword, and Cultivation Art would mutually complement each other, ultimately forming a dignified and complete system.

Ning Zhuo, however, was different.

Calamity and luck, the situation, favors, debts, mechanisms, spiritual treasures, fire furnaces, Naming Stronghold - countless factors were pushing him forward together.

Not just walking, but sprinting, racing madly!

Today he had to learn the Yang Path, tomorrow he had to repair the fire furnace, the day after he had to deal with Chunyangzi, and a few days later he had to consider the battle with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. Throughout the entire process, he had to maneuver between Danxia Peak, Chongzhen Peak, Flowing Cloud Peak, the Artifact Refining Hall, the Demon Subjugation Hall, and more...

Many fortuitous encounters fell into his hands, and before he even had time to chew them over carefully, he first had to determine whether they provided immediate combat strength, whether they held political value, and whether they had the potential to be instantly converted into situational benefits.

Sometimes, it was out of absolute necessity, forced by the situation. Other times, it was the pursuit of maximizing benefits.

"If I reference the arts of divination, my Fate Attribute is probably far from ordinary. For the previous dozen or so years, I was cooped up in Fire Persimmon Immortal City, painstakingly accumulating."

"With the incident of the Lava Immortal Palace, I entered a new stage of life, and my entire Fate Attribute revealed its sharp edge."

"Looking back on my journey since leaving the mountain, how many fortuitous encounters have practically rushed at my face along the way? How many dangers have brought me to the brink of life and death time and time again?"

"Divination, oh divination. If only I could make some gains in this memory."

His mother, Meng Yaoyin, had shown him just how powerful this cultivation art was.

Ning Zhuo yearned for it in his heart.

The big-headed youth resealed the sword embryo, carefully affixed a maintenance talisman to it, and only then stored it deep within his storage bag.

Ning Zhuo flipped his palm, and the Heavy Armored Blood Ape - Dasheng appeared in the room.

When its heavy mechanical body landed, the floor of the stone chamber even slightly trembled.

Yuan Dasheng was still encased in a layer of ice.

The outer layer of ice carried the aura of Snow-Colored Maiden Hui; the ice's color was clear and crystalline, containing threads of gentle vitality within.

It was not merely freezing, but slowly nourishing Yuan Dasheng's spiritual energy.

However, looking deeper inside, the ice within Yuan Dasheng's body was completely different.

That ice's color was darker and heavier, faintly carrying a profound, cold yin intent. It entrenched itself along the mechanical joints, internal bones, runes, and spiritual energy connections like many coiled and tangled ice snakes, tightly biting onto Yuan Dasheng's mechanical body without letting go.

Ning Zhuo raised his hand and pressed his five fingers against the outside of the ice layer.

Grandmaster-level Yang Path realm!

Although it was Five Elements spiritual energy, the lighting of the entire stone chamber still brightened slightly.

A Yang Path Grandmaster's view of Yang was that they already understood Yang was manifestation, as well as judgment. The reason Yang was Yang did not primarily lie in burning all things, but in using oneself as kindling - to illuminate, drive, and stimulate; to make obscure things reveal themselves, to awaken slumbering things, and to bring a certain answer into the light.

A ring of pale golden halo emerged from Ning Zhuo's palm, its light gentle and warm, like the rising morning sun falling on the surface of the ice.

The ice layer made a soft sound.

Crack.

A wisp of white mist rose up.

The vitality ice layer on Yuan Dasheng's surface was pulled by the sunlight, melting slightly for a fraction, but it quickly repaired itself. The deep yin ice, however, did not loosen at all; instead, several strands of cold yin aura shrank inward along the mechanical cracks.

Ning Zhuo immediately withdrew his hand.

"As expected, I still can't rush this."

He stared at Yuan Dasheng, the light in his eyes steady.

The current him was different from before.

With the realm of a Yang Path Grandmaster upon him, he was already capable of using Yang Path methods to probe out more details.

Whether the spiritual energy reacted when the nourishment method made contact.

Whether the ice layer would damage the internal mechanical bones when the filth-breaking Yang applied pressure.

Exactly what runes, what nodes, and what mechanical crevices the yin-attribute power hid in when the manifesting Yang sprinkled down.

As long as he could probe out some results, he would be able to use them to further his strategy.

Ning Zhuo understood Yang, but he did not understand Yin enough.

Yuan Dasheng being trapped by the yin-attribute power inside his body was like a wall. Possessing the Grandmaster realm of the Yang Path, Ning Zhuo could reach out from the other side, knock on the wall, and listen to determine what was hollow and what was solid behind it. With multiple attempts, he could deduce the flaws and perhaps break through this wall to rescue Yuan Dasheng from within.

"If I had the realm of the Yin Path, perhaps I could start from the root."

This would mean that Ning Zhuo would understand the wall itself very well and could coordinate from both the inside and outside.

"If my Yin-Yang realm is high enough, I could also resolve it from the direction of Yin-Yang transformation and mutual rooting and hiding."

In that case, Ning Zhuo could transform Yin into Yang, turning the wall itself for his own use. Or he could transform Yang into Yin, infiltrating into the wall body and disintegrating it from the inside.

Thinking of this, Ning Zhuo could not help but smile bitterly.

"I am being a bit too greedy here."

For conventional cultivators to raise their realms, how could it be as easy as it is for him?

Realm is the degree to which one masters principles.

At the very beginning is the Apprentice. They learn basic theories, recognize materials, tools, runes, and spell arts, and can do some of the simplest auxiliary tasks. They know how things are, but not yet why they are.

Further up is the Artisan. They master core fundamental skills, can independently complete standard procedures, have a preliminary understanding of the principles, and can stably produce qualified products. The vast majority of skilled practitioners in the Cultivation World are at this level.

Further up is the Renowned Artisan. Their skills are proficient; they can handle complex problems, optimize processes, and impart knowledge to disciples. Reaching this level, a cultivator will gradually form an intuitive feel for the craft. Their finished products will carry a personal style, and they can perform some clever and ingenious tricks.

Further up is the Master. Their skills rise to the level of art, breaking away from superficial craftsmanship. Their finished products possess a unique temperament and carry Dao rhythm in their application. A cultivator at this level will develop intuition and can significantly innovate within the original framework, improving pill formulas, artifact blueprints, formation diagrams, and rune systems.

Only after Master, comes the Grandmaster.
