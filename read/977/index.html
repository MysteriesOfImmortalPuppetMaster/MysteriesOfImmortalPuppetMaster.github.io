Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 977: Tie Kuang's Night Visit

Chen San sat upright just below the main seat. In front of him, inside a thin-rimmed celadon cup, the spirit tea had long grown cold. A few unfurled tea leaves sank to the bottom of the cup, quietly moored in the cold pool like dark green flatboats.

He was dressed much more respectably today - a blue-grey long gown, a plain waistband, and cuffs washed and starched crisp and clean. His hair was combed without a single strand out of place. Yet, the shrewdness of a street-smart Cultivator between his brows still couldn't be hidden; with every dart of his eyes, calculations churned.

Ning Zhuo's orders to him were concise and to the point: have him come to this building, rent a private room, and publicly collect all intelligence regarding the Heaven-Hanging Single-Thread Hook and the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. The grander the spectacle, the better.

Three light knocks came from outside the private room's door, rhythmic and disciplined. The door-guarding Cultivator pushed the door open and said in a low voice, "Manager Chen, someone else has arrived. They say they share an irreconcilable enmity with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect and wish to see Young Master Ning Zhuo's man."

Chen San set down his teacup, unhurriedly smoothed out his cuffs, and only then spoke, "Please, bring them in."

The door opened.

A group of five filed in.

The leader was a middle-aged Cultivator, tall and thin as a bamboo, wearing an old green robe. The hem of the robe had been washed until it was white and frayed, yet the jade pendant at his waist was incredibly valuable - though a crack on its surface had been meticulously mended with fine gold wire.

The four people behind him varied in age: a youth, a woman, a white-haired old servant, and a one-armed Cultivator. Their clothing was relatively neat, but the heavy gloom they had repressed for years lingered like the smell of rust hidden at the bottom of an old wooden chest. As soon as they entered, it caused the air in the entire private room to stagnate slightly.

The middle-aged Cultivator cupped his hands in greeting. "I am Bo Qingya of the Guixiu Bo Clan."

Chen San stood up to return the greeting, his smile attentive but not overly enthusiastic. "Fellow Daoist Bo, please have a seat. That everyone was willing to come is already giving face to my Young Master. I, Chen, will first thank you on his behalf."

Bo Qingya, however, did not immediately sit down.

"Our Bo Clan once had a Nascent Soul patriarch named Bo Wenxiao," Bo Qingya spoke. His voice was not loud, but every word was as clear as carved stone. The people in the room all held their breath and listened. "Thirty-four years ago, the Patriarch was hired to escort a scroll of Ancient Cloud Runes. On his return journey, the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, commissioned by someone, intercepted and attacked him at a wild ferry crossing. The Patriarch fought with all his might but was outmatched, perishing on the spot. Of the forty-seven clansmen accompanying him, not a single one survived."

At this point, the woman behind him suddenly got red eyes. Her fingertips desperately clutched the edge of her sleeve, her knuckles turning white.

A suppressed gasp rolled from deep within the one-armed Cultivator's throat, like an old wound caught by the wind, aching faintly deep within his bones.

Bo Qingya's voice remained as steady as before. "With the Patriarch gone, the clan lost its pillar, and packs of wolves circled us from all sides. Territories, spirit vein mines, herb gardens, and connections - we couldn't hold onto any of them. Before we were completely devoured, we simply entrusted our remaining territories to the Myriad Manifestations Sect, attaching our entire clan as a vassal. Only then did we preserve our roots and the lives of our clansmen."

However, his eyes grew even darker, like a bottomless old well. "But precisely because we joined the same Sect, we could no longer take revenge on the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect - the Sect rules are clear: fellow disciples must not slaughter each other."

Chen San felt a slight tremor in his heart as he listened, but he didn't reveal a trace of it on his face. He personally poured a fresh cup of tea and pushed it in front of Bo Qingya, the tea vapor curling upwards. "Fellow Daoist Bo, my condolences."

Bo Qingya looked at the slowly unfurling spirit buds in the teacup and suddenly smiled.

"I've been offering my condolences for thirty-four years, Manager Chen."

He raised his eyes to look at Chen San. Beneath the coldness in his gaze, a cluster of flames was dancing. "This time, Young Master Ning Zhuo is going to fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. Although my Bo Clan has declined, we are willing to devote all our strength to assist him."

Chen San's expression turned serious. "What assistance does the Bo Clan offer?"

Bo Qingya took out a small wooden box from his sleeve.

Opening the lid, a grayish-white bone bead lay inside. Faint cloud patterns flowed across its surface, and the whispers of a Remnant Soul echoed within, though upon closer listening, only Void Stillness remained.

Seeing the bone bead, the youth behind Bo Qingya immediately got red-rimmed eyes and lowered his head, not daring to look any further.

"This is a Cloud Bone Demonic-Sensing Bead refined from the Patriarch's remains," Bo Qingya said. "The Patriarch died at the hands of the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, and deep within his bones lingered the unique aura of those three. This bead cannot harm anyone, but within a hundred zhang, it can sense the changes in the Three Braves' auras. Especially when their 'Shared Life and Death' Divine Art is activated, three gray lines will appear on the bead's surface, each pointing toward the person with the heaviest flow of aura."

Chen San's eyes twitched slightly.

A good item! To be able to discern the flow of the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect's foundation in an ever-changing magical battle - even if it just provided a sliver of an advantage, it would be enough to seize the initiative.

Bo Qingya closed the wooden box. "But this bead is not given for free. If Young Master Ning Zhuo wants to use it, he must sign a contract with my Bo Clan. If he defeats the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect in the future, he must, within the bounds of the Myriad Manifestations Sect's rules, fight for an opportunity for my Bo Clan to publicly question the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect."

Chen San's mind calculated rapidly.

This was no light request. A public questioning meant bringing the thirty-four-year-old cold case into the open. At that time, who hired the killers, who covered it up, who swallowed the Bo Clan's territories, and who benefited from it all - everything could potentially be exposed at once.

That would be poking a hornet's nest.

He did not agree on the spot, though the smile on his face remained as steady as a boulder. "Fellow Daoist Bo's sincerity is deeply moving, and I, Chen, have taken it to heart. However, this matter involves the core terms of a contract, and I have no authority to agree on the Young Master's behalf. Come evening, I will personally return to the Cave Manor and report this matter in detail for the Young Master to decide."

Bo Qingya stared at him for a few breaths before finally giving a slight nod. "The people around Young Master Ning Zhuo are quite disciplined."

Chen San cupped his hands. "My Young Master values the word 'discipline' above all else!"

He personally escorted the Bo Clan party to the door of the private room.

Right before departing, the woman suddenly looked back at Chen San. Her lips parted, and her voice was extremely soft, yet it carried a heavy determination. "Tell Young Master Ning Zhuo, if he can truly make the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect pay the price, my Bo Clan is willing to exhaust all our remaining wealth to aid him."

The door closed, and the private room fell silent for a moment.

Chen San sat back in his seat, exhaling a long breath of turbid qi. He reached out to touch the recording Jade Slip in his sleeve - the Jade Slip already held records of many names and deeds.

This kind of situation had been happening constantly since Chen San rented the private room and publicly began purchasing intelligence. It was a common occurrence.

Before the Guixiu Bo Clan arrived, quite a few people had already visited quietly. The Frost Cliff Liu Clan had delivered an old battle map. Back in the day, they managed a Cold Cloud trade route. The Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect were hired to ambush and kill two of the Liu Clan's Golden Core elders, forcing the Liu Clan to abandon the trade route and suffer a massive blow to their foundation.

The Remnant Lamp Wanderer came at night. Dressed in gray, he carried an oil lamp missing half its cover, within which was sealed a wisp of his disciple's lingering thought from before death. His disciple had merely been guiding the way for someone when he was casually silenced by the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, leaving not even a name behind. The Broken Lamp Wanderer was willing to offer the "Remnant Lamp Fate-Ending Art." He didn't want Spirit Stones; he only asked that Ning Zhuo allow him to see the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect bleed with his own eyes in the future!

The Cloud Crossing Merchant Firm also sent a shopkeeper over. Round-faced and plump, he had a smile of amiable wealth on his face, yet his hand gripped an old, uncollectible debt. The merchant firm had once lost three ships of cloud brocade due to an interception by the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, suffering such heavy losses that they nearly disbanded.

A young Sword Cultivator named Jiao Chi, a man of so few words he seemed mute and wearing a black cloth over his left eye, walked in and simply set down a piece of a broken sword...

The most peculiar visitor was a scrawny old woman who called herself a former member of Crane Dock. She brought a bottle of blue and red medicinal powder named "Spark Ash," claiming it could counter the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. However, if Ning Zhuo wanted this powder, he had to offer a price that met her expectations.

At first, Chen San was shocked and doubtful, never expecting that the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect had offended so many people.

On second thought, he felt it was entirely reasonable. The defining role of the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect was being a blade - selling their lives for money, killing Nascent Souls, annihilating clans, intercepting trade routes, and severing people's Dao Paths. They killed like flies; how could they not have enemies?

It was just that previously, no one had dared to make a move.

The Three Valiants were backed by the Face Death Sect and were fellow disciples of the Myriad Manifestations Sect. Their combat prowess was so fiercely despair-inducing that their enemies could only shatter their teeth and swallow the blood.

Now that Ning Zhuo had publicly stepped up to the stage, those resentments built up over decades or even centuries finally found a fissure like an underground dark river, surging outward stream by stream.

Through this, Chen San also grasped the true intent behind Ning Zhuo's orders. This private room he rented in the Moon Gazing Tower was not an ordinary intelligence stall at all, but a target, and a tentacle that Ning Zhuo extended to the outside world.

Anyone who wanted to aid Ning Zhuo would seek him out first; anyone who wanted to probe Ning Zhuo would seek him out as well.

Chen San felt moved and honored. Young Master Ning Zhuo abided by the principle: "If you doubt a man, don't use him; if you use a man, don't doubt him." He was but a mere rogue Cultivator - what virtue or ability did he possess to act as Ning Zhuo's frontman on the cusp of such a storm? He had to do his absolute best to uphold the Young Master's reputation and not disappoint this excellent opportunity the Young Master had given him to perform!

Chen San had reaped a massive harvest. His heart felt as though it held a bundle of burning firewood, eager to rush back to the Cave Manor and report to Ning Zhuo.

However, Ning Zhuo's orders were crystal clear: leave at dusk. He forcibly suppressed that impatience, stubbornly sitting in the private room until the twilight sank into the window lattice and the last streak of golden clouds on the horizon was swallowed by the night, only then did he stand up.

He meticulously inventoried the Jade Slips, visiting cards, contract drafts, and tokens, storing them all in his storage bag. Then, he instructed the remaining Cultivators to continue receiving visitors, wrapped his outer robe tightly around himself, and hurried downstairs.

Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor.

Ning Zhuo sat upright behind his desk, listening to Chen San report the day's occurrences one by one. The lamplight cast intertwining shadows over the side of his face. His brows and eyes were a picture of tranquility, without the slightest hint of surprise.

Chen San stood below, offering the Jade Slip with both hands, the remnants of excitement still lingering in his eyes. "Young Master, this subordinate has recorded the names, origins, offered sponsorships, and requested conditions of the guests. The identities of a few individuals still need verification, so your subordinate dared not trust them easily."

Ning Zhuo took the Jade Slip and his divine sense swept over it like a tide. The Bo Clan, the Liu Clan, the Remnant Lamp Wanderer, the Cloud Crossing Merchant Firm, Jiao Chi, the former Crane Dock member's Breath-Severing Ash, and some sponsorship intentions written in a vague, secretive manner.

The youth read extremely fast. His fingertips tapped lightly on the desk - tap, tap, tap - deep and rhythmic, like a chess player's contemplation before making a move.

"Well done," he finally spoke.

Chen San rejoiced in his heart and quickly bowed his head. "It is all thanks to the Young Master's proper arrangements."

Ning Zhuo smiled slightly, a faint but genuine smile. "Since I have publicly made enemies of the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, potential allies will naturally surface. An enemy's enemy is often a friend."

Ning Zhuo looked at Chen San. "Keep doing it. But you must be clear in your heart: not all of these people necessarily have a blood feud with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. Different factions and different forces using my hand to strike at their rivals - this is an incredibly common occurrence in the Cultivation World."

Chen San memorized this earnestly, not daring to miss a single word.

Ning Zhuo waved his hand.

Chen San respectfully retreated.

Ning Zhuo's gaze fell on the dancing lamp flame. The wick was slightly bent, and the flame flickered within his eyes.

He seemed to see his own reflection in the flame.

"My current situation is incredibly similar to that of Liu Jinke back then."

"Only the chessboard is larger, the level of combat is higher, and the involved interests are heavier."

"Among those sponsoring me today, perhaps there are even major forces from Flowing Cloud Peak, using my hands to weaken the Face Death Sect."

"Wait a bit longer."

"The people who can truly afford the price have not yet appeared."

Outside the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor, cloud qi slowly drifted around.

In the distance, mountain lamps linked like stars forming a galaxy. The night scenery of the Myriad Manifestations Sect's main mountain gate was vast and profound, like a boundless chessboard spread out between heaven and earth.

Tie Kuang arrived under the cover of night!

Ning Zhuo went forward to welcome him, cupping his hands in greeting. His posture was respectful yet unhurried. "For Hall Master Tie to visit in person, this junior is deeply honored."

Tie Kuang laughed heartily. "Ning Zhuo, your limelight is so bright right now. If this old man didn't come, I'm afraid I wouldn't even be able to squeeze through the door of your Cave Manor!"

As he sat down, the hardwood chair groaned under the heavy load and sank slightly.

Gongsun Yan served the tea. Tie Kuang picked it up, took a sniff, and casually set it back down.

Ning Zhuo dismissed Gongsun Yan and sat opposite Tie Kuang. "Hall Master, you've come again for the Naming Fire Furnace?"

Tie Kuang's gaze burned like crimson flames in a furnace. "Naturally."

Ning Zhuo got straight to the point, without beating around the bush: "If Hall Master can help me defeat the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, I will agree to the cooperation we discussed earlier."

Tie Kuang laughed loudly. "Little Friend Ning Zhuo, you demand a steep price."

He raised a hand and slapped his thigh. "Help you defeat the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect? That's nearly impossible."

Ning Zhuo's expression remained calm, his features unmoving. "Why does Hall Master say that?"

Tie Kuang held up a short, thick finger. "Your cultivation level drags you down too much! Mid Foundation Establishment Stage, not even late stage yet. What about the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect? Each one is at the peak of the Golden Core stage with profound foundations. Every strike of their blades and swords was forged by slaughtering with their lives on the line."

He extended a second finger. "You are too young. Your combat experience is ultimately lacking. You defeated Liu Jinke and have slain a Golden Core cultivator, gaining quite a reputation. This old man knows that. But how many perilous situations, fierce battles, and life-and-death sieges have the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect gone through? Probably more than the magical battles you've ever witnessed."

A third finger slowly lifted. The smile on Tie Kuang's face faded, replaced by a solemn expression.

"They also have the Divine Art 'Shared Life and Death'! These three are absolute death warriors. When ordinary people fight, they always harbor thoughts of survival and retreat. They don't. Injuries can be shared, lifespans can be shared; soul power, spiritual energy, and life force can be mutually infused. If one person falls, the other two can risk their lives to yank him back from death's door."

"This is what is most terrifying."

Tie Kuang stared at Ning Zhuo, his voice deepening like a heavy hammer falling on an anvil. "Fighting this kind of person is a completely different experience. They dare to trade a life for one of your openings, dare to trade half a body for a single moment of your hesitation. That many Nascent Soul cultivators have fallen at their hands is absolutely no accident."

He leaned back a bit, pressing his thick fingers on the tea desk. "To be honest, even this old man might not have full confidence in defeating the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect."

Ning Zhuo, however, sneered in his heart, entirely unconvinced.

Tie Kuang was belittling himself and elevating his opponents; this was a meticulously orchestrated setup.

Would the dignified Master of the Artifact Refining Hall, one of the sixteen halls of the Myriad Manifestations Sect, truly fear the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect?

The gap between Nascent Soul cultivators was immense.

The big-headed youth had experienced the battle at the Thousand Peaks Forest, and had also personally witnessed the Nascent Soul clash between the three factions of the Nanming Stronghold, the Earth Expansion Alliance, and White Cloud Township. His horizons had long been broadened.

The Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect were indeed troublesome, but a figure like Tie Kuang would at most find them annoying; he would never truly be put at a disadvantage. His rhetoric was merely an attempt to suppress Ning Zhuo's spirit and expectations, laying the foundation for the subsequent negotiations.
