Chapter 1008: Constructing the Treasure Land

Tie Kuang’s tone became profound and meaningful: "Little Friend Ning Zhuo, you’ve seen it too. Today, in repairing the furnace, the Warm Cloud Marrow, Vermilion Feather Flame-Generating Sand, and Crimson Parasol Roots were all of great use. Without these three treasures, even if this furnace could be restored, it would still lack a bit of perfection."

Tie Kuang continued, "The Naming Stronghold has already occupied the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, which is very good. But one treasure land is not enough. The Flame-Roosting Cloud Nest has an extremely deep affinity with the Naming Fire Furnace. There, clouds and fire nourish each other, producing these three treasures. If you can also take down the Flame-Roosting Cloud Nest, then in the future, the Naming Fire Furnace’s nurturing, ascension refinement, and fire vein tuning will all have a foundation to rely on."

He spoke frankly, acting as if he was thinking of the Naming Stronghold's best interests.

Ning Zhuo revealed a solemn, thoughtful expression on his face and slowly nodded. "What Hall Master Tie says is absolutely right. Seeing the wondrous uses of the three treasures with my own eyes today, this junior is deeply moved. The matter of the Flame-Roosting Cloud Nest is indeed worth careful consideration."

Tie Kuang nodded in satisfaction.

Ning Zhuo lowered his eyes to look at the Naming Fire Furnace. His expression was serious, but inwardly, he secretly rolled his eyes.

"With the situation so unclear, how could I easily act according to someone else's arrangements?"

Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

After this treasure land was taken over by the Naming Stronghold, the traces previously left behind by the White Cloud Village completely disappeared, wiped entirely clean.

The mountain wind swept over the top of the altar, carrying a scorching aura of metal and iron in its howl.

The Naming Stronghold cultivators were bustling about in this exact heat.

Cloud carts traveled back and forth, formation flags fluttered, and boxes of spirit materials landed on the rocky flat one after another. Some were measuring the earth veins, some were repairing stone steps, some were transporting spirit wood, and others were setting up temporary pill refinement chambers.

The clanging of hammers, the rattling drag of formation chains, and the long cries of spirit birds carrying transports interwove into a symphony up and down the mountainside.

Even though they had just moved in, the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar already displayed a scene of thriving prosperity.

Chunyangzi stood at a high vantage point.

He had changed back into his previous crimson-gold Daoist robe, though the scriptures along the robe still had many dimmed spots.

The injuries on his body also transmitted needle-like flares of pain from time to time. The black-and-yellow jade-like scars left behind from his grand battle with Liang Ming had been forcefully suppressed by Chunyangzi, hidden beneath his skin and flesh so they were no longer visible on the outside.

But only Chunyangzi himself knew that this injury was extremely troublesome and was even showing signs of worsening. Now, with every breath he took, it felt as if residual dust from the yin earth was gently scraping against his internal organs.

His complexion remained somewhat pale.

Yet, as he stood there with a straight back and clear, broad brows, his entire person was like a crimson-gold banner illuminated by the scorching sun. When the cultivators of the Naming Stronghold saw him from afar, their hearts naturally settled by three parts.

This was the prestige gained after a great battle!

Shen Xi was setting up a formation.

Clad in a plain white formation robe, he had a handsome face and a calm, scholarly air between his brows. Today, his cuffs were tied extremely tight, and a formation ruler along with several jade formation nails hung at his waist. Standing on the scorching rocky terrace, his whole bearing was clean and sharp.

"Thirty-seven zhang to the southeast, the fire vein is rising. Suppress the earth qi by three inches first." Shen Xi raised his formation ruler, his voice clear and smooth, yet revealing an unquestionable steadiness.

Several cultivators of the Shen Clan acted immediately.

They carried over a cyan-white jade formation plate. The edges of the plate were inlaid with a Nine Palaces grid pattern, and mist circulated within the colorless crystal at its center. When the formation plate touched the ground, nine thin lines scattered in all directions, roaming close to the surface like nine tiny silver fish burrowing into the crimson-gold rock layer.

The rocky terrace trembled slightly.

An excessively violent streak of fire qi surged up from beneath the earth. Just as it was about to erupt from the surface, it was gently blocked by the Nine Palaces formation lines. Its direction immediately deflected, flowing along the grooves Shen Xi had marked beforehand toward the outer edge of the ancient altar.

A wave of heat rolled up, blowing Shen Xi's robes so they fluttered loudly.

He narrowed his eyes and flicked his fingertip against the formation ruler. The formation ruler emitted a clear, resonant chime, and one after another, formation nails flew out from his sleeves, landing in nine different spots. With every formation nail that touched down, a water-like faint light rippled outward, combing the frantic yang fire into bundles of usable fire veins.

Chunyangzi watched this scene from his high vantage point, a trace of approval flashing through his eyes.

Shen Xi's methods in the Dao of Formations were meticulous, perfectly suited for the initial layout of such a treasure land.

On the other side, Lin Jinglong was planting trees.

He had a slender figure with broad, upright shoulders. Today, he wore a dark green dharma robe, the cuffs embroidered with tiny dragon scale patterns.

Normally, he was gentle and always wore a smile, but now, as it came time to execute his Wood Element methods, his expression became extremely focused.

He took out a wooden box covered in azure scales.

The spirit soil inside the box was moist, and dozens of tree seeds were neatly arranged within. The shells of the seeds were dark green, their textures resembling coiled little dragons. When getting close, one could even hear the extremely faint sound of roots uncurling.

"Plant the Azure Hornless Dragon Armor-Embracing Seeds in the outer ring," Lin Jinglong instructed his clansmen beside him. "The Fire Date Root Seeds go next to the yang grooves. As for these three Crimson Cloud Mulberry Heart Seeds, plant them at the base of the ancient altar. They can consume fire qi and exhale wood rosy clouds. In the future, they can serve as the foundation of the Naming Stronghold's spirit botanical garden."

The Lin Clan cultivators took the tree seeds and promptly dispersed.

Lin Jinglong bent down, pressing his palm against the crimson-gold rock layer.

Wood Element spiritual energy seeped in from his palm. Though it was a verdant, vibrant qi, it was not burned away upon entering the fiery hot rock. Instead, like spring rain falling onto parched earth, it rapidly awakened a trace of vitality deep within.

He pressed a tree seed into a fissure.

After only a dozen or so breaths, a tender green sprout poked out from the crack.

The moment the green sprout emerged, it curled slightly, roasted by the underground yang fire.

Lin Jinglong chuckled softly and flicked a streak of azure light from his fingertip.

Bathed in the azure light, the green sprout instantly unfurled. It grew tiny scales and its roots thrust downward, like a young azure dragon sinking its claws into the fiery soil.

Soon, the first sapling grew to half a person's height.

The trunk was dark green, the leaves had crimson edges, and a faint golden light flowed through the veins. It didn't block the sunlight; instead, it filtered the scorching sun into fine, warm fragments of light, scattering them across the edges of the newly laid formation runes.

Meanwhile, Situ Xing was leading people to install formation instruments.

Dressed in black clothes with star patterns, star-patterned bronze boxes, jade formation pivots, and spools of silver silk wire constantly flew out from his sleeves.

"Place the Star Pivot Mother-and-Child Plate here." Situ Xing pointed at a protruding rocky platform on the eastern side of the ancient altar. "The yang fire is too intense. At night, when the spiritual energy recedes, the flow is prone to interruptions. The Star Pivot Mother-and-Child Plate can draw in starlight to fill the gaps, ensuring that this place is protected by the formation's power day and night."

Just as his words fell, two Situ Clan cultivators carried over a formation instrument that was half a person tall.

The formation instrument resembled a Go board, entirely dark silver, with intersecting lines on the surface spreading out like a galaxy. In the middle, a white stone anchored the formation's eye, while at each of the four corners, a black stone slowly rotated.

Situ Xing reached out and pressed his hand against the white stone.

He poured in spiritual energy and manipulated his divine sense to establish a connection.

With a soft click.

The entire formation instrument lit up with a cool, clear starlight. When the starlight met the crimson-gold sunlight of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar - one cold, one hot; one bright, one dim - they actually gave way to each other, each following its own path.

Chunyangzi understood clearly in his heart: the super clans behind Situ Xing, Shen Xi, and Lin Jinglong had already started increasing their stakes.

The Naming Stronghold had opened up the situation, and the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar was already in their hands. To the super clans, investing a batch of aid at this moment caused them no pressure at all.

Powerful aristocratic families never put all their eggs in one basket to begin with. This expense they paid was nothing more than a light stroke of the pen on their clan's ledger. But to the newly starting Naming Stronghold, it was a timely rain.

Chunyangzi sighed in his heart. "The Naming Stronghold was Ning Zhuo's concept. I originally thought it was just a crude assembly, but I didn't expect it to have its own unique merits."

Having governed the Pure Yang Palace in the past, he deeply knew the difficulties of managing a faction.

General organizations emphasize foundations.

Clans use bloodlines as their foundation, sects use the inheritance of Cultivation Arts as their foundation, workshops use craftsmanship as their foundation, and merchant firms use financial routes and credit as their foundation. Pushing back to ancient times, there were even divisions among Humans, Demon Cultivators, and the Spirit Race, raising banners based on their race to gather the masses for self-preservation.

Now, the Human race was greatly prosperous, and pure Demon factions were rarely seen. However, among the major factions, they still emphasized foundations and stressed uniformity.

The foundation of the Naming Stronghold was very peculiar - debt!

Almost all of its members were Ning Zhuo's creditors.

To an ordinary person, it would just sound like a joke, like a group of debt collectors temporarily forming a partnership, gathering together to prevent the debtor from defaulting.

But right now, as Chunyangzi stood on the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, watching Shen Xi lay formations, Lin Jinglong plant trees, and Situ Xing set up formation instruments, watching the cultivators of the various clans proactively bring over spirit materials and busily work away...

He began to feel deep down: perhaps the Naming Stronghold is an astonishingly innovative creation?

This organization was quite loose, therefore it could accommodate everyone.

It involved debt, so everyone had a reason to stick their hands in.

There were no strict sect rules suppressing people, no single bloodline causing exclusion, and no specific Cultivation Art restricting one's background. As long as one had a creditor relationship with the Naming Fire Furnace, they could legitimately participate.

"Ning Zhuo is young, but his mind is indeed sharp."

Old Man Baicao brought a group of cultivators, working together to repair the old beds of ruined flowers.

The exotic flowers and rare herbs of the past had all been uprooted and taken away by the cultivators of the White Cloud Village when they left.

All that remained were dead branches, withered leaves, and shattered spirit soil.

Old Man Baicao squatted on the ground, prioritizing the planting of the most important flowers and herbs.

Caressing a small curled-leaf grass, Old Man Baicao chuckled and said, "Give it a bit of water, shade it from the sun for a few days, and it'll sprout."

The cultivators behind him rolled out a carpet of vibrant green grass.

The moment the grass carpet touched the ground, it extended outward like a living creature. The dense grass stems rooted into the crevices of the crimson-gold rock layer, rapidly swallowing the excess fire qi before exhaling a clear, moist, herbaceous fragrance.

The originally scorching and dry air atop the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar suddenly gained a hint of moisture.

Chunyangzi saw it clearly: these resources were absolutely not something Old Man Baicao could take out all on his own.

"So, Baicao Peak is also placing its bets."

"There's no need for me to mobilize them; everyone wants to secure their own seat in the rankings. This concerns their status and interests within the Naming Stronghold."

"Even if Old Man Baicao doesn't have these kinds of resources himself, the faction behind him, Baicao Peak, wouldn't just sit idly by and do nothing."

Debt as the foundation.

Ledgers as the veins.

Interests as the blood.

"This organization that Ning Zhuo has created is far better at leveraging external strength than any ordinary faction."
