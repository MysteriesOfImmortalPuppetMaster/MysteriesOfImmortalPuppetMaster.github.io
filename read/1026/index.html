Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 1026: Ning Zhuo Tests His Swords

The golden light was gentle, almost making people forget it was part of the defense line.

A cultivator with rather fast movement techniques stepped in first. With a slip of his foot, his figure split into three afterimages. He thought his scheme had succeeded and was about to pass through the gaps in the mirror's light when a bronze mirror on the corridor's ceiling suddenly let out a soft ding.

The pale golden light suddenly gathered.

Those three afterimages were like thin paper meeting fire, instantly burning with scorched edges. His true body was gently enveloped by a beam of warm light, freezing him in place. The token at his waist popped out with a smack, automatically recording a failure.

The cultivator's face flushed red. He muttered a curse under his breath and dejectedly retreated.

Sun Lingtong watched with shining eyes.

If it were in the past, he would definitely have viewed this sunlight as an enemy.

Thieves feared the light.

This was the most instinctual thought of many theft path cultivators.

Hiding shadows, shrinking bones, concealing one's aura, evading sight, passing through darkness - almost all Theft Path Methods taught people how to avoid light and how to become the shadow that could not be illuminated.

But now, things were different.

The Yang Dao realm that Ning Zhuo had instilled in him, although not cultivated step by step by himself, had already left extremely deep traces of Dao principles within his divine sea.

Looking at that layer of pale golden light, Sun Lingtong understood in his heart: light is not just illumination, it is also a declaration; Yang is not just for dispelling evil, it is also for manifestation. If one blindly hid themselves as a shadow, it was instead equivalent to admitting guilt before the sunlight.

"So that's how it is." Sun Lingtong narrowed his eyes. After watching for a while, the corners of his mouth curled up; he had already seen through the deeper mechanism. "This corridor isn't afraid of someone walking into it; it's afraid of someone having a guilty conscience."

He did not use Shadow Escape.

Nor did he shrink into a corner.

Instead, he puffed out his chest and, like an honest, law-abiding Internal Affairs Hall handyman, strode into the corridor.

The bronze mirrors on the ceiling turned slightly in unison.

Ding.

Ding, ding.

The soft sounds were like tiny bells, and layers of pale golden light fell upon Sun Lingtong's body.

Sun Lingtong circulated his Cultivation Art, making his aura smooth, gentle, tranquil, open and aboveboard, completely magnanimous.

His footsteps were as steady as if he were walking in his own courtyard.

The bronze mirrors rotated one by one, but never sounded an alarm.

The other cultivators were dumbfounded. Someone tried to imitate him, but immediately suffered a failure.

Ruan Hengqiu, located outside the pavilion, saw this scene through a water screen. His slender eyes narrowed slightly. "This youth is quite interesting."

The first defensive line, [Yang Illumination Evil-Reflecting Corridor], was passed.

At the end of the corridor was a broad stone plaza. The plaza was paved with dark brown stone slabs, each bearing fine, dense earthen patterns.

Sun Lingtong was the first to arrive here. He paused for a moment before directly taking action.

"Anyway, according to the rules of the minor trial, any participant can attempt these defensive lines an unlimited number of times."

He stepped onto the stone plaza. Even though his footsteps were extremely light, the stone slabs seemed to suddenly awaken, rippling with a muddy yellow ring.

Sun Lingtong's expression changed slightly. He immediately cast a light-body technique, floating half an inch in the air.

The next moment, the ground sank slightly like a mire. A large earthen-yellow hand flipped up from the stone slab, grabbed his ankle, and forcibly dragged him back to his original spot. It didn't hurt him, but it made it impossible for him to move a single step forward.

Sun Lingtong crouched down and gently pressed his fingers against the surface of the stone slab.

Ice-cold, heavy, silent.

The Earth Dao realm Ning Zhuo had imparted to him radiated within his heart.

The nature of earth was not just carrying capacity, but also the ultimate destination. All things fall to the ground, and all return to the earth.

Soon, he passed the second defensive line, [Earth Virtue Load-Bearing Plaza].

The third defensive line was named [Thousand Mechanisms Linked Formation].

At this time, Sun Lingtong acted as a master of the Formation Dao. He utilized Magic Artifacts and activated various Theft Path Methods to test it.

A moment later, he unraveled the Formation, but was caught off guard by the fourth defensive line hidden here, which suddenly erupted. For a time, water ripples surged, filling the entire space and causing him to suffer a failure.

Smack.

Sun Lingtong was completely bounced out by a gentle yet irresistible force. He landed on his butt and slid more than ten feet across the smooth stone surface.

Sun Lingtong stood up and rubbed his butt. Not only was he not annoyed, but he looked straight ahead with sparkling eyes.

"Good, good, good!" he said three times in a row, extremely excited. "Now this is interesting!"

He turned around and left.

"Hmm?" Ruan Hengqiu had been silently paying attention to Sun Lingtong. Seeing the latter suddenly withdraw, he couldn't help but feel surprised.

Ruan Hengqiu thought for a moment, then summoned Sun Lingtong alone to a side hall outside the Treasure Pavilion.

Ruan Hengqiu pushed over a small wooden box. "You performed well. Why did you withdraw halfway?"

The wooden box opened, and two items lay inside.

One was a silver needle as fine as cow hair. The silver needle was no more than three inches long, and its body was translucent, looking as if it were kneaded from moonlight and mist.

The other was a Jade Slip, colored a faint ink black.

Ruan Hengqiu said, "The silver needle is named the Traceless Gap-Borrowing Needle. It is not a lethal weapon, but a probing needle. When encountering Formations, locks, box crevices, or door gaps, it can borrow a breath's worth of an opening to probe inside. It cannot break the restriction for you, but it can let you know some of the situation inside."

Ruan Hengqiu pointed to the Jade Slip again. "This Technique is one of the Theft Path Methods - the Aura Exchange Art! In an extremely short amount of time, it can disguise a wisp of your own aura as something else. It is very suitable for evading detection arrays."

The playful smile on Sun Lingtong's face faded slightly, his heart pounding with excitement.

Ruan Hengqiu looked at him, his voice softening a bit. "You possess attainments in Theft Path Methods, so there is no need to worry. My Myriad Manifestations Sect accepts all rivers into its sea; could it be that we cannot accommodate a little you?"

"At the upcoming Flying Cloud Assembly, there will be no shortage of top-tier geniuses. People like Ning Zhuo, Situ Xing, Ban Ji, and Zhu Fenxiang have already stirred up storms and radiated brilliance in their respective fields. But they won't come to participate in this kind of minor trial."

"Looking down on it is one reason."

"Damaging their reputation is the second."

Ruan Hengqiu continued, "If you are interested, you can come again. The Golden Coffer Trial of Theft is open every day. The defense lines of the Treasure Pavilion will also not remain the same forever."

Sun Lingtong accepted the wooden box, unable to suppress his smile. "Senior, don't worry. Next time, I will definitely steal in a much more presentable manner!"

That night, Sun Lingtong excitedly returned to the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor.

"Little Zhuo, Little Zhuo!"

Filled with excitement, Sun Lingtong recounted the Golden Coffer Trial of Theft from beginning to end.

"This Rising Cloud Minor Trial is fantastic; I'm definitely going again!"

"You've been handling affairs and recruiting talents in the Naming Stronghold lately, so you don't need these. Quickly lend the other realms to me."

Given the deep bond between Sun Lingtong and Ning Zhuo, he wasn't standing on ceremony at all.

Ning Zhuo immediately nodded. "Alright, as long as you're happy, Boss. However, I must keep the Metal Element realm. Recently, during the breaks from handling mundane affairs at the Naming Stronghold, I've also been practicing with flying swords."

"Uh-huh," Sun Lingtong nodded. "Hurry up, I'm in a rush!"

He was already too impatient to wait.

Ning Zhuo then used the Dao-Bearing Jade Page to instill the realms into Sun Lingtong.

On the jade page of the Water Element true intent, faint ink-blue water ripples surfaced. At first, they resembled a stream, then transformed into a surging river. Looking deeper, it seemed like a pool of tranquil, stagnant water.

In the center of the jade page of the Fire Element true intent, a crimson radiance lit up, like a tiny lamp flame. The flame wasn't fierce, but it carried an active, outward-reaching will.

The azure patterns on the jade page of the Wood Element true intent continuously grew, even extending beyond the edges of the jade page to form countless fine jade threads.

Sun Lingtong closed his eyes. After absorbing it all, his body swayed uncontrollably, like he had just downed three jugs of strong liquor in one breath.

"T-today we'll stop here. My head, my head isn't as big as yours, I can't quite hold it all in," Sun Lingtong actively called for a timeout.

After he took his leave, Ning Zhuo remained alone in the room, his face slightly pale.

He could clearly feel that his comprehensive Five Elements realm had also dropped by a margin.

"It can be seen that the five individual element realms are the foundation of the comprehensive Five Elements realm, having a massive impact on the latter."

Ning Zhuo lowered his eyes, silently experiencing this sensation of decline in his heart.

This was also valuable experience.

Now, Ning Zhuo understood: having more realms wasn't necessarily better, nor was their value only realized by piling them endlessly onto oneself.

He became even more certain of the value of the Dao-Bearing Jade Page!

This item could transfer, split, and circulate realms - it was simply a supreme weapon for managing a faction!

Ning Zhuo activated the martial practice Formation.

He stood in the center.

Floating in front of him were six sword boxes.

These flying swords were all recently purchased by him.

They were not some spiritual treasures, nor were they Golden Core-level heavy artifacts; they were all at the grade of Magic Artifacts. In the hands of ordinary Foundation Establishment cultivators, they would be considered decent items. But to the current Ning Zhuo, who controlled the Naming Fire Furnace and held real authority over the Naming Stronghold, they barely counted as an expense.

Ning Zhuo hadn't bought them for killing enemies on the battlefield; it was purely for practicing his swordsmanship.

Shentu Wuwei's Nascent Soul soul had already been refined by the Boat-Burning Soul-Crossing Art. His lifelong experience with flying swords lay like a cold, sharp treasury filled with resentment and untamed arrogance, sinking deep into Ning Zhuo's divine sea.

Memories were ultimately just memories, and experience was ultimately just experience. If not personally tested, it would be like holding someone else's sword in one's own shadow - visible, but unnatural to use.

Ning Zhuo waved his hand, and the first sword box opened.

The White Porcelain Sword flew out.

The body of this sword was snow-white, made of fine porcelain, so beautifully fragile that it almost didn't look like a lethal weapon. There was a faint line of golden light on the spine of the sword; usually warm, mellow, and aloof, but once activated, it could unleash a tremendous offensive!

Ning Zhuo pointed his fingers together, seamlessly channeling his spiritual energy into it.

Buzz.

The White Porcelain Sword suddenly trembled. That faint golden line on the sword's spine flared brightly, and the snow-white blade instantly erupted with an extremely sharp sword gleam. The sword gleam was not just a line, but a line so bright it pierced the eyes, like someone had sliced open a long, narrow wound in the void with a golden needle.

Sword gleam!

This was the high compression and condensation of sword light.

Ning Zhuo's gaze focused as he carefully experienced it.

Sword light was one of the most intuitive external manifestations of Sword Dao power. Fast, bright, sharp, eye-dazzling, and mind-snatching - these were all powers that could be born from sword light.

The White Porcelain Sword swept through the air.

Clink!

The sword light slashed against the stone stele, kicking up a shower of fine golden sparks. The Formation patterns caved in slightly, then quickly recovered.

Ning Zhuo raised his eyebrows slightly.

"Too straight." He spotted the problem.

The White Porcelain Sword's light was sharp enough, but when he controlled it, it was still like a Mechanism Master driving a blade wheel - seeking accurate trajectories, clean angles, and stable force. However, the flying swords in Shentu Wuwei's memory were not like this. Flying sword light needed to have a sense of "preemption." The moment the enemy's thought arose, the sword light should already be sealing off their path forward.

Ning Zhuo activated the sword again.

The White Porcelain Sword doubled back. The sword light shifted from straight to slanted, and flashed slightly halfway through.

This time, the sword light did not land on the thickest part of the Formation patterns, but sliced right through the intersecting gap between two patterns.

Sshk.

The sound was extremely light.

Yet Ning Zhuo's eyes lit up.

"Now this has the flavor of a Sword Cultivator."

With another's memories serving as guidance and comparison, Ning Zhuo's practice yielded exceptionally good results.

He retracted the White Porcelain Sword and summoned the second one.

This was the Mechanism Rainbow-Splitting Sword.

This sword suited his tastes the best. The blade was composed of seven extremely thin segments interlocking together. Usually, it was a cyan-white longsword. Once activated, the blade segments would slightly separate, spreading out like a fan. The seven sword segments were as thin as cicada wings, with razor-sharp edges and extremely exquisite Mechanism grooves hidden within.

Ning Zhuo flicked his finger.

Clack.

A faint mechanical clicking sound rang out.

Immediately after, seven sword lights - red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, and purple - flew out simultaneously. Like seven slender rainbows of different colors, they crisscrossed and wove through the sword testing room.

The third flying sword was the Crimson Feather Sword.

The Crimson Feather Sword's blade was like the tail feather of a Vermilion Bird. A speck of golden spark constantly rested at the sword's tip, trailing thin, crimson feather-like light streaks as it flew. The moment it entered the sword testing room, the air grew a few degrees warmer, and a faint red glow surfaced on the Formation patterns of the four walls.

Ning Zhuo grasped the Crimson Feather Sword, feeling the fiery sword intent within it.

"The sword intent arrives first, the blade follows."

This was the advantage of sword intent.

It could suppress the enemy's mind and alter their state before the flying sword even struck. Against ghosts, Death Qi, and ghostly creatures, a fiery sword intent like that of the Crimson Feather Sword could wound the enemy even before the blade itself did.

He put away the Crimson Feather Sword and brought out the fourth one.

The Traceless Sword.

This sword was grayish-white and lusterless, like a piece of scrap iron. It was utterly inconspicuous; it produced no sound of piercing the air when flying, and only when extremely close would a thread of cold gray gleam appear.

It had no sword light, no sword gleam, no sword qi, and no sword intent.

It relied purely on the physical body of the sword to inflict damage.

The fifth item was the Edge-Folding Ruler.

Although called a flying sword, its shape was like a straight ruler. It was entirely dark cyan, with a flat surface and sharp edges. Three shallow sword patterns were carved on both its front and back.

This sword was suited for practicing sword momentum.

Sword momentum was not simply an attack. It was a form of positioning, a kind of suppression, a field-like sensation of "I set the rules here." The Edge-Folding Ruler, shaped like a straightedge, inherently carried the meaning of measuring, dividing, and obstructing. Using it for defense allowed Ning Zhuo to experience the defensive postures of the Sword Dao.

An attacking sword is like a question.

A defending sword is like an answer.

A horizontal sweep of the Edge-Folding Ruler was the answer to the enemy's offensive: This path is blocked!

The final one was the Circling Sword.

The blade was flexible and resilient, carrying a faint blue watery glow. A small pill bead was embedded at the end of the hilt. When flying, it emitted a faint medicinal fragrance, like a wisp of fresh air rising from an herb garden after the rain.

He had already lent the Water Element realm to Sun Lingtong. Using this water-attributed flying sword right now was somewhat strenuous for him.

When the Circling Sword flew up, its trajectory was not as smooth and flawless as when he had practiced before, and several turns appeared stiff. The blue watery light trailed behind the sword intermittently, like a small stream cut off by rocks.

Ning Zhuo did not frown, but instead earnestly felt this "unsmoothness."

Previously, with the Water Element realm on him, he would naturally understand how water flowed, how it coiled, and how to use softness to overcome hardness. Now that that enlightenment was gone, he could only rely on the Sword Cultivator's memories, his Alchemy Dao bodily sensations, and his spiritual energy manipulation to slowly re-explore it, bit by bit.

It was very slow, but also very solid.

At the end of this day's practice, Ning Zhuo kept his eyes closed for a long time.

He discovered that his comprehensive Five Elements realm had indeed fallen. But precisely because of this, he experienced much less extraneous interference when practicing the sword.

Sword light was just sword light, sword qi was just sword qi, sword momentum was just sword momentum, and sword intent was just sword intent. They were no longer immediately dismantled into complex relationships by the Five Elements principles. Instead, they landed in his perception in the most direct, sharpest, and most distinct manner.

The Metal Element realm he had retained continuously sparked Inspiration during this process.

The nature of metal governed killing, and it also governed restraint.

Metal could cut down others, and it could also cut down oneself.

Metal could unleash outward sharpness, and it could also conceal its edge and return to unity.

Ning Zhuo took out a Jade Slip and recorded down the many good Inspirations and ideas one by one.

The youth's expression was calm, yet a bright radiance shone between his brows.

The Sword Dao was not his primary cultivation path, but it could already serve as his sharp weapon!
