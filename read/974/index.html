Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 974: The Foundation Establishment Representatives

Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor.

The mountain path outside the cave manor was quiet and solitary. A faint tide of clouds flowed slowly along the bluestone steps, wrapping around the mountainside like a silent strip of white silk.

A streak of escaping light descended from the heavens. Clothes fluttered in the wind, and jade pendants struck against each other, emitting a clear, melodious chime.

The visitor walked up to the cave manor's entrance, retracted the spiritual light around his body, lightened his footsteps, and submitted a visiting card to announce his name.

Ning Zhuo sat upright deep within the cave manor.

Gongsun Yan stepped forward to report. Carrying the visiting card, he walked up to Ning Zhuo and said in a low voice, "Young Master, Situ Xing has arrived first."

Ning Zhuo glanced at the visiting card and reached out to take it.

Situ Xing's handwriting was sharp, yet the strokes were deeply concealed and restrained, like a sword hidden in its sheath.

The surface of the card shimmered with faint starlight. As soon as the pad of Ning Zhuo's finger touched it, a chill seeped into his skin like a fine spring. He said, "Please invite him in."

Gongsun Yan acknowledged the order and withdrew.

Situ Xing wore a dark robe with a waist sash as pitch-black as ink, and intricate star patterns embroidered on his cuffs. The longsword on his back was unsheathed, yet a clear, cold sword intent already sliced through the air of the cave manor with his every step.

Starlight fell between his brows and eyes, further highlighting the cold sternness of his face.

"Brother Ning," Situ Xing cupped his hands in greeting, his movements simple and clean.

Ning Zhuo stood up to return the gesture, smiling. "I never expected Brother Situ to be the first to arrive."

Situ Xing's mouth twitched as if he wanted to smile, but in the end, only a faint, almost imperceptible curve appeared. "If I were any slower, Shen Xi and Lin Jinglong would have snatched the lead right out from under me."

Ning Zhuo invited him to sit.

Situ Xing rested his hands on his knees, his knuckles tightening slightly. "Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect - I heard of their many battle achievements back when I first began cultivating. The three of them share life and death, their foundations linked as one. If an ordinary Golden Core cultivator runs into them, even finding a chance to escape is difficult."

Situ Xing's voice grew heavier. "Brother Ning, you and I crossed blows once, and I also witnessed your three battles with Liu Jinke. I can say for certain that you still have many things you haven't brought out."

"But this time, what you are facing is the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect."

Situ Xing shook his head and took a small box out from his sleeve.

The box was thin and exquisite, no more than three inches long and about two inches wide, entirely crafted from star-patterned ebony.

The lid was plain and clean, devoid of complex carvings.

Situ Xing placed the wooden box on the table and pushed it in front of Ning Zhuo. "This item is called the Star Scale Calamity-Shifting Pieces."

Ning Zhuo's gaze narrowed slightly.

Situ Xing opened the wooden box.

Lying quietly inside the box were two Go pieces.

One black and one white. The black piece was as smooth and round as the night, with a very faint glimmer of starlight bobbing on its surface, like a distant river of stars reflected in a deep well.

The white piece was as clear and bright as frost, its edges glowing with a fine silver halo. Upon closer inspection, one could see concentric circular patterns embedded within it - it was a miniature star compass.

The two pieces were clearly motionless, yet there was a vague thread of Qi connecting them.

As the starlight within the cave manor fell upon them, the white piece trembled slightly, and the black piece emitted a very low hum in the box, like the lingering vibration of a plucked string.

Situ Xing reached out with two fingers and pinched the black piece.

His fingers were long and slender, the pads bearing thin calluses left by a Sword Cultivator's years of sharpening blades. The black piece flipped between his fingers. As starlight fell onto the surface of the piece, it seemed to be slowly swallowed by it, melting away into nothingness.

"Among the myriad arts of Cultivation, the art of Go emphasizes layout, vital points, ko struggles, and sacrificing pieces. This treasure draws upon that concept of 'ko' - a Calamity," Situ Xing said. "The white piece serves as the anchor for returning, while the black piece acts as the trigger for the move. Before use, you must first place the white piece in a safe location and use your spiritual energy to nail down its position. In a moment of critical danger, activate the black piece, and you can shift your body back to where the white piece is."

As he spoke, his fingertip flicked lightly.

The black piece drew a fine, bright arc in midair and landed on the table.

Ding.

With a clear ring, a palm-sized star-patterned Go board suddenly appeared on the table. The nineteen vertical and horizontal silver lines flashed and vanished, and the starlight in the cave manor shifted slightly along with it, as if some invisible orientation had been tugged by the piece.

A trace of interest lit up in Ning Zhuo's eyes. "How far can it span?"

Situ Xing replied, "It is a Golden Core magic treasure; its limit is three hundred li. If used in a place heavily layered with Formations like the main gate of the Myriad Manifestations Sect, the distance will be suppressed to a few dozen li, or even less. If you encounter a Nascent Soul-level powerhouse using their spiritual energy to lock down space, whether you succeed or not will depend on the strength of their lockdown. If you encounter a Dao Artifact... don't expect it to force its way through."

He spoke crisply and decisively, without a single word of exaggeration.

Ning Zhuo nodded. Situ Xing's candidness actually added to the item's credibility.

Situ Xing withdrew the black piece back into the box. "This is not an offensive treasure. It only has one complete shifting opportunity. After activation, the black piece will crack, and the white piece will shatter. If it's only used to shift small objects, it might last a few more times. But if used to shift a person, it only survives a single Calamity."

A single Calamity. There are ko on the Go board, and there are Calamities between the life and death of a Cultivator. A single difference in thought leads to a difference as vast as heaven and earth.

Ning Zhuo reached out and gently stroked the edge of the wooden box.

The ebony felt slightly cool to the touch. Starlight hid deep within the wood grain, quiet and solemn.

"Many thanks, Brother Situ," Ning Zhuo said.

Situ Xing stood up. He had arrived swiftly, and his departure was equally decisive. Just as he was about to step out the door, he paused for a moment.

"Ning Zhuo." He didn't turn around, and his voice wasn't loud, but every word was distinct. "If you really face off against Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, don't think about fighting for a momentary surge of pride. On the Go board, only those who survive have the right to play the endgame."

Ning Zhuo replied, "I accept your advice."

No sooner had Situ Xing left than Shen Xi arrived on his heels.

His aura was entirely different from Situ Xing's.

Shen Xi wore an ink-green long robe. The fabric was as smooth as if it had just been tailored, and intricate Nine Palaces patterns were embroidered neatly along the cuffs and collar.

After Shen Xi entered the cave, his gaze first fell on the un-stored star-patterned wooden box. His eyelashes fluttered slightly, but he immediately returned to normal.

Ning Zhuo made no attempt to hide it.

Shen Xi smiled. "Haha, I let Situ Xing beat me by a step."

Though he carried a smile on his lips, a flash of helplessness flickered in the depths of his eyes.

Both he and Situ Xing were the Foundation Establishment frontmen for super clans. They shared a deep friendship, but they were also secretly in competition. Especially regarding the matter of Ning Zhuo, the weight of being the first to give a gift versus giving it later was completely different.

Shen Xi quickly tucked away these thoughts and cupped his hands. "Brother Ning, the winds and waves are rising abruptly outside. I came here this time, firstly, on the orders of the elders in my family, and secondly, to bring a token of my own goodwill."

Ning Zhuo invited him to sit.

Shen Xi sat with an upright posture, his back slightly straight. He took out a square plate and held it in the palms of both hands.

The square plate was only about a foot wide, entirely pieced together from green-white jade and profound bronze. The surface of the plate was divided into Nine Palaces, and each palace had a small lattice window. Tiny talisman beads floated within these windows, varying in color. Some looked like blooming ink, some like sinking gold sand, and others like a drop of flame solidified in jade.

In the very center of the Nine Palaces, a colorless crystal was embedded. Pale smoke circulated endlessly within the crystal, sometimes transforming into palace gates, sometimes into winding corridors, and sometimes shattering into thousands of motes of light.

Shen Xi placed the formation plate on the table. The moment the bottom of the plate touched the surface, nine fine lines spread outward, weaving like a spiderweb.

In that instant, the teacup, wooden box, and Jade Slip on the table were all shrouded in a thin layer of rippling water, their positions shifting imperceptibly by a fraction of an inch.

Ning Zhuo's eyelids twitched slightly. He clearly hadn't moved an inch, yet he felt the spatial orientation beneath his feet silently distort by half an inch, as if gently nudged by an invisible hand.

Shen Xi raised his hand and gently pressed down on the formation plate. The sense of distortion immediately vanished, as if it had never existed.

"The Nine Palaces Misstepping Formation Plate," Shen Xi introduced. "A Golden Core-level formation plate. It leans toward disrupting the enemy, stalling, and buying time."

Ning Zhuo leaned forward to take a closer look.

Shen Xi pointed a finger at one of the Nine Palaces. "Once this plate is deployed, it can divide the surrounding ten to thirty zhang area into Nine Palaces. When an enemy steps into it, their sense of direction, distance, and the flow of their spiritual energy will suffer subtle interference. It does not excel at dealing lethal damage, but its advantage lies in its rapid activation. If the enemy blindly charges forward, they will constantly step into the wrong palace. Though they think they are moving forward, they are actually skewing to the side. What is clearly close at hand will require them to take unnecessary detours to reach."

As he spoke, he tapped the central crystal again.

Smoke suddenly rose from the formation plate, transforming into nine small doors. The doors spun, with light and shadows floating behind each one.

Amidst the light and shadows, a Cultivator could be seen raising a sword and charging straight ahead. But the moment they took a step, their body involuntarily veered to the side, delayed for a single breath by the Nine Palaces' array lines. Just one breath.

Yet in a battle, one breath was enough to trade for a life.

Shen Xi's expression was serious. "Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect have a ferocious offense, and their foundations are interconnected. If you want to defeat them head-on, this formation plate won't help you. But if you merely want to buy a few breaths of breathing room, it might prove useful."

Ning Zhuo looked at the formation plate and nodded slightly.

His mechanism system frequently required dispatching, deploying, and seamlessly connecting - swarms of mechanical birds, Xuan Weapon Armor, mechanical puppets, human puppets, and Spells all possessed their own rhythms.

When facing opponents like Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, who disregarded life and death and applied relentless pressure, what he feared most was losing his rhythm, and what he lacked most was time to adjust.

This Nine Palaces Misstepping Formation Plate could buy him exactly those few breaths of respite.

Ning Zhuo looked up. "Fellow Daoist Shen is very thoughtful."

Shen Xi gently shook his head. "The gift is not considered heavy."

This formation plate leaned toward life-preservation and stalling. It demonstrated the Shen Family's willingness to maintain their goodwill, yet didn't directly pit them against Flowing Cloud Peak on Ning Zhuo's behalf. The balance struck here was exquisitely calibrated.

When Shen Xi stood up to take his leave, he left behind a parting sentence: "Brother Ning, the elders in my family asked me to pass on a message: 'Only by preserving the green hills can one build the stronghold.' The foundation of the Naming Stronghold actually rests entirely upon you."

Ning Zhuo nodded in thanks.

Lin Jinglong was the latest to arrive among the three.

Today, he wore a green martial outfit with tightly bound cuffs, and a long, slender wooden box hung at his waist.

He was tall and slender, with clear and bright features. When he walked, his shoulders and back stretched out like a pine tree, carrying the unique briskness and pride of the Lin Clan's descendants.

The moment he entered, he spotted the Star Scale Calamity-Shifting Pieces on the table, then glanced at the Nine Palaces Misstepping Formation Plate beside it. He immediately raised an eyebrow and burst into a hearty laugh.

He took the wooden box off his waist and placed it on the table.

The box was not made of ordinary wood. Its surface was covered in a layer of green scale-like patterns, resembling the bark of some ancient vine.

When the lid was opened, it revealed a layer of moist spiritual soil lining the inside, with three tree seeds resting within it.

Each seed was only the size of a knuckle, with a green-black outer shell. Their patterns looked like coiled little dragons, complete with lifelike scales.

A faint halo emanated from the edges of the seeds. When one leaned close, the extremely faint sound of roots uncurling could be heard, like three tiny hearts beating slowly in the depths of the wooden box.

Lin Jinglong said, "These are Azure Hornless Dragon Armor-Embracing Seeds."

He reached out and picked one up. The seed rolled slightly in his palm, and the green scale patterns on its surface lit up accordingly.

The Wood Element spiritual energy within the cave manor was drawn to it. Several wisps of green light swam out from the table legs, wooden chairs, and bamboo slips, converging into the seed's shell.

"This seed is activated by Wood Element spiritual energy; it will grow the moment it enters the soil. Normally, it is just a seed, but in critical moments, it can transform into coiling Azure Hornless Dragon roots that wrap around your body for protection, propping up layers of wooden armor. If a powerful enemy gets into close range, it can block a wave of lethal attacks for you."

As Lin Jinglong spoke, a wisp of green spiritual energy shot out from his fingertip.

The tree seed landed on the table and actually took root on the jade surface. The fine roots didn't damage the table; they merely spread slowly along the lines of spiritual energy.

In an instant, more than a dozen green roots coiled together to form a small rattan armor. The scales on the armor overlapped layer by layer, and in the very center, two branches lifted upwards like dragon horns, appearing majestic and formidable.

Lin Jinglong looked at Ning Zhuo. "This item leans toward defense, and it can also synergize with your Wood Element Spells. If you first set up a Wood Element environment, the Azure Hornless Dragon Armor-Embracing Seeds will be nourished by external wood qi and can hold out much longer. If you rely solely on your own spiritual energy to fuel them, they will be consumed much faster."

Ning Zhuo reached out and touched the edge of the rattan armor. The armor shrank back slightly. Its scales were flexible and tough, and a thick surge of life force surged secretly within it, like spring wood hiding thunder, capable of rapidly healing itself after taking a hit.

"Good stuff," Ning Zhuo said.

The corners of Lin Jinglong's lips curled up. "It's good that you know its value."

After saying this, he restrained his expression slightly and lowered his voice. "Brother Ning, I won't beat around the bush with you. Since the Lin Clan has gotten involved in the Naming Stronghold affair, we won't withdraw so easily. If you die and the Naming Stronghold scatters, all of our previous actions will become a joke. People like us will become subjects of gossip."

He paused and looked directly into Ning Zhuo's eyes. "So, you have to survive."

Ning Zhuo gently closed the wooden box and smiled. "I'll do my best."

Lin Jinglong frowned. "Doing your best isn't enough."

Ning Zhuo let out a short laugh. "Then I'll try my hardest to survive."

Only then did Lin Jinglong nod.

The cave manor returned to silence once more.

Three treasures sat on the table: the Star Scale Calamity-Shifting Pieces, the Nine Palaces Misstepping Formation Plate, and the Azure Hornless Dragon Armor-Embracing Seeds. One black, one green, one white. Their Qi were completely different, but by prior agreement, they all leaned toward life-preservation, stalling, and defense.

Ning Zhuo looked at them, already turning over his thoughts in his mind.

The actions of Situ Xing, Shen Xi, and Lin Jinglong were all within Ning Zhuo's expectations. Since they were already involved in the Naming Stronghold affair, they did not want all their previous efforts to be wasted. Investing in Ning Zhuo was nothing more than an extension of their prior development strategies.

The items were good, but the level of support was restrained.

It also betrayed their view of the upcoming battle between Ning Zhuo and Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect - none of them favored him! They were all just helping Ning Zhuo stay alive.

"But this is exactly the style in which large clans operate. They show camaraderie, yet maintain boundaries. They are willing to place bets, but they always leave themselves an out."

"Hehe."

Thinking of this, Ning Zhuo chuckled softly, holding a sliver of anticipation for the next visitor.

Pi Fujie arrived.

"Young Master Ning, I have come uninvited. I apologize for my presumption, truly presumptuous!"

He wore a luxurious leather robe. The surface of the robe was a dark red hue, and fine black leather was stitched along the collar's edge. As he walked, faint demonic patterns flowed across the fabric like hidden currents in an underground river.

Ning Zhuo invited him to sit.

After sitting down, Pi Fujie leaned forward slightly, displaying a deliberate sense of intimacy. "Young Master Ning and I once had a misunderstanding, which was later resolved as we turned from enemies into friends. When this matter was reported back to the clan, the elders had much praise for me. Now that Young Master Ning is in trouble, if I were to shrink back, wouldn't it make my previous sincerity look like empty pretense?"

While he spoke, his eyes unceasingly observed Ning Zhuo's expression.

Ning Zhuo wore a smile. His eyes were as clear as a deep pool beneath the moon, betraying not the slightest ripple of emotion.

Unable to discern anything at all, Pi Fujie cursed inwardly at how difficult this person was to deal with.

He stopped beating around the bush and waved his hand.

A shadow puppet flew out from his sleeve and fluttered lightly onto the table.
