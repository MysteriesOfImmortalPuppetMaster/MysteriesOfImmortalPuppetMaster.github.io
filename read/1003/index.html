Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 1003: Yang Dao Grandmaster

Clang.

Clang.

Clang.

Waking Ning Zhuo up were the continuous hammer strikes echoing within this bizarre domain.

He slowly opened his eyes.

The first thing that caught his sight was the bizarre sky, resembling a giant, overturned furnace lid, its inner walls covered with layers of forging patterns.

Some of the forging patterns looked like hammer marks, some like tongues of fire, and some like the textures of mountain ranges. Whenever a muffled hammer strike sounded from afar, those patterns would faintly light up, as if an invisible blacksmith outside of heaven and earth was swinging a massive hammer, repeatedly pounding and tempering this domain.

Beneath him was the thick, yellow earth. The divine domain aura of True Lord Wutu of Prison Suppression permeated everywhere.

Ning Zhuo was lying right in the very center of this bizarre domain.

The hammer strikes made the air subtly undulate. With his back pressed against the warm yellow earth, the bones, meridians, and divine sea beneath Ning Zhuo's robes still retained waves of a tingling sensation.

His flesh and blood were burning hot, and his soul was resonating.

"I previously cast the Myriad Phenomena Cradle to absorb the true intent of the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill, and ended up fainting?"

Ning Zhuo did not immediately get up.

He first closed his eyes and slowly exhaled a breath of turbid qi.

This exhaled breath unexpectedly transformed into a wisp of pale golden light in mid-air. When the golden light rose half a foot high, it scattered, like morning light shining through a window lattice, quietly dissipating into the air.

Ning Zhuo opened his eyes again, his gaze clear and bright.

He could already feel his own progress.

Yang Dao Grandmaster!

He had finally truly stepped onto this level.

It was different now.

The Yang Dao realm at the Grandmaster-level allowed him to see all things emerging from the gloom, sleepers awakening, true principles being declared, and heaven and earth bringing a certain answer into the light.

It was as if in a long night, people were hiding, objects were gathering dust, and the truth was obscured by layers upon layers.

Then, a ray of heavenly light descended.

The stones revealed their textures, the blades of grass revealed their veins, ghosts and demons revealed their true forms, lies revealed their flaws, and the sleeping people opened their eyes. The answers they were unwilling to face in their hearts were laid out right before them, leaving no room for escape.

Yang is manifestation.

Yang is awakening.

Yang is declaration.

Yang is also bestowment!

It burns itself to illuminate all things. It transforms itself into light and heat, allowing vegetation to grow, warming the cold earth, forcing Yin evils to retreat, and awakening human hearts from chaos.

Ning Zhuo lightly bent his finger, and a speck of sunlight circled his fingertip before falling into his palm. It was warm, bright, and quiet, yet it contained an imposing and indisputable significance.

If used for artifact refining, mechanical constructs would gain a wisp of spiritual light capable of distinguishing the gloom.

If used for forging Medicinal Pills, the pill's medicinal properties for hanging onto life, returning Yang, and breaking evil would become far more harmonious.

If used to cast techniques, yin souls, evil thoughts, ghostly aura, and foul poisons would all be illuminated, revealing their traces.

Ning Zhuo's mind stirred as he felt the immense advantages brought by being a Yang Dao Grandmaster.

Looking back at Yang-attribute Cultivation Arts now, he would no longer just learn them by rote, nor would he merely improve upon existing methods. He could now rely on the essence of the Yang Dao to deduce new minor Yang-attribute techniques on the spot. He could connect and even wield the principles of light, heat, vitality, manifestation, and judgment.

This was a Grandmaster.

Grasping the pivot, understanding the root.

When a path of reasoning reached this stage, it could naturally open up its own tributaries.

Aside from the Yang Dao, there was also the Pill Dao.

"My Pill Dao realm has reached the Master-level!" Ning Zhuo felt a surge of pleasant surprise.

He carefully experienced it, only to feel: there was no pill furnace around him, yet pill furnaces were everywhere.

The air was the furnace wall, the spiritual energy was the charcoal fire, his thoughts were the pestle, and the Five Elements were the medicinal ingredients. Vitality, Death Qi, flesh and blood, Divine Soul, lifespan, toxicity, and aroma - everything rose, converged, separated, and settled within this invisible pill chamber.

"The Nine Fires Dragon Lord once used heaven and earth as his furnace. I understand it now. With practice, I can do it too!"

Ning Zhuo made this judgment in his heart.

His previous Pill Dao realm was only at the Artisan level. Now, it had bypassed the Renowned Master level and stepped into the Master level.

An Artisan refining pills could skillfully process materials, control the heat, complete standard procedures, and stably forge qualified Medicinal Pills.

A Renowned Master refining pills could optimize pill recipes, improve procedures, handle complex medicinal properties, and allow the finished pill to bear their own heat-control style. Skill gives birth to ingenuity; when a furnace of Medicinal Pills is produced, those in the know can recognize the refiner's techniques from the pill patterns, aroma, and the flow of medicinal properties.

A Master refining pills, however, develops intuition.

Ning Zhuo had this exact intuition at this moment.

He took out the One Life Hanging Pill.

That Medicinal Pill was translucent jade-white. Inside, a thread of gold resembled a swimming dragon, and nine pill patterns alternated between bright and dim.

Previously, when Ning Zhuo looked at it, he only felt that Nascent Soul-level Medicinal Pills were profoundly mysterious. When the medicinal aroma entered his nose, even his soul felt as if it were being held up by a warm palm.

Looking at it now, the internal structure of the Medicinal Pill was faintly unfolding in his mind.

One Life Hanging, the key lies in "Hanging."

It did not revive the dead on the spot, nor did it forcefully reverse life and death. It sustained the last breath when the Physical Body turned to mud and the soul was about to scatter. That wisp of golden thread was the life-hanging thread, and also the deepest principle of the Medicinal Pill.

Ning Zhuo could even deduce several low-grade alternative methods.

For example, using Yang-Returning True Water as a primer, Life-Hanging Grass as the main ingredient, and supplementing it with a small amount of Soul-Locking Wood Shavings, he could perhaps refine a Foundation Establishment-level "Minor Life-Hanging Pellet". Its medicinal power would be far inferior to the One Life Hanging Pill, but it would be enough to stall for three to five breaths in critical moments.

Three to five breaths could very well exchange for a life on the battlefield.

This kind of deduction did not require having a pill recipe in hand.

A Pill Dao Master already had a profound, systematic understanding of medicinal properties, heat control, and pill principles, allowing them to innovate within existing frameworks. The finished product might not be perfect, but it would already carry a personal temperament that others could not easily imitate.

A trace of joy appeared in the depths of Ning Zhuo's eyes.

Being a Pill Dao Master would also be very helpful to him in repairing the Naming Fire Furnace next.

"But it hasn't reached the Pill Dao Grandmaster realm. The Yang Dao has already broken through to Grandmaster; why hasn't the Pill Dao?"

Ning Zhuo thought for a moment, then couldn't help but chuckle.

"I'm being insatiable, wanting more after having already gained so much."

Now that he had advanced to become a Pill Dao Master, looking again at the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill gave him a profound realization.

"This pill was primarily refined to bear the Yang Dao realm, not the Pill Dao. Therefore, the true intent regarding the Pill Dao is much less than the Yang Dao true intent."

"The Pure Yang Palace aimed to forge a legacy treasure. What is passed down is the Pure Yang Dao; the Pill Dao is merely incidental."

"Developing the recipe for the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill is already an extraordinary achievement. To optimize it further from this foundation to perfectly load the Pill Dao true intent?"

Ning Zhuo shook his head in his heart.

Previously, he couldn't perceive this level of difficulty, but now that he had become a Pill Dao Master, he could feel that the difficulty was astronomical. Just the recipe of the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill alone was enough to leave him breathless in admiration.

To further improve upon this recipe would be as hard as ascending to the heavens!

"Of the Four Great Arts of Cultivation - Pill Forging, Formation Arranging, Artifact Refining, and Talisman Making - I already have two that have reached the Master-level. Artifact Refining has reached the Renowned Master level, thanks to the true intent irrigation of Lord Qingwu. Only Talisman Making is still spinning its wheels at the basic Artisan level."

Ning Zhuo revealed a slight look of oddity.

Speaking of which, the Ning Clan he came from was famous for Talisman Making. Yet among Ning Zhuo's skills, Talisman Making was ironically his weakest.

"Even though my Pill Forging is only at the Master level, it is already precious enough."

"Even within a massive scale like the Myriad Manifestations Sect, it is enough to make the masses look at me in a new light."

Finally, there was the Fire Element Grandmaster realm!

This layer of harvest made him particularly surprised.

He originally always believed that his Fire Element realm had already reached a considerably high level.

He had obtained the legacy of the Five Elements Divine Lord, and witnessed the ferocity of the fire attribute, its light and heat, and its awakening. The Naming Fire Furnace, the Blood Ember Fire Seed, the Nine Fires Dragon Lord's Nine Fires, Chunyangzi's Three Thousand Sutras Grand Fire - all kinds of Fire Element profundities had unfolded before his eyes. He felt that on the path of the Fire Element, he had already seen the scenery at the summit.

However, after the true intent of the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill was completely swallowed by the Myriad Phenomena Cradle, he realized that there was still a massive expanse of unfamiliar territory within the Fire Element.

"The successive sect leaders of the Pure Yang Palace had an extremely refined understanding of fire."

"The very same wisp of fire, when placed in a pill furnace, becomes the surging of medicinal properties. When on the battlefield, it is a blade of slaughter. When applied to a patient, it is the power of returning Yang. When cast upon evil entities, it is a catastrophic disaster. And when landing on a Dao Heart, it might become a bright lamp that illuminates and shatters delusional thoughts."

In the past, when Ning Zhuo looked at fire, he often used the relationships of the Five Elements to govern it.

Wood generates Fire, Fire generates Earth. Water counters Fire, Fire counters Metal.

What he saw was the flow, the balance, the attraction and restriction between the Five Elements.

This was looking at the Fire Element from the perspective of the Five Elements realm.

The Fire Element true intent within the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill, however, targeted the Fire Element itself exclusively.

With a thought of Artifact Refining, it fiercely presents itself as a martial fire in the furnace.

With a thought of Pill Forging, it becomes as delicate as the civil fire of a medicine furnace.

With a thought of breaking evil, it acts majestically, like the sun illuminating a dark gutter.

With a thought of killing an enemy, it becomes as sharp as a fire blade unsheathed.

A hint of doubt surfaced in the depths of Ning Zhuo's eyes.

The Fire Element was already at the Grandmaster realm. But exactly what level his Five Elements realm was at remained an unknown. The latter was profound, heavy, and vast; it was difficult to measure using the scale of the single Fire Element.

Ning Zhuo pondered, "The Five Elements Divine Lord was an ancient figure. In his era, he reached the pinnacle of heaven and earth, drawing down a calamity because he was overly powerful."

"But time flies. With the Cultivation World developing up to this day, how could it possibly stall in its progress?"

"The Fire Element has naturally undergone massive development. The Pure Yang Palace is a modern Sect. Even though it is currently in decline, its successive sect leaders must have accumulated many of the latest concepts regarding the Fire Element."

"There's one more thing."

"The Five Elements realm refers to all Five Elements. It elaborates on the relationships between them."

"The Fire Element realm refers specifically to the Fire Element. It solely elaborates on the principles of the Fire Element."

"My Five Elements realm was originally profound, but my proficiency in the individual elements was merely outstanding. It's similar to how my Yang Dao realm has now reached the Grandmaster-level, yet my path of Yin remains shallow. Because the Yin-Yang realm is backed by the Yang Dao realm, it is higher than the path of Yin, but lower than the Yang Dao."

As this realization surfaced in his mind, the bit of doubt in Ning Zhuo's eyes finally faded considerably.

In the sky of the domain, the giant divine phantom of True Lord Wutu of Prison Suppression slowly moved.

His figure was as tall as a mountain, ancient and substantial. His deep, emotionless eyes looked down at Ning Zhuo on the ground, who seemed like a mere ant.

Ning Zhuo cupped his hands in greeting, "That I achieved success this time is entirely reliant on the True Lord's protection."

"That you were able to withstand it is your own capability," True Lord Wutu of Prison Suppression said. "You used the Myriad Phenomena Cradle very well."

Ning Zhuo's gaze flickered slightly.

True Lord Wutu of Prison Suppression continued, "This method allows you to re-observe all phenomena with an infant's heart, avoiding the obscuration of old knowledge and temporarily freeing yourself from the curse of knowledge. This is quite rare."

"However, this method also has its limits."

Ning Zhuo listened earnestly, his eyes moving subtly.

True Lord Wutu of Prison Suppression said, "In the future, as you master more and more realms and your foundation grows deeper, this technique will gradually become unable to cover everything. Its effect will grow smaller and smaller, until it becomes completely useless."

Ning Zhuo thought about it for a moment and nodded in agreement.

If the Myriad Phenomena Cradle didn't have this drawback, wouldn't Cultivators possessing the early wisdom Innate Talent be able to soar smoothly, skyrocketing all the way? Forgetting old knowledge today, relearning new paths tomorrow, learning more and more, comprehending faster and faster, and ultimately becoming incredibly powerful, enough to leave a heavy and colorful mark in history.

Yet in reality, the Myriad Phenomena Cradle was completely obscure and unknown.

This in itself was the proof.

Ning Zhuo cupped his hands. "This junior understands. The Myriad Phenomena Cradle is suited for early-to-mid-stage development. Upon reaching the later stages, when the foundation is too thick and the realms are too heavy, it will no longer be effective."

"This junior wishes to ask, are there other methods similar to the Myriad Phenomena Cradle?"

True Lord Wutu of Prison Suppression said, "In the vast universe, there is no lack of wonders. Naturally, there are other methods. However..."

Changing the subject, he said, "Consider Qian Longzi, hailing from the Qian Clan of the Eastern Sea. In his youth, he had a pale gold dragon pattern on his forehead, and his pupils were like amber. This man had brilliant Innate Talent and a broad-minded disposition, but he had one flaw: he was too greedy for learning."

"Before the age of three hundred, he successively achieved Grandmaster realms in ten domains, including the Metal Element, Fire Element, Earth Element, Water Element, Wood Element, Beast Taming, and Talisman Making."

"That year, when a demon tide crashed against the shores of the Guanghai Kingdom, Qian Longzi established the Ten Tides Dragon Palace overnight. On the battlefield, he created seventy-two minor methods, interconnecting his ten Grandmaster realms, and forcibly defended three giant islands. Later generations revered him as the Perfect Dragon King, erected monuments to record his merits, and offered endless incense."

"But even as he cultivated to the Void Refinement-level, and right up until his lifespan ended, he failed to master an eleventh Grandmaster realm. If he had managed to break through, his title wouldn't be 'Perfect Dragon King'."

"Right before his death, he passed away in meditation at the Ten Tides Dragon Palace, leaving behind only one sentence: 'Knowledge is like a dragon; ten paths carried me into the blue clouds, but also entangled me and dragged me into the deep sea.'"

"When various paths become too strong, they end up suppressing any new path."

The domain fell silent.

Ning Zhuo didn't speak for a long time.

True Lord Wutu of Prison Suppression's example was extremely vivid and striking. The tale of the Perfect Dragon King, Qian Longzi, was considered common knowledge. Having read extensively, Ning Zhuo already knew about it.

The young man frowned slightly, drawing parallels to himself.

Formation Arranging, Artifact Refining, Pill Forging, Five Elements, Yang Dao, Mechanisms, Military Strategy... Every path was a harvest, yet in the future, all of them could become a restraint.

"Therefore, comprehensive realms like the Five Elements realm and the Yin-Yang realm can actually act as a mediating agent."

"Going too deep into a single path makes one prone to extremes."

"The Five Elements realm elaborates on the relationships among the Five Elements, allowing Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water to generate and counter each other, mutually providing checks and balances. The Yin-Yang realm elaborates on the relationship between Yin and Yang, which can also allow extreme Yang to recognize the existence of Yin."

"Usually, to reduce the curse of knowledge, such comprehensive realms will be the best buffer."

"Exalted God, is my analysis correct?"

Ning Zhuo tilted his head up and asked the sky.

True Lord Wutu of Prison Suppression nodded: "That is indeed the case. However, mastering these comprehensive realms is far more difficult than mastering a single path."

"Human power is limited, while heaven and earth are vast. Observing your intentions, it seems you wish to dabble in peripheral paths."

"When you learned the method of the Myriad Phenomena Cradle, you should have understood the folly of greed. Do you still want to dabble widely now, being a jack of all trades but master of none?"

Ning Zhuo sighed, "What the Exalted God says is extremely insightful. This junior will engrave it in his heart."

"I major in Mechanism Arts, and my passion lies exactly there. In the future, even if I dabble in multiple paths, I will choose carefully and not act arbitrarily."
