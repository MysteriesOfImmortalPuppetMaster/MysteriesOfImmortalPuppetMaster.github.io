Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 998: Myriad Phenomena Cradle!

Ning Zhuo had already obtained the Myriad Phenomena Cradle.

During the matter at Nanming Stronghold, he had demonstrated his worth and, through Kong Ran's connections, attracted Kong Zhaoming's investment.

"Since I obtained this technique, I have only skimmed through it a few times. I haven't truly studied it, let alone cast it."

Ning Zhuo pondered carefully and once again found himself facing a difficult hurdle.

The Myriad Phenomena Cradle was a technique cast through Innate Talent, and it wasn't a Five Elements technique. Thus, it wouldn't be easy for Ning Zhuo to familiarize himself with it. The Five Viscera Temple Spirit Art, which he had always relied upon, could not provide any help here.

"Therefore, I must study it thoroughly first!"

Time was running out, and Ning Zhuo was uncertain about the outcome of his study or the effects of the divine art. But other than this, he had no other good options and could only take this gamble.

Suspending his hair from the beam!

A cinnabar hemp rope flew out of his storage bag, soaring into the air like a spirit snake.

Since there was no beam here, the hemp rope coiled around itself several times in mid-air, forming a loop that securely tied to the roots of Ning Zhuo's hair. A continuous, subtle, yet clear pulling sensation came from his hair, preventing him from sinking into drowsiness. It kept his mind constantly focused, not allowing his thoughts to easily scatter like loose sand.

Piercing the thigh!

Ning Zhuo grasped a boxwood awl and swiftly struck, driving the tip into the inner side of his left thigh.

The awl pierced an inch into his flesh. A sharp pain instantly erupted like a cold spring breaking through the soil, penetrating his skin and shooting straight into the depths of his divine sea. It immediately infused his thoughts with a sharp clarity, making it much easier to break through mental barriers and ponder difficult concepts.

The wind of literary and martial arts rose.

A wisp of literary wind, like pale ink turning into smoke, leisurely circled his body. The sound of the wind was as subtle as spring silkworms nibbling on mulberry leaves.

Whenever his divine sense was excessively consumed, the wind would transform into Literary Qi, infiltrating his Upper Dantian strand by strand, acting like life-extending lamp oil.

The Luo Shu.

Every time spiritual light flashed, the page would softly glow, like abacus beads being flicked into their correct positions by invisible fingers.

Ning Zhuo then took out the Intent Brush and dipped it into a bit of bitter ink. The ink's color was as deep and gloomy as lacquer.

The bitterness, like a living creature, poured into his divine sea along his divine sense. It was as heavy as mercury and as cold as iron, pressing down on him so much that his breathing slightly stagnated, and even the tip of his tongue tasted a faint astringency.

Ning Zhuo began to copy the Myriad Phenomena Cradle!

A tiny character floated in the air, its ink marks as lean and clear as a newly sprouted bamboo joint.

The handwriting gradually became fuller, revealing a nascent, unpolished charm.

More and more characters appeared, suspended in the void. They were neither flashy nor ostentatious, containing Ning Zhuo's understanding of this Innate Talent technique.

Ning Zhuo consecutively studied it five or six times, and his grasp of it increased significantly. It wasn't until he felt that studying it one more time would yield minimal progress that he decisively stopped.

After resting for a moment, Ning Zhuo began his first attempt to cast the Myriad Phenomena Cradle.

Ning Zhuo closed his eyes.

In the divine sea within his Upper Dantian, countless small mirrors hovered in the clear, bright sky.

Ning Zhuo silently activated his Early Wisdom Innate Talent.

At first, it was very slow, like someone turning up a small lamp in the dark night. The light wasn't intense, but it was extremely pure. It shone into Ning Zhuo's divine sea, causing the surfaces of those small mirrors to ripple with watery light.

Ning Zhuo silently recited the incantation in his heart.

Innate Talent casting - Myriad Phenomena Cradle!

Deep within his oversized head, a hint of refreshing coolness slowly surfaced.

White mist generated, like incredibly soft silk threads, gently wrapping around Ning Zhuo's divine mirrors. They simply covered them tenderly, like a mother draping a blanket over her child - first the feet, then the hands, and finally up to the chest.

The divine sea gradually grew tranquil.

The roaring of the Five Elements faded away.

The clicking of mechanisms also faded away.

The sounds of formation deductions gradually ceased.

The subtle crackling of flickering pill fires vanished without a trace.

The judgments and explanations that would normally surface in his mind at any moment were like scrolls being gently closed and placed into a deep cabinet. The cabinet door wasn't locked, but it was blocked by a layer of soft, watery light. Ning Zhuo could still feel their existence, but he couldn't reach them for the time being.

Ning Zhuo felt a sense of weakness.

This weakness was not an injury. Instead, it was more like a newly awakened infant - limbs soft, bones lacking strength, and even his breathing carrying a fragile feebleness.

True Lord Wutu Prison-Suppressing observed silently, noticing that the light in Ning Zhuo's eyes changed at this moment.

Normally, Ning Zhuo's eyes always hid a sense of calculation. Even when he smiled, there was a meticulous layer of scheming, like thin ice covering the surface of water - beneath the clarity lay a chilling cold.

At this moment, that layer of thin ice melted away.

His gaze was blank and clear, yet astonishingly sharp, like a newly formed mountain spring. Every grain of white sand at the bottom could be seen clearly, and the slightest breeze, a speck of light, or a mote of dust from the outside world could leave a vivid reflection in his eyes.

With a slight shift in thought from True Lord Wutu Prison-Suppressing, a gust of wind swept across the flat ground.

The wind blew over gently.

Ning Zhuo blinked.

The wind brushed past his cuffs, and the fabric made a subtle rustling sound. The sound was so soft it was almost inaudible, but to Ning Zhuo's ears at this moment, it sounded like one tender leaf brushing against another.

A tiny wave of pleasant surprise rippled in his heart.

"Is this... the wind?"

So the wind actually touches people.

So sleeves actually respond to the wind.

So the air also has invisible fingers, gently caressing all things in the world.

Ning Zhuo looked down at his own hand, unexpectedly finding those five fingers exceptionally novel. The bending of the joints, the pulling of the skin, and the palm prints looked like shallow riverbeds in the dim light.

"Is this... my hand?"

So a hand actually has five fingers.

So a body of flesh and blood is actually this exquisite yet unfamiliar.

So life is actually this intimate and moving!

Joy swelled in Ning Zhuo's heart.

That joy was tender and pure, like the tip of a sprout just breaking through the soil in early spring - carrying a bit of timidity, yet possessing an unstoppable urge to grow upward.

Everything became fresh and new.

Even a small bump on the ground seemed to have adorable edges and corners. Even a tiny speck of dust in the air looked like a little sprite sprinting away with a secret on its back.

An urge to explore arose in Ning Zhuo; an intense curiosity drove him.

"What exactly are these?"

He subconsciously utilized his divine sense, trying to lift that layer of soft, watery light to seek the help of his past knowledge and cultivation realms.

Ning Zhuo's strong subconscious told him that the answers lay within!

He rummaged for a moment.

Just for a moment.

Deep within his divine sea, earth-shattering waves instantly arose!

The generation and restriction of the Five Elements immediately revived. The five colors of Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water rotated, like five awakened spirit snakes scrambling to poke their heads out.

Mechanical gears clicked and turned, and silver-white mechanisms rapidly interlocked, beginning to analyze the sound of the wind, the fabric, the five fingers of his hand, and the airflow speed on his behalf.

Formation runes unfurled, deconstructing the ground beneath his feet into nodes, formation lines, and the possible flow paths of spiritual energy.

Alchemy, Talismanry, Blood Meridians, Divine Sense, Martial Arts... all of his old knowledge surged up from the depths like a disturbed school of fish. They were too familiar with Ning Zhuo, and far too accustomed to interpreting the world for him. The moment they surfaced, they all reached out, tearing his newly formed pure white state of mind into pieces.

The wind became air currents once again.

The earth became the manifestation of the Earth Element.

This realm of heaven and earth became a gathering of the powers of a divine domain and artifact refinement.

The infant-like blankness dissipated in an instant!

The clarity in Ning Zhuo's eyes shattered directly, resurfacing with his usual calculation and sharpness.

He let out a muffled groan. The small mirrors in his divine sea trembled violently, and hundreds of them shattered with a bang, bang, bang, while nearly a thousand more developed fine cracks.

It was as if the youth's chest was suddenly pressed down by a boulder.

His breathing hitched.

His brain spasmed, as if he had suffered a vicious whip strike deep within his mind.

Following that, cold sweat rolled down from his temples, slid along his cheeks to his chin, and dripped onto his lapels.

His first casting attempt had thus failed!

"Haa... haa..." Ning Zhuo panted, summarizing the lesson.

He had encountered the first difficult hurdle of casting the technique - when he was in that infant-like state, his subconscious actions and past momentum made his first choice to flip through his past accumulations of knowledge.

"So in my heart, I am actually this dependent on them."

The Myriad Phenomena Cradle was like creating a field of snow - clean and crystal clear. Yet, he just had to reach out and dig into it, turning up the dirt buried beneath the snow. These were indeed his treasures, but the snowfield was soiled as a result.

Naturally, the Myriad Phenomena Cradle failed.

"I cannot look through them," Ning Zhuo said softly to himself.

To some extent, this was a form of abandonment. Letting go of his past dependencies was a test of his resolve and willpower!

He closed his eyes and adjusted his breathing for a long time.

Mirror Platform Spirit Communication Manual!

True to its reputation as a supreme-grade cultivation art, countless small mirrors were replenished within his divine sea, and the cracks on many of the mirror surfaces were mended, making them as smooth as new.

Once Ning Zhuo was fully prepared, he began his second attempt.

The Early Wisdom Innate Talent lit up once more. The old knowledge in his divine sea was again covered by the soft, watery light, like old scrolls being put back into a cabinet as the doors gently closed.

Ning Zhuo no longer inspected them.

He no longer rummaged for them.

He allowed himself to become weak, allowed his limbs to become unfamiliar again, and allowed his eyes to turn blank and clear. Like an infant finally willing to lie down in its cradle, he kept his eyes open, quietly waiting for heaven and earth to draw near.

The wind came.

So he listened to the wind.

The earth leaped into his view.

So he felt the earth.

The forging runes in the sky revealed themselves.

So he felt the path of artifact refinement.

It was incredibly wondrous!

Before casting the technique, when Ning Zhuo felt these forging runes, he could sense their heaviness and cold hardness, overflowing with the intense lingering aura of Tie Kuang's forging art.

But now, under the reflection of the Myriad Phenomena Cradle, they looked like a string of small bells hanging above a cradle. Every forging rune was a bell - black iron as the bone, spiritual light as the clapper. When the wind passed through them, a delicate ding-dong sound rang out.

That sound was too pleasant to hear!

Like a mountain spring dripping into a jade cup, like bamboo leaves tapping against a window lattice, like someone softly calling out to him from a very, very faraway place.

From the sound of those bells, Ning Zhuo heard "movement."

All things were moving.

The wind was moving, his sleeves were moving, and the bells were moving. Even the thick and heavy earth was shifting incredibly slowly, like a deeply slumbering yellow dragon rolling over beneath the ground.

Ning Zhuo's chest rose and fell slightly.

The joy in his heart grew increasingly rich.

But right at this moment, the "cradle" moved.

The entire heaven and earth swayed gently.

This sway was extremely gentle.

It wasn't a collision, nor was there any shockwave; it was like a mother nudging a wooden crib with her fingertip. Yet, landing within Ning Zhuo's divine sea, it felt as though the entire world was swaying along with it. Countless small mirrors, like leaves floating on the water's surface, rippled outward in unison.

With this sway, the other side of all things was revealed.

The wind was no longer just brushing past.

It was like a hand.

That hand was slender and transparent, carrying a touch of coolness as it gently stroked Ning Zhuo's forehead and slipped past his ear. It brought the sound of bells, the fragrance of ink, and the lingering resonance of the gently trembling forging runes between heaven and earth.

The earth was no longer just holding him up.

It was like a thick swaddling cloth, clumsily yet docilely wrapping around Ning Zhuo. It was silent and couldn't speak pretty words; it could only steadily bear all the weight. Ning Zhuo felt his mind being wrapped layer by layer in this swaddling, as if even time was being bundled up and slowed down.

The forging runes were no longer just bells.

As they rang, they simultaneously revealed the essence of tempering. Hidden within those crisp sounds were the memories of iron and fire, like a strict teacher softening their voice, coaxing a child to look up first before teaching them the meaning of being tempered through a thousand hammer strikes!

The world began to coax him into initiation.

It was gentle beyond imagination.

Ning Zhuo's Early Wisdom Innate Talent was completely triggered at this moment. Like an overly keen eye, it illuminated every wisp of wind, every ringing bell, and every subtle change in the forging runes with absolute clarity.

All things in the universe bent down in unison, vying to offer their own truths to the infant in the cradle.
