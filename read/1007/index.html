Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 1007: The Furnace is Complete!

Tie Kuang sent the nine Crimson Paulownia root sinews in one by one.

Ning Zhuo's Nine Heavens Profound Iron spirit threads, Situ Xing's Star River spirit veins, Chunyangzi's pure gold portion, the Nine Fires Dragon Lord's fire dragon mark, the Red-Robed Guest's blood soul essence, Zhu Fenxiang's forged patch layer, Cang Yazi's seven-colored patch, and finally, the most important core formation at the bottom of the furnace - all were connected and linked together by the Crimson Paulownia root sinews.

Once the nine roots were formed in place, the Naming Fire Furnace vibrated gently. The numerous foreign powers within the furnace, as if finally finding a path to the furnace's core, began to slowly gather toward the bottom.

Ning Zhuo's mind was shaken as he watched.

This was the generation and growth of the Wood Element, the returning to the roots of the Earth Element, and even more so, the returning to the nest of the Fire Element.

The Crimson Paulownia root wasn't merely a connecting material; it was rebuilding the internal "roots" of the Naming Fire Furnace. Previously, every repaired spot was like an isolated island floating on the furnace body. Now, these isolated islands were linked by the root tendrils, returning once more to the continent at the bottom of the furnace.

Ning Zhuo rapidly deduced in his mind, and even couldn't help but actively separate a strand of divine sense to adjust the angle of the third root sinew for Tie Kuang.

Tie Kuang was just about to speak up to stop him when he suddenly realized that after Ning Zhuo's adjustment, the conflict between the Pure Yang Crimson Gold Mist and the formation patterns at the bottom of the furnace had decreased slightly.

His gaze hardened, and his heart experienced an unprecedented shock. This kid's Formation Dao standards are equally impressive!

Seeing that Tie Kuang didn't stop him and that the results weren't bad, Ning Zhuo continued to adjust the fourth root sinew. He made the fire dragon mark avoid the most frigid section of the Star River spirit vein, rerouting it to bypass near the Vermilion Feather pattern instead. By doing this, the berserk nature of the dragon fire was weakened, and the frigid nature of the star attribute wasn't scattered.

A sharp light shot from the depths of Tie Kuang's eyes.

In an instant, he understood Ning Zhuo's line of thought.

Ning Zhuo was adjusting the positions using the mutual generation and restriction of the Five Elements. Pure Yang belonged to fire, and the fire dragon was also fire. The star attribute leaned toward metal and water, the blood soul leaned toward water and earth, and the pill qi leaned toward wood and earth. Where they clashed with each other, he used the generating nature of the Crimson Paulownia root to avoid their sharp edges, while using the formation patterns at the bottom of the furnace to bear everything.

Such judgment was something that even the Renowned Artisans of Artifact Refining in the Artifact Refining Hall might not be able to achieve.

Tie Kuang couldn't help but ask, "Just how many things have you studied?"

Ning Zhuo's hands didn't stop as he replied softly, "Senior overpraises me. This junior only understands a little."

Tie Kuang chuckled dryly.

Understands a little?

If this is called understanding a little, half the deacons in the Artifact Refining Hall should be thrown back into the furnace and reforged.

Tie Kuang also made his move, adjusting the specific routes of the Crimson Paulownia roots. Even if Ning Zhuo hadn't acted, he would have had to. By Ning Zhuo stepping in to assist, he was saved a lot of effort.

Under their combined efforts, this step was quickly completed.

Tie Kuang took the initiative to explain, "After the Crimson Paulownia returns to its nest, the next step is the Vermilion Feather continuing the veins."

He had already confirmed that although Ning Zhuo was merely at the mid Foundation Establishment Stage, he was absolutely qualified to serve as his assistant.

Tie Kuang took out some Vermilion Feather Flame-Generating Sand and melted the Li-Zhu Phoenix Patterned Copper. The vermilion copper liquid blended with the fine crimson feather sand, turning into a pool of soft fiery magma. The magma lacked the overbearing fierceness of ordinary molten metal; instead, it gently stretched out like a living creature, spawning strands of fine feather-like veins.

"This layer requires the most patience," Tie Kuang said. "The severed parts of the fire veins cannot be forcefully connected; you have to let them grow across on their own."

Ning Zhuo nodded.

His Yang Dao had already reached just over the edge of the Grandmaster realm, and his Fire Element had also entered the Grandmaster realm, giving him a profound comprehension of the "fire of generation." Fire was not just for burning; it could also catalyze growth. Yang was not just fierce; it could also gently nourish. The Vermilion Feather Flame-Generating Sand precisely embodied this principle.

The two, one on the left and one on the right, began to reconnect the fire veins.

Tie Kuang took charge of the main veins, while Ning Zhuo handled the fine branches.

Inside the Naming Fire Furnace, the old fire veins were revealed one by one. The broken sections looked like dried-up riverbeds with scorched black edges, but deep inside, a dark red fiery intent still lingered. After the Vermilion Feather magma flowed in, it did not solidify immediately. Instead, it slowly spread along the fractures, like spring grass taking root or bird feathers growing out.

Ning Zhuo's divine sense was as fine and dense as a woven net. He guided those tiny fire veins to avoid the areas where pill qi had accumulated, bypassed the deep layers of blood soul essence, and finally connected them to the Crimson Paulownia roots at the bottom of the furnace.

He worked more and more smoothly as numerous comprehensions swarmed into his mind.

The Naming Fire Furnace was a fire-attribute spirit treasure, as well as a furnace for refining pills.

When using fire in alchemy, the heat could be gentle or fierce, rising or falling, hidden or expressed. The Yang Dao governed manifestation, movement, ascension, illumination, and emission. The Fire Element governed light and heat, incineration and refinement, generation and destruction, and awakening. At this moment, the Vermilion Feather Flame-Generating Sand continuing the veins perfectly displayed the "catalysis of Yang" within the "growth of fire."

Watching those fire veins grow and fuse together, Ning Zhuo suddenly realized something: the most precious aspect of the Naming Vermilion Bird Fire probably wasn't its fierce burning, but rather its nirvanic rebirth, ascension, refinement, and regeneration.

The movements of his hands became increasingly precise.

Tie Kuang's workload plummeted.

More than ten fine veins that originally required his personal supervision were actually handled by Ning Zhuo alone. The latter was not a burden in the slightest; instead, he acted like an artifact refining assistant who had cooperated with him for years, occasionally even patching up details that Tie Kuang had overlooked.

Tie Kuang inwardly praised him once more.

If this kid joins the Artifact Refining Hall, he will definitely become a pillar of the hall within ten years. In a hundred years, he might even be able to compete with me for the position of Hall Master.

The Vermilion Feather vein continuation took the longest time.

The fireworker deacons in the outer formation rotated three times, and the furnace-measuring formation masters changed their spirit tea twice. However, inside the inner formation, Tie Kuang and Ning Zhuo never stopped their hands.

The fire veins of the Naming Fire Furnace were connected little by little.

Initially, the luster of the various parts of the furnace body was distinct. Star patterns were star patterns, dragon patterns were dragon patterns, Pure Yang was Pure Yang, and blood soul was blood soul. Later on, fine vermilion feather-like veins began to appear between these patterns. The fine veins were like rivers, linking the different regions together into a single whole.

The temperature of the furnace body gradually became uniform.

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit stood in the Warm Cloud Nest, its little head nodding slightly as if it was drowsy, or perhaps it was just feeling comfortable. The fire feathers on its body alone were much softer and smoother than before.

The final major step of the repair was the Heavenly Furnace Bone Patching!

Tie Kuang tossed the Heavenly Furnace Crimson Marrow into the furnace chamber. As soon as the crimson marrow entered, it transformed into a dark red skeletal frame, slowly propping itself up along the inner walls of the chamber. The Li-Zhu Phoenix Patterned Copper supplemented the feather veins, while twelve Mica Fire Womb Crystals were arranged around the furnace core, acting like twelve tiny fire wombs to buffer the integration of the artifact spirit and the furnace body.

The Hundred Refinements Thousandfold Hammer rang out once more.

This time, the sound of the hammer was no longer driving out poison, but forging and fusing.

Ning Zhuo's Nine Heavens Profound Iron spirit threads lit up.

Zhu Fenxiang's forged patch layer was pressed deep into the furnace wall.

Situ Xing's Star River spirit veins intersected with the Vermilion Feather fire veins.

The crimson-gold Yang mist left behind by the Pure Yang Golden Core rose from the furnace core.

The fire dragon mark circled the furnace once, emitting a low dragon roar.

The blood soul essence surged at the bottom of the furnace, tethered by the Crimson Paulownia roots, and was then gently nourished by the pill qi.

The residual power of the Seven-Colored Heaven-Patching Stone scattered into a faint rosy light, coating the weakest sections of the furnace walls.

With every hammer strike, the Naming Fire Furnace became a degree more harmonious, a degree more unified.

Ning Zhuo felt as though he too was being forged by the hammer strikes. The Five Elements, the Fire Element, the Yang Dao, Artifact Refining, Alchemy, and the Formation Dao - these various realms reflected one another within his divine sea. He saw how fire veins formed arrays, witnessed how formation patterns bore the fire, discovered how the furnace walls concealed pill qi, and understood how the artifact spirit used the furnace body as its physical form.

Clarity dawned in his heart: the reason a spirit treasure was a spirit treasure lay in the mutual fulfillment of its spirituality and its physical vessel. The vessel gave the spirituality boundaries, while the spirituality gave the vessel life. If the two merely coexisted, it was like a person staying at an inn. Only when the two truly fused together could they become one body and soul.

What Tie Kuang was doing right now was precisely reforging the unity of body and soul for the Naming Fire Furnace.

On the ninety-ninth strike, Tie Kuang suddenly stopped his hands.

"The Soaring Fire Seed," he said.

A crimson-gold fire seed floated in his palm. The fire seed was lightweight and ethereal, with wind patterns swirling around its edges, resembling a tiny bird that had yet to spread its wings.

Tie Kuang looked at Ning Zhuo. "You do this step."

Ning Zhuo's gaze flickered. "Are you sure, Senior?"

Tie Kuang replied, "Originally, for this step, you were supposed to control the artifact spirit and cooperate with me. But after our previous collaboration, I am certain your strength is completely sufficient for the task!"

Ning Zhuo nodded. He lifted the Soaring Fire Seed and slowly sent it into the furnace core.

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit stood in the Warm Cloud Nest, staring at the fire seed with both fear and longing in its eyes. The Soaring Fire Seed wasn't violent; it floated gently in front of it, like a wisp of wind and fire that could lift it into flight.

Ning Zhuo illuminated it with the Spirit Communication Mirror and quietly guided it using Life Hanging by a Thread.

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit opened its small beak and caught the Soaring Fire Seed.

Boom.

A fiery light rose from the furnace core, yet it didn't explode. It was like a long, warm breeze, buoying up the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit and the fire veins of the entire Naming Fire Furnace, slowly beginning to rotate.

The Crimson Paulownia roots at the bottom of the furnace lit up.

The Vermilion Feather fire veins lit up.

The Pure Yang Crimson Gold Mist, the fire dragon mark, the Star River spirit veins, the deep blood soul scars, the three centuries of pill qi, and the seven-colored patching light - all of them were pushed into rotation by the Soaring Fire Seed at this moment.

The Naming Fire Furnace emitted its first truly complete furnace chime.

Om...

The sound was both heavy and clear, transmitting out of the inner formation and sweeping past the outer formation, vibrating so intensely that countless auxiliary furnaces chimed in response. The fireworker deacons outside all raised their heads, and the crystal furnace plates in front of the furnace-measuring formation masters simultaneously lit up in crimson-gold!

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit spread its wings and took flight from the Warm Cloud Nest.

It was still tiny, but it no longer huddled in a ball. Its fiery plumage unfurled, its fine golden feathers bright, and a spiritual light drifted in its eyes. It flew a circle around the furnace core, trailing a line of soaring firelight behind it, before finally landing back in the furnace.

All the patch marks on the surface of the Naming Fire Furnace slowly faded away at this moment.

The dark red furnace body was as perfectly whole as it originally was, yet it now possessed a more complex foundation than before. On the furnace walls, star patterns, dragon patterns, feather patterns, and Crimson Paulownia root patterns faintly interwoven with one another. They could be seen if one looked closely, but from a distance, the furnace simply appeared as a single flawless masterpiece of nature.

Tie Kuang put away his hammer. His chest heaved violently as he panted heavily.

Ning Zhuo also took half a step back, his face pale. His divine sense had been vastly depleted. The light of the Spirit Communication Mirror dimmed slightly as it flew back into his palm.

The fire curtain of the inner formation slowly dissipated.

In the outer formation, everyone stared at the complete Naming Fire Furnace. For a time, no one spoke a word.

Tie Kuang was silent for a moment before suddenly bursting into loud laughter.

"It's done!"

His laughter was like an iron hammer smashing against a furnace, causing the entire Earth Fire Chamber to buzz and echo. The Naming Fire Furnace seemed to respond to him as the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit poked its head out and chirped twice. The sound was much clearer and brighter, no longer like its initial terrified, mournful cries.

Looking at the little Vermilion Bird in the furnace, Ning Zhuo's heart finally relaxed slightly, and he revealed a heartfelt smile.

Tie Kuang turned to look at Ning Zhuo, his gaze vastly different from before.

"Little friend Ning Zhuo," Tie Kuang said slowly. "If I had lacked your cooperation today, I indeed could have still repaired it, but it would have taken at least three times the effort and involved much greater risks."

Ning Zhuo cupped his hands. "Senior presided over the overall situation; this junior merely assisted from the side."

Tie Kuang waved his hand. "Save the pleasantries. This Hall Master isn't blind. You have deep foundations in the Five Elements, the Formation Dao, Artifact Refining, and Alchemy. Your Yang Dao and Fire Element are no joke either. You've hidden yourself very deeply, kid."

Ning Zhuo smiled bashfully. "With debts weighing on me, if I don't learn a bit more, I'm afraid I won't be able to pay them off."
