Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 993: Chunyangzi vs Liang Ming

The Cloud Beast's movements stiffened.

Sun Lingtong grinned, "Qing Chi, chop its right knee. Don't chop the head, the head is hard; the knees are soft."

Qing Chi had already lunged forward.

Her figure was like a streak of dark cyan firelight, her short blade carrying a ghostly aura as she slashed diagonally. The blade landed on the intersection of the cloud patterns on the right knee, letting out a crisp clank. The Cloud Beast's knee first lit up with a white light, then cracked open with a kacha sound.

The rhinoceros Cloud Beast fell to a half-kneel.

The cloud patterns on its chest suddenly shone brightly as a heavy burst of demonic power exploded from its body, intending to send Sun Lingtong flying.

Sun Lingtong's eyelids twitched. He slipped off the Cloud Beast's back as if the soles of his feet were oiled, and upon landing, he didn't forget to slap a yellow Paper Talisman onto its waist.

"Run!" he said, and ran without the slightest hesitation.

Qing Chi froze for a moment, then immediately turned and sprinted away.

The next moment, the rhinoceros Cloud Beast suddenly shuddered as the cloud patterns around its body exploded into countless red-iron sparks, blooming in midair.

Sun Lingtong and Qing Chi ran side-by-side, but then suddenly stopped.

The cloud path ahead surged, and Cloud Beasts appeared once more.

But this time, there were two of them.

Sun Lingtong's eyes shone brightly as he transmitted a message via divine sense, "Qing Chi, the time to test you has come. Hold off one of them first."

For Qing Chi, this was definitely a daunting task. However, the Ghost Clan Girl did not retreat at all, merely whispering in her heart, "Everything for the Young Master!"

She made the first move, lunging straight ahead.

Sun Lingtong let out a, "Hehe!" Feeling highly thrilled, he followed closely behind.

Another day and night passed.

The doors of the quiet room opened, and Chunyangzi slowly walked out.

The red-gold Daoist robes on his body were clean as new. His eyes and brow were like stagnant water in a deep pool, lacking the slightest bit of restlessness.

The Clear Heart Evil-Reflecting Mirror hovered by his side, its surface as clear as freshly wiped jade.

A Three-Time Sundial slowly revolved around him, flashing intermittently with the sunlight of dawn, noon, and dusk.

A Yang Fish gently flicked its tail beside him, sprinkling a layer of warm golden radiance that blanketed the ground like crushed gold.

The elders had been waiting for a long time. Upon seeing Chunyangzi, they greeted him one after another.

Chunyangzi gave a faint nod and looked up at the high sky. Mobilizing his divine sense, a golden flying chariot burst out from the cloud layer above.

Its golden wheels rolled across the vast sky, emitting a rumbling sound. It was precisely the Noon Heaven Sun-Patrolling Chariot!

Chunyangzi boarded the chariot and flew away.

Above Chunyang Peak, it was as if a high noon sun had slowly lit up, rising rapidly before tearing across the sky.

The martial arena, the Great Noon Heaven Terrace, hung high above the sea of clouds. Three sides were empty and unobstructed, while a Crimson Yang Vein was buried underneath. When the midday sunlight fell straight down, the entire stone terrace became as bright as a furnace chamber. Even a person's shadow would be forced down to the soles of their feet, shrinking into a tiny black dot.

Standing on this stone terrace, Chunyangzi was practically standing in half of his own Dao Field. Beneath his feet, every inch of Earth Qi surged for him. Above his head, every ray of sunlight helped him build momentum.

The five-win martial contest arrangement had reached its final battle!

High noon was at its peak as Chunyangzi was the first to enter the arena.

He stood upon the Noon Heaven Sun-Patrolling Chariot, its golden wheels rolling in midair. The golden pure Yang light slanted down, illuminating the Great Noon Heaven Terrace in a majestic and radiant glow, rendering every fine crack on the stone surface visible in minute detail.

Liang Ming stood alone at the other end of the stone terrace. He wore a gray-black robe. The earthy-yellow vertical mark between his brows was like a tomb nail driven deep into his flesh. The aura around him was gloomy, like an old coffin buried deep underground.

Chunyangzi looked down at him. The three thousand characters of the Pure Yang Alchemy Classic on his robe slowly lit up. His voice was magnificent, echoing throughout the martial arena: "Who are you? State your name."

Liang Ming raised his eyes to the sky, his gaze as calm as a deep well. "Fellow Daoist Chunyangzi, you may call me True Lord Nether Tomb."

As his words fell, the sounds of chimes and bells rang out.

Chunyangzi slightly narrowed his eyes and made the first move.

The Noon Heaven Sun-Patrolling Chariot abruptly ascended, and the light from the Sun Wheel's First Patrol illuminated the entire arena.

Instantly, golden light crashed down like a waterfall. The entire Great Noon Heaven Terrace seemed to drown in a blinding golden ocean.

The gray-black robe around Liang Ming's feet fluttered wildly as if torn by a gale. His protective Yin Qi hissed under the scorching light, like boiling oil meeting water, and the edges of his cuffs even curled up with faint scorch marks.

Liang Ming took out the Netherworld Shovel. Just as he raised it, Chunyangzi took a step down from the chariot. His entire person transformed into a streak of golden light, lunging in front of Liang Ming almost instantly.

High Noon Shadowless Steps!

Nine Suns Knocking Gate Finger!

Chunyangzi closed in on Liang Ming. As his first finger struck out, the golden glow at his fingertip solidified, aiming straight for the center of Liang Ming's brows.

Liang Ming was forced to retreat, simultaneously holding his shovel horizontally to block. The blade of the shovel clashed with the finger light.

Boom.

A muffled explosion sounded, like thunder falling into a deep valley.

Liang Ming's wrists sank as the impact pushed him backward. He retreated several steps in succession, stamping spiderweb-like cracks into the stone terrace beneath his feet.

Chunyangzi's second finger followed close behind!

Liang Ming's expression remained calm. Unwilling to meet it forcefully again, he flicked a Gloom Earth Coin from his sleeve.

The Gloom Earth Coin fell to the ground and immediately transformed into a coin hole, from which a small patch of grayish-white earth haze surged out.

The earth haze rose to block Chunyangzi's second finger. It was reduced to ash in an instant, but it successfully diverted the finger's trajectory by a fraction.

Liang Ming took the opportunity to retreat, dropping a Gloom Earth Coin from his sleeve with every step he took.

As those coins landed on the red-gold stone terrace, they instantly emitted smoke and spontaneously combusted without fire, forming coin holes one after another on the stone ground.

From within the coin holes, grayish-white earth haze continuously gushed out.

Upon seeing this, the Cultivators outside the arena immediately understood that Liang Ming was setting up his battlefield.

"Chunyangzi chose the battlefield here and has too great of a geographical advantage. Can Liang Ming successfully set it up?" This was the question lingering in everyone's minds.

Liang Ming concentrated his energy on setting up the battlefield, exposing a flaw.

Nine Suns Knocking Gate Finger!

As Chunyangzi's third finger fell, a bloody gash burst open on Liang Ming's shoulder.

Liang Ming let out a muffled groan, his heart tightening. "Even the Ashen Fetal Soil Garment can't block him? Has the power of his finger strikes surged this violently?"

Liang Ming remained resolute in his tactics. He mobilized his divine sense, opportunistically using his own blood to blend it into a nearby coin hole.

The next moment, a Nascent Soul appeared above Liang Ming's head.

The Nascent Soul's entire body was dark yellow, as if molded from mud. Its hands held a one-inch earth tablet. Under the intense light, the tiny baby's face wore a tense expression.

The Nascent Soul opened its mouth and softly chanted, "Nether soil below, departed bones in my embrace. I summon the Bone-Embracing Boy to guard my first tomb!"

The stone terrace collapsed with a boom. A crooked, gloomy tomb emerged from the fissure. In front of the tomb was a child's arm bone planted into the ground, terrifyingly white.

The child's arm bone climbed and grasped outward, soon pulling out the Bone-Embracing Boy.

He crawled out of the tomb, hugging a thick, long white bone in his arms. His eye sockets were hollow like two small bottomless wells, and his laughter was shrill, sounding like shattered porcelain scraping against glazed glass: "Heeheehee."

Ghosts wailing and gods howling!

The soundwaves surged, forming a massive shockwave.

Chunyangzi let out a muffled groan and took a step back, his combo interrupted as a result.

He mobilized his divine sense and took out the Clear Heart Evil-Reflecting Mirror, suspending it in front of his chest. The mirror's light bloomed and enveloped the Bone-Embracing Boy.

The Bone-Embracing Boy smoked all over, and the soundwaves abruptly stopped.

Thoughts flashed like lightning within Chunyangzi's divine sea, and he quickly made a decision.

He re-initiated the Nine Suns Knocking Gate Finger. The first finger shook bones like a tolling bell; the second finger was fierce like an axe splitting wood; the third finger was sharp like an arrow piercing the heart. A rapid sequence of three fingers, aimed neither at Liang Ming nor the Bone-Embracing Boy, but at the gloomy tomb.

"What sharp judgment!" Seeing this, Liang Ming's pupils shrank slightly, flashing with a glint of sharp light. "But if it could be easily broken by you like this, I might as well just admit defeat."

Liang Ming was extremely confident in his techniques.

Sure enough, the next moment, the Bone-Embracing Boy tightly hugged the tombstone with both arms and struck the ground with the white bone. Pale white bone spikes broke out from the fissures one after another, like bamboo shoots piercing through the soil!

Chunyangzi investigated with his divine sense and realized the sharpness of these bone spikes. He decisively chose to retreat to temporarily avoid their sharp edge.

After opening up some distance, just as he was about to thrust his fourth finger, Liang Ming's Netherworld Shovel swept over horizontally.

After a bout of entanglement, his fifth finger veered off course, and the chain of momentum was broken.

What followed was a battle of attrition.

Chunyangzi consistently held the upper hand.

The Crimson Yang Vein of the Great Noon Heaven Terrace faintly harmonized with the Noon Heaven Sun-Patrolling Chariot in the sky, providing Chunyangzi with a massive terrain advantage.

Liang Ming was suppressed to the point where it was hard to breathe, continually sustaining injuries.

Yet, he used every injury, every shovel of dirt, and every falling Gloom Earth Coin to slowly expand the foundation of the first tomb.

Time gradually passed; the two fought from high noon into the night.

Moonlight outside shone upon the sea of clouds, but the interior of the Great Noon Heaven Terrace was still forcibly sustained as high noon by the Noon Heaven Chariot.

Wary of The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook, Chunyangzi maintained the initiative at all times, doing his utmost to attack the gloomy tomb.

The Bone-Embracing Boy was an invoked deity, and his figure gradually thinned out.

Liang Ming constantly healed himself, but the burn wounds on his shoulder and chest were like branding iron marks, unable to be eliminated in a short time.

Seeing that the first gloomy tomb was about to be breached, Liang Ming had no choice but to take out a handful of Yin soil and sprinkle it in.

The Yin soil was of exceptional quality, shockingly at the Nascent Soul-level. After being sprinkled, it immediately blended in, causing the damaged parts of the gloomy tomb to be instantly repaired.

Receiving the enhancement, the Bone-Embracing Boy immediately became vigorous and fierce.

Seeing the opportunity, Liang Ming decisively retreated, pulling back to create distance from Chunyangzi.

Chunyangzi was slightly surprised. Liang Ming's move was leaving the gloomy tomb behind for him to attack to his heart's content. This left Chunyangzi a bit uncertain: should he abandon the gloomy tomb to pursue Liang Ming, or focus his attacks on the tomb and temporarily give up on Liang Ming?

Chunyangzi possessed the Three-Time Yang Body Art, which could split into three avatars. According to common sense, now was the perfect time to use it. However, Chunyangzi did not do so.

He pondered for a moment and chose to continue attacking the gloomy tomb, temporarily giving up on Liang Ming.

The Bone-Embracing Boy occupied the gloomy tomb, regrouped, and engaged in a fierce battle with Chunyangzi.

On the other side, seeing Chunyangzi make such a choice, a look of joy appeared on Liang Ming's face, and he initiated his second divine summoning. "Dusk returns to the well, a solitary lamp hides the sun. I summon the Lamp-Hiding Tomb Woman to guard my second tomb!"

A second gloomy tomb protruded with a boom.

Chunyangzi's face tightened. He immediately cast Techniques from afar, attempting to obstruct it with ranged attacks.

Liang Ming was also decisive, directly taking out a magic treasure, the Dusk Well Sunken Urn.

Dusk flowed out from the urn like well water, spreading across the stone terrace like a layer of dark, sheer veil.

Chunyangzi's Technique's Yang fire surged over, crashing like angry tides against a shore, burning away more than half of the sheer veil of dusk.

But under such protection, the second gloomy tomb stood tall.

The Underworld deity, the Lamp-Hiding Tomb Woman, walked out from the shadow of the tomb. She wore mourning clothes as white as frost and snow, holding a fireless black lamp in both hands.

She slowly raised her head. As the black lamp swayed slightly, a sliver of the light sprinkled by the Noon Heaven Chariot was stolen away.

Swaying back and forth like this, the midday sunlight shining on the martial arena noticeably weakened.

Chunyangzi took a deep breath and once again activated magnificent Techniques, whipping up blazing Yang fire to fiercely scorch the area.

The two gloomy tombs echoed each other from afar, and Liang Ming resisted with all his might.

The Netherworld Shovel in his hand was burned as red as iron in a furnace. On the surface of the Ashen Fetal Soil Garment he wore, a grayish-white fetal membrane bulged out, looking like blisters scalded onto skin after being soaked in boiling water.

Liang Ming's injuries increased once more, but he ultimately managed to withstand this wave of Chunyangzi's fierce onslaught.

The first night passed. He had successfully established two gloomy tombs, clawing back a portion of the geographical advantage.

"Chunyangzi is wary of The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook; he's fighting with his hands tied."

"What exact methods is Liang Ming trying to use? Setting up two gloomy tombs and invoking two minor Underworld gods, what is he trying to do?"

The spectating crowd saw through Chunyangzi's mentality, but they couldn't figure out Liang Ming's tactics.

Intelligence regarding Liang Ming had always been extremely sparse.
