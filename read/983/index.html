Chapter 983: Pure Yang Patriarch Pill

The Yang Fish swam half an inch closer to Ning Zhuo. Then, it stopped again.

Ning Zhuo's fingertip twitched ever so slightly, and the Yang Fish immediately shrank back a little. The trace of gloomy coldness in its ink-jade eyes flashed and disappeared, like a dark light suddenly sweeping across the deep bottom of a thousand-year-old ancient well.

Ning Zhuo immediately understood in his heart.

The Yang Fish had already sensed the pure Yang Qi on his body and was willing to get closer, but it still maintained its distance.

It wasn't truly close to him, let alone accepting of him - it was interested in him only because his physical body was clean, his Primordial Yang was abundant, and his virgin boy body was unstained and flawless.

This kind of favor was exactly like a spirit fish sniffing a sweet spring vein in a clear stream; it only smelled it, had not yet drank from it, and had never even thought of sinking into the depths of that spring to inhabit it.

Chun Yangzi asked, "How does it feel?"

Ning Zhuo said solemnly, "This junior's experience is shallow, so I can only say that Senior's treasure is unfathomably profound!"

The corners of Chun Yangzi's mouth twitched slightly, clearly quite pleased with this answer.

He lowered his eyes to look at the Yang Fish swimming leisurely in the golden light, an uncontainable sense of pride and endearment rising in his expression once again. "It has followed my Pure Yang Palace for too long. Before the past sect masters passed away, they would all refine it with a mouthful of their lifebound Pure Yang Qi. After a thousand years of passing down, the Yang Path atmosphere it carries is no longer something ordinary magic treasures can compare to."

"Alright, I will leave the Yang Fish with you for now. Cultivate well. To defeat the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, it is what you can rely on the most. Once you have absorbed the medicinal power of the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill and elevated your Yang Path realm, the Yang Fish will become closer to you, until it eventually accepts you." Chun Yangzi intended to leave.

Ning Zhuo respectfully saw him out of the cave manor.

Returning to his cave manor, he immediately activated the martial practice formation to cultivate alone.

Ning Zhuo's palm stopped in midair.

The Yang Fish drifted in midair, maintaining its distance from Ning Zhuo. At the closest, it only swam slowly in a circle around his fingertip.

Every time the Yang Fish swam by, Ning Zhuo could clearly feel the vast might contained within its tiny body!

On the surface, it was no more than three inches long, its scales as thin as cicada wings, and its swimming posture as agile as a child playing in the water. However, in Ning Zhuo's divine sense perception, this little fish's body seemed to hide a boundless golden ocean.

With every gentle flick of its tail, that golden tide would silently churn deep within its scales, whipping up ten-thousand-foot-tall ripples!

This was no ordinary Yang Qi, but a thousand-year profound Pure Yang foundation accumulated by the past sect masters of the Pure Yang Palace after repeatedly refining, nourishing, and ceremonially tempering it with their lifebound Pure Yang Qi. It was of the utmost truth and purity, profound as rivers and seas, vast as the endless sky.

An intuition even surfaced in Ning Zhuo's heart - if this Yang Fish truly became furious, it would only need to open its mouth slightly and spit out a thread-like ray of golden light to pierce through, incinerate, and melt the mechanical puppets, profound weapon armor formations, and Five Elements Spells he had painstakingly built into a puddle of flowing light.

As a cultivator at the Mid Foundation Establishment Stage, he was as insignificant as a fallen leaf in front of such a Spirit Treasure, drifting unsteadily on the boundless golden sea, not even counting as a single bubble atop a wave.

What was even more terrifying was that the Yang Fish didn't need anyone to constantly urge it; it was alive.

It could feel closeness, disgust, it could evade, and it could probe.

Its intelligence was as ignorant as a newborn beast, yet it already possessed a sense of joy, anger, and avoidance. It would joyfully flick its tail at Chun Yangzi's praise, curiously sniff at Ning Zhuo's approach, and warily retreat from a stranger's touch.

This was a Spirit Treasure.

Ning Zhuo slowly retracted his hand, ripples surging in the lake of his heart.

He had come into contact with the Naming Fire Furnace before and witnessed the frail appearance of the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit curled up in the shadows of the furnace's core.

The Naming Fire Furnace was a heavily injured and shattered body, and the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit was slumbering and wounded, so it couldn't provide Ning Zhuo with an intuitive display of a Spirit Treasure's divine might.

The Yang Fish in front of him, however, was completely different - it was whole, vivid, and bright. It bore no crumbling cracks on its body, nor any accumulated old, severe injuries. Its entire body circulated a nearly perfect aura, like a true morning sun that had just risen and not yet begun to set in the west.

It allowed Ning Zhuo to directly witness exactly what kind of ten-thousand-rayed brilliance an intact Spirit Treasure should possess!

Above magic treasures, there were Spirit Treasures.

Above Spirit Treasures, were Dao Artifacts.

Dao Artifacts contained complete Great Dao textures and could even unleash the might of Divine Arts on their own. That Heaven-Hanging Single-Thread Hook was of this kind.

However, Dao Artifacts also had their own limitations - the Dao principles were too heavy!

They were like tracks firmly cast in place by the laws of Heaven and Earth - powerful, profound, and stable, yet stiff precisely because of this.

For a Dao Artifact to exert its might, trigger conditions had to be met - whether it be refining methods, Qi mechanism guidance, karmic destiny, or a certain battlefield situation. Once the conditions were lacking, no matter how strong the Dao Artifact was, it could only act like a solitary peak suspended high in the sky: visible, but immovable.

Spirit Treasures, however, had another profound aspect - Spirit Treasures possessed spiritual energy.

Having spiritual energy, they possessed autonomous will. Having autonomous will, they could independently judge, avoid danger, and mobilize their might.

Thus, even if their master's reaction was a split second late, a Spirit Treasure could protect its master in advance. Even if the master had not yet seen where the danger lay, a Spirit Treasure could react a step ahead based on its own instincts.

The Naming Fire Furnace was once like this, and the Yang Fish was even more so.

It was extremely rare for spiritual energy to be born within Dao Artifacts, as the Great Dao textures were too heavy. They pressed down layer by layer, like ten thousand tons of mountains pinning down the water's surface. Spiritual energy was flexible and ever-changing, favoring gaps, twirls, and the continuous cycle of life and death.

In a place where Dao principles were too heavy, it was difficult for spiritual energy to take root. Even if a sliver of spiritual light occasionally sprouted, it would be ground down, crushed, and assimilated by the heavy, iron-like principles of the Dao Artifact itself.

However, if a Dao Artifact truly possessed spiritual energy, that would be an entirely different realm - an Immortal Artifact!

Ning Zhuo's eyes flickered as he thought of the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect.

"If I start a battle, it's highly likely that the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect will be in control of the Heaven-Hanging Single-Thread Hook."

"The Heaven-Hanging Single-Thread Hook possesses immense power. For the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect to trigger it, they must satisfy specific conditions. And the three of them live and die together; what they are best at is trading lives, using their own injuries as bait to lure opponents into their pre-set traps. With the Heaven-Hanging Single-Thread Hook in their hands, it is indeed terrifying to the extreme!"

"But if I can gain the Yang Fish's approval, I can borrow its power to resist. Even if the Heaven-Hanging Single-Thread Hook is activated, I will be able to defend against it!"

Although the Yang Fish's Dao principles were not as numerous or profound as those contained in the Heaven-Hanging Single-Thread Hook, its reserve of Pure Yang power was the thick foundation accumulated by past generations of the Pure Yang Palace over a thousand years.

In contrast, the Heaven-Hanging Single-Thread Hook was only borrowed and hadn't been refined by the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. Thus, it couldn't borrow their spiritual energy, and its power reserves were highly limited.

Ning Zhuo's gaze fell back upon the Yang Fish. As if sensing his gaze, the Yang Fish gently flicked its tail in midair again, its golden tail fin leaving a thin, bright trail of light in the air.

It didn't come closer, still maintaining its distance from Ning Zhuo.

Relying solely on my own virgin boy body and abundant Pure Yang Qi, this is the most I can achieve - making the Yang Fish not dislike me and having some slight, extremely brief interest.

Chun Yangzi was absolutely right.

The key still lies in the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill.

Ning Zhuo then took out the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill.

It was truly too ordinary - no pill fragrance lingered around it, no brilliant light flowed over it, no rosy clouds supported it, and no spirit runes coiled around it. If it were thrown into a pile of crushed rocks by a mountain path, many Foundation Establishment cultivators would probably mistake it for a stubborn stone and kick it away.

But holding it in his hand, Ning Zhuo felt its immense weight.

It was incredibly heavy, genuinely weighing down his hand. Although Ning Zhuo's physical foundation was vigorous, his wrist was dragged down by its weight. Even exerting full strength in his fingers, he could barely support it right after taking it out.

According to Chun Yangzi's instructions before leaving, the method of swallowing this pill was different from the norm.

It was not to be swallowed by the physical body, but rather to be taken into the divine sea, observed by the Divine Soul, ground down by divine sense, borne by the mind's intent, and digested by the Dao Heart.

This process harbored unique dangers.

When a physical body swallowed a pill, there were meridians, Qi seas, and flesh and blood to bear the load layer by layer, like rivers flowing into canals, possessing a final destination.

Entering the divine sea, however, meant letting the medicinal pill directly confront one's soul, thoughts, and Dao Heart, like plunging a blazing sun straight into the Upper Dantian.

If one was even slightly careless and the true intent of the Yang Path suddenly erupted, the countless mirror surfaces in the divine sea would be illuminated into a blazing white. Thoughts would be incinerated, the soul would be wounded, and one would be reduced to a state of eternal damnation.

Ning Zhuo sat cross-legged and closed his eyes.

Within his divine sea's Upper Dantian, it was cloudless for ten thousand miles, and Heaven and Earth were like a mirror.

Two massive mirror surfaces above and below unfurled, flat and smooth like a frozen lake, reflecting All Phenomena of Heaven and Earth. Countless small mirrors suspended within them, numbering in the tens of millions, like a sky full of stars, or indivisible tiny lenses within a colossal mechanism.

Thoughts were generated, collided, and annihilated within them. Occasionally, a wisp of spiritual light would burst forth, streaking like a meteor across the cold, boundless mirror surface before dissipating in an instant.

Ning Zhuo infused a sliver of spiritual energy and guided it with his divine sense. The Pure Yang Patriarch Pill vibrated gently. Instead of flying into Ning Zhuo's mouth, it transformed into an incredibly heavy stream of gray light and passed directly through his glabella.

Ning Zhuo's physical body trembled imperceptibly.

The next moment, the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill appeared above his divine sea.

At first, it remained a dusty gray stone pellet, hanging in midair, as quiet and silent as a stone sunk into an ancient well. The countless surrounding mirror surfaces reflected it, yet they revealed no abnormal phenomena whatsoever.

But soon, the first crack appeared.

Crack.

An extremely faint, crisp sound echoed throughout the entire expanse of the divine sea.

A single thread of golden light seeped out from the crack in the pill's shell. With just one thread, the sky above the divine sea suddenly brightened by thirty percent.

Countless mirror surfaces trembled simultaneously, as if awakened from a deep sleep by a morning bell. The thoughts within the mirrors paused in unison for a fleeting moment.

Crack, crack, crack.

The gray outer shell continuously fractured, and golden light gushed out from the cracks. It grew ever more abundant and intense, like a flood bursting through a dam, like the morning light tearing through the long night.

The ordinary disguise on the surface of the stone pellet rapidly peeled away, like an old mountain that had slept for a thousand years violently bursting open. From the belly of the mountain, a true, blazing sun rose! Complete, heavy, and overbearing, it hung over the divine sea, dyeing the two colossal mirror surfaces above and below a deep crimson-gold.

The millions of small mirrors were illuminated into a dazzling expanse. Faint, crisp sounds emanated from their edges, as if they could not withstand such a magnificent and blazing radiance.

In the center of that sun, countless characters circulated endlessly - they were precisely the scriptures of the Pure Yang Pill Classic!

Its contents, word by word, sentence by sentence, appeared as if cast from gold and tempered by fire.

Beside the scriptures, blurry figures faintly emerged: some sat cross-legged before pill furnaces, some stood beneath a scorching sun, some held Golden Cores in their hands, and some wore crimson-gold Daoist robes.

Some were as youthful as the rising morning sun, while others were as aged as the afterglow of a setting sun.

Some had solemn expressions like iron walls, while others possessed gazes as gentle as the spring breeze.

Some had bodies as scrawny as dry firewood, while others had auras as magnificent as towering mountains...

These were all the true intents of the Yang Path left behind by the past sect masters of the Pure Yang Palace. They were the unbroken thousand-year inheritance of the Pure Yang Pill Classic, the comprehension and accumulation of the Yang Path, Fire Path, and Pill Path achieved by many Nascent Soul cultivators over their entire lifetimes.

They were like inscriptions carved into metal and stone, remaining blindingly radiant even after a thousand years!

Outside, the Yang Fish also seemed to sense something. As it swam through the air, the frequency of its tail flicks visibly quickened, its golden scales glittering in the glow like dancing flakes of shattered gold.

Ning Zhuo's divine sea was a field of searing heat!

His thoughts were like pieces of paper tossed into a furnace, their edges rapidly curling and turning scorch-yellow.

Mirror Platform Spirit Communication Manual!

Countless mirror surfaces played a crucial role at this moment - millions of small mirrors flipped over layer by layer, dismantling, refracting, and cushioning the overbearing golden sunlight, then distributing it to Ning Zhuo bit by bit, doing their utmost to ensure their master could withstand it.

Even so, Ning Zhuo still felt his glabella swelling as if it were about to split open, and the depths of his soul burned like a branding iron.

Boundless light, boundless heat, still spreading!

The Fire Element Heart Temple opened at this exact moment!

Ning Zhuo's profound Fire Element realm also helped him rapidly comprehend the Dao of Pure Yang right now.

Initially, Ning Zhuo had no specialized cultivation in the Yang Path.

But his Primordial Yang was full, like water stored in a deep pool, calm yet ready to burst forth. He possessed Pure Yang Qi, like a newborn rosy dawn - bright but not scorching.

His Five Elements realm had already reached a towering height that ordinary people could hardly attain.

All of this had already allowed him to place one foot within the threshold of the Yang Path.

And now, he officially pushed open the door and entered!

In almost an instant, he officially became an Apprentice in the realm of the Yang Path.

The reason was singular: the big-headed youth's accumulation in other aspects was truly profound. Although he hadn't formally or specifically studied the Yang Path, he had already become familiar with it.

His pill-refining experience told him that if the Yang fire was too fierce, the medicinal properties would wither and scorch; but if nourished properly, even withered medicines could regain a final thread of vitality.

His artifact-refining experience told him that if the dry nature of Scarlet Yang materials wasn't suppressed beforehand, the artifact embryo would spontaneously crack during tempering, ending in shattered jade and clinking metal.

His Formation Path experience told him that the circulation of Yang Qi was just like the extension of formation lines - there must be formation cores to support it and formation bases to ground it. Otherwise, the light would merely scatter with an astonishing display, when in reality, it would just be an empty waste, like building a tower on sand.

Not to mention his own Primordial Yang virgin boy body and Pure Yang Qi, which naturally inclined his physical body toward the Yang Path, granting him an excellent foundation for comprehension from the start.

Ning Zhuo recalled his several sessions of Dual Cultivation.

During the intertwining of Qi mechanisms in Dual Cultivation, when Yin and Yang made contact, the ways in which Yang Qi was drawn out, how it fluctuated in tandem with the rise and fall of his partner's aura, and how it maintained that delicate cycle of vitality between restraint and release - all transformed into tiny specks of glimmering light, gently falling into the depths of his divine sea.

He also thought of his various experiences using the Yin-Yang One Qi Kettle. Inside the kettle, Yin and Yang energies flowed into one another; Yin could turn into Yang, and Yang could enter Yin. The turning of this singular Qi was unparalleled in its refinement and subtlety.

These feelings that could once only be sensed but barely articulated, these scattered, unstructured, and complex experiences, all fell into their proper places at this moment. They were like scattered chess pieces falling onto a chessboard, each one landing exactly where it was meant to be.
