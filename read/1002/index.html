Chapter 1002: I Want You All to Submit

The other factions of Flowing Cloud Peak were also in discussion.

"The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar... The Earth Expansion Alliance operated it in secret for so many years, only for it to fall into the hands of the Naming Stronghold in the end. Truly laughable."

"Watching the battle between Chunyangzi and Liang Ming, the former specializes in pure Yang Cultivation Arts. The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar should be of great benefit to him."

"Chunyangzi was even able to break through a Divine Art. Truly remarkable. I am no match for him."

"Fortunately, he suffered heavy injuries. Without a period of recuperation, he certainly won't recover. That was a wound inflicted by a Divine Art!"

"Sigh... If we had known, we shouldn't have tormented Ning Zhuo. We placed high hopes on the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, and everyone pushed their chips over to them, but Ning Zhuo simply turned around and vanished."

"He is currently being detained. It's almost been seven days, right? Can he even get out?"

"So what if he does? I believe he publicly promised to spar with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. But the five-win martial contest is already over. Ning Zhuo's match no longer affects the bigger picture."

Heavy Formation Peak.

Deep within the main hall at the peak's summit, Dong Chen sat in the seat of honor.

Yin Changgeng stood below him, his head slightly lowered, holding a long box in both hands.

Inside the box, The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook lay quietly.

Dong Chen took a glance, the tension in his eyes finally easing a fraction. "As long as it has been retrieved, that is good."

Yin Changgeng's face showed a look of shame. "This subordinate failed to handle matters properly. We chose Liang Ming, yet still lost. Although we have now recovered the Dao Artifact, The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook, Liang Ming has been crippled. Chunyangzi suffered severe injuries, and his Yang Fish was heavily damaged. The Naming Stronghold has now become the new master of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. What should our next steps be?"

He didn't mention the Void-Embracing Blade-Breaking Guard. In the eyes of Nascent Soul cultivators, this Golden Core-level mechanical construct taking the brunt of the Dao Artifact's lingering might for Chunyangzi meant that even if it was destroyed, it was completely worth the price.

Dong Chen tapped his fingers on the formation desk, making a soft thud, thud sound. "For the Naming Stronghold to establish a foothold on the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, they will inevitably need to build a protective Grand Formation. I worry that if they investigate too carefully, they might unearth our hidden hands."

He pondered for a moment. "Send people to secretly sneak underground and keep a constant watch to ensure our hidden arrangements remain perfectly concealed. If they are discovered, have the watching cultivators strike directly to destroy them. Do not leave behind any evidence or clues."

Yin Changgeng cupped his fists. "Yes, Peak Master!"

Dong Chen's tone shifted slightly. "Additionally, the Naming Stronghold's successful peak assault is a foregone conclusion. This stronghold is special; it hasn't been established for long, making it extremely easy to infiltrate. Dispatch a few teams to proactively join the stronghold and act as inside agents."

Chunyangzi's victory over Liang Ming had changed Dong Chen's view of the Naming Stronghold. He shifted from his previous stance of suppression and obstruction to one of befriending and infiltration.

On Danxia Peak, pill clouds resembled a rosy sunset, and the pill rooms dotting the mountainside exhaled a warm, mellow medicinal fragrance.

Wang Yu stood before a high platform.

Wearing a cloud-and-sunset Daoist robe, his expression was gentle and elegant, completely composed and calm.

A trusted subordinate stood behind him, having just reported the intelligence from the five-win martial contest.

The trusted subordinate said, "Peak Master, the Naming Stronghold is currently riding a wave of momentum. Chunyangzi's prestige has skyrocketed, and Nine Fires Dragon Lord, Tan Zhu, and Tu Yuanzi have aggressively taken over the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar."

Wang Yu said, "The Naming Stronghold acquiring the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is better than White Cloud Village getting it, and far better than the Earth Expansion Alliance."

"Go send a secret message. Tell Tan Zhu to investigate the underground and strive to find clues and evidence left by Heavy Formation Peak."

The trusted subordinate replied, "Yes, this subordinate will arrange it immediately."

Wang Yu waved his horsetail whisk and said indifferently, "Oh, and dispatch two more teams to proactively join the stronghold. Now is a perfect opportunity."

Although Tan Zhu had successfully joined the upper echelon of the Naming Stronghold, his lifespan limit was fast approaching.

Wang Yu was already beginning to arrange for follow-up espionage personnel.

Artifact Refining Hall.

Tie Kuang's hammer fell with a heavy bang, shaking the eardrums of the nearby disciples until they went numb.

"Did you see that clearly?" Tie Kuang was in the middle of instructing when a flying message arrived.

"Chunyangzi won?" Tie Kuang unrolled the letter, taking a glance before grinning widely, his teeth gleaming white in the light of the forge's fire. "So this old thing still had this kind of skill."

Tie Kuang deduced the situation in his mind: With things as they are, there's no need for Ning Zhuo to come out.

"The Void-Embracing Blade-Breaking Guard blocked The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook?" Lu Ban muttered, a look of obsession appearing on his face. "A mechanism from the lineage of the Empty Valley Syllable Azure Machine Tube actually managed to play a slight role at that level of combat. That's indeed quite interesting."

When Che Zhuzi heard the news, he was first stunned, then he slapped his thigh.

"Ha! Ning Zhuo really managed to pull off this grand feat!"

"The Naming Stronghold actually succeeded in their peak assault."

A sharp light flashed continuously in Che Zhuzi's eyes. Unable to sit still, he stood up immediately. "I need to prepare a gift as soon as possible and go congratulate them. The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is waiting to be rebuilt; they lack everything. Whatever they lack, we will sell to them. The price is highly negotiable; the main thing is to establish goodwill first."

Previously, the impending battle between Ning Zhuo and the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect had caused a storm of uncertainty, making it hard for anyone to be optimistic about it.

Che Zhuzi had maintained strict neutrality and made no stance.

Now that the Naming Stronghold had succeeded, as a merchant, what Che Zhuzi loved doing the most was adding flowers to brocade!

At the main mountain gate of the Myriad Manifestations Sect, the discussions regarding this battle did not cease for a full day and night.

"To think Chunyangzi could fight to such an extent!"

"Liang Ming was definitely the strongest hidden trump card of the Earth Expansion Alliance. He grasped a Divine Art like the Seven Gui Death Sacrifice, yet he actually lost."

"Though Chunyangzi won, it was a pyrrhic victory."

"Ning Zhuo is unreliable. I was still looking forward to his battle against the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. I was very curious about just how far this young genius could go. As a result, he didn't even make it out of this minor trial. It's truly disappointing."

"In the five-win martial contest, Chunyangzi fought twice and won both times. For the Naming Stronghold's successful peak assault, he is the foremost contributor. Ning Zhuo? He has a big reputation, but he didn't help much."

These discussions were like rolling tides of clouds, crashing repeatedly among the various peaks.

After a day and a night passed, the first batch of Naming Stronghold flags had already been erected on the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

The arrangement of these formation flags was exquisite. Provided by Shen Xi, they could temporarily protect the surroundings, allowing the Naming Stronghold to build their Grand Formation with more peace of mind.

At dawn, sunlight spread from the edge of the sea of clouds. The crimson-gold radiance fell upon the Yang-Locking Well, where the tri-colored vortex at the bottom emitted a faint glow.

Chunyangzi arrived.

He arrived riding a cloud, his face still pale.

His Daoist robe had been changed, but it couldn't conceal the black and cyan Gui marks on his body. The wound on his forehead still bore a thin, red line, looking like wet cinnabar.

The fingers of both his hands were wrapped in white gauze, with blood faintly seeping through from underneath.

Nine Fires Dragon Lord, Tan Zhu, and Tu Yuanzi were all within the altar. Upon hearing of his arrival, they immediately came out to greet him.

Nine Fires Dragon Lord raised his eyebrows and smiled. "Chunyangzi, you are injured like this, yet you still came for an inspection?"

His attitude toward Chunyangzi was already different. Although he was joking, his feelings of respect were evident in his words. After all, during the five-win martial contest, Chunyangzi had placed high hopes on Nine Fires Dragon Lord, only for him to suffer defeat.

Tu Yuanzi cupped his hands. "The Chief Stronghold Master has worked hard in this battle."

Tan Zhu didn't speak but gave Chunyangzi a nod. Since the other party hadn't fallen into his schemes and had won purely relying on his own combat prowess, Tan Zhu had nothing to say.

Chunyangzi narrowed his eyes slightly. The pain in his body remained, but an indescribable sense of gratification welled up from the bottom of his heart.

He could clearly feel that the attitudes of the three Nascent Soul cultivators in front of him had all undergone a subtle shift.

The moment his feet stepped onto the surface of the altar, Chunyangzi felt a surge of warmth traveling up through the soles of his boots.

"This is the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar."

"This is my opportunity for Soul Formation!"

Secretly thrilled, he began his inspection. First, he checked the Yang-Locking Well, then the cloud ring at the altar's waist, followed by the fire veins at the base of the altar, and finally, the Grand Formation foundation that the Naming Stronghold cultivators were busily constructing.

During this time, Shen Xi, Situ Xing, Lin Jinglong, Mu Yuehua, and others paid their respects to him, respectfully addressing him as Chief Stronghold Master.

Everyone was well aware that Chunyangzi's contributions were the greatest in winning the five-win martial contest!

Since everyone would benefit from this, they naturally felt indebted to Chunyangzi!

This was prestige.

Chunyangzi scanned his surroundings, his eyes slightly cold. "Ning Zhuo still hasn't come out?"

Someone suggested, "The seven-day period has already arrived, but Fellow Daoist Ning Zhuo is still in the Rising Cloud Minor Trial. Chief Stronghold Master, should we take advantage of the momentum of our great victory to pressure Tie Kuang into releasing him?"

Chunyangzi coldly snorted in his heart.

"The situation under the Truth Bell was nothing but a good show staged by Ning Zhuo."

"He appeared to have been detained, but in reality, he used the circumstances to avoid the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. By admitting his fault, he preserved his reputation; by returning the sponsorships, he won the hearts of the people. He borrowed my Chunyang Ancestor Pill, and in return, lent me the Void-Embracing Blade-Breaking Guard."

"What a great arrangement. What an excellent scheme."

Chunyangzi muttered in his heart.

While most onlookers couldn't tell, he, who was deeply versed in the Righteous Path, had seen through the truth long ago.

"I exerted the most effort, fighting to the death, while Ning Zhuo played with political maneuvering. What 'dealing with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect'? He didn't even set foot on the martial arena, yet he reaped immense rewards."

"Not just wealth, but also the hearts of the people."

"At the critical moment, he shrank back. What else could I do? I could only let myself be pushed forward to break through the predicament."

What made Chunyangzi feel most "uncomfortable" in his heart was that, ironically, he really had to thank Ning Zhuo. Setting aside the Void-Embracing Blade-Breaking Guard, simply by drawing fire and attracting heavy financial backing from the numerous major factions of Flowing Cloud Peak - thus helping Chunyangzi "lure away" the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect - Ning Zhuo had provided a massive amount of help.

Although Ning Zhuo huddled in the rear, he truly was helping Chunyangzi, and his help genuinely made a difference!

"Ning Zhuo's interconnected schemes... he's practically like an old monster reborn."

"If I am to control the Naming Stronghold in the future, he will be my biggest obstacle!"

Nine Fires Dragon Lord could fight but wasn't skilled in management.

Tan Zhu was nearing the end of his lifespan and had other calculations.

Tu Yuanzi's foundation was a mystery. Though he didn't participate in this battle, his bottom line had already been exposed - his combat power was definitely lacking!

Chunyangzi had sacrificed so much to finally seize control of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. This was his opportunity for Soul Formation-level ascension; he absolutely would not allow the Naming Stronghold to remain under anyone else's control!

"External enemies have been temporarily deterred; next, I must eradicate all voices of internal dissent!"

"Right, the Chunyang Ancestor Pill is still in Ning Zhuo's hands. It must be retrieved immediately!"

Chunyangzi was worried that Ning Zhuo might use the Chunyang Ancestor Pill to vastly elevate his insights into the Yang Path and Pill Path, which would consequently allow him to see through the immense significance the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar held for Chunyangzi.

"It's only been seven days. It's impossible for Ning Zhuo to have seen through the secrets here. But he has certainly made leaps and bounds on the Yang Path."

"Heh, this was originally my plan - to let him taste the sweetness."

"With a big enough taste of sweetness, supplemented by the threat of my fists, I don't fear he won't submit to me!"

"Now that this Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is beneath my feet, in the future, the Naming Stronghold and you, Ning Zhuo, will all have to submit!"
