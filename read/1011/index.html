Chapter 1011: A Brand-New Treasure Furnace

Inside the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor, three-colored radiance slowly flowed.

Sunlight, moonlight, and starlight cascaded from the dome, illuminating the formation runes on the stone walls. Like three tranquil rivers of light, they silently circulated deep within the cave manor.

Returning to the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor, Ning Zhuo instructed Old Chef, "I am going into secluded cultivation. No matter who comes to visit, put them off for now."

Old Chef accepted the order. "Yes, Young Master."

The stone door slid shut, and the formation runes lit up layer by layer. Only Ning Zhuo remained inside the chamber.

Ning Zhuo sat cross-legged and first inspected the Naming Fire Furnace.

When Ning Zhuo first laid eyes on this spirit treasure, it had been heavily shattered, with the vast majority of its body missing. The remaining portions were dilapidated, crisscrossed with charred cracks. Many of its formation runes resembled snapped meridians, scattered haphazardly across the furnace walls.

Meanwhile, the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit had cowered deep within the furnace's core, terrified to the extreme. It trembled constantly, ready to seal itself away at the slightest disturbance.

Looking deeper into it back then, the furnace was thick with pill toxicity. Stale pill qi, baleful ash toxins, medicinal residues, and charred fire veins were tangled together in layers, resembling a water well completely choked with rubble.

After the Rising Cloud Minor Trial, Ning Zhuo had acquired the Naming Fire Furnace, and since then, it had undergone repairs from various parties.

At that time, traces of everyone's handiwork could clearly be seen upon the furnace.

The Nine-Fire Qi left by the Nine-Fire Dragon Lord was domineering and high-spirited; Chunyangzi’s contribution was bright and fierce; the Red-Robed Guest’s blood-path essence was profound and heavy; and Zhongli Mei’s pill qi was warm and continuous. The repairs made by Cang Yazi, Zhu Fenxiang, Situ Xing, and the others each manifested their own unique anomalies as well.

Everywhere, one could tell it had once been shattered; everywhere, there were glaring signs of various parties forcibly bridging gaps, mending its "bones," and reconnecting its "veins."

But things were different now.

As Ning Zhuo scrutinized the spirit treasure once again, he clicked his tongue in silent praise.

Tie Kuang’s major overhaul had utilized the robust facilities of the Artifact Forging Hall’s Earth Fire Chamber. Employing auxiliary furnaces and external formations, he had incorporated Mystic Obsidian Spirit-Fixing Sand, Nine-Revolution Earth Fire Liquid, and Mica Fire Womb Crystal. Coupled with Warm Cloud Marrow, Vermilion Feather Flame-Birthing Sand, and Crimson Paulownia Root - precious materials perfectly suited for the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit - he had completely melted down and reforged all the previous repair marks.

This wasn't merely a cosmetic smoothing over; it had truly been refined into a single, cohesive entity.

The furnace walls no longer featured a patch of dragon fire patterns here and a streak of pure yang dao there, nor did it conceal blood-colored veins within. All these disparate traces looked as if they had been steeped by the passage of time into a single piece of ancient bronze, transforming into the innate texture of the furnace body itself.

The dark crimson walls of the furnace felt warm, smooth, and substantial.

Rings of fine, delicate vermilion feather patterns surfaced across its exterior. These patterns weren't ostentatious; they unfurled naturally along the body of the furnace, looking like actual feather veins growing upon a Vermilion Bird's plumage.

Sweeping it with his divine sense, he discovered that the Nine-Fire Qi, the Pure Yang Light, the lingering pill qi, the blood-path essence, and the silver traces of starlight had all been corralled deep within those feather veins. They no longer conflicted with one another, nor did they operate independently.

The fibrous roots of the Crimson Paulownia at the bottom of the furnace had thoroughly integrated with the core formation runes.

No longer appearing as foreign objects, they looked like a root system naturally born of the furnace itself. These roots extended into the walls, the inner chamber, the handles, and the legs of the furnace, bridging every fire vein back into the furnace core.

Whenever fire qi circulated within, it flowed with steady, smooth, and natural ease - like rivers settling into their beds, or birds returning to their familiar nests.

Deep within the inner chamber, the "fire bones," patched together using Heavenly Furnace Crimson Marrow, radiated a warm, rich, crimson glow.

The fire veins, formed from the fusion of Li Zhu Phoenix-Patterned Copper and Vermilion Feather Flame-Birthing Sand, threaded delicately throughout. With every rhythmic "breath" of the furnace fire, these veins would pulse with light simultaneously, traveling from the base to the shoulder, from the shoulder to the mouth, and finally cascading from the mouth back into the core in a perfectly flawless cycle.

Ning Zhuo reached out, hovering his palm just near the furnace wall.

He didn't actually touch it.

Separated by merely half an inch, he could still feel the wholly integrated aura radiating from inside the Naming Fire Furnace.

Gone was the sluggish friction of the fractures.

Gone was the painful hollowness of its old wounds.

And gone, too, was the chaotic turbulence brought on by the artifact spirit's terror.

It was as if this pill furnace had regrown its flesh and bones. Its meridians were unblocked, its vital energies had settled into their rightful places, and all its wounds had scabbed over and fallen away to reveal a newborn yet resilient texture.

A look of marvel surfaced in Ning Zhuo's eyes.

The Nine-Fire remained.

The Pure Yang remained.

The blood-path essence remained.

The pill qi remained.

The starlight, divine path tempering, spirit beast materials, and Five Elements treasures were all still there as well.

Yet, they now all submitted to the Naming Fire Furnace itself.

The furnace body acted as the skeleton, and the fire veins as the meridians; the Crimson Paulownia roots had anchored themselves, the Vermilion feathers were renewed, the Warm Cloud soothed the spirit, and soaring forces propelled it forward.

A flawless, unified furnace!

"Tie Kuang's methods are like nothing I’ve ever seen in my life. Even though I participated throughout the entire process, there are still numerous profound subtleties that I cannot comprehend."

"What I lack is still too much."

"If I have the opportunity in the future, I must learn from and consult Tie Kuang more often."

Ning Zhuo shifted his attention back to the artifact spirit.

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit had fallen into a deep slumber.

Deep within the furnace core, the Warm Cloud Marrow had been refined into a layer of soft, luminous clouds.

These were no ordinary clouds. Radiating a faint warmth, they were like a thin blanket sun-baked on a spring morning, gently swaddling the tiny Vermilion Bird.

Previously, the furnace explosion had blasted it into this juvenile state. Its fiery feathers had been disheveled, its eyes perpetually filled with terror. Whenever any external force approached, it would bury its head under its wings as its fiery glow violently trembled.

Now, however, it was sleeping soundly.

And very sweetly.

The little Vermilion Bird remained curled up deep within the core. Its wings drooped naturally, and its gold-and-red feathers lay smooth and flat against its body. The tufted golden down on its head gently rose and fell in time with its breathing. Occasionally, it would give a slight twitch, sending a few tiny sparks sliding off the tips of its feathers. The sparks would drift into the warm clouds, only to be tenderly cradled by the luminous vapor.

Its breathing was incredibly slow.

With every breath, the Naming Fire Furnace’s fire veins faintly flickered in sync. It resembled a patient who had just undergone a major surgery - finally out of the woods and resting securely on a clean, warm bed.

Ning Zhuo gazed at it, his eyes softening.

The large-headed youth did not disturb it.

He even took the initiative to retract the presence of Life Hanging by a Thread, maintaining only the faintest, thinnest sliver of connection.

He recalled Tie Kuang's advice: "It is already difficult enough for a spirit treasure to form a spirit. Surviving that explosion was a miracle in itself. From now on, you need to focus on warming and nourishing it. Don't be in a rush to fire up the furnace and refine pills."

"This major overhaul of the Naming Fire Furnace is equivalent to reforging its very foundation - a complete rebirth. The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit also needs to adapt to this brand-new 'body,' so you have to give it a grace period to adjust."

"Just as a blessed land with good feng shui can nourish a person, the brand-new Naming Fire Furnace will nourish the Vermilion Bird spirit. As time goes on, the spirit's form will undergo changes in response to the new furnace body."

"The day its form completely solidifies is the day it has fully adapted. Only then will it be in perfect harmony with the new furnace, pushing your pill refinement success rate to its absolute peak."

Taking Tie Kuang's advice to heart, Ning Zhuo felt no rush to start refining pills.

"I'm not the one in a hurry - my creditors are."

"I am the master of the Naming Fire Furnace. When to refine pills is a decision that rests entirely with me."

"However... this matter of the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit changing its form is something that still needs further thought."

A pensive glint flashed through Ning Zhuo's eyes. Shortly after, he stowed the Naming Fire Furnace away and took out the Three Valiants Life-Claiming Spike.

This was a long, rust-blackened spike.

Its seven-inch body was covered in fine, dense barbs. Embedded at the tail end of the spike was half of a shattered soul crystal. Curled up inside the crystal was a transparent human silhouette with a blurry face. Across its chest lay three overlapping scars, as though three different weapons had skewered its body simultaneously.

The moment Ning Zhuo drew the spike, it released an incredibly faint and low soul shriek, radiating an intense hatred that brought a bone-piercing chill to the air.

Ning Zhuo remembered perfectly well what the masked cultivator who handed him the spike had said: Within this spike resides the remnant soul of my clan's Nascent Soul patriarch. Years ago, the patriarch was jointly slain by the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. When his Nascent Soul was destroyed, only a single wisp of his soul remained intact. Driven by an unwavering vow for vengeance, he exhausted our clan's entire foundation to lay down a grand formation, using his own soul as a forging material to ultimately refine this Nascent Soul-level magic treasure.

"This treasure is a single-use item. Once it strikes its target, it can temporarily nullify their 'Shared Life and Death' Divine Art!" - Back then, those words had deeply moved Ning Zhuo. He felt he had finally obtained the means to deal with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect.

However, Ning Zhuo was cautious by nature. Faced with all sorts of sponsored items, he stroked his Mechanism Ring and silently asked himself: What would happen if I use these to fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect?

As a result, the ring constricted so violently it almost snapped his finger bone.

Ning Zhuo was already quite familiar with the Mechanism Ring, having relied on it numerous times. He sensed something extremely fishy; logically, the warning shouldn't have been this "severe."

Therefore, he narrowed down the sponsored items one by one, screening them individually.

Very quickly, he singled out the Three Valiants Life-Claiming Spike.

"Good lord."

"Using this spike will actually make my situation worse. What does that imply?"

"In all likelihood, this is a trap specifically laid out for me by the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect!"

Since the very item Ning Zhuo had pinned his highest hopes on - the Three Valiants Life-Claiming Spike - turned out to be a sinister, despicable, and shamelessly cunning trap, he came to the stark realization that he had absolutely zero chance of defeating the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect in combat.

From that moment onward, he began carefully plotting his escape plan for when he would inevitably be detained under the Truth Bell.

Of course, this had merely been Ning Zhuo's speculation.

Thus, after successfully escaping, he specifically instructed Chen San to make a covert arrangement: deliberately omitting this item from the public list.

And what happened?

The masked cultivator who had provided the Three Valiants Life-Claiming Spike never showed up!

This was completely illogical.

This was a Nascent Soul-level magic treasure, purportedly forged from the remnant soul of his own clan's patriarch. Even if the masked cultivator had absolute faith in Ning Zhuo's credibility and was certain he would fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, he still should have come forward just to confirm its status.

To not even bother checking up on it - wouldn't that make him an unfilial descendant?

The facts had validated Ning Zhuo's theory. Therefore, now that he had finished with the far more important task of "repairing the Naming Fire Furnace," he finally set to work dealing with this item.

Boat-Burning Soul Crossing Art!

Innate Talent: True Self!

Ning Zhuo’s mind violently jolted.

Countless fragmented images surged like a flood, pouring from the Nascent Soul spirit inside the spike directly into his divine sea.

A figure in white robes trimmed with gold, with two flying swords orbiting his body.

Shentu Wuwei!

A snow-white hall.

Inside the hall, a young Shentu Wuwei possessed a pale, beardless face and a slender figure. At this time, he was only sixteen or seventeen years old.

He knelt before a low desk, upon which lay a snow-white narrow sword.

The sword's name: Light Illuminating Courage.

The blade was like thin porcelain, purely white without the slightest impurity. As the edge was brought close to the lamplight, the light itself was seemingly sliced in two, falling onto the desk in split halves.

The young Shentu Wuwei reached out and stroked the blade.

His fingers were long and slender, his nails immaculately trimmed.

"From this day forth, you shall be my Lifebound Sword," his voice was gentle, as casual as someone commenting on the temperature of their tea.

Yet, as his fingertip traced the edge and his blood was absorbed by the blade, Light Illuminating Courage emitted an incredibly low, clear ring.
