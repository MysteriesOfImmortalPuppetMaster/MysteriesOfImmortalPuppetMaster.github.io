Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 986: Ning Zhuo Takes an Oath

A young Confucian cultivator immediately unrolled a sheet of plain paper, another slowly ground the ink, and as someone else took out a jade brush, a faint white light floated up from the surface of the paper, like moonlight falling upon fresh snow.

From among the group of Confucian cultivators, Chu Xuangui took a step forward with an expressionless face. "Little Friend Ning Zhuo, today allow this old man to compose a poem for you."

He raised the brush and began to write - the moment the tip of the brush touched the paper, an anomaly manifested. The ink-qi on the paper transformed into a wisp of pure, righteous breeze, floating gently in the direction of Ning Zhuo.

A slight commotion went through the crowd.

The expressions of the cultivators from Flowing Cloud Peak darkened even further. The Confucian cultivators stepping forward to write a poem to bolster his momentum pushed Ning Zhuo's reputation in the Righteous Path even higher.

On the other side, Zhu Fenxiang and Zhu Guizhi also appeared.

Zhu Fenxiang’s features were bright and sharp. Today, she wore a terracotta-colored dharma robe, with a divine-patterned jade belt buckled around her waist, her expression even more solemn than usual.

When she looked toward Ning Zhuo, a flash of complexity flickered through the depths of her eyes, like an undercurrent surging beneath the water's surface.

Zhu Guizhi stood beside her, her temperament as elegant as an autumn chrysanthemum, her features carrying the shrewdness and gentleness unique to a mature female cultivator.

Looking at Ning Zhuo, her lips curved into a smile. "Little Friend Ning Zhuo, my family is also one of your creditors. Since you are acting on behalf of the Naming Stronghold, it is only right that we show our support. Today, my daughter shall summon the deity for you to bear witness!"

"Fellow Daoist Ning Zhuo," Zhu Fenxiang said as she approached him.

Ning Zhuo showed a hint of puzzlement and returned the salute. "Fellow Daoist Zhu."

Zhu Fenxiang walked over to a clearing of black stone and raised her hand to retrieve a divine statue.

The moment the divine statue hit the ground, it swelled to the size of a temple, its impact cracking the stone tiles beneath it.

Everyone's expressions grew even more solemn; they felt the divine statue was heavy and ancient, radiating the majesty of Wutu Suppression Prison True Monarch, like a miniature mountain descending upon the mortal realm.

Zhu Fenxiang formed hand seals with both hands. Her face quickly turned pale, and fine beads of sweat seeped from her forehead like morning dew condensing on flower petals.

Yet her voice was clear and firm, carrying a solemnity tempered by long practice of divine rituals: "Today at the No-Retreat Plaza of the Verification Peak, I, Zhu Fenxiang, respectfully invite the True Lord of Wutu Prison Suppression to cast down his divine gaze and bear witness to Ning Zhuo's oath!"

An earthy-yellow divine light slowly kindled upon the statue. A heavy, boundless aura settled over the plaza, like an invisible mountain descending ponderously from the void, weighing down the churning minds of the crowd.

The Truth Bell remained silent, but outside of that silence was now an added layer of coercive pressure from a divine witness.

Ning Zhuo looked deeply at Zhu Fenxiang and gave a solemn, cupped-fist bow in gratitude.

Though Zhu Fenxiang's face was pale, she gave a slight tilt of her chin as she looked back at him.

Seeing this, Chunyangzi could not help but narrow his eyes. Logically speaking, the group of Confucian cultivators and the Zhu mother and daughter ascending the peak to build momentum for Ning Zhuo should be a good thing.

Yet a feeling of unease welled up in Chunyangzi's heart.

He immediately seized upon this peculiar sense of unrest. Pondering carefully, he swiftly realized its source - judging by Ning Zhuo's expression, the boy hadn't known about this arrangement either. Therefore, the current situation was out of control!

On the side of Flowing Cloud Peak, someone could not help but let out a cold snort. "Confucian cultivators, the Divine Dao, Qinghuangzi... quite the lively gathering."

The cultivator beside him had a face as dark as a storm cloud. "The livelier they get, the more the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect cannot afford to lose. If Ning Zhuo is allowed to secure his footing today with the help of the Truth Bell, things will be immensely troublesome moving forward."

"Rest easy," a female cultivator said, her eyes narrowing, her voice crisp yet laced with a chilling cold. "The Three Valiants will make sure Ning Zhuo dies a very ugly death. The grander their momentum is today, the more tragic - and laughable - it will be when he is defeated and killed in the future."

Someone else spoke up impatiently. "Hurry it up. This is nothing more than a show! Take the oath quickly, set a date for the martial demonstration, and let us step forward to give our consent. The fire in my alchemy furnace hasn't even been extinguished yet."

Right at that moment, a sudden boom echoed from the sky.

The crowd looked up to see a streak of iron-red light plummeting from the sky.

Bang.

The newcomer landed on the plaza like a falling anvil, carrying a heavy gale of wind pressure.

Several lower-cultivation practitioners instinctively took half a step back, intimidated by the arrival's sheer presence.

Countless people turned to look, their faces flashing with surprise.

For this newcomer possessed a status superior to almost everyone present - he was the current Master of the Artifact Refining Hall, Tie Kuang!

Tie Kuang was built as burly as an iron pagoda, with a rugged face and the unreserved, sweeping aura of a Nascent Soul-level expert.

Tie Kuang strode toward Ning Zhuo. His gaze swept first over the Truth Bell, then over the group of Confucian cultivators, the Zhu mother and daughter, and Qinghuangzi, before finally landing on Ning Zhuo’s face. His mouth split into a rough grin. "Little Friend Ning Zhuo, I've come to join in on the fun today too."

Ning Zhuo bowed. "Greetings, Senior Tie Kuang. May I ask..."

Tie Kuang waved a massive hand, his sleeve catching the wind. "At the request of Qinghuangzi, I have come to lend you a hand as well."

As he spoke, he flipped his palm to produce an object.

A Spirit Treasure - the Thousandfold Hundred-Refined Hammer!

Ning Zhuo’s gaze sharpened as he took in the Spirit Treasure. It measured a mere one foot seven inches, completely pitch black, looking like old iron that had endured years of smoke and roaring flames. The head of the hammer was square, its faces uneven and pitted with dense, countless divots.

Dark red iron wire was wrapped around the handle, looking as though it had long been soaked through by palm sweat, furnace fires, and spiritual blood, its deep color resembling dried scabs.

Out on the field, cultivators quickly recognized the origins of this Spirit Treasure. It was Tie Kuang's Lifebound Spirit Treasure. It could put an artifact through a thousand trials and a hundred refinements; fixing a tool mid-battle was a mere side effect, its true function was its ability to continuously amplify an artifact's potency in a specific direction.

"Tie Kuang is taking action too!"

"He's actually lending out his Spirit Treasure? Is he siding with the Naming Stronghold?"

"Why would he suddenly show such favor to Ning Zhuo?"

"That part I can actually understand. Everyone knows Tie Kuang's personality; he wanted to repair the Naming Fire Furnace before, but was thwarted by its artifact spirit. Now that Ning Zhuo has pacified the spirit, he wouldn't be able to resist itching to repair it."

"But since Ning Zhuo is only taking an oath today, why would he bring out his Lifebound Spirit Treasure for it?"

The crowd buzzed with chatter, tossing out one speculation after another.

The faces of the various Flowing Cloud Peak cultivators had turned profoundly ugly by this point.

Tie Kuang carried vastly different weight; he was one of the sixteen Hall Masters, an absolute high-ranking figure inside the Myriad Manifestations Sect!

Just Tie Kuang's appearance alone was enough to bring heavy pressure down upon the various factions of Flowing Cloud Peak. Let alone the fact that he was now bringing out his Lifebound Spirit Treasure to assist Ning Zhuo in public!

The envoy from Heavy Formation Peak frowned slightly, instantly recalculating the trajectory of the entire board.

The envoy from Danxia Peak's gaze sank even deeper.

Meanwhile, the cold-faced deacon hailing from the Demon Subjugation Hall stared at the duo of Ning Zhuo and Tie Kuang, his expression highly convoluted as he pondered inwardly: Could it be that after Ning Zhuo distanced himself from our hall, he immediately threw his lot in with the Artifact Refining Hall?

No matter what the truth was, everyone's attitude toward Ning Zhuo changed because of Tie Kuang.

Ning Zhuo displayed a confused look, cupping his fists. "Many thanks for Senior Tie Kuang's assistance, it's just that..."

Tie Kuang cut him off once more. "Little Friend Ning Zhuo, time is short. Hurry up and take the oath."

Ning Zhuo nodded, turning to stand before the Truth Bell.

Ning Zhuo could feel the innumerable gazes converging from all directions, hitting his back like ten thousand lanterns simultaneously pointed at him.

At the precipice ahead of him, the mountain winds howled. The black Truth Bell was as silent as a deep well, the sea of clouds roiling beyond the cliffside as though heaven and earth were holding their breath, waiting for his next words.

Ning Zhuo took a deep breath, his chest slowly expanding like a bow being pulled taut.

He raised both hands, slowly cupping them in a salute as his sleeves billowed slightly in the wind.

From within the Great Thousand Mechanical Resonance Robe, a delicate tinkling chimed out, like a clear spring dripping into stone crevices, ethereal and crisp.

Ning Zhuo spoke, his voice steady and clear, carrying across the entire plaza. "Seniors and fellow disciples of the Myriad Manifestations Sect above. With the Truth Bell before me and the divine statue of the True Lord of Wutu Prison Suppression as my witness: today, I, Ning Zhuo, take this oath."

Across the No-Retreat Plaza, countless cultivators fell quiet as one. Even those hostile practitioners from Flowing Cloud Peak reined in the sneers on their faces at this moment.

"Senior Red-Robed Guest died because of the Naming Stronghold, his soul cast adrift and subjected to another's control. As the founder of the Naming Stronghold, I, Ning Zhuo, am unwilling to stand idly by, nor do I wish to shirk this duty."

"From this day forth, I, Ning Zhuo, shall seek out his soul, strive to deliver him from his plight, and secure his peace in the afterlife."

On the side of the Confucian cultivators, the scent of ink thickened like mist. The plain paper had already been unrolled to a length of thirty feet, gently rising and falling in the wind like a white river.

The jade brush in Chu Xuangui’s hand continued to strike downward, his strokes firm, weathered, and vigorous, like an old pine taking root in a sheer precipice. With every sentence written, a wisp of white Literary Qi curled up from the paper, turning into a gentle breeze that wound lazily around Ning Zhuo like an invisible sash tied to his waist.

Dong Nishang sat cross-legged, taking out a guqin. Its body was dark-greenish black as though soaked in old ink, its strings as bright as frost and snow. The instant her fingers settled over the strings, a crisp note rippled out - the sound of the qin was not showy or melodic, but dignified and upright, unfolding layer by layer, steadily subduing the restless, agitated thoughts floating in the hearts of the crowd...

Standing in front of the divine statue, Zhu Fenxiang's fingers were already flying through hand seals, her terracotta robe lit up by the divine radiance like the evening glow.

Her face was as pale as paper, but her lips were pressed into a tight, knife-carved line.

Standing behind her, Zhu Guizhi's gaze was as gentle and still as an autumn lake, but inside her sleeve, her fingers lightly tapped against a jade talisman to guard against the unexpected.

Ning Zhuo spoke again: "The demonic soul of Senior Red-Robed Guest has fallen into another's hands. I, Ning Zhuo, am willing to fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect - "

Suddenly, Tie Kuang's Lifebound Spirit Treasure, the Thousandfold Hundred-Refined Hammer, gave a soft shudder. Its pitch-black body abruptly flared with a dull red spiritual light.

The dull red light radiated out in all directions, as layer upon layer of forging runes rippled outward along with the spiritual light, smithing the surrounding space.

Halo after halo of light drifted down, shrouding the immediate vicinity around Ning Zhuo.

Ning Zhuo paused, his face expressing astonishment.

At that exact moment, Zhu Fenxiang cried out sharply, "I beseech the True Lord to cast down His hand!"

Her ten fingers flew like falling blossoms and dancing butterflies. The earthy-yellow light on the divine-patterned jade belt abruptly exploded in intensity.

The temple-sized divine statue of the True Lord of Wutu Prison Suppression roared in response, a heavy, primeval divine light flaring from its eyes.

The divine light was not blinding, but it pressed down like the loess earth, landing instantly beneath Ning Zhuo's feet.

The black stone plaza convulsed. Beneath Ning Zhuo's feet, an earthy-yellow divine domain rose up in the perfect shape of a square prison! Divine patterns draped down on all four sides of the domain, their linework as thick as iron locks, sealing off every single inch of retreat to Ning Zhuo's front, back, left, and right.

At the exact same time, the poetry and prose of the Confucian cultivators arrived.
