Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 990: Return

Chen San returned to the tavern.

As he passed through the lobby, many gazes fell upon him, gently pricking him like fine needles.

"I heard Ning Zhuo was taken away by Hall Master Tie Kuang."

"What is he still doing here?"

"I bet a lot of money on the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, and now they aren't fighting? Are they messing with us?!"

Chen San heard them clearly, but his expression remained as steady as a boulder, and his footsteps didn't falter in the slightest.

Today, he had changed into an ash-cyan long robe. Hanging at his waist was the token Ning Zhuo had given him. His cuffs were neat, his boots clean. Although his Foundation Establishment cultivation was nothing special in the Myriad Manifestations Sect, having Ning Zhuo standing behind him naturally made him straighten his back much more.

He entered the long-term rented private booth, lit a stick of mind-clearing incense on the table, and then laid out a stack of rosters, contract deeds, and item lists one by one, just like a general inspecting his weapons before marching into battle.

Ning Zhuo's orders were very clear: Chen San was to express gratitude on his behalf and offer to return the items. For items that had already been consumed or damaged, he was to sign a debt contract, agreeing to compensate them according to their market price. For those who could not be contacted, he was to publicly post a notice announcing the matter, explaining the situation, and sincerely apologizing.

"Young Master Ning Zhuo is indeed different from those scions of noble clans. He truly takes responsibility!"

Chen San's heart grew warm. It wasn't just because Ning Zhuo had entrusted such a heavy responsibility to a lowly Foundation Establishment cultivator like him, but also because Ning Zhuo's character made him increasingly certain that choosing to follow him was the absolutely correct decision!

The first to arrive was Shen Hongyao.

Shen Hongyao wore a red dress that rested on her shoulders like rosy clouds, with fine medicinal patterns embroidered along the hemlines. A crimson jade hairpin adorned her hair, and her features were as bright and beautiful as fiery autumn maple leaves, her eyes seemingly containing a cluster of warm firelight.

Chen San stood up, bowed, and offered a jade bottle with both hands. "Miss Shen, my Young Master is currently trapped in the Rising Cloud Minor Trial and cannot schedule the duel with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect in the short term. My Young Master said that he deeply remembers your profound kindness in his heart. This pill is precious, and since he cannot use it at the moment, he asks that you take it back for now."

Shen Hongyao looked at the jade bottle, the smile in her eyes pausing slightly. She reached out, her fingertips resting on the bottle, but she didn't immediately pick it up.

A ray of sunlight happened to slant in from outside the window, falling on her lowered eyelashes and reflecting tiny, fragmented spots of golden light.

"He asked you to return this?" she asked softly, her voice betraying neither joy nor anger.

Chen San respectfully replied, "My Young Master said that one cannot slight the favors received from others. He asked me to personally thank everyone who could be found."

Shen Hongyao suddenly smiled. She picked up the jade bottle and tucked it into her sleeve, her expression as brisk and straightforward as ever. "Alright, I'll take it back. Tell Ning Zhuo that if he goes to war with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect in the future, I will still lend him this demonic pill."

Chen San solemnly clasped his fists. "I will definitely deliver your words, Miss."

A middle-aged cultivator, still dressed in a cloud-patterned long robe with features as gentle as distant spring mountains, was the second to arrive after receiving the flying letter.

Chen San repeated Ning Zhuo's words verbatim, then handed over the Cloud Beast to the middle-aged cultivator.

"This beast is precious. My Young Master told me I must return it in perfect condition."

The middle-aged cultivator nodded. He took out a Cloud Bell and gave it a gentle shake - the Cloud Beast transformed into a wisp of white cloud and silently drilled into the bell. "I will pass the message to Yunyouzi."

The middle-aged cultivator put away the Cloud Bell and said warmly, "Since your Young Master took the initiative to return it, borrowing it again in the future won't be difficult. It's just that I'm afraid you won't be able to contact Yunyouzi directly, so just contact me first."

Chen San bowed. "Thank you, Senior."

Cao Gui ran over hurriedly.

Beads of sweat still clung to his forehead, and a faint medicinal scent lingered on his body.

"My Lord personally instructed me that this pill should remain in Young Master Ning's hands; there is no need to return it."

Chen San frowned. "This..."

Cao Gui lowered his voice. "Go back and tell Young Master Ning Zhuo that if he truly feels guilty for receiving it, he can just come to Danxia Peak when he has time, have a cup of tea, and discuss the Dao of Alchemy with the Peak Master."

Chen San's heart jolted at these words. He took back the treasure box and said solemnly, "I will definitely bring your words back, Brother Cao."

After Cao Gui left, Chen San felt even more admiration for Ning Zhuo, thinking to himself, "It's exactly as the Young Master told me it would be."

After this, flying letters arrived one after another.

"These Azure-Veined Bamboo Cores, Cloud Silk Soft Copper, and Fine-Tooth Silver Springs are just a small sum of money to begin with. If Young Master Ning returns them all to me, where am I supposed to put my old face?"

"What would I take them back for? To pile up in my warehouse and gather dust? Little Friend Ning Zhuo has the Empty Valley Syllable Azure Mechanism Tube inheritance; these things have only found their true purpose by falling into his hands."

"Young Master Ning didn't break his promise. He swore his intent beneath the Truth Bell; he will definitely fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect in the future. What we sponsored was this intent, and also this person. Once the items are given out, they are given out."

...

Chen San recorded these responses one by one, and suddenly understood: Ning Zhuo's act of returning the items this time had actually made many sponsors even more resolute, massively boosting Ning Zhuo's reputation!

Elegant Study.

Outside the window, bamboo shadows swayed like freehand ink brushwork, and a half-opened scroll of the Record of Knowledge and Action lay on the desk.

Kong Zhaoming read the urgently delivered intelligence from Kong Ran. The content primarily detailed how Ning Zhuo was trapped in the Rising Cloud Minor Trial, and subsequently took the initiative to return the sponsorships from all parties and apologize to them one by one.

At first, Kong Zhaoming didn't think much of it, but after finishing reading, he suddenly clapped his hands and sighed in admiration, "Excellent!"

Kong Zhaoming pressed his palm against the desk, his eyes filled with undisguised approval. "He knows when to advance and when to retreat, and can yield or stand tall as needed. For a young man to reach the crest of such a storm and still be able to proactively take a step back, and to properly handle every single sponsorship... it is rare. Truly very rare."

Kong Zhaoming thought for a moment and smiled slightly. "Since he understands etiquette and keeps his promises, what harm is there in handing this National Power Brick over to him?"

He then picked up his brush and, without hesitation, wrote a flying letter. As the brush moved, the ink spread across the paper like living water.

Compared to these people, the atmosphere at Bai Qingya's end was much heavier.

Bai Qingya and the other higher-ups remained silent for a long time.

A middle-aged clansman finally couldn't help but speak, his voice laced with uncontainable anxiety, "Clan Uncle, we have already exposed ourselves. The news of our sponsorship of Ning Zhuo has definitely reached the Death-Facing Sect. The Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect might not have time to deal with us now, but what about the future?"

A female cultivator said in a low voice, "With the Myriad Manifestations Sect's rules in place, our lives are probably safe. But our businesses, spirit fields, and the future prospects of the clan's younger generation could be stonewalled at every turn."

Bai Qingya gripped his walking stick, the veins on the back of his hand bulging like the twisted roots of an old tree.

Such an unexpected incident suddenly occurring with Ning Zhuo was no different from a bolt out of the blue when the news reached them.

The flying letter sent by Chen San made the Bai clan even more restless, prompting them to convene an emergency meeting.

A young clansman suddenly raised his head, his voice carrying the unique recklessness and fiery passion of youth. "Didn't Young Master Ning take in Huo Nian'er? If a loose cultivator can defect to him, why can't our Bai clan? We even brought out the Patriarch's skeletal remains; if we take them back, can we really just pretend this never happened?"

The hall instantly fell silent.

Bai Qingya's eyes trembled as he slowly looked toward the young man.

The young man's face flushed bright red, but he gritted his teeth and continued, "Rather than waiting for the Death-Facing Sect to suppress us, it would be better for the entire clan to defect to the Nanming Stronghold! Young Master Ning values faith and righteousness. The fact that he sent someone to return the treasures today proves that he is not a man without honor."

Bai Qingya scanned the hall, observing the changing expressions of the crowd as they went from hesitant to tempted.

He slowly closed his eyes, and what surfaced in the darkness was the blood feud of the past.

He abruptly opened his eyes, let out a long breath, and spoke in a voice that was weary yet underlined with absolute resolution: "Then the entire clan shall go and defect to him!"

Frost Cliff Liu Clan.

The Liu Clan Elders were also currently discussing the matter, centered around Chen San's flying letter.

Some advocated taking the items back immediately to avoid the storm. Some suggested they might as well just defect to Ning Zhuo. Others gritted their teeth and said that since they had already stepped forward, shrinking back now would only cause them to be looked down upon, ultimately leaving them in a bad spot on both ends.

Finally, the Liu Clan's Grand Elder sighed wearily, "We won't take back the Old Battle Map. Tell Young Master Ning that since the Liu Clan has sent it out, we are willing to wait for the day he goes to war in the future."

Daoist Waning Lamp was staying in a dilapidated guest house.

Carrying the oil lamp missing half its shade on his back, he sat by the window. The room was filled with a dim, yellow light, like an old dream.

By the lamp's light, he read Chen San's flying letter and slowly shook his head. "Ning Zhuo swore an oath in public; he cannot go back on his word. If he doesn't fight today, and doesn't fight tomorrow, he will still have to fight one day. This old man's waning lamp can still afford to wait."

The manager of the Yundu Merchant Firm had a round face and a plump body. As he read the flying letter, his hands habitually flicked the beads of his abacus.

The abacus beads clacked and popped like a fine drizzle of rain. With a beaming smile, he muttered to himself, "The items sent out by this firm follow the principle of 'no regrets once given'. Taking them back would be the real loss-making business."

The young sword cultivator Jiao Chi came directly to the tavern to meet Chen San face-to-face. He remained a man of few words, as cold as iron. His left eye was covered with a black cloth, and his back was as lean and hard as a sword.

Chen San handed the broken sword back to him, explaining Ning Zhuo's apologies.

Jiao Chi reached out and took the broken sword. He lightly swiped his finger across the broken edge; the sharp, jagged stub cut the pad of his finger. A bead of blood welled up and was immediately absorbed by the broken sword.

"Wait until he sets the date for the battle, then come find me again." After saying this, he turned and left.

A scrawny old woman who claimed to be a former subordinate of Crane Dock also came forward on her own accord.

She grinned, revealing a few sparse teeth. "Return what? This old woman asks you: does Ning Zhuo want more Starfire Ash?"

Chen San was slightly taken aback.

The old woman tapped the medicine bottle with her finger, and the powder inside instantly glowed with faint blue and red light, flickering like sparks in the dark. "The Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect are still alive; this old woman's price remains right here. If Ning Zhuo can bring out something to my satisfaction to trade, this old woman has plenty of stock available!"

There were many hidden sponsors who could not be contacted. Thus, following Ning Zhuo's orders, Chen San posted notices in front of the tavern, at the entrance to the market, and outside a few of the most well-informed teahouses.

The notice was written properly and earnestly: Ning Zhuo is temporarily trapped in the Rising Cloud Minor Trial and cannot fulfill the scheduling of the duel in the short term. Any previous anonymous sponsors who wish to withdraw their sponsorship may come to the private room on the third floor of this tavern at noon three days from now to retrieve their sponsorship using their tokens. If the sponsorship has been consumed, the Nanming Stronghold will compensate according to market price. Ning Zhuo is deeply grateful for the profound kindness of all parties; he will definitely fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect in the future. This resolve remains unchanged!

As soon as the notice was posted, a crowd quickly gathered around it.

"He's really returning them?"

"This Ning Zhuo is quite interesting. Others can't wait to swallow any treasures they get their hands on, yet he's taking the initiative to post a notice."

"This is what you call smart. If it were me, I'd also be willing to deal with someone like this."

"He made his words clear, 'This resolve remains unchanged' - it seems this battle with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect will happen sooner or later."

...

Amidst the crowd, a masked cultivator squeezed his way in.

He was of average height, wearing a baggy gray robe and a bamboo hat pressed extremely low, revealing only a chin as pale as paper. His movements were very light, like a shadow drifting close to the ground; the people around him didn't even realize anything was amiss when they were pushed aside.

He raised his head, his gaze sweeping over the notice like a falcon's.

The first time, it wasn't there.

The second time, it was still not there.

His expression sank slightly as he read every single word again, from top to bottom, from the left column to the right.

Still not there!

The Three Valiants Life-Claiming Nail was not there!

The masked cultivator's breathing stopped for an infinitesimal moment, like the surface of the water suddenly returning to dead calm after being wrinkled by the wind.

He had come under orders to retrieve that Demonic Path magic treasure pinned with a Nascent Soul's soul. Now that Ning Zhuo was trapped in the Rising Cloud Minor Trial and ought to be returning everyone's sponsorships by taking advantage of the situation, the Three Valiants Life-Claiming Nail should logically appear on this list.

Yet, on the notice, not a single word mentioned it.
