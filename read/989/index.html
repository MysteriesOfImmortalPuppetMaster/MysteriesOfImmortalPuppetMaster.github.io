Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 989: Winning Before the Battle Even Begins!

Although Chunyangzi established himself with the Pure Yang Dao methods, his centuries of cultivation also involved mechanisms, artifact refining, and formations; he was by no means a mere novice in them. He only touched the cultivation art for a moment, letting his divine sense sweep over it hastily, yet he recognized its solid foundation. It was balanced in offense and defense, and exceptionally adept at close-quarters protection and blade-breaking intercepts.

In the battle for the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, a Golden Core-level personal guard couldn't change the overall situation, but it could block a fatal strike at a critical moment.

Ning Zhuo's compensation could not be considered light.

Chunyangzi's expression softened slightly.

He knew that in Ning Zhuo's arrangement, there was apology, but also calculation.

Returning the Yang Fish indicated that Ning Zhuo had no intention of embezzling the Pure Yang Palace's sect-guarding spiritual treasure. Temporarily keeping the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill was to continue comprehending the Yang Dao so he could fulfill their agreed martial battle in the future. Loaning out the Void-Mind Blade-Breaking Guard was telling Chunyangzi that the plan for Nanming Stronghold to vie for the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar was still advancing; even though Ning Zhuo was trapped, he hadn't forgotten Chunyangzi's goal.

The youth spoke beautifully and handled matters comprehensively.

Chunyangzi still felt stifled in his heart, yet he found it hard to seize upon this matter to throw a temper.

In the No-Retreat Plaza, the chime of that bell still rang in his ears. It had simply been suppressed by a group of old fogeys.

Chunyangzi suddenly sighed. Within this sigh, there was anger, annoyance, and a trace of indescribable exhaustion.

"So be it," he retracted his hand and said indifferently, "The Pure Yang Patriarch Pill will remain in Ning Zhuo's hands for now. Only for now."

Chunyangzi still harbored a scheme in his heart. In a short period, the Five-Win Martial Contest would yield a result.

Ning Zhuo wouldn't take much time, so it wouldn't affect the foundational essence of the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill.

If Ning Zhuo absorbed too much Pure Yang true intent and his mind was affected, I'm afraid he would have no choice but to switch his cultivation to the Pure Yang Pill Scripture.

His mind made up, Chunyangzi stowed the Void-Mind Blade-Breaking Guard and turned to leave. He would just let Ning Zhuo stay trapped.

The situation in the Nanming Stronghold couldn't stall inside the Rising Cloud Minor Trial alongside Ning Zhuo.

He had to fight for the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. And he had to be fast!

This place formed a world of its own.

Ning Zhuo sat cross-legged on the crimson-black earth. There were no mountains, rivers, or trees around him, nor were there seas of clouds or sweeping winds; there was only a vast domain illuminated by forging fires.

Above his head was an earth-yellow dome, as heavy and thick as an inverted mountain. Deep within the dome, the silhouette of the True Lord Wutu Prison-Suppressor's idol faintly emerged.

The idol sat properly with its eyes closed, its face blurred, yet beneath its knees hung thousands of chains. One end of the chains vanished into the void, while the other plunged into the earth, as if pinning this entire world in place.

Beneath the earth, furnace fires flowed. Threads of crimson, dark gold, and iron-black fire slowly traversed along the earth veins, sometimes converging into hammer patterns, sometimes dispersing into furnace smoke. Every few moments, a deep hammering sound echoed in the void.

Thud.

As the hammer sound fell, the earth quivered slightly in response.

Ning Zhuo's bones, flesh, spiritual energy, and divine soul all felt a faint numbness from that shockwave. The hammering didn't injure him directly, but it felt like an artifact refiner's testing strike before letting the hammer fall, measuring his material, texture, flaws, and endurance limit over and over again.

This was the Divine Domain of the True Lord Wutu Prison-Suppressor. It was also Tie Kuang's forging cultivation art. Furthermore, it contained the power of the spiritual treasure, the Hundred-Forged Thousand-Fold Hammer.

The convergence of these three things forged this world of the Rising Cloud Minor Trial.

The Divine Domain focused on suppression, its thick earth acting as a prison, cutting off Ning Zhuo from borrowing external power. Tie Kuang's forging art focused on smelting, transforming everything around into a furnace, bellows, and anvil. The Hundred-Forged Thousand-Fold Hammer focused on testing the artifact - every hammer strike a question, every vibration a refinement, as if trying to strike out the truest resonance from Ning Zhuo's body.

Ning Zhuo exhaled a breath of turbid qi. The moment the turbid qi left his lips, it was swept up by a wisp of crimson-gold fire in mid-air, turning into grayish-white smoke before instantly dissipating.

The youth was somewhat apprehensive, yet harbored a certain expectation.

The Yang Fish had to be returned. This was only proper. Chunyangzi's willingness to lend him the Yang Fish was a tremendous show of support. Now that he was trapped in the Rising Cloud Minor Trial and unable to fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect anytime soon, keeping the Pure Yang Palace's sect-guarding spiritual treasure by his side would be unjustifiable.

But the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill... Ning Zhuo curled his fingers slightly.

I really don't want to return it.

That gray, pebble-like medicinal pill was currently still deep within his divine sea. On the surface, it appeared plain and dormant, but inside, it hid a boundless, blazing Yang Dao sun. With every observation, Ning Zhuo could absorb vast amounts of true intent from it.

Absorbing true intent felt incredibly exhilarating! Ning Zhuo had tasted the sweetness and could not extricate himself.

But if Chunyangzi insisted on retrieving it, he would definitely return it. "If I ruin my credibility and reputation for the sake of temporary cultivation, that would truly be destroying my foundation."

Just as the thought fell, a subtle vibration suddenly echoed within his divine sea. It was a message relayed by Tie Kuang from the outside.

Ning Zhuo's eyes lit up slightly. Chunyangzi had actually agreed to let the youth borrow the Pure Yang Patriarch Pill temporarily!

"Excellent."

The sunlight within the pill exploded outward. Ning Zhuo began to comprehend once again.

But this time, it was different from before.

A cinnabar hemp rope flew out of his storage bag, as agile as a snake, shooting straight up into the air. Since there were no roof beams here, it coiled a few times in the void, while the other end tightly bound Ning Zhuo's hair.

A faint yet distinct tugging pain immediately came from his scalp. This pain was constant and continuous, like a fine needle forever piercing through any laziness. The moment a trace of distracting thoughts surfaced, it would be scattered by that slight pain, instantly making his divine sea several degrees clearer.

Hair suspended from a beam! This was to awaken oneself from laziness and force deep self-reflection.

Ning Zhuo then took out a boxwood awl and gripped it in his palm.

He looked down at his left leg and, without hesitation, pressed his wrist down firmly. The wooden awl pierced into his flesh, about an inch deep.

A sharp pain shot straight into his divine sea. Ning Zhuo's throat tightened, and a layer of fine sweat immediately beaded on his forehead. Yet his gaze became even brighter, his thoughts like a sharpened awl's tip, piercing straight toward the barrier of the Yang Dao that he had so far been unable to break through.

Awl piercing the thigh! This was to overcome pretense, fear of difficulty, and ambiguity through intense, sharp pain. The clearer the pain, the sharper his thoughts.

Immediately after, a wisp of Civil and Martial Wind swirled out of his sleeve, circling his body.

At first, the Civil and Martial Wind was extremely faint, like the clear air stirred by turning book pages. As Ning Zhuo's divine sense was rapidly consumed and his Upper Dantian divine sea grew faintly hot, the Civil and Martial Wind would consume itself, nourishing his sea of consciousness wisp by wisp. Like a clear spring flowing over scorching stone slabs, it suppressed the restlessness brought by excessive deduction.

Finally, he also activated the Luoshu page.

An indescribable sense of mathematical logic reflected from the unseen void. Black and white dots wandered within Ning Zhuo's mind, gathering and scattering unpredictably, ultimately falling into their correct positions time and time again.

Ning Zhuo felt his direction of calculation being constantly corrected. He was no longer blindly fumbling through the complex true intent of the Yang Dao. Every deconstruction, every association, every choice of what to keep and discard, advanced along a much more correct path.

The Pure Yang Patriarch Pill fully revealed its true appearance!

Within his divine sea, the gray stone pill fractured with countless fine lines. Golden-white radiance surged out from the cracks, the light gathering brighter and brighter until it finally transformed into a massive sun hanging above Ning Zhuo's divine sea.

Within the great sun, there seemed to be blazing sunlight, furnace fire, crimson gold, warm winds, spring thunder, and surging blood energy.

These were the various external manifestations of the Artisan-level Yang Dao realm.

Ning Zhuo had previously used his Five Elements realm to deconstruct the nature of fire. He had also contemplated Pure Yang Qi and bore Primordial Yang within him, understanding the manifestations of the Yang Dao in external objects. Yang was heat, Yang was light, Yang was motion, Yang was ascension; it was the sliver of brightness that illuminated the gloom.

The Renowned Artisan-level Yang Dao allowed him to unify these external manifestations into underlying principles. It also gave him a stronger tolerance, enabling him to use other methods to assist his learning and accelerate his progress!

As the true intent was constantly consumed, Ning Zhuo felt a ravenous thirst.

He didn't know how long had passed when Ning Zhuo suddenly saw a breath of qi.

That breath rose from the chest. At first, it was extremely faint, like a spark of vitality returning to the eyes of a sick person. Then, it passed through flesh and blood, through meridians, through the mind and soul, like a young man standing in the morning light, his back straightening inch by inch.

Ning Zhuo's heart trembled slightly.

So the Yang Dao didn't merely exist in the external world.

It existed within the human body.

In a breath of qi that refused to sink.

In the split second a dying person kept their eyes open.

In the spark of light that emerged in the eyes of a trapped person looking up at the exit.

He then saw ghostly creatures trembling under the sunlight. Shadows were illuminated, their boundaries defined; evils were forced into their true forms. All hidden, decaying, and slumbering things revealed their traces under the sun.

Ning Zhuo's breathing grew rapid.

His Five Elements realm surged up in response.

The peerlessly domineering Pure Yang true intent was once again deconstructed strand by strand, divided, assimilated, and merged into his understanding.

The faint pain from his hair suspended on the beam kept him focused.

The sharp pain from the awl piercing his thigh kept him anchored in reality.

The Civil and Martial Wind allowed him to push through the exhaustion of his divine sense time and time again.

The Luoshu page ensured that every step of his deduction landed in the correct place.

Thud.

Ning Zhuo's shoulder jolted, a trace of blood spilling from his lips.

Thud.

The great sun in his divine sea suddenly pulsed, its sunlight exploding into thousands of shattered rays.

Thud.

The shattered rays reconverged, falling into his chest.

Ning Zhuo slowly opened his eyes.

His eyes held no erupting flames, nor blinding golden light; there was only a clear, warm vitality. It was like a window finally being pushed open in a dim room, allowing the morning light to stream in, illuminating every speck of dust clearly.

Master-Level.

The Yang Dao realm, at this moment, officially broke past the limits of a Renowned Artisan, stepping through the gates of a Master.

A Renowned Artisan understands Yang, can wield Yang, and can distinguish Yang. A Master observes Yang, able to see beyond the blazing sun and furnace fires, to perceive that singular breath of brightness in the hearts of all things.

Ning Zhuo lowered his head and looked at his palm.

A wisp of Yang Qi rose from his palm. It wasn't as blazing and extroverted as before; instead, it was gentle, clear, and composed. It was like a lamp's flame, yet possessed the majesty of sunlight. It was like blood energy, yet held the vitality of spring thunder. It was like a breath of qi, rising from the deepest parts of his lungs and organs, supporting the youth's spine.

Ning Zhuo gently exhaled a breath of turbid qi.

The wooden awl in his leg was still embedded in his flesh, and the cinnabar hemp rope still pulled his hair, making his roots ache. His face was pale, and sweat dripped from his jaw onto the crimson-black ground, quickly evaporated by the furnace fire into a wisp of white smoke.

Yet his eyes were brighter than they had ever been.

Inside the Demon Subjugation Hall, the lamplight was as cold and white as frost, casting a chilly and chillingly austere glow upon the four walls.

Zhong Dao sat upright behind a long desk. His posture was as straight as a pine, his shoulders and back like a sword, and his face as cold and hard as iron.

A jade slip lay open on the desk, recording the entire sequence of events of the incident at the No-Retreat Plaza on Zhengshi Peak, as well as the various undercurrents of the different factions after Ning Zhuo was taken away by Tie Kuang.

After Zhong Dao finished reading, he tapped his knuckles gently on the desk twice. Tap. Tap. The sound was extremely light, yet it rang like two pieces of cold iron striking together.

The subordinate standing below couldn't help but stiffen his back a bit straighter.

After a long time, Zhong Dao spoke, his voice as deep as a stone grinding over sand. "It was I who underestimated Ning Zhuo."

The subordinate paused slightly, a hint of confusion in his eyes. Cupping his hands, he said, "Hall Master, Ning Zhuo is currently trapped by Tie Kuang in the Rising Cloud Minor Trial. He cannot battle the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect in the short term. The future outcome is still unknown."

He paused for a moment, then added, "Ning Zhuo did indeed receive Tie Kuang's sponsorship, but what Tie Kuang seeks is the Naming Fire Furnace. His intervention is aimed at repairing the furnace and interfering in the interest dynamics of the Nanming Stronghold. In the end, every party has their own underlying motives."

Hearing this, Zhong Dao raised his eyes and looked at him. This glance was incredibly calm, yet it made the subordinate's throat tighten, as if lightly gripped by invisible iron tongs.

Zhong Dao shook his head. "Ning Zhuo has already won."

"Won?" Obvious doubt appeared on the subordinate's face. "Your subordinate is dull-witted. Please enlighten me, Hall Master."

Zhong Dao reached out and picked up the jade slip on the desk. The jade slip slowly twirled between his fingers, reflecting a sliver of cold white light.

"You only see that he is trapped for seven days, that he failed to finalize the martial duel agreement, and that Tie Kuang seized the opportunity to act. You fail to see - over these past few days, how many people proactively delivered medicinal pills, magic treasures, automaton materials, intelligence, and favors straight into his hands."

The subordinate hurriedly replied, "But most of those sponsorships were borrowed items. Some had contracts drawn up, and some were meant to be returned anyway. This subordinate just received news that Ning Zhuo has dispatched people to visit everyone, returning the sponsorships on a large scale and apologizing one by one. He stated that since he temporarily cannot fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, he doesn't dare accept the various parties' profound kindness for nothing."

Upon hearing this, a sharp gleam flashed across Zhong Dao's eyes. "That is precisely his brilliance."

The subordinate stood frozen in place.

Zhong Dao placed the jade slip back on the desk, his voice growing deeper. "By accepting the sponsorships, he let everyone in the world see how many people he, Ning Zhuo, could leverage, and how many factions were willing to place their bets on him. By returning the sponsorships, he let those factions see that he, Ning Zhuo, has a sense of propriety, understands credibility, is neither greedy nor ignorant, and will not renege on his debts."

"When he took the sponsorships, what he gained was wealth and resources. When he returned them, what he gained was the hearts of the people."

"Those magic treasures and medicinal pills, sitting in a storage bag, could only save him once or twice. But those returned favors will resonate again and again in the future. People will say that Ning Zhuo is young but knows propriety. People will say that Ning Zhuo keeps his promises even in times of crisis. People will say that Ning Zhuo can be entrusted, befriended, and continued to be invested in."

Zhong Dao's knuckles pressed against the desk, his force slightly increasing. "Between this advance and retreat, he didn't just gain wealth; more importantly, he won people over."

The subordinate opened his mouth but found himself unable to speak for a moment. His mind was usually filled with things like the martial arena, the flash of blades, demonic cultivators, soul-searching, and beheadings - who was strong, who was weak, who killed whom, and who was killed. He could see all of these clearly.

Yet the intricate twists and turns Zhong Dao spoke of were like a dense, invisible spiderweb spread out before his eyes, the threads of which he simply could not distinguish.

Zhong Dao looked at his expression and felt disappointed in his heart. These people are fine for brave combat, but their brains are truly insufficient.

He waved his hand, dismissing him.

Left alone, Zhong Dao looked at the jade slip on his desk, his enthusiasm waning for a moment.

He stood up and paced around the great hall. The cold white lamplight in the Demon Subjugation Hall shone upon him, stretching his shadow immensely long.

Zhong Dao sighed inwardly. Cultivation is cultivation, and politics is politics; the two are completely different. A Nascent Soul expert who only knows bitter cultivation is nothing more than a battle blade.

Ning Zhuo is different!

This kid has a lot of political awareness. At such a young age, he already knows how to leverage power, create momentum, yield strategically, and establish trust. If such a person entered my Demon Subjugation Hall, he would certainly be able to hold up an entire theater of operations in the future.

What a pity, what a pity. His attitude towards Demonic Cultivators is far too soft.

At this thought, Zhong Dao's gaze suddenly grew cold. A Demonic Cultivator is a Demonic Cultivator. Even though the Red-Robed Guest has been in the Myriad Manifestations Sect for many years, he still can't wash clean that body full of demonic qi!

Ning Zhuo believes he is walking the Righteous Path.

But his Righteous Path still needs correcting!
