Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 978: Sincerity!

Ning Zhuo shook his head, expressing his insistence: "If you cannot help me defeat the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, it will be difficult for me to agree to a cooperation."

Tie Kuang seemed to have expected this response long ago. A confident and certain smile surfaced on his face: "Ning Zhuo, you might not be quite clear on this yet - having me repair the Naming Fire Furnace is your best choice right now, bar none."

He leaned forward, his gaze burning: "For every day the Naming Fire Furnace remains unrepaired, you cannot use it, and thus cannot truly clear those debts. Many Nascent Soul and Golden Core cultivators of the Naming Stronghold are orbiting around you, yet the furnace remains broken. Are you truly not anxious in your heart?"

Ning Zhuo did not answer.

Tie Kuang smiled and added, "As for why it is most suitable for me to repair it, I can demonstrate it for you right here and now. Give me the Naming Fire Furnace."

Ning Zhuo raised his eyebrows slightly.

He pondered for a moment, and eventually raised his hand, taking out the Naming Fire Furnace.

A scorching yet weak aura slowly surfaced within the room.

The Naming Fire Furnace had undergone a minor trial and was already in a much better state compared to its initial crumbling appearance. Slightly less than half of its outline was relatively intact. Charred black scars crisscrossed the body of the furnace, and the edges were densely stitched together with crimson-gold mending patterns.

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit was hidden from sight, concealing itself deep within the furnace body.

The moment Tie Kuang saw the Naming Fire Furnace, a flash of fire leaped in the depths of his eyes.

An Artifact Refiner facing a spirit treasure would naturally be excited, as well as heartbroken. It was like a sword cultivator seeing a peerless famed sword broken before their eyes - a heart-piercing pain that made them unable to resist wanting to reforge it with their own hands!

Tie Kuang slowly stood up.

He did not touch the Naming Fire Furnace, but instead took out a smoke censer from his waist.

The smoke censer was only the size of a fist, cast entirely from black iron. The body of the censer had nine orifices, and the edges of each orifice were carved with fine, dense fire patterns that gleamed with a dull red light under the lamp's glow.

As soon as the smoke censer appeared, the Fire Element spiritual energy within the cave manor was drawn by it, turning into rings of crimson ripples spreading outward in all directions.

Tie Kuang gave a low shout, his palm slamming down onto the top of the smoke censer!

Buzz...

The nine orifices of the smoke censer opened simultaneously. The fire patterns on the black iron body shifted from crimson to azure, and from azure to purple, transitioning layer by layer like a flower blooming nine times. Nine thin streams of smoke curled out from the orifices, like nine nimble fire snakes, shaking their heads and swishing their tails as they burrowed deep into the cracks of the Naming Fire Furnace.

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit was startled and curled up even tighter. Ning Zhuo's brow twitched slightly, forcing it to calm down.

The Vermilion Bird artifact spirit buried its small head deeper into its wings.

The nine thin streams of smoke followed the cracks, permeating into the furnace body.

At first, there was no movement. After about a few breaths, a fine sizzling sound suddenly emerged from deep within the Naming Fire Furnace.

A wisp of pale purple aura was slowly drawn out from the deepest part of the furnace's belly by the nine thin streams of smoke.

This aura was extremely faint, yet it carried an indescribably cloying, sweet scent. It was the pill fragrance of a Medicinal Pill overcooked due to excessive heat - a fragrance so sweet it was cloying, so cloying that it made one's throat tighten and chest feel stuffy.

As soon as the pale purple toxic gas emerged, the lamplight within the cave manor instantly dimmed for a moment.

Ning Zhuo's face paled slightly. He immediately stood up and took half a step back.

He hastily held his breath, staring at that wisp of toxic gas, genuine astonishment surfacing in his eyes: "Pill Toxin?"

Tie Kuang gave him a deep look, the technique in his hands not stopping.

Those nine thin streams of smoke gently guided the pale purple pill toxin, merging the toxin's smoke into themselves to carry it out of the furnace body, eventually drilling back into the black iron smoke censer.

The smoke censer vibrated slightly. A layer of purple spots surfaced on its body, spreading like snake scales.

Crimson firelight surged from Tie Kuang's palm, swiftly suppressing and refining it, dissipating it into nothingness.

Ning Zhuo instantly felt the tense, curled-up bird body of the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit relax a fraction.

Ning Zhuo's eyes remained fixed on the smoke censer, a sharp light radiating from them: "What an excellent censer, to actually be able to extract pill toxins! Such a heavy treasure is absolutely priceless!!"

Tie Kuang put away the smoke censer and looked at Ning Zhuo with a smile: "Little Friend Ning Zhuo, your attainments in the Dao of Alchemy are not bad. With your bright Innate Talent, you saw its value at a single glance. Though your cultivation base is thin, you are proficient in multiple cultivation arts. I am quite curious - did you start cultivating and comprehending the Dao while still in your mother's womb?"

Ning Zhuo restrained the shock on his face and cupped his hands: "This junior is merely willing to put in grueling effort. In this world, cultivators with more talent and aptitude than this junior can be found everywhere, as numerous as the stars."

Tie Kuang laughed heartily and shook his head repeatedly: "Your words are far too modest."

Ning Zhuo's gaze fell back onto the Naming Fire Furnace, letting out a heartfelt sigh of admiration: "Lord Hall Master's strength is unfathomable, to actually be able to extract pill toxins from the Naming Fire Furnace! Such methods truly leave this junior in awe."

This was the crucial point.

Pill toxins were the most stubborn.

As cultivators ingested Medicinal Pills over a long period, pill toxins would accumulate in their bodies. The medicinal efficacy would decrease day by day, eventually harming their physical bodies, souls, meridians, and dantian. For many cultivators whose cultivation staggered in the later stages, much of the harm was due to the buildup of pill toxins.

Although there were quite a few methods to purge pill toxins, those that were truly effective were very few.

The Naming Fire Furnace had a long history, having refined countless spirit pills. After hundreds and thousands of years of accumulation, the pill toxins inside the furnace had long been deeply entangled with the furnace walls, fire veins, spirit runes, and the state of the artifact spirit. Trying to extract them with ordinary methods was akin to a fool's dream.

Yet Tie Kuang had just accomplished it - even if he only extracted a single wisp, it was enough to prove the rare exquisiteness of his methods!

Tie Kuang was very satisfied with Ning Zhuo's reaction.

He sat back down and, for the first time, picked up his teacup and took a leisurely sip.

"Extracting pill toxins has many benefits," Tie Kuang said unhurriedly.

"Firstly, the old toxins in the Naming Fire Furnace are heavy, and the artifact spirit is already in a state of shock. The pill toxins pressing down on it will make it increasingly weak. Removing the pill toxins is like lifting a heavy burden from the artifact spirit's shoulders."

"Secondly, during the repair process, the pill toxins will interfere with the fusion of precious materials and pollute the newly formed fire veins. By eliminating them first, the reforging of the furnace body will be cleaner and purer."

"Thirdly, once the old toxins are gone, the performance of the newly forged furnace will inevitably be stronger. In future alchemy, the pills' color will be clearer, their medicinal properties purer, and it can even lower the toxin content within each Medicinal Pill, elevating the pill's grade."

"Fourthly, and the most crucial point - right now is the last chance!"

"Only because the pill furnace is broken and its cracks have not closed can the pill toxins leak out, allowing me to draw them out. If you let someone else repair the furnace body first and seal the cracks, the pill toxins will be trapped inside forever. At that time, the Naming Fire Furnace will appear complete on the surface, but the severe illness of the old toxins will remain hidden inside, causing endless future troubles. It will be too late for regrets."

Ning Zhuo found it hard to suppress his temptation.

He knew this was Tie Kuang's negotiation tactic. But he was also clear that the chips the other party had put on the table were something he could not refuse!

This was not a method he could master himself, and the chances of finding an outsider who could achieve this level and replace Tie Kuang were extremely slim.

Ning Zhuo remained silent for a long time.

Finally, he cupped his hands and said: "Congratulations, Hall Master Tie."

Tie Kuang raised an eyebrow: "Where does this joy come from?"

Ning Zhuo said earnestly: "Congratulations to Hall Master on soon becoming a creditor of the Naming Stronghold. If we calculate based on the ranking of debt claims, Hall Master contributing both funds and effort to repair the Naming Fire Furnace this time carries extremely heavy weight. Even - the position of Chief Stronghold Master might not be out of the question for you to fight for."

Tie Kuang stared at him for two breaths, then suddenly threw his head back and laughed aloud. The laughter bounced back and forth in the room, making one's ears ring: "Good kid, turning around and trying to pull this old man onto your chariot right away!"

Ning Zhuo's expression was open and calm: "If Hall Master can help me deal with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, and if the Naming Stronghold can seize that treasured geomantic land of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, it will be equally immensely profitable for Hall Master."

Tie Kuang waved his large hand: "Spare me this routine!"

His smile faded, and his expression turned serious, speaking with earnest sincerity: "Ning Zhuo, the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is too conspicuous, surrounded by a pack of wolves. You should seriously consider the Roosting Flame Cloud Nest that this old man proposed earlier."

Ning Zhuo's gaze flickered slightly.

"The Roosting Flame Cloud Nest has several rare benefits." Tie Kuang reached out, dipped a finger into the leftover tea in his cup, and drew a rough map on the table. "That place is where fire clouds intersect, and within the cloud nest are natural fire vein pores, which are extremely suitable for the nourishment and warming of the Naming Fire Furnace after its repair. The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar leans towards Yang fire; its momentum is vast and fierce. It is suitable for establishing prestige and making a name, but it is easy to stimulate the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit negatively. The fire of the Roosting Flame Cloud Nest is softer and more continuous, with a stronger sense of ascension, which is much more beneficial to the artifact spirit."

He tapped the side of his drawing: "Scattered around the Roosting Flame Cloud Nest are plenty of fire materials, cloud copper, and light flame sand. They are easy to mine and can subsidize the future consumption of the Naming Stronghold for artifact refining, furnace repair, and furnace maintenance. Although the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar has a grand reputation and many contenders, the subsequent expenses will be a bottomless pit."

Tie Kuang's finger paused on the tabletop, lifting his eyes to look at Ning Zhuo: "The most crucial point - the Roosting Flame Cloud Nest is currently not in the hands of any major power. If you pivot to attacking there, you can avoid the current head-on conflict."

Ning Zhuo shook his head: "It cannot be avoided."

Tie Kuang frowned: "You really want to rescue that Red-Robed Guest's demonic soul? Just for the sake of reputation, you're willing to go this far?"

Ning Zhuo looked Tie Kuang in the eye, his gaze as clear as water, his expression holding a calmness and resoluteness unsuited for his age: "The Naming Stronghold was established by my own hands. Though I sit at the lowest seat, I must take responsibility for it. I, Ning Zhuo, walk the Righteous Path; this is my Dao Path."

"Dao Path?" Tie Kuang gave him a deep look. In that gaze, there was neither approval nor opposition, only a silent contemplation.

A moment later, he said in a deep voice: "Then you should pivot to the Roosting Flame Cloud Nest even more."

Ning Zhuo did not reply.

Tie Kuang continued: "The root cause of the immense obstruction the Naming Stronghold faces right now is that you are too much in the limelight! All the major and minor powers on Flowing Cloud Peak are worried that after you settle in, you will continue to expand and encroach on their territories. If you occupy the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, you will inevitably extend outward afterward, and the territories of those old powers might all be affected."

He tapped his finger on the water stain on the table, and the moisture spread out in all directions, like the map of powers being slowly pushed apart by an invisible hand: "But if the Naming Stronghold successfully charges the peak and establishes a foothold first, the situation will be entirely different."

"Because with the established fact right before their eyes, and with sect rules forbidding internal strife, the mentality of the other powers will immediately become subtle. Those further away will choose to observe conservatively, while those closer will be schemed against, roped in, or coerced by others. A new pattern of gamesmanship will naturally form, without you needing to painstakingly push for it."

Tie Kuang looked at Ning Zhuo: "The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar is too heavily involved, because the Earth Expansion Alliance and White Cloud Township are both staring fixedly at it. If you charge there, it's equivalent to directly threatening the core interests of two major powers. Shifting to the Roosting Flame Cloud Nest will reduce the frontal pressure significantly. This old man can also mobilize connections to secretly pave the way and build bridges for you, allowing you to settle in smoothly."

Ning Zhuo sighed softly: "What Hall Master says, this junior needs to ponder carefully."

Tie Kuang shook his head: "You can consider it. But as for the matter of repairing the furnace, set a date first."

Tie Kuang gave the youth absolutely no room for delay.

This Hall Master of the Artifact Refining Hall appeared rugged and rough on the outside, but in reality, every step he took was extremely precise. Throughout this entire conversation, Ning Zhuo had been pushed into a passive position.

Ning Zhuo only pondered for a moment before nodding: "Alright. We'll set the date first. As for the materials, based on our previous exchange, they will all be borne by the Artifact Refining Hall!"

Tie Kuang grinned: "Straightforward!"

He stood up, his broad, powerful palm slapping heavily onto Ning Zhuo's shoulder - the force was as heavy as a furnace hammer falling lightly, jarring Ning Zhuo's shoulder bone until it felt slightly numb.

"Ning Zhuo, you do things decisively. This old man likes talking business with decisive people."

Ning Zhuo smiled bitterly: "Hall Master's methods are brilliant, this junior concedes."

Tie Kuang laughed heartily. Taking action personally, he had achieved his main goal in one fell swoop, and was in an excellent mood.

Before leaving, he looked back at Ning Zhuo once more: "Think carefully about the Roosting Flame Cloud Nest. If you listen to this old man, the Naming Stronghold can avoid many detours."

Ning Zhuo personally saw him out of the cave manor.

After turning back, Ning Zhuo issued a new order to Chen San: "Release the news."

Chen San immediately cupped his hands: "Please give your orders, Young Master."

Ning Zhuo's voice was not loud, but as clear as a carving: "Just say that in the near future, I will choose a day to make an oath beneath the Truth Bell - to rescue the Red-Robed Guest's demonic soul, and officially establish the martial sparring appointment with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect."

Chen San's heart pounded wildly.

Making an oath under the Truth Bell! Once this news was released, Ning Zhuo would absolutely have no way of retreat.

He looked up at Ning Zhuo, only to see the youth's face calm and his gaze clear and steady.

Chen San's Adam's apple bobbed as he swallowed the words of dissuasion he had instinctively wanted to speak, thinking to himself: "The Young Master's strategy is above mine; he must have a plan."

Chen San hurried back to the Moon Gazing Tower.

As soon as the news was out, it immediately kicked up a wave of clamor within the restaurant, like boiling water churning.

In less than half an hour, a mysterious cultivator quietly slipped into the fifth-floor private room - the other party's aura was tyrannical and thick, their face obscured by a cloak, and their footsteps soundless. The cultivators guarding the door had not even noticed his arrival before he was already standing quietly in front of Chen San.

The hairs on Chen San's back stood on end, but he still forcefully steadied his mind and said in a deep voice: "What business does Your Excellency have?"

The mysterious cultivator raised a hand and placed a talisman on the table.

The talisman was entirely dark gold, but the talisman paper seemed to be made of some kind of dried animal skin. The patterns twisted like coiled serpents, faintly forming the shape of a sharp awl, with a blood-red point of light condensing like a dewdrop at the tip.

"This is the Life-Rending Demonic Awl Talisman." The mysterious cultivator's voice was as hoarse as a broken gong. "Used skillfully, it can catch them off guard and severely injure any one of the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect."

Chen San's breathing hitched.

The mysterious cultivator said no more. Leaving the talisman behind, he retreated into the shadows like smoke.

Chen San reached out, wanting to stop him, but all that remained before his eyes was a wisp of faint black smoke, dissipating into the dark spots unreachable by the lamplight.

This mysterious cultivator had surprisingly not demanded any compensation or benefits!

Following that, an endless stream of powerful representatives came visiting once again.

Some demanded that Chen San clarify the specific direction of Ning Zhuo's funding needs. Some offered to lend out peak Golden Core battle puppets, but required the Naming Stronghold to provide a quota of Medicinal Pills in the future. Some were willing to contribute secret medicines that countered soul power sharing, only asking that Ning Zhuo sign a contract to keep the source a secret...

Chen San was so busy that sweat beaded on his forehead, and he didn't even have the time to sip his tea.

Good news was continuously relayed back to the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor.

After hearing the reports, Ning Zhuo only gave a slight nod.

He wasn't surprised in the slightest.

He knew that releasing this information had already displayed his sincerity to the maximum extent. After all, in the current situation, countless pairs of eyes were staring at his every move. If he dared to back down even half a step before the Truth Bell, the reputation he had painstakingly built up would collapse like a sandcastle, and his Righteous Path image would also suffer a fatal blow.

"Next, it depends on what kind of bargaining chips they can offer." Ning Zhuo looked at the Life-Rending Demonic Awl Talisman on the table, the anticipation in his eyes flickering like sparks.

This destined tribulation was baring its fangs at him. But at the same time, opportunities were quietly growing in the gaps between those fangs.
