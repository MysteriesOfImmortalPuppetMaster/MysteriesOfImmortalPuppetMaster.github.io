Chapter 987: The Youth Sheds Tears!

Chu Xuangui's brush came to an abrupt halt as the final character was set upon the paper.

The final line of the poem read: "To establish oneself, first establish trust; to walk the Dao, never walk alone!"

The poetic verses flew off the plain paper, transforming into glowing white characters. Like ten stars, they hovered around Ning Zhuo, each one constantly rotating.

Dong Nishang's guqin melody took a sudden turn, shifting from clear and resonant to deep and heavy, like tumbling boulders, aiding in the suppression.

Duanmu Zhang recited in a ringing voice: "A gentleman must possess courage, but he must also possess strategy. With responsibilities upon his shoulders, he must cherish his own life. To abandon the trust of the masses and act alone is to slight the people. To betray the expectations of everyone and seek danger is heartless. With creditors before you, allies beside you, and seniors protecting you, Little Friend Ning Zhuo, how can you obstinately proceed alone?"

As the ten white characters orbited him, the seven strings of the other Confucian cultivator's guqin vibrated, sending out rays of white light like fine silk threads that entangled Ning Zhuo's Divine Soul and aura. Yet another unfurled a bamboo slip.

One after another, Confucian techniques took effect!

Literary Qi acted as ropes, the guqin melody as locks, and the poetic characters as seals.

Ning Zhuo's figure violently sank, his feet feeling as though they were being dragged down by a weight of a thousand jun.

He circulated his spiritual energy. The dense mechanical patterns on his Grand Thousand Mechanism Harmonic Garment expanded like living creatures, sending ripples of dark cyan sound waves in all directions.

But in the next moment, the Hundred-Forged Thousand-Weight Hammer struck the empty air.

Spiritual light flared brilliantly, and its forging patterns vibrated in unison. In an instant, Ning Zhuo's Grand Thousand Mechanism Harmonic Garment was firmly suppressed!

Ning Zhuo struggled, crying out, "Everyone! What is it you're trying to do?"

A commotion broke out among the surrounding Cultivators; none of them had anticipated this sudden turn of events.

Tie Kuang took a massive stride to the edge of the domain and stood firm!

With his hands clasped behind his back, his expression was calm and thoroughly self-justified. "Little Friend Ning Zhuo, do not act rashly."

He turned slightly, scanning the entire plaza.

As the dignified Master of the Artifact Refining Hall, his imposing aura immediately intimidated everyone present.

Tie Kuang's gruff voice, resembling the heavy strikes of an iron hammer, rang clearly in Ning Zhuo's ears, as well as those of the assembled Cultivators. "Ning Zhuo, this is your serendipity."

"We have joined forces to set up a Rising Cloud Minor Trial for you. Since you wish to challenge the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, you must first pass this minor trial."

Ning Zhuo's face grew taut. "A minor trial? Senior, I have no desire to participate."

Tie Kuang's expression turned stern. "Pass this test, and I, Tie Kuang, will support your decision to fight. Otherwise, you will obediently follow me and prioritize repairing the Naming Fire Furnace!"

At this point, a long-suppressed fiery temper finally flared up uncontrollably. "Although the Naming Fire Furnace is already in your hands, there has been a glaring lack of any real effort to repair it. Little Friend Ning Zhuo, you might be able to tolerate it, but watching this delay is absolute torture for me!"

"The Naming Fire Furnace originated from my Myriad Manifestations Sect; it embodies the blood, sweat, and tears of countless Artifact Refiners and pill Cultivators."

"As the Hall Master of the Artifact Refining Hall, I am willing to help you repair this furnace and rebuild its foundation. This is out of both personal desire and a sense of righteous duty!"

"If you were to die, wouldn't it completely ruin this grand undertaking?"

Ning Zhuo clenched his jaw slightly, his chest heaving like a bellows.

He suddenly turned his head to look at Qing Huangzi, a hint of a tremor in his voice. "Senior Qing Huangzi, why did it have to come to this?!"

Qing Huangzi took two steps forward, his withered fingers lightly brushing his cuff, his face lined with bitter wrinkles. "You brat, you are the inheritor of my [Empty Valley Syllable Azure Mechanism Cylinder]. I have waited my entire life, and at the very end, I finally found a truly suitable and outstanding successor in you."

He looked up at Ning Zhuo, his eyes - as clear as spring water - concealing a deep exhaustion and worry. "You are far too young, and your spirit is too fierce. You say you want to shoulder the responsibility; I believe you. You say you want to save the Red-Robed Guest; I believe that too. But watching you willingly walk straight into the blades of the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect... I simply cannot stand idly by and applaud you."

Ning Zhuo's lips moved slightly, but no words came out.

Qing Huangzi continued, "Resorting to this foolish plan shames me, and lacking the strength to do it alone, I had to seek helpers. I thought I would have to waste a lot of breath convincing them, but to my surprise, everyone agreed. The Confucian cultivators agreed, the Zhu Clan agreed, and even Lord Tie Kuang agreed. Ning Zhuo, do you truly think you are the only one bearing responsibility today?"

Ning Zhuo's face turned pale.

Zhu Fenxiang stood beside the divine statue, beads of sweat slowly trailing down the side of her face. "Ning Zhuo, have you forgotten? My Zhu Clan also has a share in this. I am a creditor as well."

She raised an eyebrow slightly, her gaze flashing sharply. "If you die, who am I supposed to settle accounts with? The Red-Robed Guest's demonic soul? That group of Nascent Soul cultivators in the Naming Stronghold? Or should I go to the martial arena to collect your mangled corpse?"

Zhu Fenxiang spoke without the slightest courtesy; her words were like knives.

Her mother, Zhu Guizhi, supplemented from the side, speaking softly, "Little Friend Ning Zhuo, Fenxiang may speak bluntly, but she speaks the truth. You are now tied to far too many people. The weight of your life is no longer yours alone to measure."

From the group of Confucian cultivators, Duanmu Zhang finally spoke. His features were sharp and square, his tone as steady as a mountain. "Little Friend Ning Zhuo, we acknowledge the resolve behind your vow today. But we must ask you: what does it truly mean to shoulder responsibility?"

Ning Zhuo looked up at him.

Duanmu Zhang's gaze was stern and upright. "You see the soul of the Red-Robed Guest as the most urgent crisis, yet you make light of the trust and expectations placed upon you by your seniors, Fellow Sect Members, and creditors. That is the first point."

Chu Xuangui wore a cold expression, his jade brush still held firmly in his hand. "You claim to bear the responsibility for the Naming Stronghold, yet you throw yourself into a situation with a nine-in-ten chance of death. If you perish, who will settle your debts? Who will repair the furnace? Who will uphold the covenants? And who will soothe the hearts of the people? The Sage said, 'A scholar must be resolute and broad-minded, for his burden is heavy and his journey is long.' How can one with such a heavy burden simply cast it aside, leaving the living to clean up the mess? That is the second point."

Dong Nishang stroked the strings of her guqin, her voice carrying slowly alongside the melody. "You accepted Qing Huangzi's inheritance, received Cao Gui's heavy treasures, and benefited from the financial aid of numerous fellow Daoists. These seniors supported you with their wealth, legacies, and reputations in the hope that you would walk further and stand firmer. The Sage said, 'A gentleman harbors lifelong concerns.' By treating your own life so lightly, you are making light of everyone's sacrifices, and abandoning the concerns you ought to carry. That is the third point."

Situ Gu unrolled his bamboo slip, a deep, simmering anger in his eyes. "The truly brave advance knowing the dangers, but they also know when to bide their time. Our Confucian classics state: 'The wise are free from perplexities; the virtuous from anxiety; and the bold from fear.' True courage is not a momentary surge of hot blood. Courage is recognizing the hardship ahead and having the restraint to sheath your blade while you solidify your foundation. You are only at the Mid Foundation Establishment Stage, facing formidable foes with insufficient odds of victory, yet you hastily seek a binding duel. Your youthful spirit is commendable, but your recklessness is lamentable. That is the fourth point."

Lastly came Li Guanyu. "Young Master Ning, you speak incessantly of the Righteous Path. Does the Righteous Path merely mean marching to one's death with head held high? The Righteous Path also requires protecting those behind you; it requires planning and endurance. The Analects state: 'To see what is right and not do it is want of courage.' But after recognizing what is right, you must possess the ability to actually accomplish it. If you simply die, bystanders will praise your martyrdom for three days, but the Naming Stronghold will crumble, and many fellow Daoists will suffer for three years, or even thirty years. That is the fifth and final point!"

The five reprimands fell, each sentence heavier and more profound than the last.

Accompanying them, the Literary Qi of their Confucian techniques pressed down. Every word acted as a measuring rod, striking directly at Ning Zhuo's heart.

Ning Zhuo's face flushed a fiery red, as if he were being roasted over a flame. He wanted to retort, but his throat felt as though it had been firmly blocked by some invisible force.

"I... I..." The youth had completely lost his previous composure. He looked panic-stricken and entirely at a loss.

Duanmu Zhang shouted furiously, "Ning Zhuo, will you still not realize your errors and turn back?"

Ning Zhuo staggered a step back and shook his head, his voice low and hoarse. "No... no... I never intended to make light of everyone. I just..."

"Whether you intended it or not, and whether your actions would cause such an outcome, are two completely different matters," Chu Xuangui interjected coldly.

Ning Zhuo clutched his chest, his face draining of color.

Tie Kuang spoke in a deep voice. "Brat, we trapped you today not to destroy your Dao Path. If you are truly capable, break out of this minor trial. If you can't, then you will follow this old man and fix the furnace first. Once the Naming Fire Furnace is fully repaired, the foundation of your Naming Stronghold will be vastly strengthened. When that time comes, you can talk about saving souls or issuing challenges, and you will have far more confidence to do so."

Ning Zhuo swayed unsteadily; it wouldn't have been surprising if he had collapsed onto the ground in the very next second.

Countless gazes were fixed upon him - some filled with astonishment, some with doubt, some gloating in his misfortune, and others simply enjoying the spectacle...

"I... I..." he stammered, clenching his hands into tight fists as his expression gradually twisted into a fierce scowl.

Chu Xuangui bellowed once more, "Brat, your eyes are fixed solely on the dead! With such a narrow-minded capacity, what great deeds could you possibly achieve in the future? Open your eyes and take a good look at Qing Huangzi!"

Bearing a look of absolute bewilderment, Ning Zhuo shifted his gaze toward Qing Huangzi.

Qing Huangzi appeared decrepit and frail, like a withered bamboo stalk swaying precariously in the mountain wind. "Today, beneath the Truth Bell, this old man bares his heart to you. My days are numbered, and my sole wish is for my inheritance to be passed down smoothly. Little Friend Ning Zhuo, you are the singular inheritor of our Green Bamboo Peak. If you die, how will I ever face the successive generations of Patriarchs when I go down to the Nine Springs?"

"Resorting to such a foolish scheme today was the work of my own selfish desires. I only beg that you spare a little thought for Green Bamboo Peak. Will you?"

Clang.

The Truth Bell tolled, perfectly verifying the absolute sincerity of Qing Huangzi's words!

Ning Zhuo felt as though he had been struck by lightning. His body convulsed violently.

His eyes grew red as he stared at Qing Huangzi. Suddenly, two streaks of hot tears rolled down from his eyes. "S - Senior!"

"I... I..."

Ning Zhuo's lips trembled, and he finally managed to force the words out with great difficulty. "I was wrong."

"I was wrong..."

"I was so wrong."

Ning Zhuo apologized three times in a row.

Under the watchful eyes of the crowd, the young man choked back a sob, wiped away his tears, and gave a deep, respectful bow to everyone present.

"Seniors, Fellow Sect Members, it was I, Ning Zhuo, who was narrow-minded." His voice trembled, revealing the intense surge of his emotions.

"My rash and reckless advance was the courage of a fool."

"I... I spoke of bearing responsibility, yet I only thought of carrying the burden of Senior Red-Robed Guest's tribulation, entirely forgetting the seniors and friends who have truly supported and placed their hopes in me."

"Senior Qing Huangzi, I am deeply ashamed to face you!"

As he spoke, the tears continued to flow. For a moment, Ning Zhuo covered his face and wept quietly.

Complete silence blanketed the Blackstone Plaza.

The Cultivators observing the ceremony had never anticipated such an incredible twist. Most wore peculiar expressions, their eyes gleaming brightly as they watched this grand spectacle unfold.

Among the ranks of Flowing Cloud Peak, quite a few Cultivators wore strange expressions.

Hidden within the crowd, Tu Yuanzi exhaled a long breath, his eyes as red as if they had been smoked over a fire. "This is good... this is good."

Situ Xing, Shen Xi, and Lin Jinglong looked at each other in utter dismay.

Chunyangzi wore a look of complete bewilderment.

Duanmu Zhang stroked his beard. "To realize one's errors and correct them is the greatest of virtues."

Tie Kuang, on the other hand, threw his head back and laughed heartily. "Good! Being able to admit your mistakes means our efforts weren't in vain. Ning Zhuo, next, you will properly participate in the Rising Cloud Minor Trial within this domain. This domain completely isolates the inside from the outside, meaning you can only rely on yourself."

"If you can break out within seven days, then I will concede!"

With that said, he pointed a finger at the Hundred-Forged Thousand-Weight Hammer hovering in midair.

This Lifebound Spiritual Treasure shot downward, turning ethereal in midair. Right before everyone's eyes, it fused seamlessly with the domain, acting as its core suppressing artifact!

Tie Kuang then spread his five fingers and made a grasping motion at the empty air.

In the next moment, the domain shrank into a dark red orb of light the size of a baby's fist. Incredibly dense and solid, it flew directly into his palm, where he firmly grasped it.

Finally, Tie Kuang cast a cold glance over the crowd before rocketing into the sky, transforming into a streak of light as he departed.
