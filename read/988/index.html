Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 988: Return My Yang Fish

Tie Kuang left first, taking Ning Zhuo with him. No one dared to stop a person of his status.

Following that, the Confucian cultivator group, Qing Huangzi, the Zhu family mother and daughter, and others also left the scene one after another.

The expressions of the cultivators from Flowing Cloud Peak were as gloomy as an impending mountain storm.

"With Ning Zhuo being taken away just like that, the date for his arranged battle with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect can't be set! Didn't we come here for nothing?"

"In the short term, this battle between Ning Zhuo and the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect won't happen!"

"So what if it doesn't happen? Seven days from now, if he breaks out, he will still have to fight. If he fails to break out, he will be detained by Tie Kuang to repair the Naming Fire Furnace. Either way, Nanming Stronghold's momentum to contest for the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar has been stalled."

"Is that really true?"

"..."

The crowd fell silent. Some sank into deep thought, their brows furrowing tighter and tighter.

Ning Zhuo wasn't the only person in the Nanming Stronghold!

Although Ning Zhuo was a crucial core figure, there were plenty of people with higher cultivation bases than him.

Ning Zhuo had left, but the others were all still here!

In fact, because of this incident, Nanming Stronghold no longer had Ning Zhuo as an obvious weakness and flaw!

Realizing this, the faces of these Flowing Cloud Peak cultivators grew even fouler. Having lost all interest in staying, they turned around and left.

The envoy from Danxia Peak muttered, "I didn't expect Tie Kuang to act so fast. He wants to get his hands on the Naming Fire Furnace too! I must return immediately to report this to the Peak Lord and ask for his decision. If Ning Zhuo follows Tie Kuang to repair the furnace seven days from now, we have to find a way to intervene. The Naming Fire Furnace cannot fall entirely into the hands of the Artifact Forging Hall!"

The envoy from Heavy Array Peak also quietly slipped away: "What a lively show!"

The envoy from Demon Subjugation Hall sneered inwardly: "Without the support of our hall, Ning Zhuo was actually set up by his own surrounding allies. How laughable."

Chunyangzi flicked his sleeves and left.

Situ Xing, Shen Xi, and Lin Jinglong stood together, staring at each other in silence for a good while.

Situ Xing suddenly said, "Let's go. I want to drink."

Lin Jinglong immediately agreed, "Sounds good. We can drink and chat."

Shen Xi revealed a bitter smile. "Fellow Daoist Ning Zhuo truly exceeds all expectations."

"Let's go, let's go." Situ Xing turned first, leading the way.

The discussions in the No Retreat Square finally exploded.

"Ning Zhuo was taken away!"

"What is this? Did his vow at the Truth Bell get interrupted?"

"Interrupted or not, his intentions were verified by the bell's toll. His desire to save the Red-Robed Guest was genuine, and his admission of fault was also genuine."

"He won't be fighting the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect anytime soon. Tie Kuang said seven days, so there will be no martial duel for at least seven days."

"And after seven days? If he breaks out, wouldn't his momentum be even greater?"

"If he fails to break out, he'll be taken by the Artifact Forging Hall Master to repair the Naming Fire Furnace. In that case, Nanming Stronghold's foundation will become even more stable."

"Good heavens, how does he win either way?"

"He lost face. He was scolded to tears in public and then shoved into the minor trial."

"What do you know? With the Truth Bell as a witness, this is called having a pure, innocent heart, or the prodigal son turning over a new leaf."

Opinions varied greatly, like a mountain wind sweeping up dust, growing larger and larger.

Soon, news of this matter spread from Zhengshi Peak to the various peaks of the Myriad Manifestations Sect.

Some said that Ning Zhuo was brave enough to recognize his own shame, and by admitting his missteps before the Truth Bell, he was destined for greatness.

Some said that while Ning Zhuo seemed clever, he was ultimately young. He was publicly manipulated by a group of older seniors and couldn't even successfully complete his vow.

Some said that Qing Huangzi was shielding his own, Tie Kuang wanted to seize the furnace, the Zhu family was collecting debts, and the Confucian cultivators were selling their reputation. Each party harbored their own motives in this bustling spectacle. Compared to these people, Ning Zhuo was still far too young!

Danxia Peak was perennially shrouded in pill aura. From the mountainside up, the clouds and mist glowed like fire. The rosy light was absorbed and exhaled by countless alchemy rooms, forming a faint crimson-gold halo at the peak.

Wang Yu sat before an alchemy furnace.

He wore a cloud robe, the rosy pill-light on it dimming and brightening with his breathing. A horsetail whisk rested in the crook of his arm, while a Purple Yang Alchemy Furnace slowly exhaled and inhaled rosy smoke in front of him. A fine wisp of medicinal fragrance drifted from the gaps in the furnace lid. Upon entering the nose, the scent was first bitter, then sweet, finally leaving a faint, refreshing coolness at the base of the tongue.

A Danxia Peak deacon stood respectfully below, recounting the events that had occurred on Zhengshi Peak in a low voice.

After listening, Wang Yu's face remained calm.

The furnace fire reflected on his face, illuminating his features so that half was bright and half was cast in deep shadow.

After a long while, he finally spoke, "Looking at it this way, the battle between Ning Zhuo and the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect has been delayed indefinitely."

The deacon said, "Ning Zhuo admitted his fault in public and lost his momentum. Furthermore, he originally intended to establish the martial duel agreement beneath the Truth Bell but failed to fulfill his promise in the end. Right now, his reputation has plummeted, and he is at the lowest point of his life. Peak Lord, you have long intended to recruit him into our peak. This moment might be a golden opportunity."

Wang Yu raised his eyes and gave him a faint glance.

The deacon immediately lowered his head, his heart tightening.

Wang Yu shook his head and sighed. "He has already stated his intentions beneath the Truth Bell, and they were verified by its chime. Rescuing the Red-Robed Guest's demonic soul is genuine, and his willingness to take responsibility for the Nanming Stronghold is also genuine. Anyone with a discerning eye knows that he will definitely challenge the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect; only the timing has become uncertain."

He gently flicked his horsetail whisk. The tail swept through the air, stirring up a wisp of pill fragrance.

"Therefore, Ning Zhuo did not go back on his word. He was merely stopped by the crowd, temporarily halting his steps."

The deacon froze, then revealed a thoughtful expression.

Wang Yu continued, "As for his admission of fault, that matter is even more brilliant. Will the crowd laugh at him for crying? Those who mock him are the foolish ones. Those with true foresight will only value Ning Zhuo even more and approve of his decision."

"Qing Huangzi is the key figure. His days are numbered, and he only has Ning Zhuo as a suitable successor. He spoke with absolute sincerity, and the Truth Bell even chimed once to testify for him. The world most easily empathizes with such a figure."

"By bowing his head in public, Ning Zhuo was bowing to Qing Huangzi, to the seniors who funded him, and to the many creditors of Nanming Stronghold. Bowing one's head in such a manner is not shameful."

The deacon's brow slowly relaxed.

The corners of Wang Yu's lips curled into a faint, cryptic smile. "On the contrary, this incident served as a reminder to everyone. Although Ning Zhuo is a newcomer and has just arrived, standing behind him are already Qing Huangzi, the Confucian cultivators, the Zhu family, Tie Kuang, and Chunyangzi. Numerous factions are supporting him, whether openly or in secret. Who would still dare to treat him as a lone wolf?"

Dawn broke in the deacon's eyes.

He remembered his own initial feelings of disdain when he first heard that Ning Zhuo was going to challenge the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect.

"This subordinate understands," the deacon said softly. "Previously, many people felt he was reckless and impulsive, unaware of his own limits. Even though they admired his sense of responsibility, they still looked down on his arrogance and rashness. Now that he has admitted his mistakes, shown that he can correct them, and proven he knows when to yield and when to stand tall, it actually makes people value him more."

Wang Yu nodded slightly. "Exactly."

A hint of approval appeared in his eyes. "This child handles matters smoothly, possessing excellent control over when to advance and when to retreat. In the path of alchemy, being stiff and rigid is the most dreadful flaw. One degree too hot, and the pill burns; one degree too cold, and it remains raw. You cannot rush it, nor can you be timid. If he were to come to my Danxia Peak to learn alchemy, he would definitely become an expert."

The deacon's heart skipped a beat upon hearing this.

It was rare for Wang Yu to evaluate a Foundation Establishment cultivator so highly.

He thought for a moment and probed cautiously, "Peak Lord, that Nascent Soul-level Life-Hanging Survival Pill... should we take it back?"

Wang Yu's expression slightly stiffened.

Inside the hall, the furnace fire gave a soft flicker.

His expression became somewhat peculiar.

This Life-Hanging Survival Pill had originally been delivered to Ning Zhuo through Cao Gui's hands. Danxia Peak had even secretly leaked rumors to let outsiders vaguely know that this item was connected to Wang Yu. After all, if a favor was to hold weight, the people who needed to know had to be aware of the origins.

But now, Ning Zhuo had pulled a feint. The battle agreement wasn't finalized on the spot, and he had even been taken away by Tie Kuang to participate in the minor trial.

Could the Life-Hanging Pill still be taken back?

It couldn't.

Wang Yu knew this all too well.

With a Nascent Soul-level medicinal pill like this, once given away, turning around to demand it back would completely ruin the Danxia Peak Lord's face! What's more, the rumors had already been spread. If he took it back today, tomorrow the entire Myriad Manifestations Sect would know that Wang Yu, having given a pill halfway, saw the situation change and reached his hand out to snatch it back.

That wasn't being cautious.

That was being petty.

Wang Yu's expression returned to calm. "There's no need."

A flash of heartache flickered in the deacon's eyes. "We're really just leaving it in his hands?"

"Let him keep it," Wang Yu sighed faintly, then waved for the deacon to withdraw.

Left alone, Wang Yu's gaze returned to the Purple Yang Alchemy Furnace.

The furnace fire reflected in his eyes like two tiny specks of light hidden deep within a rosy dawn.

He calculated in his heart: "In the upcoming situation concerning the Nanming Stronghold, Ning Zhuo is out of the game, at least temporarily. For Nanming Stronghold, he stalled the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect and also diverted much of Flowing Cloud Peak's attention. However, the five-win martial contest won't just be abandoned simply because Ning Zhuo is trapped."

"If I were Chunyangzi, I would push the five-win martial contest forward at full speed right now!"

"The fifth match determines the ownership of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. If Nanming Stronghold wins, it will be most beneficial for me. But if it falls into the hands of the Territory Expansion Alliance, the rumors I've prepared will need to be released immediately."

Wang Yu knew exactly how far Heavy Array Peak's reach extended. External affairs, internal administration, formation laying - layer upon layer, like an invisible formation diagram, it had long since covered far too much of the Myriad Manifestations Sect.

Nanming Stronghold, this newly added chess piece, could not be easily swallowed by Heavy Array Peak.

"I absolutely cannot just sit back and watch Heavy Array Peak's grand scheme succeed!"

When Chunyangzi stepped into the Artifact Forging Hall, Yang Qi surged out continuously.

He did not conceal his aura.

Golden-white Pure Yang spiritual energy seeped out from the edges of his robes, radiating the astonishing presence of a Nascent Soul-level expert.

"Lord Chunyangzi!" The cultivator on duty hurriedly stepped forward to bow.

"I have specifically come today to see your Hall Master." Chunyangzi's face was solemn, his lips pressed into a tight line, making his bearing exceptionally intimidating.

The on-duty cultivator hastily replied, "My Hall Master had already instructed us that if you came, you could enter directly."

Under the guidance of the on-duty cultivator, Chunyangzi made his way deep into the Artifact Forging Hall.

The stone door ahead opened, and Tie Kuang's booming laughter rang out before he was even seen. "You came so fast? I thought you would at least endure until tomorrow."

"Tie Kuang." Chunyangzi stood at the doorway. His gaze was deep, and he spoke without politeness, "You took Ning Zhuo away, but my Nanming Stronghold still intends to vie for the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. Furthermore, return my Yang Fish!"

Tie Kuang nodded and beckoned with a raised hand.

A speck of golden light flew out from inside the stone chamber, transforming into a three-inch goldfish in mid-air. The Yang Fish swished its tail, its golden scales glittering like fragments of sunlight and its ink-jade eyes calm and deep. As it swam in front of Chunyangzi, it clearly acted somewhat affectionately.

Chunyangzi extended his hand.

The Yang Fish swam a circle around his knuckles before landing in his palm. A golden glow seeped through his fingers, illuminating the lines on his palm brightly.

The taut string in his heart finally relaxed a little.

The Yang Fish was the defining spiritual treasure of the Pure Yang Palace. Even though Pure Yang Palace had now merged into the Myriad Manifestations Sect, he still valued this spiritual treasure more deeply than his own life. Since Ning Zhuo was going to fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, he had gritted his teeth and lent out the Yang Fish. Now that Ning Zhuo had been dragged into the Rising Cloud Minor Trial by Tie Kuang and couldn't fight for seven days, the Yang Fish naturally had to return to his hands.

The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar was Chunyangzi's most pressing hurdle on his Dao Path right now.

He stored the Yang Fish away and shifted his gaze back to Tie Kuang. "What about the Pure Yang Ancestor Pill?"

Tie Kuang replied, "It is still in Ning Zhuo's hands. His resolve for the battle against the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect remains as firm as ever and has never changed. So, he asked me to pass a message, requesting you to allow him to continue drawing upon the pill's power to absorb the Yang Dao insights in preparation for the future."

Chunyangzi frowned tightly.

The Pure Yang Ancestor Pill!

That pill had been passed down through generations of Pure Yang Palace masters. They would borrow it in their youth to forge their path and find a way to replenish it before stepping down. This pill did not merely contain medicinal power; it also carried the Yang Dao insights of successive sect masters, the sect's destiny, and the remaining blessings of their ancestors.

Giving a legacy pill for an outsider to use fundamentally violated the palace rules.

He had been willing to make an exception earlier because Ning Zhuo was about to face the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect immediately. If he won, Nanming Stronghold's momentum in vying for the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar would skyrocket. Chunyangzi could then use that treasured land to attempt breaking through to Soul Formation. If he reached the Soul Formation stage, even if Pure Yang Palace changed its name to Pure Yang Peak, it would truly be able to establish a firm foothold within the Myriad Manifestations Sect, perhaps even gaining semi-independence.

He had calculated this exchange very clearly.

But now, Ning Zhuo wouldn't be able to fight in the short term.

The Yang Fish had been retrieved, but the Pure Yang Ancestor Pill remained in Ning Zhuo's hands.

Should I leave it with Ning Zhuo for now? Chunyangzi fell into deep thought.

Chunyangzi didn't doubt Ning Zhuo's resolve to fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect in the slightest.

Because he knew very well: If Ning Zhuo did not rescue the Red-Robed Guest and fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect in the future, his reputation within the Righteous Path would instantly collapse. Given Ning Zhuo's intelligence, he would not destroy his own foundation.

That consequence would be far too severe!

Not to mention, Chunyangzi had personally witnessed the scene where Ning Zhuo swore his vow and received the Truth Bell's validation.

"Leaving the Pure Yang Ancestor Pill in Ning Zhuo's hands will indeed help him defeat the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect in the future."

"Whereas keeping it in my hands serves absolutely no purpose!"

Chunyangzi couldn't help but think this way.

For him, the biggest hurdle in taking out the Pure Yang Ancestor Pill was the first time - after all, giving it to an outsider went against the sect's ancestral teachings!

But since he had already broken that rule once, that greatest hurdle was no longer there.

Ning Zhuo's exceptional aptitude for cultivating Pure Yang cultivation arts also surfaced in his mind at this moment, swaying his decision.

"Oh, that's right. Ning Zhuo had me bring you this. It might be of some help in dealing with the Sky-Hanging Thread Hook," Tie Kuang said as he brought out a humanoid combat puppet and handed it to Chunyangzi.

It was a spirit bamboo puppet.

The puppet was about the height of a normal person, its entire body crafted from azure spirit bamboo and an intricate mechanical skeleton. There were silver-white mechanical clasps at the bamboo joints, and a mechanical seal of the Azure Mechanism Tube lineage with the syllable of the Empty Valley was carved on its chest. Its shoulders and back were broad and straight, and its arms were slender. Though it held no long blade in its hands, hidden folding blade mechanisms were concealed on both sides of its wrist bones. Even without being unsheathed, an intense sharpness was contained within, waiting to be unleashed.

Empty-Minded Folding Blade Guard.

A Golden Core-level spirit bamboo puppet.

Tie Kuang said, "Ning Zhuo specifically lent this to you."

"Oh?" Chunyangzi raised a brow slightly and placed his hand on the bamboo puppet's shoulder.

The internal mechanisms of the puppet immediately whirred into motion, sounding like a string of bamboo chimes ringing in a valley breeze.
