Chapter 980: Generous Assistance

Ning Zhuo's gaze fell upon that rusty black long nail, then slowly shifted to the masked cultivator, his eyes somewhat complex.

"What is the price?" the youth asked.

The masked cultivator sensed the scrutiny and vigilance in Ning Zhuo's gaze. "This item was forged specifically for the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect. If you are willing to use it, I will give it to you. But you must guarantee that you will use it on the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect!"

"Therefore, we must sign a contract."

"Aside from this, my clan asks for nothing else."

Ning Zhuo remained silent for a moment. "Alright."

After signing the contract, the masked cultivator turned and left without the slightest intention of staying.

Tonight was destined to be restless.

Not long after the masked cultivator's departure, Cao Gui arrived.

When he entered, he looked nervous, with visible beads of sweat seeping from his forehead. His eyes darted around, as if terrified someone was tailing him.

Upon seeing Ning Zhuo, he presented a treasure box made of golden-thread nanmu. Its surface was affixed with three layers of sealing talismans in white, azure, and gold.

Cao Gui gently placed the treasure box on the desk and whispered, "Young Master Ning, please... accept this item."

Ning Zhuo gave him a glance. Cao Gui's Adam's apple bobbed as he swallowed hard. "This item was sent down to me by my Cao Clan, with instructions to specifically gift it to you, Young Master. The elders urged repeatedly, saying its value is extremely high and that it is of utmost importance to you!"

Ning Zhuo opened the treasure box.

Inside the box was a layer of snow-white velvet padding, and resting quietly in the center of the pad was a Medicinal Pill.

The Medicinal Pill was the size of a longan, its outer shell a translucent jade-white. Inside, a strand of golden thread swam slowly like a wandering dragon, sealed deep within the pill as if it could break out at any moment.

Nine fine lines surfaced on the Medicinal Pill. Each line alternated between light and dark like breathing, and with each rise and fall, it emitted a very faint yet highly penetrating medicinal fragrance.

Ning Zhuo merely took a light sniff and felt the depths of his Soul being firmly supported by a warm palm. His flesh and blood, meridians, spiritual energy, and divine sea all instantly generated the illusion of "being able to hold on for one more breath," as if even fate itself had been snagged by the corner of its robes by this medicinal fragrance.

"This pill is called the Single Breath Suspended Life Pill, of the Nascent Soul-level!" Cao Gui's voice lowered even further.

"After swallowing it, even if one's soul is shattered and their physical body crushed into mud, it can suspend your last breath and keep it from dissipating. As long as rescue is timely, there will still be room for further medical treatment."

Ning Zhuo stared at this Medicinal Pill; it was his first time encountering a Medicinal Pill of this tier. 'Single Breath Suspended Life' - its name was extremely plain, yet its effects were practically Heaven-defying and fate-altering.

Ning Zhuo knew clearly in his heart that this was absolutely not Cao Gui's own doing, nor could it be an olive branch from the Cao Clan.

Someone had used the Cao Clan and Cao Gui as a channel to deliver this Medicinal Pill to him!

Ning Zhuo closed the treasure box and said solemnly, "Thank the one who gifted the pill on my behalf."

Cao Gui smiled bitterly. "Young Master Ning, I may not necessarily be able to pass on those words."

Ning Zhuo looked at him.

Cao Gui raised his hand and wiped the sweat from his forehead. "But I will try my best."

Ning Zhuo didn't press further and carefully stored the treasure box away.

Shortly after Cao Gui left, another person paid a secret visit.

The visitor was a middle-aged cultivator, and Ning Zhuo recognized him. Back when alliances and strongholds were formed in the Heavenly Treasure Pavilion, this person had attended on behalf of Yun Youzi and had even fought for a portion of the Naming Fire Furnace's debt claims for Yun Youzi.

The middle-aged cultivator wore a long robe patterned with clouds, his features mild and gentle.

After entering the cave, he offered Ning Zhuo a deep bow.

"Young Master Ning, my good friend Yun Youzi has heard of your deeds. He was especially moved when he learned that you intended to swear an oath under the Truth Bell to rescue the remnant soul of the Red-Robed Guest. Thus, he took the initiative to lend out this beast."

He raised a hand and beckoned, and a small cloud beast squeezed out from his sleeve.

The cloud beast looked like a foal, its entire body composed of condensed white clouds. Its four hooves stepped through the midair silently, as if walking on flat ground. Its eyes were as clear as the sky just after a rain, and with every breath, wisps of cloud and mist gently puffed from its nose.

Ning Zhuo revealed an expression of surprise and shock. "This is actually...?!"

He had never expected that the middle-aged cultivator's network would deliver such a massive aid at this very moment.

This was assistance comparable to the Three Valiants' Life-Claiming Nail, capable of bringing a qualitative change to Ning Zhuo's battle against the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect!

A wave of warmth welled up in Ning Zhuo's heart. He stood up and returned a solemn bow. "Please convey my gratitude to Senior Yun Youzi. If there is an opportunity in the future, I, Ning Zhuo, will surely share a drink and converse joyfully with him."

The middle-aged cultivator smiled. "I will definitely pass on Young Master Ning's words. Please... take good care of yourself!"

As the night grew deeper, yet another unexpected visitor paid a quiet visit to Ning Zhuo.

It was Shen Hongyao.

Her long hair was tied high with a red string, revealing a smooth forehead and a slender neck. Her almond eyes shone with a piercing light, and her pace was extremely fast, the hem of her skirt fluttering behind her like butterfly wings.

She wore a fiery red dress embroidered with large red peony-like flowers on the hem, their petals layered and lifelike.

"Daoist Friend Ning Zhuo, are you truly going to fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect?"

Ning Zhuo replied, "If I am to rescue the Red-Robed Guest's Soul, this battle is likely unavoidable."

Shen Hongyao nodded and decisively took an old jade bottle out of her sleeve. The jade bottle was a dark red color, the sealing wax at its mouth had already turned black, and there was a distinct chip on its body, clearly indicating it had been stored for a long time.

"For you." Ning Zhuo took the jade bottle and slightly opened the stopper just a sliver.

In that instant, a dense Demonic Qi mixed with medicinal fragrance rushed out, struggling at the bottleneck like a trapped beast.

Shen Hongyao gritted her teeth, staring first at the medicine bottle, then at Ning Zhuo's face. "This is a Nascent Soul-level Demonic Pill. It's expired; much of its medicinal efficacy has dissipated, and the pill toxicity is heavy. But it is ultimately Nascent Soul-level. At a critical moment, it can save your life, or allow you to risk it all."

"I also obtained it by chance in a ruin many years ago."

"According to my investigation, this pill should be the Blood-Reversing Soul-Igniting Pill. After consuming it, your flesh and blood, Soul, and spiritual energy will all burn together, erupting with immense power for a short period of time. The price is huge, and the latent consequences are numerous."

"Whether you use it or when you use it, Daoist Friend, you must judge for yourself."

Ning Zhuo grasped the old jade bottle. Its body felt cold, like holding a piece of aged Cold Iron, yet the Medicinal Pill inside seemed to be slowly pulsating even through the jade wall, as if a beating heart was sealed within.

"Daoist Friend Shen, this pill is too precious," Ning Zhuo said.

Shen Hongyao waved her hand. "Spare me these polite words. Since I brought it, I have no intention of taking it back."

She looked at Ning Zhuo, her eyes revealing an undisguised sincerity. "Everyone outside is saying you will die. Many people are sending you things, but most just hope you can preserve your life. But I want to see you win!"

She stepped forward, her red dress like fire, her gaze burning like the crimson flames in a furnace. "Ning Zhuo, slay the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect."

Ning Zhuo's heart skipped a beat!

When Shen Hongyao said those words, her emotions were sincere and passionate, like a furnace of medicinal power compressed to its absolute limit, ready to burst through the top lid at any moment.

For a moment, Ning Zhuo was unexpectedly at a loss for words.

Many people had provided him with assistance during this period of time, and almost all of them only hoped he could keep his life.

Shen Hongyao was the only one who had wished him success in slaying the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect to his face.

And the support she brought to Ning Zhuo was already immense for her personally - this was a Nascent Soul-level Medicinal Pill! Although there were many side effects, using it at a critical moment could preserve his life or turn defeat into victory.

This was clearly Shen Hongyao's bottom-of-the-box treasure, yet she handed it over to Ning Zhuo.

Ning Zhuo asked what Shen Hongyao wanted in return.

Shen Hongyao smiled. "If I said I just wanted to see you win, would you believe me?"

Ning Zhuo was slightly stunned.

Shen Hongyao added, "Alright, I don't know what I need right now either. Why don't we put it on a tab for now?"

Ning Zhuo proposed signing a contract, but Shen Hongyao just waved her hand, turned, and left.

Ning Zhuo was completely baffled in his heart.

Why did Shen Hongyao value him so much? If there was someone else behind her, why not use the much safer route of Chen San?

Yet Shen Hongyao gave him a feeling of true sincerity; the fire in her beautiful eyes was open and bright, without the slightest trace of pretense.

Ning Zhuo could only solemnly tuck the jade bottle away, secretly committing this gesture of Shen Hongyao's to memory.

Tao Liweng subsequently arrived at the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor.

"Daoist Friend Ning Zhuo, has your brain turned to mush?" was Tao Liweng's opening sentence. "Are you really going to fight the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect?"

Sitting upright behind his desk, Ning Zhuo gave a slight nod.

Tao Liweng paced a circle in front of the desk, his sleeves whipping through the air with a swoosh. "For the remnant soul of that Red-Robed Guest? You don't even want your own life anymore?"

He glared, his lips quivering several times as he forcefully swallowed down harsher words, finally changing his tune to say, "The point is, if you die, who is going to recognize my share of the debt claims in the Naming Fire Furnace? I put in real gold, silver, and genuine materials for that!"

Ning Zhuo slowly shook his head, lacking the patience to explain further to Tao Liweng. He said indifferently, "My mind is made up. There's no need for you to persuade me further, Daoist Friend Tao."

Anxious, Tao Liweng paced back and forth.

Ning Zhuo let out a sigh and said, "This matter cannot be undone. I will absolutely not go back on my word. If Daoist Friend Tao truly wants to help me, you might as well bring out something practical."

Tao Liweng was just about to speak when Ning Zhuo raised a hand to interrupt him. "Do you know just how much assistance I've received within these past two or three days?"

Ning Zhuo revealed a small portion of it.

Tao Liweng's eyes went round, his Adam's apple bobbed, and his entire person seemed nailed to the spot.

Just the massive quantities of Mechanism materials alone, upon a quick mental calculation, amounted to a figure that would make a Golden Core cultivator's scalp tingle. Let alone those few Nascent Soul-level Medicinal Pills.

The corners of his eyes twitched a few times, and his voice lowered significantly. "You kid... you actually received this much stuff?"

Ning Zhuo's expression remained flat. "Life-Buying Coins."

Tao Liweng smacked his lips. "Right, they are exactly Life-Buying Coins... But you have to be alive to take them, and alive to spend them."

As he spoke, his eyes suddenly lit up, and he fiercely clapped his hands together. "Ah! I have a great idea!"

Ning Zhuo's eyelid twitched. Tao Liweng leaned in closely, lowering his voice and speaking rapidly, "You should use all these precious materials and assistance as bargaining chips and gift them to Tan Zhu! Invite him to take action and help you deal with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect!"

Ning Zhuo looked at him, speechless.

The more Tao Liweng spoke, the more brilliant he found his idea. "Tan Zhu is the strongest Nascent Soul in our Naming Stronghold. If he makes a move, it will be a guaranteed capture. And you won't be in any mortal danger; isn't that wonderful!"

Ning Zhuo laughed out of sheer exasperation. He didn't even have the energy to point out how shallow, naive, and impractical this idea was.

Ning Zhuo dealt with him dismissively, "Tan Zhu won't agree to it. His time is running out. Senior Chunyangzi and I had previously invited him, but he said he endured too much in the previous martial exhibition and no longer has the strength to fight."

Tao Liweng furrowed his brows. "That sounds like an excuse. If you place these things in front of him, he will definitely be moved - especially by those Nascent Soul-level Medicinal Pills!"

His eyes shone. "I've already seen through his poison techniques; they are pill toxins. The appeal of these Medicinal Pills to him will absolutely not be small!"

"Alright, alright." Ning Zhuo waved his hand. "Gongsun Yan."

Gongsun Yan entered immediately. "Young Master."

Ning Zhuo ordered, "See Daoist Friend Tao out."

Tao Liweng instantly bulged his eyes. "Ning Zhuo, I'm doing this for your own good!"

Ning Zhuo did not stand up. He simply cupped his hands with a smile. "Thank you for your good intentions, Daoist Friend Tao. The night is deep; take care on your way out."

Gongsun Yan had already walked up to Tao Liweng's side, politely yet firmly extending a hand in gesture. "Senior Tao, please."

Tao Liweng was half-invited, half-shooed out of the cave manor. Right at the entrance, he indignantly turned his head and shouted, "Ning Zhuo, you will regret this! You've completely taken my good intentions for granted like a donkey's liver and lungs!"

The cave manor's main doors slowly closed together, sealing out the final trace of his voice.

Sitting at his desk, Ning Zhuo rubbed his forehead and let out a helpless sigh.

The Death-Facing Sect lay dormant at the western foothills of Flowing Cloud Peak, where the cloud qi was as heavy and gloomy as lead year-round.

The nearby rocks were covered in a layer of pale gray moss patterns. These patterns slightly writhed with the rise and fall of the mountain wind. Looking from afar, the entire mountain peak resembled a colossal corpse that had not yet completely grown cold and stiff, sprawled across the vast twilight, ghastly and terrifying.

Deep within the sect was a stone chamber.

The chamber had no windows. Suspended on its four walls were thirty-six white bone lamps. The lamp fire was a cold cyan color, casting a faint, eerie glow on the ground and submerging the entire stone chamber in a halo of light as chilling as icy water.

A long desk sat horizontally in the center. Laid open upon it were more than ten Jade Slips, three painted portraits, and a copper basin. The basin held a half-pool of black water, and floating on its surface was the image of Ning Zhuo - it was precisely the scene of his previous battle with Liu Jinke.

Beside the long desk stood three cultivators. The three bore striking resemblances to each other. They were all tall and thin, with slightly protruding cheekbones and eye sockets sunk deep like ancient wells. Their brow ridges heavily pressed down, exuding an oppressive aura akin to falcons folding their wings or a cold blade hidden within its sheath.

They were exactly the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect!

The person on the left was the first to raise his head.

A dark white lamp-like mole grew between his brows, deeply embedded in his flesh, its luster gloomy and cold like frost.

His eye sockets were sunk deep like ancient wells. The depths of his pupils shimmered with a freezing and hollow light, as if they could swallow all surrounding light. The fire from the white bone lamps fell on his face but was silently absorbed by those eyes, causing the play of light and shadow around him to pale, as if separated by a thin water screen.

He was none other than the eldest of the Three Valiants, Xiang Hundeng.

In the middle was Xiang Sishui. His breaths were drawn out and profound, like a subterranean river silently surging. This person's facial features were exceedingly similar to Xiang Hundeng's, only his eyes were even deeper - as deep as a stagnant deep pool; even a falling leaf drifting in would not stir a single ripple.

Hanging at his waist was a Dead Spring Jade Urn. The jade urn was a grayish-white hue, its surface covered in dense, water-patterned cracks. The urn's mouth was sealed with a thin layer of water mist, seemingly there and yet not there.

The person on the right was Xiang Zican. His shoulders and back were as broad as an iron wall. There was a faint circle of bone patterns on the side of his neck, as if a white bone shackle had been deeply dug into his flesh, leaving a mark that would never fade.

His arms hung by his sides, the veins on the back of his hands knotted and protruding like the twisted roots of an old tree. His knuckles were covered in old scars.

His Three Calamities Body-Locking Armor was draped over his body year-round. The armor plates interlocked tightly like dragon scales, their surface riddled with blade marks, sword gashes, and scorch marks. They crisscrossed like an iron scroll that had been repeatedly written upon.

Leaning against a corner of the wall not far away was a Weeping Life Long Lance. The lance's blade was as pitch-black as if soaked in the long night, with congealed dark red bloodstains on the edge. Occasionally, faint weeping sounds would emanate from it, like Wailing Souls deep within the lance grinding their teeth, waiting for the next time to drink blood.
