Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 1005: Repairing the Furnace

Chunyangzi’s shock was no small matter.

The calm demeanor he held just moments ago seemed to have been smashed to pieces by an invisible iron hammer.

He hastily reached out and grabbed the flying letter that had been delivered along with the pill.

The thin sheet of letter paper made a slight crinkling sound between his fingers.

As Chunyangzi’s divine sense swept over it, the contents of the letter instantly reflected in his heart-lake.

In the letter, Ning Zhuo’s words were earnest. First, he thanked the Pure Yang Palace for the grace of their inheritance, and then he thanked Chunyangzi for his kindness in lending the pill to protect his Dao. Afterward, he confessed that while trapped in the Rising Cloud Minor Trial, facing the challenge from the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, he grew anxious. By a stroke of luck and error, he had absorbed the true intent within the Pure Yang Ancestor Pill a bit "over the top."

Over the top.

"Over the top?!"

These three words landed in Chunyangzi's eyes like three red-hot iron nails, piercing deeply into his heart one by one.

His heart ached so much he could hardly breathe.

Yet this big-headed thief had just lightly tossed out these three words - over the top!

Holding the Pure Yang Ancestor Pill in one hand and the letter in the other, Chunyangzi’s chest heaved slightly beneath his red-gold Daoist robe.

"The Pure Yang Ancestor Pill is gone."

"Gone, just like that..."

Back when he lent the pill, Chunyangzi had considered many outcomes.

He had thought Ning Zhuo might absorb too little, wasting a golden opportunity in vain.

He had thought Ning Zhuo might become addicted to the true intent of the Yang path, gain its benefits, and subsequently be easier to persuade into joining the Pure Yang lineage in the future.

But he had never imagined that the Pure Yang Ancestor Pill would be hollowed out so thoroughly!

In this instant, a chill shot up his spine.

"How could Ning Zhuo possibly digest all the medicinal power in such a short period of time?"

"Even if his Pure Yang aptitude is exceptionally outstanding, even if he still possesses a virgin body with abundant Primordial Yang, it shouldn't be like this."

After Chunyangzi's shock came an even deeper bewilderment.

A trusted aide standing by noticed the anomaly: "Peak Master, did this subordinate botch the errand this time?"

Chunyangzi fell silent, offering no response.

The aide marveled inwardly. Having followed Chunyangzi for many years, he rarely saw the latter lose his composure like this.

Reminded of his surroundings, Chunyangzi looked at the aide, and then imperceptibly swept a glance over Zhao Wangyang.

"All of you, step down for now. Zhao Wangyang, I am already aware of your entry into the fortress. Someone will naturally hand things over to you later."

Zhao Wangyang hastily bowed, his voice much lower than before: "This junior takes his leave."

He walked away very fast.

It was obvious that something had gone wrong - something major - and Chunyangzi was losing his grip. Under these circumstances, Zhao Wangyang didn't dare stay long. Slip away, slip away.

The aide also left. Left alone, Chunyangzi tightly shut the doors.

He extended his divine sense once more.

This time, he was much more meticulous.

Spiritual energy acted like golden threads, drilling into the interior of the pill shell strand by strand and slowly wandering along the pill patterns.

The pill wall wasn't damaged, and the outer shell remained exactly as before; the lingering charm of the Ancestor was still present. The auras left behind by the refining of past generations of sect leaders also remained deep within the pill's skin.

This pill was indeed, without a doubt, the Pure Yang Ancestor Pill.

Chunyangzi closed his eyes.

The cruel reality had struck him with a heavy blow to the head!

With no outsiders present, his chest heaved violently. His injuries were aggravated by his fury, and a sweet, metallic taste surged up his throat.

Chunyangzi forcefully swallowed it back down.

In truth, beneath his Daoist robe, the wounds left from six days and six nights of fierce battle still throbbed with pain. The injuries caused by the Seven-Gui Death Sacrifice Divine Art were extremely troublesome, and the scars left by The Heaven-Draping Single Line Hook were the same.

Chunyangzi appeared to be at the height of his prestige, but in reality, his internal depletion was incredibly severe.

"The most foolish act of the decade." Chunyangzi suddenly let out a low chuckle, his laughter completely devoid of joy. "The stupidest thing I, Chunyangzi, have done in the past ten years was handing the Ancestor Pill over to Ning Zhuo!"

If Ning Zhuo had only taken a small taste, Chunyangzi could still use that little bit of sweetness to slowly manipulate him in the future.

But the true intent within the pill was entirely empty.

"The worst-case scenario is that Ning Zhuo truly absorbed all the medicinal power. If that's the case, he should now be a Grandmaster of the Yang path."

A Grandmaster of the Yang path.

When these words surfaced in Chunyangzi's mind, his fingers couldn't help but tremble slightly.

He immediately thought of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar.

Once Ning Zhuo became a Grandmaster of the Yang path, coupled with his foundation in the Alchemic Dao, the Fire Element, and the Five Elements, when he conducted on-site inspections of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, it was highly likely he would see through the truth and realize that the altar was actually Chunyangzi's Soul Formation opportunity.

The final layer of paper hiding the truth would be pierced by Ning Zhuo!

"Once Ning Zhuo discovers the truth, I will fall completely into a passive position."

"I have to... endure it!"

The Pure Yang Palace's most valuable inheritance treasure was practically lost, and Ning Zhuo had obtained the greatest benefit!

"I need to send someone to keep a close eye on the Artifact Refining Hall. The repair of the Naming Fire Furnace has given me some buffer time. At least for now, Ning Zhuo has his hands full and won't come to conduct an on-site survey."

"So, I must be fast."

"Before he returns, I must immediately lay down a defensive formation. With the help of the formation, I'll conceal it!"

Originally, Chunyangzi had wanted to proceed at a measured pace, but now he had made the painful resolution to exhaust the Pure Yang Palace's accumulated treasury if necessary, all to lay down the defensive formation with all his might as soon as possible!

Deep within the Artifact Refining Hall.

The earth fire was like an ocean.

As one of the sixteen halls of the Myriad Manifestations Sect, the Artifact Refining Hall had never liked flashy decorations. Layers of the mountainside had been hollowed out into furnace chambers, black iron corridors crisscrossed, and the walls were covered with dark marks smoked out by thousands of years of fire.

The deeper they went, the more stifling the air became, and the heat seeped up endlessly from the cracks in the stone beneath their feet.

When Ning Zhuo followed Tie Kuang inside, the Cultivators on duty along the way all cast sideways glances.

Most of them had bare arms, hammers hanging from their waists, broad backs and shoulders, and their skin was roasted to the color of red copper by the furnace fires. Upon seeing Tie Kuang, everyone immediately bowed in greeting. Upon seeing Ning Zhuo, their gazes carried a few more shades of scrutiny and respect.

Tie Kuang walked at the very front, his stature as burly as an iron tower, his footsteps heavy.

Ning Zhuo followed behind him. The cuffs of his Great Thousand Mechanism Sound Garment drooped slightly, while the hem of his Green Profound Garment swayed gently in the hot wind. His expression was calm as he constantly observed his surroundings.

The two stepped into a corridor.

On both sides of the corridor, six auxiliary furnaces had already been ignited. Each auxiliary furnace was taller than an average person, with different fire patterns carved onto their bellies - some like clouds, some like scales, some resembling feathers, and some looking like the gnarled roots of an old tree.

Ning Zhuo immediately understood that he was walking along a formation line.

This massive artifact-refining formation was likely spherical, dominating the vast space inside the mountain.

Up ahead, Tie Kuang slightly halted his steps.

Before him was an earth fire chamber.

Tie Kuang glanced back at Ning Zhuo: "We're here."

Saying that, he pushed open the stone doors in front of him.

The moment the stone doors opened, an intense wave of heat immediately assaulted their faces.

Before them was a massive, circular furnace hall.

The floor formed a nine-layered ring. The outermost layer had thirty-six fire channels, and the six auxiliary furnaces in the outer corridor likely corresponded to one of these channels.

The middle layer was where the furnace-monitoring formation masters stationed themselves. Nine furnace-monitoring formation masters each occupied a position, with crystal furnace dials hovering in front of them. Within the dials, the firelight, cloud qi, pill qi, and toxic smoke were divided into different colors, constantly shifting and transforming.

The innermost layer was a black iron circular platform sunk into the ground.

Right in the center of the platform, the Naming Fire Furnace had already been placed.

For this repair, Tie Kuang was incredibly cautious and had borrowed the Naming Fire Furnace from Ning Zhuo in advance.

Ning Zhuo wasn't worried about him playing any tricks. On one hand, someone with the status of the Artifact Refining Hall Master wouldn't stoop to ruining their reputation over a spirit treasure - it wouldn't be worth the loss. On the other hand, Ning Zhuo and the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit constantly maintained a connection via Life Hanging by a Thread.

Eighty percent of the Naming Fire Furnace's body had already been repaired, and a warm glow circulated across its dark red walls. However, upon closer inspection, one could see many repair marks of different origins. In one place, there were silver-white spirit veins like a starry river; in another, there was a red-gold Pure Yang mist of light; elsewhere, golden dragon patterns faintly appeared, and there were also deep grooves of blood souls that had been purified.

At the bottom of the furnace, seven-colored mending light was faintly visible, while warm and rich pill qi drifted and sank within the furnace's belly.

Tie Kuang brought Ning Zhuo - just the two of them - to the innermost layer, and they leaped down onto the black iron circular platform situated below ground level.

Ning Zhuo stepped two paces closer and gently pressed his fingers against the furnace wall. In front of Tie Kuang, he activated the Spirit Communication Art to "soothe" the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit.

Under Ning Zhuo's control, the Vermilion Bird artifact spirit immediately acted as if it had been comforted, displaying a relaxed appearance.

Tie Kuang stood beside Ning Zhuo, taking all of this in.

He praised inwardly: "With this skill alone, Ning Zhuo is an esteemed guest of mine. If a situation similar to the Naming Fire Furnace occurs in the future, having Ning Zhuo step in to soothe the artifact spirit will cause the difficulty of my repairs to plummet tremendously."

On the surface, however, Tie Kuang spoke up: "It's a good furnace, and it has a good foundation, but if not handled well, it'll just be a tangled mess."

Ning Zhuo stopped his "soothing," cupped his hands, and said, "That is precisely why I asked Hall Master Tie to personally oversee this."

Tie Kuang laughed heartily and, with considerable straightforwardness, simply raised his hand: "Activate the outer formation!"

Boom.

Outside the stone chamber, a large number of auxiliary furnaces vibrated and roared simultaneously. Crimson light dashed through the fire channels, like thirty-six fire pythons slithering along the ground into the furnace hall.

The nine furnace-monitoring formation masters formed hand seals. The crystal furnace dials rotated in midair, and layers of formation patterns lit up outside the black iron circular platform.

Tie Kuang then slapped his storage bracelet.

Piece after piece of precious materials landed around the circular platform.

Three catties of Heavenly Furnace Crimson Marrow, colored like coagulated heart-blood of a furnace, letting out a muffled thud as it hit the ground.

Nine taels of Li Zhu Phoenix-Patterned Copper; the surface of the copper block naturally bore feather vein patterns, and when illuminated by the firelight, it looked as if vermilion bird feathers were gently opening and closing inside.

One box of Profound Obsidian Spirit-Fixing Sand; the grains of sand were as dark as the night, yet each grain contained an extremely fine speck of golden light, which stood out dazzlingly in contrast.

Seven bottles of Nine-Revolution Earth Fire Liquid; the liquid inside the bottles was divided into nine layers, with colors ranging from dark red to red-gold. The heat was restrained, but it caused the surrounding fire channels to brighten noticeably.

Twelve Mica Fire Womb Crystals; the crystals were translucent, appearing as if a tiny fire womb was curled up inside.

Afterward, Tie Kuang brought out three even more unique items.

The first was a cluster of soft, warm cloud marrow, white with a hint of red, contained in a glazed cup. It gently rose and fell, like a piece of warm cloud freshly torn from the morning twilight. When getting close, Ning Zhuo could hear a very faint, echoing chirping sound, as if this object was naturally suited for avian spirits to build their nests.

The second item was a bag of Vermilion Feather Flame-Generating Sand. The grains of sand were as fine as powder, yet each grain looked like fragments of crimson bird feathers. They lacked violent fiery energy, but instead carried a sense of growth and vitality, like flames sprouting from moist soil in springtime.

The third item was three sections of Crimson Paulownia Roots. The root hairs grew backward, shaped like phoenix claws, with red twilight coiled around the tips of the roots. Although they appeared to be of the wood element, they contained an extremely gentle fire rhythm inside, harboring vitality within stability.

Ning Zhuo's eyelids twitched slightly at the sight.

The latter three items were all local specialties of the Flame-Perching Cloud Nest.

Tie Kuang noticed his expression: "You'll know in a moment just how well-suited these three treasures are for the Naming Fire Furnace."

He turned around, faced outward, and shouted in a gruff voice: "Outer formation, heed my orders. The auxiliary furnaces are to stabilize the earth fire; we do not seek ferocity, only an even burn. Furnace-monitoring formation masters, keep a close eye on the four metrics: pill qi, toxic gas, fire veins, and spirituality. Within the inner formation, aside from Ning Zhuo and myself, no one's divine sense is allowed to cross the boundary."

Everyone acknowledged the order in unison.
