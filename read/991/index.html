Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 991: Arrangements

The masked Cultivator's fingers, hidden within his sleeves, slowly tightened until his knuckles turned white.

Someone nearby was still laughing and chatting: "Look, Ning Zhuo even listed the anonymous talismans. He can't even find the person who gave him this 'Life-Rending Demonic Deduction Talisman', yet he still specifically marked it 'to be returned'."

"That's why they say he is truly trustworthy."

The masked Cultivator's eyes narrowed. He listed the Life-Rending Demonic Deduction Talisman, but not the Three Valiants' Life-Claiming Nail. What does this mean?

He felt a sense of differential treatment.

He raised his head, looking past the milling crowd toward the tightly shut wooden window on the third floor of the restaurant.

There was no silhouette behind the window, yet he inexplicably felt that a pair of eyes was quietly and impassively watching him through the thick wooden boards, the permeating tea smoke, and the clamor of the crowd.

The masked Cultivator took half a step back, then turned around, vanishing into the flow of people like a drop of ink blending into the night.

The Life-Rending Demonic Deduction Talisman indeed gave Chen San a headache.

Back then, that mysterious Cultivator had dropped the talisman and left without even leaving a name.

Chen San had absolutely no clues regarding this, so he could only passively wait for the mysterious Cultivator to come looking for him.

This situation was naturally quite passive, but Chen San had no other choice.

Heavy Formation Peak.

Mystic light layered and cascaded like a thousand rolling water waves, one over another.

A formation diagram hovered in midair, slowly rotating.

Countless talismans poured down as densely as a galaxy, and like a myriad of swimming fish, they continuously shuttled around the formation diagram. Every flicker of light tugged at subtle qi currents that were difficult for the naked eye to catch.

Whenever the formation diagram turned, the mountain winds at the peak were sliced into extremely fine air currents. As they swept past the hall's ridges and flying eaves, they emitted a low hum, like breathing emanating from deep within the earth's veins—endless and profound.

Deep within the main hall, Dong Chen sat upon a high seat.

His face was calm, like an ancient well without ripples, and his brow showed no exposed sharpness. The profound azure Daoist robe draped over his body laid out like still water. He looked up, using his divine sense to manipulate the formation diagram, test-running this Formation.

On his desk lay the latest intelligence from within the Sect. Among the various matters, big and small, was the news of Ning Zhuo dispatching Chen San to proactively return the sponsorships.

"This boy is full of schemes; his methods are seasoned," Dong Chen suddenly spoke up while testing the Formation. "This time, the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect didn't even get to see his face. Just like the many major powers of Flowing Cloud Peak, they were all defeated by Ning Zhuo!"

Below the dais, Yin Changgeng stood with his hands behind his back.

His face was gaunt like slender bamboo, the fine lines at the corners of his eyes faint like marks eroded by the wind. His eyes were always slightly narrowed, resembling an old chess player patiently observing the board.

Yin Changgeng said softly, "Sect Master, Ning Zhuo is currently trapped in Hall Master Tie Kuang's Cloud-Rousing Minor Trial, and cannot set a battle date with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect within seven days. The matter of Nanming Stronghold charging the peak has also gained more variables because of this. Isn't it a bit early to say he has won right now?"

Shen Xi's Nine Palaces Misstep Formation Plate, Situ Xing's Star Scale Calamity Shifting Piece, Lin Jinglong's Azure Hornless Dragon Armor-Embracing Seed, the One Life Hanging Pill gifted by Cao Gui, boxes of mechanical materials, the Life-Rending Demonic Deduction Talisman, the Cloud Bone Baleful Sense Bead, the Old Battle Map, the Remnant Lamp Life-Ending Spell, the Spark Ash...

All these sponsorships were still in Ning Zhuo's hands.

How were these merely simple treasures and resources? They represented credibility and connections!

Not only that, even the entire Bai Clan had come as a whole to seek refuge with him!

And what did Ning Zhuo pay for this?

He merely sat properly in his Cave Manor, and these sponsorships were almost all actively delivered into his hands.

He used a portion of them, and then made an agreement acknowledging his debt to the respective sponsors.

For the portion he didn't use, he publicly announced that he would return them all, assuming the utmost posture of propriety.

Some took them back, some refused to take them, and some even wanted to increase their stakes!

Again, what did Ning Zhuo actually pay?

Outsiders all said he was trustworthy and understood etiquette.

But the way Dong Chen saw it: The Truth Bell had become Ning Zhuo's prop, and Qing Huangzi had been reduced to a pawn for the youth. A single act in the No Retreat Plaza had directly "defeated" the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, making Ning Zhuo the biggest winner.

The funny thing was, the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect hadn't realized it at all. Later, when Dong Chen sent people to reclaim the Heaven-Dangling One-Line Hook, they even met with a bit of resistance.

Previously, Dong Chen felt that Ning Zhuo was too full of youthful arrogance and overly reckless. This time, the youth shedding tears and being "apprehended" by Tie Kuang actually made Dong Chen appreciate Ning Zhuo's political maneuvering!

The Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect were very strong, yet they didn't even brush past Ning Zhuo or step into the martial arena before being thoroughly used!

Ning Zhuo's gains were tremendous!

Yin Changgeng thought for a moment, nodded in agreement, and then praised Peak Master Dong's foresight.

Dong Chen's voice echoed like an ancient well, eerie and faint: "How is our peak's grand plan coming along?"

Yin Changgeng's expression turned solemn. He cupped his hands and said, "Peak Master, currently the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar has become a flaw."

"Originally, the chaotic situation could conversely conceal many traces. Now that Ning Zhuo has been taken away by Tie Kuang, the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect cannot apply pressure on him in the short term, and Nanming Stronghold might not retreat. Chun Yangzi will likely take this opportunity to push forward the five-match martial exhibition."

"The best-case scenario is for the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar to fall into the hands of the Territory Expansion Alliance."

"The second best is White Cloud Village. There are also our pawns within that faction."

"The worst outcome is for Nanming Stronghold to win! Chun Yangzi, Tie Kuang, Wang Yu, the Zhu Clan, the Situ Clan, the Lin Clan, the Shen Clan, and so on—they could all take advantage of the situation to reach their hands in. The many arrangements beneath the altar cannot withstand such churning."

Dong Chen's fingertips lightly tapped twice on the armrest, like someone pondering before placing a chess piece: "The five matches are the key. Do you have a candidate?"

Yin Changgeng: "There is one person."

Dong Chen looked at him.

Yin Changgeng said, "Within the Territory Expansion Alliance, the strongest combatant at the Golden Core level is named Tuoba Pan."

As he spoke, he cast a Spell, forming an image.

The figure of a burly Cultivator immediately appeared.

He stood nearly nine feet tall, his shoulders and back as broad as a sprawling mountain ridge, and his skin was a dark bronze. On his temples, the backs of his hands, and his neck were earthen-yellow patterns, like cracks in rock strata, or like the earth's veins surfacing from within his flesh and blood.

His hair was shaved extremely short, almost clinging to his scalp. His brow ridges were heavy like two earthen ridges, and his eyes were dark, turbid, and carried a sense of oppression. Standing there, he resembled a giant boulder carved out from the very roots of a mountain—heavy and unshakable!

Yin Changgeng continued his introduction: "Tuoba Pan is at the peak Golden Core stage, mainly cultivating the Thick Peak Army-Swallowing Manual. This art does not seek agility, nor does it seek variation..."

"His main magic treasures include the Yellow Peak Sinking Shoulder Armor and the Heavy Mountain Spade..."

Dong Chen looked at it for a moment, finally speaking, "Then let him fight."

Yin Changgeng cupped his hands: "Yes."

Dong Chen added, "Since Ning Zhuo has already borrowed momentum to set his board, we must no longer treat him as an ordinary genius. With Chun Yangzi pushing forward the five matches, Wang Yu won't sit idly by. Tie Kuang has his eyes on the Naming Fire Furnace, and might also become an obstacle. The flaw that is the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar must be patched up as soon as possible!"

Yin Changgeng lowered his head, "This subordinate will make the arrangements immediately."

Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor.

The atmosphere was as heavy as a layer of wet leaden clouds pressing down, and even the three-colored radiant light flowing on the stone walls seemed to have dimmed somewhat.

After Ning Zhuo was taken away by Tie Kuang, the people in the Cave Manor seemed to have suddenly lost their backbone. They each stayed in their own corners, but were all absent-minded.

Old Chef was still in the kitchen simmering Spirit Food. The spirit soup in the pot boiled and rolled, but the bubbling sound lacked its usual warmth to the ears.

Gongsun Yan stayed in the Mechanism room, with dissection diagrams of over a dozen Mystic Soldier Armors spread out before him. His fingertips pressed on the edges of the diagrams, but his eyes involuntarily drifted toward the direction of the Cave Manor's main gate several times, as if hoping that in the next moment, that door would be pushed open by someone.

Qing Chi was even more restless, pacing back and forth in the front hall, her footsteps making the stone floorboards faintly echo.

Huo Nian'er leaned against a pillar with his arms crossed, his complexion as ugly as over-burnt furnace ash. His temples throbbed in time with the anger burning in his heart.

Suddenly, a burst of brisk footsteps came from outside the Cave Manor. Not too fast, not too slow, carrying a kind of completely indifferent casualness.

The next moment, Sun Lingtong entered the Cave Manor.

It was the Young Master's pageboy!

Regarding Boss Sun's identity as a pageboy, Old Chef, Qing Chi, and the others were all very clear about it.

Sun Lingtong gathered everyone in the front hall.

"Yo. Why all the long faces? It's not like our Young Master is dead. There's no need to mourn."

"You're the one who's going to die!" Qing Chi spat, her eyes spitting fire in anger.

It was Huo Nian'er's first time meeting Sun Lingtong, and upon hearing this, his face also revealed anger.

Gongsun Yan knew how formidable Sun Lingtong was, and his brows furrowed tightly.

Old Chef stepped forward instead. "So, the Young Master already has arrangements?"

Sun Lingtong chuckled, "Relax, he definitely won't die, and he's actually doing very well. What Tie Kuang is after is the Naming Fire Furnace, Qing Huangzi needs an inheritor to stay alive and well, the Confucian cultivators all support the Young Master, and the Zhu Clan is even more terrified of the Young Master dying."

Old Chef couldn't help but fall into deep thought.

Sun Lingtong added, "The Young Master has already issued orders for me to make the arrangements. Until he returns, I'm to take charge of the overarching situation in the Cave Manor."

Everyone looked at each other in dismay.

Old Chef was the first to speak, stating his stance, "Please give your orders."

Sun Lingtong then said, "Old Chef, you'll manage the Cave Manor's food, the comings and goings of the storehouse, and pacifying the servants as usual. Starting today, add ten percent more heat to the Spirit Food to nourish everyone's qi. Don't make it seem like the Cave Manor has run out of rations just because the Young Master isn't here."

Sun Lingtong looked toward Gongsun Yan. "Gongsun, you'll continue refining mechanical components for the future assembly of a mechanical bird flock. Just because the Young Master is in the minor trial doesn't mean you can stop working. The five-match martial exhibition outside could progress at any moment, and it's highly likely Chun Yangzi's side will make a move. When the Young Master needs the mechanical flying birds in the future, if you haven't made enough, you'll face the punishment yourself!"

Gongsun Yan lowered his head and answered, indicating his obedience.

Sun Lingtong pointed a finger at Huo Nian'er. "You there, what's-your-name, you will assist Gongsun Yan."

Huo Nian'er hesitated for a moment but ultimately nodded. "Yes."

Sun Lingtong finally looked at Qing Chi. "As for you, come outside with me."

Qing Chi's eyes instantly lit up. "Are we going to see the Young Master?"

Sun Lingtong rolled his eyes at her. "If you go now, you'll simply be causing trouble for the Young Master. We're going to do something more useful."

Qing Chi frowned. "What is it?"

Sun Lingtong grinned, revealing a mouthful of white teeth. That smile looked exceptionally bright in the dim light of the Cave Manor: "Participate in the Cloud-Rousing Minor Trial."

Everyone was taken aback.

Sun Lingtong chuckled, putting his hands on his hips and instructing with absolute confidence, "Old Chef, pack up a few portions of Spirit Pastries for me. I'll eat them while queuing up for the Cloud-Rousing Minor Trial."
