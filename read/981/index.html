Chapter 981: Wanting to "Beat Ning Zhuo to Death"

The dark water in the bronze plate suddenly rippled.

In the image, Ning Zhuo's Profound Soldier Armor formed the Shouzhuo Wheel Slash Formation, forcing Liu Jinke to retreat rapidly. Layers of protective golden light were sliced open, and shattered golden fragments rained down.

Xiang Zican grinned, revealing a mouthful of stark white teeth, his voice coarse like rubbing sandstones: "A mid-stage Foundation Establishment cultivator, capable of forcing a Golden Core cultivator to such an extent, is quite impressive."

Xiang Sishui's gaze heavily pressed upon the bronze plate: "His combat power leverage is quite clever. With a shallow foundation of spiritual energy, he compensates with mechanisms, formations, magic artifacts, and intelligence. Each patch isn't particularly robust on its own, but when combined, they can effectively exploit Liu Jinke's limitations and then tear open a gap."

Xiang Hundeng's fingertip lightly touched the white lamp's wick, and the lamplight immediately illuminated the precise moment Ning Zhuo seized Liu Jinke's hesitation during the battle.

Xiang Hundeng gazed for a long time before slowly saying, "His on-the-spot judgment is extremely fast. Several times when Liu Jinke shifted his spiritual energy, Ning Zhuo preemptively suppressed his momentum. If this child reaches the Golden Core stage, he will be an extremely troublesome opponent."

Xiang Zican let out a low chuckle, the sound rumbling in his throat: "What a pity. He's destined to fall into our hands."

Xiang Sishui didn't smile. He pushed a jade slip in front of them, which contained detailed records of Ning Zhuo's battles, trials, transactions, and sponsorships since arriving at the Myriad Manifestations Sect.

"Don't underestimate him," Xiang Sishui's voice was low and steady. "Most of the methods he's currently displaying were only mastered after he arrived at the Myriad Manifestations Sect - the Profound Soldier Armor, mechanical bird swarms, Shouzhuo Wheel Slash Formation, and so on."

Xiang Hundeng swept his divine sense over it, then pushed the jade slip to Xiang Zican, one of the Three Valiants: "I've seen similar things. This young man is very likely from a major clan, deeply versed in the power schemes of the Righteous Path. By relying on the White Rainbow Righteous Integrity Bone and the Naming Fire Furnace, he made a name for himself throughout the main sect, established alliances and strongholds, stirring up great commotions. In past Flying Cloud Assemblies, countless geniuses emerged. But geniuses like him are rare."

Xiang Zican lowered his head to look at the bronze plate. In the image, Ning Zhuo was using the Shouzhuo Wheel Slash Formation to force back Liu Jinke, then seized opportunities presented by the terrain, magic artifacts, and his opponent's mentality, step by step pushing the battlefield in his favor.

Xiang Zican's cruel grin lessened somewhat: "He is adept at borrowing and building momentum, and he's good at using his brain. After arriving at the Myriad Manifestations Sect's main gate, he has deliberately kept a low profile, not revealing many of his trump cards. This made our combat preparations difficult."

Xiang Sishui took out several more jade slips from his sleeve: "These are intelligence reports on the sponsorships Ning Zhuo has recently received. We can infer his foundation from these."

The jade slips revealed that the Star Scale Tribulation-Shifting Pearl, Nine Palace Misplaced Step Formation Plate, Azure Dragon Armored Seed, Soul-Burning Reverse Blood Pill, Life-Hanging Survival Pill, as well as Profound Armor Cave, Huonian'er, and more, accounted for the majority of the truth.

Xiang Zican's brow bones lowered, and a hint of gloom surfaced in his eyes: "He has indeed received quite a lot."

Xiang Hundeng: "Most of them are already invalid; we are prepared and can handle, or even counter, them."

Xiang Sishui: "The Life-Hanging Survival Pill came from Wang Yu, the Peak Master of Danxia Peak. It seems this Peak Master wants to take the opportunity to show goodwill and recruit Ning Zhuo to the main peak."

Xiang Zican: "This is a Nascent Soul-level life-saving item. This item is somewhat tricky; it can keep Ning Zhuo clinging to his last breath. Therefore, we must kill him in one blow!"

At this moment, the bronze plate showed a scene where Ning Zhuo had once used the White Rainbow Righteous Integrity Bone to send surging righteous qi soaring into the sky.

"Fortunately, this time, the Demon Subjugation Hall seems to be standing by, not lending out the White Rainbow Righteous Integrity Bone. Otherwise, it would still be a bit troublesome."

"We all know Zhong Dao's temperament. How could Zhong Dao give Ning Zhuo a good look after he publicly announced his intention to rescue the demonic soul?"

"It might not be a layer of disguised smoke either."

"Have the Three Valiants Soul-Reaping Nails been delivered to Ning Zhuo?" A low chuckle rumbled in Xiang Zican's throat.

Xiang Sishui nodded: "They have."

It turned out that the so-called Three Valiants Soul-Reaping Nails were actually a scheme by the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, a sinister trap laid out for Ning Zhuo.

Xiang Hundeng's eyes glowed with a cold, frosty light: "At that time, once he uses these nails, hehe... a dignified new star of the Righteous Path, swearing an oath under the Truth Bell to save someone, yet turning around and producing a Demonic Spirit Treasure. And then being backlashed by the treasure - at that point, I wonder how many people in the martial arts arena will be laughing so hard they can't straighten up."

The Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect aimed not just to kill Ning Zhuo but also to shatter his reputation, striking at his very heart!

Even when facing a mid-stage Foundation Establishment cultivator, the three individuals - at the peak of Golden Core cultivation and with illustrious achievements - did not show the slightest carelessness. They strictly adhered to the principle of a lion using its full strength to hunt a rabbit, never underestimating, and giving Ning Zhuo no opportunities.

"Ning Zhuo received sponsorships, but we've also received quite a few."

"Who would have thought that ambushing a Foundation Establishment cultivator this time would be such a rare fat assignment?"

"After this battle, will we be able to keep the Heaven-Hanging Thread Hook?" Xiang Zican licked his lips.

The Dao Artifact, Heaven-Hanging Thread Hook, had also been handed to the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect.

But it was only a temporary loan, limited to this battle.

A rare glint of fervor appeared in Xiang Hundeng's eyes: "This Dao Artifact is truly perfect for us! After this battle, we must find a way to keep the Heaven-Hanging Thread Hook!"

Xiang Sishui stared at Ning Zhuo in the bronze basin, remaining silent, as killing intent continuously brewed and accumulated within him.

The night had deepened like the bottom of an inkwell.

Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor.

Atop the cave manor, three colors of light circulated ceaselessly - warm gold of sunlight, clear silver of moonlight, and cold blue of starlight - flowing along the array patterns into the stone walls, like three gentle streams silently intertwining.

Ning Zhuo sat alone at his desk, reviewing the existing sponsorships.

Situ Xing's Star Scale Tribulation-Shifting Pearl, Shen Xi's Nine Palace Misplaced Step Formation Plate, Lin Jinglong's Azure Dragon Armored Seed, Profound Armor Cave's brand-new Golden Core-level Profound Soldier Armor, Cao Gui's Life-Hanging Survival Pill, Yun Youzi's Cloud Beast, Shen Hongyao's old demonic pill... and mountains of mechanism materials, such as Green Pattern Bamboo Core, Cloud Silk Soft Copper, Fine Toothed Silver Reed, Lightning Pattern Red Rivet, and Floating Sound Jade Dust, piled up in boxes to fill half a stone chamber.

"The Pi family ultimately didn't give me Nascent Soul-level shadow puppets. Profound Armor Cave also didn't lend out Nascent Soul-level Profound Soldier Armor."

"The Unity of Knowledge and Action Art was not obtained."

"From Kong Ran's side, there was no follow-up on sentient mechanical battle puppets and the like."

Ning Zhuo had also proposed a True Intent, but it was rejected by all parties; they must have been scoffing at Ning Zhuo's deluded fantasies in their hearts.

"Three Valiants Soul-Reaping Nails."

A black bone casket lay on the corner of the desk, and inside it rested the Three Valiants Soul-Reaping Nails, the only item recently that seemed capable of directly threatening the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect.

"Someone else hasn't arrived yet, wait a little longer."

Ning Zhuo's patience was rewarded the next day.

The entrance to the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor slowly opened, and a stream of crimson-gold light poured in from outside, filling the entire stone chamber like morning glow.

Chunyangzi stood outside the door, still clad in his crimson-gold Daoist robe, on which the entire Pure Yang Pill Scripture was embroidered. The characters of the scripture glittered under the illumination of the three lights.

Ning Zhuo rose to greet him: "Senior has graced us with your presence; I apologize for not coming out to meet you."

Ning Zhuo invited him to sit and personally poured him spirit tea: "This junior is deeply honored that senior has come to assist."

Chunyangzi did not touch the tea, merely stared fixedly at Ning Zhuo, golden light surging in his eyes: "How confident are you that you can defeat the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect?"

"This junior knows his limits. The chances of victory in this battle are slim. If I can swear an oath under the Truth Bell and preserve my life in the martial arts demonstration, that will be an explanation to all under heaven. Senior Red-Robed Guest's soul is still in their hands; they are reluctant to kill, so this junior will have opportunities to slowly rescue him in the future," Ning Zhuo said with a smile.

Chunyangzi was expressionless, silent for a moment.

If one were to ask who most anticipated Ning Zhuo's victory, it wasn't Shen Hongyao, who was the only one previously optimistic and encouraging of Ning Zhuo's success, but rather the Nascent Soul Grand Cultivator before him.

Chunyangzi was determined to obtain the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar; he was the one truly concerned about the outcome of Ning Zhuo's battle.

Others, such as Shen Xi, Situ Xing, and the Confucian cultivators, were more concerned with Ning Zhuo's life and safety.

It could be said that Chunyangzi was one of the people Ning Zhuo was truly waiting for!

"Ning Zhuo," Chunyangzi finally spoke after a long silence. "Not long ago, you publicly declared that you would save the Red-Robed Guest's remnant soul. Now, before even fighting, you're retreating the victory to merely saving your life? Where is your youthful ambition?"

Ning Zhuo met his gaze, his expression unwavering: "Senior's reprimand is correct. However, the disparity in combat power is clear; this junior cannot deceive himself with empty words."

"Furthermore, this junior did indeed say he would rescue Senior Red-Robed Guest's soul, but his strength is insufficient; what else can he do?"

"For now, only by first overcoming the obstacle of the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect, even if it means stumbling and bleeding profusely, as long as my life is preserved, there will be hope."

"The matter of rescuing the Red-Robed Guest's remnant soul is a long and arduous journey, and can only be pursued gradually."

Chunyangzi took a deep breath. He could no longer maintain a composed demeanor, entirely because Ning Zhuo's move had placed him in an extremely awkward position.

This was entirely because the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, a geomantic treasure land, was truly too important to him.

Pure Yang Palace merging into the Myriad Manifestations Sect - was this Chunyangzi's will?!

He only did so because he could no longer hold out.

Chunyangzi led his entire sect to pledge allegiance. To put it nicely, he was a wise man who knew the times; to put it bluntly, he couldn't bear to abandon his ancestors' foundation.

At the level of a nation, he would be a fugitive monarch of a fallen kingdom.

The vexation and depression in Chunyangzi's heart were imaginable!

And now, as long as he obtained the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, he would have great hope of advancing to Soul Formation!

What kind of concept was this?

His entire sect joining the Myriad Manifestations Sect was already a done deal. Although he couldn't directly leave the Myriad Manifestations Sect, Chunyangzi could make Chunyang Peak semi-independent.

At that point, what could people under heaven criticize him for?

Just the cultivation level of a Soul Formation cultivator would command respect from almost everyone!

Once he reached this stage, Chunyangzi would no longer feel ashamed before his ancestors.

Soul Formation!

These two words had burned in his heart for how many years. They were like a crimson charcoal, especially scorching in the dead of night. The ancestral tablets of Pure Yang Palace seemed to hang behind him, and Chunyangzi dared not look back.

"As long as I obtain the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, I can achieve Soul Formation."

Chunyangzi's hand in his sleeve slightly clenched: "If I achieve Soul Formation, how could Pure Yang Palace not be revitalized?"

But now, he was stuck here, stuck with Ning Zhuo.

Ning Zhuo's declaration to rescue the Red-Robed Guest's remnant soul made it impossible for Chunyangzi to "retaliate"!

He truly couldn't "retaliate."

Because Chunyangzi held the first seat; he was the nominal Grand Chieftain, the leader of Naming Stronghold!

Now, the Red-Robed Guest had fought for Naming Stronghold's array patterns, but his soul was denied. Ning Zhuo, sitting in the last seat, publicly announced that he would rescue the Red-Robed Guest's demonic soul.

What was Chunyangzi to do?

Was he going to single-handedly prevent Ning Zhuo from doing this?

If he did so, his prestige would plummet to rock bottom. Everyone in Naming Stronghold would look down on him, his reputation would be ruined, and it would even implicate Juyang Peak.

The seat Chunyangzi occupied had become a constraint on his actions.

He could only remain silent, fortunately, his enmity with the Red-Robed Guest was well-known, so the public could understand his silence.

And amidst this silence, Ning Zhuo did not retreat from the difficulty; instead, he pressed his advantage, announcing that he would swear an oath under the Truth Bell and arrange a date for a duel with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect.

Chunyangzi truly couldn't sit still!

The current development of the situation was something he absolutely did not want to see. Because the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar was truly too important to him, yet now, Ning Zhuo's battle with the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect was to determine the final outcome of the five martial arts demonstrations, deciding whether he could obtain this geomantic treasure land!

Chunyangzi was distressed.

The core reason for his distress was that he knew he could only watch, unable to prevent any of it from happening.

Setting aside his reputation in the Righteous Path, even if he abandoned his reputation, he still couldn't stop it - because the major forces of Flowing Cloud Peak would absolutely not act according to his wishes!

These people would only focus on Ning Zhuo's core identity, firmly grasping the key point that "defeating Ning Zhuo would cause Naming Stronghold to collapse," and actively promote a battle between the Three Valiants of the Death-Facing Sect and Ning Zhuo.

Chunyangzi was a Nascent Soul-level powerhouse and the Grand Chieftain, but he was not the orchestrator of this conflict.

It could be said that Ning Zhuo's sudden move at the last moment directly seized control of the situation from him!

Chunyangzi had endured bitterly, watching with cold eyes for many days, finally making his appearance today.

When he heard Ning Zhuo's goal of merely preserving his life, he wished he could crush Ning Zhuo to death on the spot!

If I had known this, why did you, brat, insist on proclaiming that you would rescue the Red-Robed Guest's demonic soul?!

You put yourself in an impossible position, and you put my grand plan, Chunyangzi's plan, at risk!

I know you want to protect your reputation. But I need to obtain the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar!

Your petty reputation, if it's damaged, then it's damaged. But the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar concerns my advancement to Soul Formation.

You're ruining my great endeavor, ruining my great endeavor!

This upstart is not worth strategizing with!!!

Chunyangzi was extremely vexed, because he couldn't explain. Advancing to Soul Formation was an absolutely critical matter of life and death, and he naturally had to keep it a strict secret.

"Therefore, Ning Zhuo made a misjudgment. When his reputation was about to be jeopardized, he was provoked into action, stubbornly declaring that he would rescue the demonic soul."

"He thought that the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar was only beneficial to me, and only to a limited extent. He thought that even if this assault on the peak failed, he could still switch to other goals."

"This brat..."

Chunyangzi understood Ning Zhuo in his heart, and also really wanted to "beat Ning Zhuo to death."

Of course, "beat to death" was merely an emotional expression of extreme anger. Chunyangzi naturally knew that Ning Zhuo was the core and key to Naming Stronghold, and he absolutely could not suffer any mishaps, otherwise the entire Naming Stronghold would collapse.
