Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 1019: Breaking the Altar

It was as if a golden sun had suddenly turned around in the night, plummeting down violently!

Shi Qian's expression changed drastically.

A flash of wariness also swept through Tan Zhu's eyes.

Chunyangzi had discovered them!

He held a round mirror in his hand.

The back of the mirror was crimson-gold, and its surface was as clear as morning dew. Engraved along its edge were four words: "Yang Illuminates, Nothing Hides."

The Clear Heart Evil-Illuminating Mirror!

The black spots on this precious mirror had been mostly removed, rendering it usable once again.

Chunyangzi aimed the mirror at the ground to the southwest and channeled his pure yang spiritual energy.

Yang Element - Illuminate Evil, Reveal Form!

Buzz.

The mirror's surface emitted a clear, ringing sound.

A warm golden light pierced into the earth. Wherever the golden light passed, the soil, rock layers, fire veins, and formation lines became nearly transparent. The outline of the small underground cavity immediately surfaced in Chunyangzi's vision.

He saw Shi Qian, and he also saw Tan Zhu.

Furthermore, he saw three formation nails and an Earth Element formation plate.

A sharp glint erupted in Chunyangzi's eyes, and rage churned in his heart, yet he did not shout out loud.

At this moment, he was calmer than ever before.

Because this concerned his opportunity for Soul Formation!

If he made a big fuss, the attention of all factions within the Myriad Manifestations Sect would be drawn here. The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar had not yet closed, and the critical points of the formation were still exposed. If too many outsiders came, the risk of his greatest secret being leaked would sharply escalate!

Chunyangzi lifted his foot and stomped.

Noon Shadowless Steps!

The shadow beneath his feet instantly shrank to a point. His entire person transformed into a streak of gold-white light, and with almost no process of traversing space, he landed on the southwest ground from the formation platform.

The next moment, he brought his right fingers together and pressed down.

Pure Yang Palm!

Boom.

A crimson-gold palm print plunged into the earth. The edges of the palm print glowed brightly, yet its inner power was tightly condensed. It did not blast the ground apart; instead, like a golden wedge, it drove precisely into the top of that cavity.

Inside the cramped cavity, the two men simultaneously felt a heat above their heads.

The Sinking Mountain Inverted Seal was pressed down by the crimson-gold palm print, emitting a harsh, creaking sound like two massive boulders grinding against each other.

Shi Qian cursed inwardly. Chunyangzi had come too fast!

Tan Zhu, however, slightly narrowed his eyes.

His goal was already mostly achieved.

"This enemy wishes to destroy this precious geomantic land. Let the two of us join forces to subdue, imprison, and interrogate him!" Tan Zhu immediately spoke up, reaching out to Chunyangzi.

Shi Qian cried out inwardly that things were going terribly wrong. Tan Zhu was, after all, a member of the Nanming Stronghold. He hastily sneered, "Tan Zhu, I stumbled upon your sinister schemes and was just about to report to the Grand Chieftain, using this merit to aid my entry into the stronghold. You certainly know how to make false countercharges."

Tan Zhu burst into laughter and pointed at the three formation nails. "Did I place these items as well?"

Shi Qian: "..."

All three were Nascent Souls who had cultivated for over a thousand years. They could be called millennium-old foxes. Through this brief verbal confrontation, Chunyangzi had already seen through the essence of the matter.

Chunyangzi took a deep breath, forcefully suppressing his fury. "Stop! Put down the magic treasures in your hands and follow me to Verification Peak. Before the Truth Bell, the truth will speak for itself."

He pretended to be unable to distinguish right from wrong to suppress the matter and prevent it from blowing up.

Shi Qian's eyes grew gloomy.

Go to Verification Peak?

How could he!

If he actually went, there would be no turning back. Dong Chen wouldn't protect an exposed pawn. Heavy Formation Peak wouldn't admit that he had been sent under orders either. In the end, he alone would bear the blame for secretly infiltrating the earth veins of the Nanming Stronghold and destroying the treasured geomantic land.

He absolutely could not go.

A look of sheer resolve flashed deep in Shi Qian's eyes.

He abruptly bit the tip of his tongue and spat a mouthful of earth-yellow vital essence blood onto the Sinking Mountain formation plate.

Earth Element - Mountain-Crushing Return to Dust!

The Sinking Mountain formation plate suddenly sank.

The nine rings of mountain patterns on the plate's surface shattered simultaneously, emitting nine muffled boom, boom, boom sounds. Each sound was like a small mountain collapsing underground. A dark yellow light surged madly from the plate's surface, no longer concealing the nails, but directly crushing forward.

Chunyangzi's expression changed dramatically. "Stop!"

He flipped his right palm, and the remnant scriptures on his crimson-gold Daoist robe lit up one by one.

Three Thousand Scriptures of Flourishing Fire - Pulse Sealing!

Over three hundred characters flew out from the robe, each burning with a gold-white fiery light. Rather than attacking Shi Qian, they transformed into lines of fire that swiftly wrapped around the southwest earth veins, attempting to bear the terrifying impact of the Mountain-Crushing Return to Dust technique on behalf of the earth pulse.

Rumble, rumble, rumble...

Deep within the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, the three formation nails shattered simultaneously. Not a single fragment splashed outward; they were all crushed into dark yellow powder by the Sinking Mountain formation plate.

Destruction is always easier than creation!

The ground in the southwest corner collapsed downward by half a foot with a loud crash.

The closing Sun-Patrolling Night-Illuminating Ring on the surface suddenly dimmed.

Several formation lamps exploded with a crack, scattering sparks like a string of crimson-gold fireflies blown apart by the wind. The disciples of Chunyang Peak who were working on the construction were knocked off balance, stumbling around; someone grunted muffledly as blood trickled from the corner of their mouth.

"The earth pulse is in chaos!"

"The southwest fire eye has lost balance!"

"Three roots of the Azure Hornless Dragon have been severed, quickly repair them!"

Chaos instantly broke out above.

Chunyangzi felt as if his heart had been struck by a heavy hammer.

He could clearly sense that a crucial earth vein of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar had been torn open. The wound was not large, but it was in a critical spot, much like an almost closed pill furnace having a crack forcibly hammered into its base.

A sharp glint flashed in Tan Zhu's eyes.

"Now."

This was the very moment he had been waiting for.

The remaining three bone clasps on his Seven-Hidden Poison Pouch sprang open simultaneously.

Poison Method - Three Hidden Concurrence - Bone Cyan, Blood Ash, Soul Purple!

Three streams of poisonous gas spewed out at once.

One stream was cyan-green, pungent and chilling to the bone, specifically eroding bone marrow.

One stream was grayish-white, light and drifting like ash, traveling along the blood.

One stream was deep purple, soundless and odorless, lunging straight for the Divine Soul.

The three streams of poisonous gas converged in midair, emitting a piercing sizzle like a red-hot iron rod plunged into ice water. The poisonous gas transformed into a three-colored poison seal, its surface shaped like a withered flower, its edges lined with fine serrations.

Tan Zhu struck out with a palm.

Mixed Poison - Withered Flower Three-Life Seal!

The poison seal passed through the chaotic earth light and viciously struck Shi Qian's left ribs.

Pfft!

Shi Qian sprayed a large mouthful of blackish-yellow blood, his entire body crashing against the wall of the cavity. His protective treasure garment rapidly blackened, and the sand patterns on his cloak peeled off in large swaths.

The Withered Flower Three-Life Seal burrowed into his body, poisoning his bones, blood, and soul simultaneously.

Shi Qian's eyes twitched from the agonizing pain!

He knew he could no longer stay.

The Sinking Mountain formation plate was destroyed, and only one of his previous hidden cards remained. But he was already powerless; if he fell into the enemy's hands, it would be even more troublesome.

Shi Qian plunged both hands into the ground, the veins on his forehead bulging.

Earth Element - Earth Infant Shell Shedding!

His Nascent Soul aura abruptly contracted. The spiritual energy on the outer layer of his true body condensed, turning into a crust of earth. This crust rapidly expanded, becoming a mud statue identical to him.

His Nascent Soul suddenly enlarged, merging with his physical body. Transforming into a ray of light, it burrowed out from beneath the feet of the mud statue and fled via Earth Evasion!

At the same time, the mud statue opened its eyes and lunged toward Chunyangzi.

A golden light flashed in Chunyangzi's eyes.

"Trying to leave?"

He pointed his conjoined fingers forward.

A golden-red finger light shot out from his fingertips, the sound as sharp as a shattered golden bell. The finger light pierced into the soil layer and instantly penetrated the mud statue's glabella. The mud statue exploded with a boom, the rolling mud sizzling as it was roasted by the Pure Yang Fire.

However, Shi Qian's true body had already bought half a breath of time using the mud statue.

Half a breath of time was more than enough for a Nascent Soul earth cultivator.

The yellow line burrowed into the deeper earth veins and vanished in the blink of an eye.

Tan Zhu did not stick around either.

Having grown cunning with age, he would absolutely not remain at the scene to await Chunyangzi's interrogation.

The Ink Nightmare Poison Staff lightly spun in his palm, and the purple-gray poison orb at its tip lit up. Tan Zhu tapped it at his feet.

Poison Method - Poison Shadow Retreat!

A layer of grayish-black poisonous shadow peeled off from his body and remained in place. His true body, masked by the poison shadow, drifted toward another crevice.

How could Chunyangzi let him go?

The Clear Heart Evil-Illuminating Mirror reflected a trace of his true shadow. Chunyangzi waved his right sleeve, and a length of golden fire rope flew out. Fine scriptures swam along the surface of the fire rope, like a spirit snake that had been sunbaked until it was translucent.

Pure Yang Evil-Binding Rope!

Whoosh!

The golden fire rope pierced through the poison shadow, chasing straight after Tan Zhu's true body. The fire rope swept through the underground fissures, burning the damp, cold earth qi until it crackled. A bitter, scorched scent permeated the air.

Looking back, Tan Zhu suddenly felt his heart leap in fright.

He raised a hand to clutch his chest and let out a low sigh.

The next moment, his shadow suddenly shortened by a segment, and his complexion also paled significantly.

This was an evasion technique that came at a great cost. It required using one's own vitality as a catalyst, and could temporarily deceive the pursuit of spells. Just as the fire rope was about to wrap around his back, Tan Zhu's figure scattered with a snap, like a drop of ink falling into water.

The fire rope constricted around a patch of poisonous fog.

Burned by the Pure Yang Fire, the poisonous fog emitted sharp, shrill hisses, like countless tiny snakes writhing in the flames.

Tan Zhu's true body had already retreated into the other side of the earth veins. Without looking back, he left behind a single sentence.

"Fellow Daoist Chunyangzi, you have worked hard tonight."

As his voice faded, the poisonous gas also dissipated entirely.

Chunyangzi stood above the fractured cavity, his chest heaving violently.

He did not pursue.

Nor could he pursue.

The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar was "bleeding."

The southwest earth veins were destroyed, the Sun-Patrolling Night-Illuminating Ring was unstable, and one corner of the Red Paulownia Pulse-Locking Formation had lost balance. If he left here, everything they had rushed to build tonight could be destroyed in a flash.

What chilled his heart even more was that there was actually a third aura on the edge of the battlefield.

Like a small fish, it swam briefly among the waterweeds before vanishing without a trace.

Sun Lingtong was nearby!

He originally hadn't planned to get this close.

The task Ning Zhuo had given him was to investigate the truth behind the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar. He was to see as much as he could, and absolutely avoid forcing his way in.

Sun Lingtong had the Life Hanging by a Thread upon him.

At this moment, far away in the Three Lights Dao Heaven Cave Manor, Ning Zhuo was sitting in a quiet room, keeping a constant eye on Sun Lingtong's movements.

Along the way, the more Sun Lingtong saw, the more shocked he became.

The truth of the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar gradually unfolded before his eyes.

The preciousness of this place lay not only in its dense Yang Qi.

"If it were merely abundant sun essence and vigorous fire veins, one might not be unable to find a substitute within the Myriad Manifestations Sect. The truly formidable aspect is that this place can combine all three: supporting the sun, locking the yang, and ascending the clouds!"

The sun essence descended from high above, splitting into twelve bright streams as it passed through the sun-supporting stone patterns. The underground fire veins surged upwards, being refined into a warm, thick foundational fire as they passed through the red paulownia root veins. The middle-layer cloud apertures continuously exhaled and inhaled cloud qi, supporting, buffering, and smoothing out the excessive yang fire.

Finally, the twelve bright streams, the foundational power of the earth fire, and the buffering cloud qi all converged into the Yang-Locking Well.

That well was not on the surface.

It was located at the core where the earth veins, cloud apertures, and sun patterns intersected. Like an invisible hand, it could press down the overwhelmingly intense Yang Qi, allowing it to become self-consistent rather than self-destructive.

"No wonder." Seeing through it all, Sun Lingtong directly used his divine sense to communicate with Ning Zhuo along the Life Hanging by a Thread. "Little Zhuo, this place can lock up excessive pure yang, and then use cloud qi to grind it down. Ground to its absolute limit, it can avoid the fate of declining after reaching its peak, transforming from hard and unyielding to flexible and circumambient. For Chunyangzi, this will lead to a whole new realm!"
