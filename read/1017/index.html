Mysteries Of Immortal Puppet Master

Chapter 1017: Night Battle

"Did Chunyangzi manage to spy out anything?"

Wang Yu looked at the increasingly dense red-gold points of light on the formation diagram, his brows twitching slightly.

This contemporary Chunyangzi of the Pure Yang Palace was originally, in Wang Yu's eyes, someone with formidable combat power but lacking in management skills.

The fact that Pure Yang Palace had been reduced to joining the Myriad Manifestations Sect proved that Chunyangzi's faction-management skills were not top-tier. However, his current move had somewhat changed Wang Yu's opinion.

First, he won a Pyrrhic victory over Liang Ming to seize prestige. Then, he took advantage of the momentum to take over the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, working day and night, using heavy sums of money to forcefully establish the core defensive formation.

This way, no one within the Nanming Stronghold could oppose him for the time being. And if outsiders wanted to make a move, their window of opportunity would quickly close.

"This person still has some skills," Wang Yu smiled faintly.

"But acting with such haste has inevitably revealed his true intentions."

"The Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar definitely holds special value for Chunyangzi!"

Initially, Wang Yu instructed Tan Zhu to destroy the cover and expose the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar specifically because he saw that this place could attract Chunyangzi.

Wang Yu set down his teacup and took out a small jade talisman.

The word "Poison" was carved onto the jade talisman, its strokes as thin as insect trails.

This was a secret talisman message used to contact Tan Zhu.

Wang Yu did not activate it immediately.

He merely pinched the jade talisman between his fingers, waiting quietly.

The firelight from the pill furnace shone on his face, making his expression seem exceptionally gentle.

Yet, there was not a trace of warmth in those eyes.

He had already given the order for Tan Zhu to act tonight.

At the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar!

After midnight, the wind grew more urgent.

Standing at a high vantage point, Chunyangzi suddenly coughed.

A trusted subordinate hurriedly stepped forward. "Palace Master?"

Chunyangzi raised his hand.

There was a wisp of pale gold blood qi in his palm, which he forcefully suppressed back into his body.

"It's nothing."

The worry in the subordinate's eyes deepened.

Chunyangzi was clearly unhealed from his heavy injuries, yet he insisted on personally keeping watch on this final night. Others couldn't persuade him, nor did they dare to.

Below, a formation master reported loudly, "The outer ring's Nine Palaces Interlocking Illusion Gate Formation, the Southeast and Southwest gates have merged!"

A voice came from another direction: "The Azure Horned Dragon Root-Coiling Shielding Wall, the western root system connection is complete!"

"The Big Dipper Star-Guiding Shadow-Reflecting Restriction, the last three little star sand towers have been placed!"

"The Crimson Paulownia Meridian-Locking Formation, out of forty-nine earth meridian nodes, forty-six are complete!"

Chunyangzi's eyes narrowed. "The remaining three?"

The trusted subordinate immediately answered, "All on the southwest side. The earth qi there is the most turbid. The Crimson Paulownia roots are facing some resistance entering the soil. It will take another half an hour."

Chunyangzi looked towards the southwest.

Over there, the clouds and mist hung low, and the crimson patterns on the ground were relatively faint. Several Golden Core cultivators were gathered around a deep well, putting their full effort into suppressing the surging earthy-yellow earth qi. The Crimson Paulownia roots, like little crimson snakes, were slowly being fed into the well by the formation masters.

Chunyangzi frowned slightly.

He did not like this kind of resistance.

Any resistance was a flaw.

"I'll go take a look."

His trusted subordinate hastily said, "Palace Master, your injuries..."

"Stay here and oversee the overall situation." Chunyangzi had already started walking.

His figure transformed into a line of golden light, descending toward the southwest formation opening.

At almost the exact same moment.

Deep underground.

A wisp of gray shadow silently glided along the edge of the earth meridian.

Shi Qian seemed to blend entirely into the soil.

His grayish-yellow Dharma robe clung tightly to his skin, its surface covered in tiny earth patterns. Every inch of his muscles was contracting slowly, his breathing lowered to a near-silent level.

The underground Crimson Paulownia roots emanated a warm aura, like growing fire snakes. If he were entangled by them, he would be exposed immediately.

Shi Qian was not in a rush.

Carrying the Mountain-Sinking formation plate in his robes, he inched forward along the crevices of the earth meridian.

The Pure Yang qi from above grew heavier and heavier.

Fine sweat seeped from his forehead, but the moment it appeared, it was dried up by his Earth Element spiritual energy.

"The last three formation openings are indeed here," Shi Qian's eyes were calm.

He first heard the shouts of the formation masters above, and then he sensed Chunyangzi rapidly approaching.

That Pure Yang aura was too distinct.

Like a wounded but still blazing great sun descending from the clouds.

Shi Qian stopped; he couldn't go any further.

Any further, and he would crash into Chunyangzi's perception range.

But the Mountain-Sinking formation plate was still half a zhang away from being placed into the old node.

Crouching within the earth meridian, a trace of hesitation finally appeared in his eyes.

Just then, the soil not far from his side suddenly flashed with a faint purplish-black hue.

An extremely fine thread of poison drilled out from the other side of the underground.

The poison thread was completely silent, like a frozen little snake. It glided an inch along the Crimson Paulownia root before stopping again.

Shi Qian's pupils contracted slightly.

Tan Zhu!

He couldn't see Tan Zhu himself, but he recognized this aura.

The poison thread stopped at the edge of the old node. It didn't destroy the Formation, nor did it touch the Crimson Paulownia root; it just gently coiled, as if marking a specific spot.

The next moment, the poison thread quietly retreated.

A chill swept through Shi Qian's heart.

So, the ones keeping an eye on this place weren't just Heavy Formation Peak. The faction behind Tan Zhu had also chosen to act tonight!

Above, Chunyangzi had landed.

His red-gold Daoist robes fluttered loudly in the night wind.

He stood beside the deep well, looking down, his gaze like a golden lantern illuminating the earth.

Shi Qian held his breath.

With the Mountain-Sinking formation plate tucked in his chest, he remained motionless.

Chunyangzi's gaze swept over the well opening, the Crimson Paulownia roots, the formation masters, and then the ground beneath his feet.

In that instant, Shi Qian felt as if his Divine Soul had been roasted by sunlight.

Chunyangzi frowned.

He seemed to sense a slight abnormality.

However, his injuries were too severe, he had been overworking for days, and he still had to suppress the residual Yin Earth Jade marks within his body. The abnormality was too faint, like a speck of dust in the wind.

While assisting with his spells, Chunyangzi urged in a deep voice, "Speed up."

The nearby formation masters hurriedly complied.

The Crimson Paulownia roots were continuously fed into the well.

The warm roots rapidly extended, pressing directly toward Shi Qian's location.

Shi Qian knew his opportunity would only last a split second.

Deciding to take the risk, he flicked his finger lightly.

The Mountain-Sinking formation plate slid out silently, dropping into a tiny crevice at the edge of the old node.

Almost simultaneously, the Crimson Paulownia roots covered the area.

Shi Qian shrank back, detaching from the earth meridian like a clump of gray soil, and quietly fled into the distance.

Chunyangzi abruptly lowered his head.

"Who?"

Golden light explosively illuminated the deep well.

The earth qi in the well churned, and the Crimson Paulownia roots trembled violently. Several formation masters turned pale from the shockwave.

The underground was already completely empty.

There was only an extremely faint wisp of dust floating up under the golden light before quickly dispersing.

The trusted subordinate rushed over, looking tense. "Palace Master?"

Chunyangzi stared at the bottom of the well, golden light dancing in his eyes.

After a long while, he slowly ordered, "Seal the well."

The subordinate was taken aback. "But the formation lock still needs one last adjustment..."

"Seal it first." Chunyangzi's voice grew deeper. "Adjust it after it's sealed."

The subordinate's heart skipped a beat, and he immediately turned and shouted the order.

"Seal the well!"

Several Golden Core cultivators struck at the same time.

The Crimson Paulownia roots crisscrossed like fire snakes. Three Golden Scale Fire Jades were tossed into the well, explosively transforming into a ball of red-gold firelight. The last corner of the Sun-Supporting Light-Closing Watchtower subsequently lit up. A pale golden light barrier rose from underground, layer upon layer, thoroughly enveloping the well opening.

Chunyangzi stood before the light barrier, his face stern.

"Everyone, double your speed." Chunyangzi's voice overpowered the mountain wind.

"Before dawn, I want the Sun-Supporting Light-Closing Watchtower completely shut."

The cultivators acknowledged in unison.

The night deepened.

On the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, countless formation runes lit up, slowly closing together on the ground like a massive golden sun.

In the distant clouds, a thread of purplish-black poison quietly retracted into the darkness.

Further away, Shi Qian emerged from the shadows of the mountain, his face pale. "The first formation plate was placed by sheer luck, but I still have two more here."

His brows were tightly knit, his expression grave.

Logically, he knew the enemy had become more vigilant, and any slight disturbance could be discovered. But he currently bore a heavy responsibility; no matter how great the risk, he had to bite the bullet and finish it!

Shi Qian pressed himself entirely against the underground rock layer.

Even though the entire Formation was rushing to close, he didn't hastily continue his actions.

He extended his right hand, and a yellowish-brown talisman slightly lit up in his palm. The talisman looked like a severed mountain root; it was exactly the Earth Element secret talisman he had painstakingly cultivated for years - the [Earth Listening Seal].

Shi Qian closed his eyes.

Beneath the Sun-Supporting Yang-Locking Cloud Ascending Altar, the sounds of fire meridians flowing resembled distant drumbeats, slow and heavy.

The sound of the Azure Horned Dragon Spirit Tree taking root was like thin snakes slithering through stone crevices.

The slight tremors of the Nine Palaces formation plate setting into position, the subtle noises of the star-position formation artifacts projecting, the sound of spirit boots landing as Chunyang disciples changed shifts - all were gathered one by one by the Earth Listening Seal, transforming into ripples in the lake of his mind.

Shi Qian counted silently in his heart.

"Thirty-two disciples of Chunyang Peak."

"Nineteen external aides."

"Three fire eyes of the Sun-Patrolling Night-Illuminating Ring have closed. There is still a sliver of a gap in the southwest earth vein."

"There is still a sliver of a chance."

With a shift in thought, Shi Qian's figure slid forward.

He cast his evasion technique, completely soundless.

In no time, he arrived at the concealed depths of the southwest earth vein.

He successfully slipped into a small cavity wrapped in fire and earth.

The cavity was about the size of a stone chamber, its four walls crimson-black. Golden-red fire veins were distributed within the rock layer, like countless red-hot blood vessels. In the center stood three ancient formation nails, each only about a foot long, colored black and yellow, with overlapping mountain formation runes carved onto their heads.

This was one of the secret points for Heavy Formation Peak's hidden maneuvers.

Shi Qian took out a palm-sized earthen plate from his sleeve.

The earthen plate was entirely dark yellow, its surface covered in concentric mountain-like reliefs. Between each relief lay unimaginably tiny talisman teeth.

Shi Qian pressed his fingertips against the plate's edge and infused it with spiritual energy. The earthen plate emitted a low hum.

It was still the [Mountain-Sinking formation plate]!

It possessed no offensive power, but it excelled at suppressing formations, cloaking presence, and shifting traces. As long as it was clasped next to the old formation nail, it could disguise the aura of the Three Peaks Sun-Folding Nail as natural earth meridian sediment. Once the Sun-Supporting Light-Closing Watchtower was shut, the difficulty of finding any clues would multiply several times over.

Shi Qian crouched down, forming a hand seal with his left hand while holding the plate in his right.

Seeing that the second formation plate was about to succeed, a trace of relief appeared in Shi Qian's eyes.

Just half a breath away.

At this very moment, a soft chuckle suddenly came from the corner of the cavity!

All the hairs on Shi Qian's body instantly stood on end.

He didn't turn around. His grayish-brown cloak acted like an overturned earthen lid, instantly protecting his back.

Clink!

A thread of dark purple poisonous light pierced the cloak.

The sound was as fine as a needle dropping onto a jade plate.

The line of light appeared slender, but upon hitting the cloak, it violently exploded into a ring of purplish-black ripples. Wherever the ripples passed, the sand patterns on the cloak's surface turned black grain by grain, rapidly decaying as if a living creature had been poisoned.

Shi Qian's expression changed drastically, and he swiftly turned around.

From the shadows in the corner, Tan Zhu slowly walked out.

Pinched between two fingers of Tan Zhu's left hand was a jade-green needle, as fine as an ox hair.

[Purple Nightmare Bone-Corroding Needle]!

The body of the needle was nearly transparent, with a wisp of dark purple poison thread frantically swimming inside it. When it pierced human flesh, its toxicity would not erupt immediately; instead, it would slowly trace the meridians to find the joints and vital points. Once it encountered a place where spiritual energy was circulating the slowest, it would violently dissolve, causing the Cultivator's bone marrow to feel as if it were being scorched by hot fire, rapidly reducing it to nothingness.
